## 05 — WE OUGHT TO OBEY GOD

You cannot build a life with God on dishonesty. Ananias and Sapphira want the honor of giving everything while secretly keeping part of the money, and their deception brings judgment. Later, the apostles are arrested for preaching Jesus, but an angel releases them, and they return to the temple to teach. When the authorities demand that they stop, Peter answers, **“We ought to obey God rather than men.”** The apostles are beaten, yet they rejoice and continue preaching. **If you want to follow Jesus, be truthful before God and obey Him even when doing so costs you something.**

## The Savior Test

> **“And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?” — Luke 6:46**

Jesus asks you to examine the difference between claiming to follow Him and actually doing what He says. Ananias and Sapphira wanted the appearance of devotion, while the apostles demonstrated their devotion through obedience. Acts 5 shows both sides of that choice.
