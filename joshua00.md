## 00 — BEYOND THE JORDAN

Joshua is about possessing what God has already given you. Israel has been delivered from Egypt, brought through the wilderness, and given the Promised Land—but they are still standing on the other side of the Jordan. The land is theirs, but they must cross over, face what still occupies it, and actually live there. The same thing happens in you. **God can give you an inheritance that you have not yet learned to possess.**

> **“Why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?” — Luke 6:46**

Jesus makes the same distinction Joshua does. Hearing the word and receiving the promise are not the end. **What God gives you must become the life you actually live.**

# JOSHUA — CHAPTERS

### 01 — BE STRONG AND OF GOOD COURAGE
Moses is dead. Joshua must stop looking backward, arise, cross the Jordan, and lead Israel into the land.

### 02 — THE SCARLET CORD
Rahab has heard what God has done. She believes before Jericho falls, acts on what she believes, and binds her household to the people of God.

### 03 — INTO THE JORDAN
The priests step into the river carrying the ark, the waters stop, and Israel crosses on dry ground. God's Presence goes before them, but they still have to cross.

### 04 — TWELVE STONES FROM THE RIVER
Israel carries twelve stones out of the Jordan so the crossing will not be forgotten. What God has brought you through must become something you remember.

### 05 — THE CAPTAIN WITH THE DRAWN SWORD
Israel is circumcised, keeps the Passover, eats from the land, and the manna stops. Then Joshua meets the Captain of the LORD's host and discovers that even the commander must stand under God's command.

### 06 — THE WALLS OF JERICHO
The first great resistance inside the land cannot be overcome by Israel's strength. They obey what God tells them to do, and He brings down what they cannot.

### 07 — THE HIDDEN THING
Israel has defeated Jericho but is defeated by little Ai because something forbidden is hidden inside the camp. Victory outside does not make hidden compromise harmless.

### 08 — THE AMBUSH AT AI
Israel returns to the place where it failed. This time they go under God's direction, and the place of defeat becomes the place of victory.

### 09 — THE WORN-OUT SANDALS
The Gibeonites present convincing evidence, and Israel believes what it sees without asking counsel of the LORD. Appearances can be examined carefully and still lead you wrong.

### 10 — THE DAY THE SUN STOOD STILL
Five kings gather against Israel, but the LORD fights for them. Joshua continues the battle while God does what Joshua could never do for himself.

### 11 — THE WATERS OF MEROM
A vast coalition gathers against Israel. The resistance becomes greater, but the promise has not changed.

### 12 — THE DEFEATED KINGS
The kings that once ruled the land are named one by one. Possession means that the powers which once governed the territory no longer govern it.

### 13 — THERE REMAINETH VERY MUCH LAND
Joshua is old and great victories have already been won, yet God says there is still much land to possess. Real progress is not the same thing as finished work.

### 14 — GIVE ME THIS MOUNTAIN
Caleb has waited forty-five years, but he has not forgotten the promise. At eighty-five he asks for the mountain where the giants still live.

### 15 — THE INHERITANCE OF JUDAH
The promise begins becoming specific territory. Inheritance is no longer merely something Israel believes in; it becomes something they must actually inhabit.

### 16 — THE INHERITANCE OF JOSEPH
Ephraim receives its inheritance but does not completely drive out the Canaanites. Something can belong to God while an old government is still being allowed to remain within it.

### 17 — THE FOREST AND THE IRON CHARIOTS
Joseph's children want more room but fear the people with iron chariots. Joshua tells them to clear the land and possess it anyway.

### 18 — THE LAND BEFORE THEM
Seven tribes still have not possessed their inheritance. Joshua asks the question that reaches through the whole book: **“How long are ye slack to go to possess the land?”**

### 19 — THE LAST INHERITANCES
The remaining tribes receive their portions, and Joshua finally receives his own. The land God promised is becoming the actual dwelling place of His people.

### 20 — THE CITIES OF REFUGE
The possessed land must become a place governed by justice and mercy. Victory is not enough; what has been won must now be ordered according to God.

### 21 — CITIES FOR THE LEVITES
The Levites are given cities throughout Israel, and the book declares that not one good thing God promised has failed. God's presence and service are established throughout the inheritance.

### 22 — THE ALTAR BY THE JORDAN
An altar beside the Jordan nearly starts a war because Israel assumes the worst before hearing why it was built. Zeal for God still requires you to listen before you judge.

### 23 — THE LAST WARNING
The battles are nearly over, but Joshua warns Israel about a different danger: becoming joined again to what God brought them out from under. What could not defeat them openly can still regain influence if they return to it willingly.

### 24 — CHOOSE YOU THIS DAY
Joshua brings Israel back to the final question. After the river has been crossed, the walls have fallen, the kings have been defeated, and the inheritance has been divided, one question remains: **Who will govern you?**

> **“Choose you this day whom ye will serve.” — Joshua 24:15**
