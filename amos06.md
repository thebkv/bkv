# AMOS 06

## 06 — WOE TO THOSE WHO ARE AT EASE IN ZION

### Amos 6 — The Law of Self-Deception  
### *The fatal delusion of security and the consequences of spiritual complacency.*

> *“Woe to them that are at ease in Zion, and trust in the mountain of Samaria.”*  
> — **Amos 6:1 KJV**

---

## **BKV–NR — Narrative Reading**

Amos 6 exposes the soul’s **illusion of safety**—the belief that past revelation ensures present security.  
This is the **Law of Self-Deception**: mistaking comfort for confirmation.

### **I. Ease in Zion (vv 1–7)**
**Zion** symbolizes inner sanctuary and authority.  
To be “at ease in Zion” is to rest on memory rather than present obedience.  
The self congratulates its past victories while ignoring its **“breach of Joseph”**—the fracture of integrity.  

The indulgent faculties—those reclining on ivory beds, drinking from bowls, playing idle songs—will be the **first to go into captivity**.  
What once felt like safety becomes bondage.

### **II. The Oath and the Turning of Justice (vv 8–14)**
God swears by the **Excellency of Jacob**—the divine ideal of Overcoming itself—that arrogance will be corrected.  

Two piercing questions follow:  
1. *Can horses run upon the rock?* — Can human effort move on the firm law of Spirit?  
2. *Will one plough there with oxen?* — Can natural means produce spiritual fruit?  
Both imply futility. The soul has turned **Judgment into gall** (bitterness) and **Righteousness into hemlock** (poison).  
Truth has become distasteful; virtue, inconvenient.  

The result is inevitable: a new captivity that restores right proportion.

---

## **Core BKV Principle — Amos 6**

> **The Law of Self-Deception is the fatal spiritual error where the soul relies on past achievement (ease in Zion) while neglecting present integrity (the breach of Joseph). This complacency in the face of decay leads directly to inversion of values, where judgment becomes bitter and righteousness becomes poison.** 🕊️
