## 12 — THE GATES OPEN OF THEIR OWN ACCORD

You may find yourself trapped in circumstances you cannot change by your own efforts. Herod has already killed James, and now Peter is chained between soldiers, guarded through the night, and awaiting execution. The church prays for him, and while Peter sleeps, an angel wakes him, his chains fall away, and the prison gates open. Peter follows the angel until he finds himself standing free in the street. **When you cannot see a way out, keep your attention on the Lord and follow Him as He shows you the next step.** Herod's power comes to an end, but the word of God continues to grow.

## The Savior Test

> **“With men this is impossible; but with God all things are possible.” — Matthew 19:26**

Jesus teaches you to trust God's power where your own strength has reached its end. Peter could not open the prison, but when God opened the way, Peter had to get up and follow.
