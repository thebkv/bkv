## 06 — THE SLEEPLESS KING

Faithfulness does not become worthless because nothing seems to come from it. Mordecai once saved the king and received no reward, but the record remained. On the very night before Haman intends to ask for Mordecai's death, the forgotten act is read, and Haman is forced to give Mordecai the honor he imagined for himself. Esther did not arrange this, and Mordecai did not arrange it. **What seemed forgotten was still part of the story, while the pride that looked powerful had already begun to fall.**

> **“Whosoever exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.” — Luke 14:11**

Jesus names the same reversal that Esther 6 makes visible: self-exaltation carries a person toward abasement, while honor does not have to be seized.
