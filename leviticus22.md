# LEVITICUS 22

## 22 — THE HOLY THINGS

The disciple learns that holy things are not common things. Priests themselves can become temporarily unable to eat what is holy, outsiders cannot simply take what belongs to the priestly household, and defective offerings cannot be presented to God as though anything will do. The disciple is learning reverence for what God has set apart. Spiritual gifts, revelation, worship, covenant, body, time, calling, and whatever has been consecrated cease to be treated as ordinary possessions. What belongs to God is handled according to its true nature and brought before Him with integrity.

> “Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine.” Matthew 7:6
