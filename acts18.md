## 18 — I HAVE MUCH PEOPLE IN THIS CITY

You may be ready to give up when Jesus still has work for you to do. Paul faces opposition in Corinth, but the Lord speaks to him in a vision and tells him not to be afraid. He promises to be with Paul and says, **“I have much people in this city.”** Paul stays another year and six months, teaching the word of God. Later, Priscilla and Aquila meet Apollos, a man who knows the Scriptures and teaches diligently but still has more to learn. They take him aside and explain the way of God more perfectly. **Keep doing what the Lord has given you to do, and remain willing to learn from those He sends to help you.**

## The Savior Test

> **“Fear not: from henceforth thou shalt catch men.” — Luke 5:10**

Jesus gave Peter this promise when He first called him. Now He gives Paul the same assurance in Corinth. The Lord already knows the people He intends to reach, and He calls His disciples to trust Him and continue the work.
