## 00 — THE HIDDEN QUEEN

Esther is a palace intrigue novella about what happens when belonging to God becomes more important than protecting yourself. Esther enters the palace with her identity hidden, and for a time she can live safely that way. But eventually a crisis comes that makes hiding impossible. She must decide whether she will protect the life she has built or openly stand with the people to whom she belongs. **The same moment comes in you when what you know inwardly must finally determine how you live outwardly.**

> **“Whosoever shall seek to save his life shall lose it; and whosoever shall lose his life shall preserve it.” — Luke 17:33**

Jesus brings you to the same decision Esther eventually faces. There comes a point when protecting yourself can no longer be the thing that governs you.

# ESTHER — CHAPTERS

### 01 — THE QUEEN REFUSES
Ahasuerus displays the glory of his kingdom, but Vashti refuses his command and loses her place as queen. The story begins with a kingdom whose ruler can command outward obedience but cannot command the human will.

### 02 — THE HIDDEN QUEEN
Esther is brought into the palace and becomes queen, but her identity remains hidden. Something belonging to God has entered the kingdom before the kingdom realizes what it is.

### 03 — THE LOT IS CAST
Haman's anger toward Mordecai grows into a decree against the entire Jewish people. A private hatred becomes a power of destruction when it is given authority.

### 04 — THE DECISION
Esther can no longer remain safely hidden. She must decide whether preserving herself matters more than being faithful when her life and the lives of her people are at stake.

### 05 — THE GOLDEN SCEPTRE
Esther carries her decision into action. She enters the king's presence at the risk of her life, receives the sceptre, and begins patiently bringing the hidden enemy into the light.

### 06 — THE SLEEPLESS NIGHT
The king cannot sleep, Mordecai's forgotten faithfulness is discovered, and Haman is forced to honor the very man he intended to destroy. The reversal begins before Esther can see what God is doing.

### 07 — THE SECOND BANQUET
Esther finally names both who she is and who the enemy is. What had remained hidden is spoken openly, and the destructive power that threatened her is exposed before the king.

### 08 — THE COUNTER-DECREE
Haman is gone, but the decree he created is still in force. Removing the source of destruction does not automatically remove everything that source has already set in motion.

### 09 — THE DAY OF DELIVERANCE
The day appointed for destruction becomes the day of deliverance. The power that had been turned against God's people is broken, and the reversal becomes something they are commanded to remember.

### 10 — MORDECAI EXALTED
Mordecai ends where Haman wanted to stand: beside the king and exercising authority for the good of the people. The book closes with a different kind of government established beside the throne.
