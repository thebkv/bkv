# Exodus 24 — The Covenant Is Sealed and the People Draw Near to God

## BKV Insight

Exodus 24 shows **the delivered faculties formally entering the covenant God has established with them, and that covenant opens the way into His Presence**. The Word is spoken, written, read, and accepted; blood is placed upon the altar and sprinkled upon the people; then Israel's representatives ascend the mountain, see the God of Israel, and eat and drink before Him. Moses is called still higher, into the cloud itself, where he will receive God's pattern for the sanctuary. The chapter gives the disciple a clear movement: **hear the Word, come under it, receive the covenant established by blood, and draw near to Almighty God.** In the larger biblical pattern, this reaches directly to Jesus Christ, whose own blood establishes the new covenant and brings His people into communion with God.

## Canonical History & Context

Exodus 24 completes the covenant-making sequence at Sinai. The Ten Commandments were spoken in Exodus 20, the covenant judgments were given through Exodus 21–23, and now the covenant is formally confirmed. Moses reports the LORD's words to Israel, writes them down, builds an altar with twelve pillars for the twelve tribes, offers sacrifices, reads the Book of the Covenant, and sprinkles the people with “the blood of the covenant.” Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders then ascend the mountain, where they see the God of Israel and share a covenant meal in His Presence. Afterward Moses is called farther up the mountain to receive the tables of stone. He enters the cloud and remains there forty days and forty nights. This prepares directly for Exodus 25–31, where God gives Moses the pattern of the Tabernacle, priesthood, and worship by which He will dwell among Israel.

## Natural Reading

### v1–2 — God Establishes the Order of Approach

The LORD calls Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders to come up toward Him:

> “Come up unto the LORD... and worship ye afar off.”

Then the order narrows:

> **“Moses alone shall come near the LORD.”**

The covenant people belong to one body, yet their approach has an appointed order. The people remain below, the elders ascend farther, and Moses is permitted to come nearest.

The physical arrangement on the mountain makes the structure visible: **approach to the Presence proceeds according to God's invitation and God's order**.

This prepares for the Tabernacle, where the same principle will take architectural form in the court, holy place, and most holy place.

### v3–4 — The Word Is Heard, Accepted, and Written

Moses comes down and tells Israel:

> “all the words of the LORD, and all the judgments.”

The people answer with one voice:

> **“All the words which the LORD hath said will we do.”**

The faculties consciously place themselves beneath the Word they have received.

Then:

> “Moses wrote all the words of the LORD.”

The spoken covenant is given durable form.

Moses builds an altar beneath the mountain and sets up:

> “twelve pillars, according to the twelve tribes of Israel.”

The physical structure expresses the covenant body:

**one altar before God, twelve tribes gathered around it.**

The whole Israelite order is being brought into covenant with the LORD.

### v5–8 — The Covenant Is Sealed in Blood

Young men offer burnt offerings and peace offerings unto the LORD. Moses takes the blood:

> “half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.”

Then he takes:

> **“the book of the covenant”**

and reads it before the people.

Again Israel answers:

> **“All that the LORD hath said will we do, and be obedient.”**

The people have heard the covenant twice and answered twice. Their second response makes the submission explicit:

**“we will do, and be obedient.”**

Then Moses takes the remaining blood and sprinkles it upon the people:

> **“Behold the blood of the covenant, which the LORD hath made with you concerning all these words.”**

The chapter's central physical act is blood touching **altar and people**.

The altar stands on the Godward side of the covenant. The people stand on the human side. The sacrificial blood binds the enacted covenant together.

The Word has now been:

**heard,  
written,  
read,  
accepted,  
and sealed in blood.**

The faculties have formally entered the covenant order of God.

### v9–11 — Covenant Leads Into Communion

After the covenant is sealed, Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and the seventy elders ascend:

> **“And they saw the God of Israel.”**

The text describes what appears beneath His feet:

> “as it were a paved work of a sapphire stone, and as it were the body of heaven in his clearness.”

The imagery is physical and luminous: **sapphire, pavement, heaven, clearness**. The representatives of Israel have risen from the dust beneath Sinai into a vision of extraordinary heavenly clarity before God.

And:

> “upon the nobles of the children of Israel he laid not his hand.”

Instead:

> **“they saw God, and did eat and drink.”**

This is the destination of the covenant movement in this chapter: **communion in the Presence of God**.

The people who began Exodus making bricks under Pharaoh are now represented by elders sitting at a covenant meal before the God of Israel.

The movement is immense:

**BONDAGE → DELIVERANCE → COVENANT → COMMUNION.**

### v12–14 — Moses Is Called Higher

After the covenant meal, the LORD says to Moses:

> **“Come up to me into the mount, and be there.”**

There is a deeper approach still.

God will give Moses:

> “tables of stone, and a law, and commandments which I have written.”

Earlier:

> “Moses wrote all the words of the LORD.”

Now God speaks of that:

> **“which I have written.”**

The covenant Word is moving toward inscription by God's own act.

Moses rises with Joshua and goes toward:

> “the mount of God.”

Joshua, whose name means **“the LORD saves,”** accompanies Moses partway into this ascent, while Aaron and Hur remain available below to judge matters among the people.

The whole body remains ordered while Moses enters more deeply into the Presence.

### v15–18 — Moses Enters the Cloud

> “And Moses went up into the mount, and a cloud covered the mount.”

Then:

> **“the glory of the LORD abode upon mount Sinai.”**

The cloud covers the mountain six days.

On the seventh:

> “he called unto Moses out of the midst of the cloud.”

Again the sevenfold rhythm appears. Moses waits beneath the covering cloud until God calls him farther.

To Israel below:

> “the sight of the glory of the LORD was like devouring fire on the top of the mount.”

Then:

> **“Moses went into the midst of the cloud.”**

He enters the very place that appears as devouring fire from below.

There he remains:

> “forty days and forty nights.”

Exodus 24 therefore ends with Moses hidden inside the divine Presence.

And what follows is crucial.

From within that cloud, God will begin to say:

> **“Let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them.”**

The ascent of Moses prepares for the descent of the divine pattern.

Israel has been brought to God's mountain.

Now God will reveal how His Presence can dwell **in the midst of Israel**.

## Fractal Notes & References

Exodus 24 gathers the Sinai covenant into a clear sequence:

**WORD → HEARING → OBEDIENCE → SACRIFICE → BLOOD → COVENANT → APPROACH → COMMUNION.**

This pattern reaches directly into the Gospel. At the Last Supper, Jesus takes the cup and identifies His own blood with the covenant: “This is my blood of the new testament” (Matthew 26:28; Mark 14:24). The wording deliberately recalls Moses' declaration in Exodus 24:8. The blood of sacrificed animals seals the Sinai covenant; **Jesus Christ gives His own blood for the new covenant and brings His people into communion with God**.

Hebrews 9 explicitly returns to this scene, recalling Moses' reading of the covenant and sprinkling of blood before turning to Christ, who enters the greater and more perfect tabernacle by His own blood. The Exodus pattern therefore remains intact while reaching its fulfillment in Christ: **covenant blood establishes access to the Presence**.

The covenant meal also belongs to the same movement. Exodus 24 places eating and drinking before God immediately after the blood of the covenant. The Last Supper joins covenant blood and table fellowship again. Scripture ultimately carries this pattern toward the completed communion of God and His people.

The chapter also marks a major transition within Exodus itself. Up to this point, Israel has been moving toward God:

**Egypt → wilderness → Sinai → covenant → ascent.**

Beginning in Exodus 25, the direction begins to turn. Moses enters God's Presence, receives the heavenly pattern, and brings it down so that a sanctuary can be built among the people. **The people have come to God; now God prepares to dwell among the people.**

## Onomastics & Key Terms

| Name / Term | Textual / Linguistic Meaning | BKV Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Moses — Mosheh** | Exodus 2:10 connects the name with *mashah*, “to draw out” | **The Drawn-Out One**, now entering the cloud as mediator to receive God's pattern for the dwelling below |
| **Israel — Yisrael** | Genesis 32:28 associates the name with striving with God and prevailing | **God-Prevails**; the complete body of faculties formally brought under the covenant of the LORD |
| **Aaron — Aharon** | Etymology uncertain; Scripture does not self-define the name | Functionally, **the developing priestly representative**, permitted to ascend with Moses and the elders |
| **Nadab — Nadav** | From a root associated with willingness, generosity, or voluntary giving | One of Aaron's sons included in the covenant ascent and developing priestly order |
| **Abihu — Avihu** | Commonly understood as “He is my father” or “My father is He” | One of Aaron's sons brought into representative proximity to the divine Presence |
| **Joshua — Yehoshua** | “The LORD saves” / “YHWH is salvation” | **The faculty of divinely grounded salvation and overcoming**, being prepared beside Moses for future leadership |
| **Hur — Chur** | Etymology uncertain; sometimes associated with whiteness or nobility | Functionally, **supporting government**, remaining with Aaron while Moses ascends |
| **Sinai — Sinai** | Ancient place-name; precise etymology uncertain | **The mountain of covenant encounter**, where Israel receives God's revealed order |
| **Covenant — Berith** | Covenant, binding agreement | **The divinely established relationship under which the faculties belong to God and approach Him** |
| **Blood — Dam** | Blood | **Sacrificial life sealing the covenant**, pointing forward to the blood of Jesus Christ and the new covenant |
| **Sapphire — Sappir** | Sapphire or lapis-like precious stone | **Heavenly clearness beneath the divine Presence**, taken directly from the vision experienced by the elders |
| **Glory — Kavod** | Weight, honor, splendor, glory | **The substantial manifest Presence of the LORD**, appearing as devouring fire and filling the cloud into which Moses enters |

Exodus 24 has one governing movement:

**God establishes covenant relationship, and covenant relationship opens the way into His Presence.**

The people hear the Word.

They agree to obey it.

The covenant is sealed in blood.

Their representatives ascend.

They eat and drink before God.

Moses goes farther and enters the cloud.

And there, hidden in the glory of the LORD, he is about to receive the pattern for the sanctuary.

The direction of Exodus is changing.

The LORD said in Exodus 19:

> **“I brought you unto myself.”**

He has done it.

Now He will reveal the next purpose:

> **“Let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them.”**
