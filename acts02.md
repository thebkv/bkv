## 02 — THE FIRE FALLS

Jesus promised His disciples the Holy Ghost, and now they receive Him. The Spirit fills them, and they begin speaking in languages they have never learned. Peter, who once denied Jesus, stands before the crowd and boldly proclaims that Jesus has risen from the dead. The people are moved to repentance, and three thousand are baptized. They begin praying together, sharing what they have, and caring for one another. **When you receive the Holy Ghost, you receive power to follow Jesus, speak His word, and begin living as He taught.**

## The Savior Test

> **“But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.” — John 14:26**

Jesus promised that the Holy Ghost would come and teach His disciples. Acts 2 shows that promise fulfilled as the Spirit gives them understanding, courage, and power to live what Jesus taught.
