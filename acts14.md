## 14 — THROUGH MUCH TRIBULATION

Following Jesus will not always make your life easier. Paul and Barnabas preach the Gospel, heal a crippled man, and see many people believe. But the same crowd that wants to worship them as gods is soon persuaded to stone Paul and leave him for dead. Paul rises, continues preaching, and even returns to the cities where he was persecuted to strengthen the new disciples. He tells them that they must enter the kingdom of God through much tribulation. **When following Jesus brings trouble, keep following Him. God can strengthen you through the very things that make you want to give up.**

## The Savior Test

> **“In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.” — John 16:33**

Jesus told His disciples to expect trouble, but also gave them a reason to take courage. Paul lives out that teaching in Acts 14. He suffers, gets back up, and continues the work Jesus gave him.
