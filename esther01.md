## 01 — THE QUEEN REFUSES

You will not always recognize the beginning of change when it happens. Esther begins with a powerful king, a royal feast, and a queen who refuses his command. Vashti loses her place, and the whole event seems to be nothing more than disorder inside the palace. Esther has not even appeared yet. **Something in the old arrangement can give way before you have any idea what God is preparing to put in its place.**

> **“No man also seweth a piece of new cloth on an old garment.” — Mark 2:21**

Jesus shows that the new cannot always be added while everything old remains exactly as it was. Sometimes room has to be made before what comes next can appear.
