## 10 — NEXT TO THE KING

The final test of power is what you use it for. Haman wanted authority because he needed his own importance confirmed; Mordecai ends the book next to the king, using authority to seek the good of his people and speak peace to them. The empire is still there, but the authority within it is now being used differently. **The same kind of authority that Haman used to serve pride and destruction is now being used to serve life.**

> **“Whosoever will be great among you, shall be your minister: And whosoever of you will be the chiefest, shall be servant of all.” — Mark 10:43–44**

Jesus gives the same measure of greatness. The highest place is not given so that you can make life serve you, but so that you can use what you have been given to serve others.
