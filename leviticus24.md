# LEVITICUS 24

## 24 — LIGHT, BREAD, AND THE NAME

The disciple learns to maintain the sanctuary continually: the lamp burns, the bread remains before God, and the divine Name is treated as holy. Then a man born of an Israelite mother and an Egyptian father blasphemes the Name, bringing the unresolved mixture of Israel and Egypt directly into the camp's holy order. The disciple is being gathered toward an undivided center. Light must remain burning, communion must remain before God, and God's Name—His revealed identity and authority—must not be carried by the divided consciousness as though it were common speech.
