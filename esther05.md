## 05 — THE GOLDEN SCEPTRE

A decision to be faithful eventually has to become an action. Esther has already said, “if I perish, I perish”; now she puts on her royal clothing and walks into the inner court while the danger is still real. The king extends the sceptre, and the way forward opens, but Esther does not rush the rest. **Do the faithful thing that is actually yours to do, then take the next step when it comes.** Haman moves the other direction: one man's refusal to honor him becomes so important that all his wealth and favor mean nothing, and resentment begins building the instrument of its own destruction.

> **“Why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?” — Luke 6:46**

Jesus makes the distinction plain. What you have settled inwardly must eventually become something you actually do.
