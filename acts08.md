## 08 — THE WAY IN THE WILDERNESS

God can lead you somewhere you never expected to go. Persecution drives the disciples out of Jerusalem, but Philip carries the Gospel into Samaria, where many believe and are baptized. Simon sees the power of the Holy Ghost and tries to buy it, but Peter tells him that God's gift cannot be purchased. Then an angel sends Philip into the wilderness, where he meets an Ethiopian reading Isaiah. Philip listens to the Spirit, joins the man, and shows him how the Scripture points to Jesus. The Ethiopian believes and is baptized. **When you follow the Lord, even an unexpected change in your plans can bring you to someone who needs what He has given you.**

## The Savior Test

> **“The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit.” — John 3:8**

Jesus teaches that the life of the Spirit moves beyond what you can predict or control. Acts 8 shows that movement through Philip. He follows God's direction, and the Gospel reaches people he could never have reached by remaining where he was.
