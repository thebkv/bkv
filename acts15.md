## 15 — WE SHALL BE SAVED THROUGH GRACE

You can make following God harder than He has made it. Some believers insist that the Gentiles must be circumcised and keep the law of Moses before they can be saved. But Peter reminds them that God has already given the Gentiles the Holy Ghost and purified their hearts by faith. Why put another burden on people whom God has received? The apostles agree and send a letter telling the Gentile believers what they must abstain from, without requiring them to become Jews. **Receive the grace Jesus gives you, and be careful not to make other people earn what He has freely given.**

## The Savior Test

> **“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.” — Matthew 11:28**

Jesus calls those who are carrying heavy burdens to come to Him. Acts 15 shows the apostles learning to protect that invitation. Salvation comes through the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and those who come to Him must not be burdened with requirements God has not placed upon them.
