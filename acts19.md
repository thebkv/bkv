## 19 — THE NAME ABOVE EVERY POWER

You cannot use the name of Jesus as a substitute for knowing and following Him. In Ephesus, Paul lays hands on disciples who receive the Holy Ghost, and God works extraordinary miracles through him. But when seven men try to cast out an evil spirit using Jesus' name, the spirit answers, “Jesus I know, and Paul I know; but who are ye?” Their attempt fails, and the people of Ephesus begin to recognize the difference between the power of God and the magical practices they have trusted. Many confess their deeds and burn their books of magic. **When you come to Jesus, you must also let go of the things you once trusted to give you power over your life.** Even the city's idol-making trade begins to feel the effects as the Gospel spreads.

## The Savior Test

> **“Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.” — Matthew 7:21**

Jesus warns that people may even claim to cast out devils in His name without truly following Him. Acts 19 shows exactly that danger. His name is not a magic formula. The power belongs to the living Lord, and those who follow Him must learn to trust and obey Him.
