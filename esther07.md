## 07 — THE SECOND BANQUET

Eventually you have to name a destructive thing by what it is actually producing in you. Esther first says, **“I and my people,”** openly joining herself to the people she had once kept hidden. Then she names Haman: **“The adversary and enemy is this wicked Haman.”** Anger, fear, pride, or resentment may have begun with a reason, but the deeper question is what it has become and what it is now doing. **When you can see the fruit clearly, you no longer have to keep defending the thing that produced it.**

> **“Ye shall know them by their fruits.” — Matthew 7:16**

Jesus gives the same test. Look beyond the explanation a thing gives for itself and examine what it actually produces.
