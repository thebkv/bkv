## 01 — THE PROMISE OF THE FATHER

Jesus has risen from the dead, and His disciples are eager to know when He will restore the kingdom to Israel. But Jesus directs their attention to something they must receive first: the Holy Ghost. He tells them to wait in Jerusalem until they are given power to become His witnesses. After He ascends, they return together, continue in prayer, and restore the number of the twelve apostles. **You may know what Jesus has called you to do, but you still need His power to do it.** Before you rush ahead with your own plans, seek the Holy Ghost and follow the Lord's instructions.

## The Savior Test

> **“Tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from on high.” — Luke 24:49**

Jesus gives His disciples the same instruction before His ascension. He knows the work ahead of them, and He tells them to receive the power they will need before they begin.
