## 09 — THE DAY TURNED AROUND

Freedom that has been given still has to be lived when the old threat actually returns. The appointed day comes, the Jews gather and stand, and the people once marked for destruction prevail. Yet the chapter does not end with victory: they refuse the spoil, enter into rest, send portions to one another, give to the poor, and establish a remembrance for those who come after them. **Deliverance becomes complete in the way you live after the battle is over.** What God brings you through can become rest and generosity instead of another reason to live for yourself.

> **“Freely ye have received, freely give.” — Matthew 10:8**

Jesus shows the direction of a life that has received something from God: what you have been given becomes something you can give.
