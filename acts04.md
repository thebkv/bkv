## 04 — WE CANNOT BUT SPEAK

Following Jesus may bring you into conflict with people who want you to stop. Peter and John are arrested for healing a man and preaching the resurrection, but they refuse to deny what they have seen and heard. When the authorities threaten them, they return to the other believers and pray, not for the opposition to disappear, but for courage to keep speaking God's word. The Holy Ghost fills them, and they continue with boldness. Their faith also changes how they live together, as they share what they have so that no one among them lacks what he needs. **When fear tells you to hold back, turn to the Lord in prayer and do what He has given you to do.**

## The Savior Test

> **“And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul.” — Matthew 10:28**

Jesus had already prepared His disciples to face opposition without surrendering to fear. Acts 4 shows them doing exactly that. They trust Him, pray for courage, and continue His work.
