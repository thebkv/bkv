# LEVITICUS 11

## 11 — CLEAN AND UNCLEAN

The disciple learns to distinguish what really belongs with God-centered life. And what doesn't. Israel practices this  constantly even while eating, touching, and going about ordinary life. the discernment between clean and unclean becomes central to how they live. You need the same discernment. Not every desire should be followed, not every thought should be believed, and not every influence should be allowed to shape you. Let God show you what belongs with that new life and what must be left behind.

And does this pass the Savior test?

> “That which cometh out of the man, that defileth the man. For from within, out of the heart of men…” Mark 7:20–21
