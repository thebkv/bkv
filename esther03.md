## 03 — THE ENEMY'S DECREE

Be careful what you allow to gain authority within you. Haman begins with anger toward one man, but he is not satisfied until that hatred becomes a decree against an entire people. What begins as resentment can grow until it starts governing what you think, what you choose, and how you treat others. **Do not give a destructive thing the authority to rule you.**

> **“Whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin.” — John 8:34**

Jesus points to the same danger. What you keep obeying eventually becomes your master.
