## 03 — THE LOT IS CAST

Watch what you allow to rule within you. Haman has rank, power, and honor, but he cannot get past one man who refuses to bow to him. His resentment grows until one man's refusal becomes a reason to destroy an entire people. Then the king gives him the signet ring, and what began inside Haman starts using the whole empire to carry it. **A resentment you give authority to can begin governing the way you think, speak, judge, and act.**

> **“Whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin.” — John 8:34**

Jesus gives the warning in its simplest form: what you keep obeying can become your master.
