# LEVITICUS 26

## 26 — THE TWO PATHS

The disciple is shown plainly what alignment with God produces and what persistent resistance does to the life. Walking in God's statutes leads to fruitfulness, security, peace, divine Presence, and freedom from the old yoke. Refusing His order produces an escalating disintegration until strength, land, sanctuary, and community collapse. Yet even in exile God remembers His covenant. The disciple sees the architecture of the two paths: obedience gathers the life into divine order, resistance fragments it, and humbled return opens again toward restoration.

> “If ye keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love.” John 15:10
