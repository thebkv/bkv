## 20 — THE BATTLE IS THE LORD'S
You will face things that seem stronger than you. Israel sees horses, chariots, and armies greater than its own, but God tells His people not to be afraid. He reminds them that the same Lord who brought them out of Egypt is going with them into battle. **You do not have to defeat everything by your own strength. Your part is to trust the Lord, obey Him, and stand where He has called you to stand.** The size of what opposes you does not determine what God can accomplish.
> **“In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.” — John 16:33**
Jesus does not promise you a life without conflict. He tells you why you can face it without fear: He has already overcome the world.
