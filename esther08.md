## 08 — THE SECOND DECREE

Removing what ruled you does not automatically repair everything it taught your life to do. Haman is dead, but his decree is still moving through the empire. The ring changes hands, a new word goes into the same territory, and the people who were marked for destruction are given authority to gather and stand. The same thing can happen in you: fear, resentment, pride, or an old habit can lose its place while reactions and patterns formed under it remain. **The old ruler must be replaced by a new government that reaches the places where the old one used to rule.**

> **“No man can serve two masters.” — Matthew 6:24**

Jesus brings the issue down to government. Freedom is not only the removal of an old master; the life must come under the one you will now serve.
