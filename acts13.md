## 13 — A LIGHT TO THE GENTILES

When the Holy Ghost calls you to serve, you must be willing to go where He sends you. Barnabas and Saul are praying and fasting when the Spirit sends them out to preach Jesus. They meet opposition from Elymas, who tries to keep a governor from believing, but the governor receives the word of God. Later, Paul tells the people at Antioch how God fulfilled His promise to Israel by raising Jesus from the dead. Some believe, while others reject the message, and Paul turns to the Gentiles, who gladly receive it. **You cannot make another person believe, but you can faithfully bring them the truth and leave their response to God.**

## The Savior Test

> **“And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words, when ye depart out of that house or city, shake off the dust of your feet.” — Matthew 10:14**

Jesus taught His disciples to carry His message without trying to force others to receive it. Acts 13 shows Paul and Barnabas doing exactly that. They proclaim Jesus, welcome those who believe, and continue their work when others refuse to listen.
