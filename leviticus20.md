# LEVITICUS 20

## 20 — LEAVE THE OLD LIFE BEHIND

God calls you to leave behind the things that once ruled you. Leviticus 20 deals with practices that belonged to the nations around Israel, including false worship, sexual sin, and seeking guidance from spirits. God had separated Israel from those nations so they could belong to Him. You cannot follow God and keep returning to the old powers, desires, and ways of living that He called you out of.

> “No man can serve two masters.” Matthew 6:24
