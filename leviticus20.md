# LEVITICUS 20

## 20 — WHEN THE PEOPLE LOOK AWAY

The disciple learns that what has been separated to God cannot be handed back to the powers from which it was separated. Molech, familiar spirits, forbidden unions, and the command not to look away all concern the government of life: allegiance, desire, generation, spiritual counsel, and community responsibility. God says, “I am the LORD your God, which have separated you from other people.” The disciple therefore withdraws his faculties from former masters—fear, appetite, inherited patterns, false spiritual authority, and divided allegiance—and keeps what God has claimed under God's government.
