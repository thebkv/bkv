## 07 — I SEE THE HEAVENS OPENED

You can know the Scriptures and still resist the God they reveal. Stephen reminds the council how God spoke to Abraham, raised up Joseph, and delivered Israel through Moses, even though His people repeatedly rejected those He sent. Now the council is defending the temple and the law while refusing to hear the truth about Jesus. Stephen tells them they are resisting the Holy Ghost, and they become so angry that they stone him. Yet Stephen sees Jesus standing at the right hand of God and asks the Lord to forgive his killers. **When God shows you something that challenges what you believe, listen to Him instead of defending yourself.** Stephen follows Jesus even when the people around him refuse to listen.

## The Savior Test

> **“O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, thou that killest the prophets, and stonest them which are sent unto thee, how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!” — Matthew 23:37**

Jesus had already confronted this same refusal to receive those whom God sends. Stephen shows both sides of His teaching: the council repeats the rejection Jesus warned about, while Stephen follows His example by asking forgiveness for the very people putting him to death.
