# DEUTERONOMY 19

## 19 — THE CITY OF REFUGE
Your anger does not give you the right to condemn someone. God commands Israel to establish cities of refuge because a man who kills accidentally must not be treated like a murderer. He also forbids moving your neighbor's boundaries and condemning someone on the word of a single witness. **You must learn the truth before you judge, protect those who have done no wrong, and refuse to use anger or false testimony against another person.** Justice begins when you stop treating what you feel as proof of what someone has done.
> **“Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.” — John 7:24**
Jesus gives you the same responsibility. Do not let anger, appearances, or accusations decide what is true. Judge rightly.
