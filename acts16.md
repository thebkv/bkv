## 16 — THE PRISON DOORS OPEN

God may close the way you intended to go because He has somewhere else for you to be. Paul wants to preach in Asia, but the Holy Ghost redirects him toward Macedonia. There he meets Lydia, delivers a young woman from an evil spirit, and is beaten and imprisoned for it. At midnight, Paul and Silas pray and sing praises to God. An earthquake opens the prison doors, but instead of escaping, they remain and save the jailer from killing himself. He and his household hear the Gospel and believe. **When you follow the Lord, keep trusting Him even when obedience brings you somewhere you never wanted to go.** Paul entered that prison as a captive, but before the night was over, the jailer had found salvation.

## The Savior Test

> **“And he must needs go through Samaria.” — John 4:4**

Jesus went through Samaria and met a woman whose life was changed by their encounter. Paul is led into Macedonia, where unexpected encounters also bring people to salvation. Jesus shows you how to follow the Father's direction and recognize the people He places before you.
