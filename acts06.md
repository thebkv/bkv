## 06 — FULL OF FAITH AND POWER

As the number of disciples grows, some widows are being neglected in the daily distribution of food. The apostles recognize the problem and appoint seven men full of the Holy Ghost and wisdom to take care of it. They continue in prayer and preaching, while others take responsibility for meeting the people's needs. The word of God spreads, and Stephen, one of the seven, becomes a powerful witness for Jesus. **When God gives you work to do, serve faithfully where He has placed you.** The Holy Ghost can work through you whether you are feeding the hungry, caring for others, or speaking God's word.

## The Savior Test

> **“Whosoever will be great among you, let him be your minister.” — Matthew 20:26**

Jesus teaches His disciples that greatness is found in serving others. Acts 6 shows His teaching taking shape in the growing church. The apostles and the seven have different responsibilities, but all are serving the same Lord.
