## 18 — HEAR YE HIM

You do not need to search everywhere for a voice that can tell you what to do. Israel is entering a land filled with people seeking direction through divination, spirits, enchantments, and the dead, but God tells His people not to follow them. He promises instead to raise up a Prophet and gives Israel one simple command: **“unto him ye shall hearken.”** Jesus is that Prophet. **God has already told you which voice to follow.**

> **“This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him.” — Matthew 17:5**

The Father gives the same command Moses gave Israel. When other voices compete to direct your life, hear Jesus.
