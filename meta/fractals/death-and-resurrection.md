## The Pattern

One of the deepest movements in Scripture is also one of the simplest: **something dies, and God brings life out of it.**

You see it everywhere once you notice it. A seed goes into the ground before it bears fruit. Israel goes down into Egypt before God brings them out, and then passes through the sea to leave the old life behind. Jonah goes down into the deep before he is brought back up and sent again. The sacrifice goes onto the altar. Jesus goes into the tomb.

These are very different stories, but they move in the same direction. **The way to new life passes through the end of the old one.**

That matters to the disciple because death and resurrection is not only something we believe happened to Jesus, or something we expect after physical death. Jesus says the pattern begins in us now. There are things we keep trying to preserve that cannot come with us into the life He is bringing forth.

**Death → Burial → Resurrection → New Life**

That is the pattern this page follows through Scripture.


## The Savior Test

Jesus does not merely teach this pattern. He lives it.

And then He says:

> "If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me."
>
> — Matthew 16:24

The disciple follows the same movement. Not by seeking suffering for its own sake, but by refusing to preserve the old life when following Christ requires surrendering it.

Jesus gives the promise on the other side:

> "Whosoever will lose his life for my sake, the same shall save it."
>
> — Luke 9:24

That is the pattern: **what you keep trying to save cannot become the new thing God is bringing forth.**

The question for the disciple is:

**What am I trying to keep alive that God is asking me to surrender?**
