# Sansannah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** san-san'-nah

## Meanings

- palm branch
- secret instruction

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of southern Judah (Josh. 15:31).

## Metaphysical

Victory, through knowledge of Truth (palm branch, secret instruction; palms 
denote victory, conquest, triumph, resurrection, life; secret instruction bespeaks the inspiration or inner 
teaching of Spirit). "Ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."
