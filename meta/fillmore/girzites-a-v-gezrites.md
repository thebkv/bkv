# Girzites (A.V., Gezrites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Gezer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Inhabitants of Gezer (I Sam. 27:8).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the consciousness in man that was symbolized by the city 
of Gezer while under the Canaanite, or error, dominion. (See GEZER.)
