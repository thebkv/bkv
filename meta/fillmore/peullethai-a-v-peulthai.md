# Peullethai (A.V., Peulthai)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Peulthai), pe-ul-leO-thai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Obed-edom, and a doorkeeper in the tabernacle, in DavidOs reign (I Chron. 26:5).

## Metaphysical

Love serving, and guarding the door of one's thoughts (Peullethai was a 
doorkeeper in the tabernacle, in David's reign. David represents love, and a doorkeeper suggests that 
which guards one's thoughts. Wages and works signify service too, as does Obed-edom the father of 
Peullethai.) Wages of Jehovah, works ofJah, my wages, my works, reveal two things: the fact that this 
idea of loving service is still mixed with personal, carnal beliefs and tendencies that give to service the 
feeling of burden and labor; and a deep sense of unity in consciousness, a realization by the individual 
that he and Jehovah are one.
