# Menuhoth (A.V., Manahethites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Manahethites), menu'-hoth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The same people as the Manahathites. They were inhabitants of a place, half of whom were 
descended from Shobal, the founder of Kiriath-jearim, and half from Salma, who founded Beth-lehem 
(see I Chronicles 2:52, 54).
