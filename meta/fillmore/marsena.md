# Marsena

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mSr'-se-nCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the seven princes of Media and Persia who were next to the king and were his counselors, 
"who saw the king's face, and sat first in the kingdom" (Esth. 1:14).

## Metaphysical

One of the seven creative principles, or dominant ruling thoughts, that are 
developed in the natural man (seven is the number of the natural man). (See CARSHENA.) This 
foundation thought, which in its true essence is spiritual and of great value (dignified man, worthy, 
honorable) to the individual, is at this stage of its unfoldment ruled over by the puffed- up personal will 
(King Ahasuerus) and gives its substance to the building up and sustaining of the egotistical phase of the 
will.

Mars' Hill (in A. V., Mars' Hill; in A. S. V., Areopagus), mars hill.

A hill in Athens, with an open space, where the court of the Areopagus convened. It was there 
that Paul preached his sermon to the men of Athens, taking for his text the inscription that he found on 
one of their altars, "To AN UNKNOWN GOD" (Acts 17:22, 23).

Metaphysical. See AREOPAGUS.
