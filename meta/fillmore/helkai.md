# Helkai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hel'-kai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A priest, and head of the house of Meraioth (Neh. 12:15).

## Metaphysical

Meraioth means revelations; it also has directly opposing meanings, rebellions 
and perversions. This shows a divided mind, a consciousness that entertains both Truth and error, God 
and Devil. Helkai the priest, and head of the house of Meraioth, symbolizes a decision made in favor of 
Truth— a choosing of that which pertains to Spirit only ( Jehovah is m lot).
