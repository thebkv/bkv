# Nephtoah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** neph'-to-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The fountain of the waters of Nephtoah" was a place on the border between Judah and 
Benjamin (Josh. 15:9; 18:15).

## Metaphysical

Freeing the consciousness from one's obstructing thoughts and beliefs, through 
prayer, praise, and faith (Judah and Benjamin), so that the understanding may be renewed and the waters 
of life may spring up and flow in freely (opening, loosening, unbinding, spring, entrance, insight, 
instruction ).
