# Salmone

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** sal-mo'-ne

## Meanings

- agitated by the sea
- tossing
- billow
- commotion
- of the sea

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A high, rocky point on the eastern shore of the island of Crete. The boat in which Paul was being 
taken as a prisoner to Rome passed "over against Salmone" (Acts 27:7).

## Metaphysical

A very unrested state of thought (agitated by the sea, tossing, commotion).
