# Jered

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je'-red

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ezrah (I Chron. 4:18). The same name as Jared.

## Metaphysical

Virtually the same meaning as Jared: the descent of the higher Truth thoughts into 
the subconsciousness (flowing south) and the body consciousness in one's seemingly mortal state 
(brought low), there to act as leaven and so to raise the whole of the individual to a higher, even to 
spiritual, understanding and demonstration. (See JARED.)
