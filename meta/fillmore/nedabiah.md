# Nedabiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ned-a-bi-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jeconiah the captive (I Chron. 3:18).

## Metaphysical

The fruit of the will activated by I AM (whom Jehovah impels; Jeconiah, father of 
Nedabiah, was a onetime king of Judah, and a king always represents a phase of the will or ruling power 
in individual consciousness). This fruit of the will impelled by I AM is insight into the largeness, 
broadness, nobility, and generosity of Spirit in its dealings with and expression in and through man 
(Jehovah gives spontaneously, give willingly to Jehovah, largess of Jehovah).
