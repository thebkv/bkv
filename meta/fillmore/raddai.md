# Raddai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** rad'-dai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Lifth son of Jesse, and brother of David (I Chron. 2: 14).

## Metaphysical

A belief in expansion and dominion (walking, expanding, subduing, ruling), but 
mixed with the sense idea of rulership, in which a domineering, oppressing tendency exists (treading 
down, trampling, overlaying, dominating).
