# Jada

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ja'-dCE

## Meanings

- perceiving
- learning
- coming to know
- knowing
- wise

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Onam, and grandson of Jerahmeel, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 2:28).

## Metaphysical

The quality of perceiving, knowing, understanding, in man, which comes about by 
the individual's making a conscious union in his thoughts with the divine intelligence— the one all- 
knowing Mind, or wisdom, which is God.
