# Lassharon (A. V., Lasharon)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Lasharon), las-sh%orO-on

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Canaan. Its king was killed by Joshua (Josh. 12: 18).

## Metaphysical

A state of consciousness that is honest (according to Sharon, like the plain), frank, 
and open, and stands for just what it is. Here its king, or ruling thought, is one of the enemies in the land 
of Canaan (Canaan representing man's consciousness and body), and must be overthrown by Joshua— I 
AM. (See SHARON.)
