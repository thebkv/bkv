# Eleph

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-leph

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Benjamin (Josh. 18:28).

## Metaphysical

Union with infinite strength, through understanding and faith. (An ox stands for 
physical strength; Benjamin relates to an active faith; learning bespeaks understanding.)

## Preserved OCR Source

great number; an ox, i.e., one broken and trained, symbol of directed strength.
