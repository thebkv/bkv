# Bera

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-ra

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A king of Sodom, in Abraham's time (Gen. 14:2).

## Metaphysical

The directing thought and desire of the sensual state of consciousness that Sodom 
signifies. (See SODOM.)
