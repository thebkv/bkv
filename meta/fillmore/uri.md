# Uri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u'-ri

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah (Exod. 31:2). b Father of Geber, who was the officer 
appointed by Solomon to gather provisions for his household for one month in each year from the land 
of Gilead (I Kings 4:19). c A porter in the Temple. He had taken a foreign wife, but gave her up at the 
command of Ezra (Ezra 10:24).

## Metaphysical

Purified understanding (filled with light, fiery, enlightened, light of Jehovah, my

light).
