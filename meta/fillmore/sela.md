# Sela

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** se'-lCE

## Meanings

- rock
- large rock
- cliff
- firm
- solid
- elevated
- lofty
- fortress
- stronghold

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The capital of Edom (II Kings 14:7, "the rock, " margin). It is called Selah in Isaiah 16:1; in 
margin, Petra.

## Metaphysical

Sense man's trust centered in physical strength and power (rock, cliff, firm, 
elevated, fortress, the capital of Edom and a stronghold in Edom). Sela is the Hebrew equivalent of the 
Greek word petra (rock), that Jesus used in regard to Peter (faith) in Matthew 16: 18. (See JOKTHEEL 
for the significance of this city's being conquered by the king of Judah and renamed Joktheel.)
