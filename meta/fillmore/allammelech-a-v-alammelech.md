# Allammelech (A. V., Alammelech)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Alammelech), allam'-me-lech

## Meanings

- king's oak

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Asher (Josh. 19:26).

## Metaphysical

A state of consciousness in man that realizes that "the joy of Jehovah" is his 
fortress and strength ( the king's oak; a place in Asher, Asher meaning happy). An oak tree stands for 
something very strong and protective; it signifies the defense of the Almighty.
