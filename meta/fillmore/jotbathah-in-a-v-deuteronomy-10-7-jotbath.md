# Jotbathah (in A. V., Deuteronomy 10:7, Jotbath)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Deuteronomy 10:7, Jotbath), jot'-ba-thah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A camping place of the Children of Israel in the wilderness, (Num. 33:33); "and from Gudgodah 
to Jotbathah, a land of brooks of water" (Deut. 10:7).

## Metaphysical

Like Jotbah, a place in consciousness where the waters of life and Truth flow in 
freely and refresh the whole consciousness of man, the soul. This excellent, healthful, and good 
experience is always realized after one goes through a period of letting go error and darkness, such as is 
symbolized by Gudgodah, from which place the Israelites journeyed to Jotbathah. (See GUDGODAH.)
