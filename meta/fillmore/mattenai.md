# Mattenai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mat-te'-nai

## Meanings

- liberality
- generosity
- gift of Jehovah
- gift ofJah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Two Israelites who had taken foreign wives after returning from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 
10:33, 37).

MATTHEW 
b A priest (Neh. 12:19).

## Metaphysical

The significance is almost the same as that of MATTATTAH, which see. In 
Mattenai there is also a thought of divine forgiveness— an erasing of error from consciousness (liberality, 
generosity)— which increases the individual's belief in the attainment of the good that he has perceived.
