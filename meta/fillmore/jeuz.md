# Jeuz

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je'-uz

## Meanings

- advising
- counseling
- impressing
- fertilizing

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite, one of those who were "heads of fathers' houses” (I Chron. 8: 10).

## Metaphysical

An influential thought that is of a discriminating, guiding, counseling, enriching 
nature. It belongs to the Benjamite or active faith consciousness in the spiritually awakening individual. 
(Faith is the inner sight, which sees into spiritual realities and makes them very real to the consciousness 
of the individual, even before there is any outward sign or proof of them.)
