# Besai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-sai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His descendants were among the Nethinim who returned from the Babylonian captivity with 
Zerubbabel (Ezra 2:49)

## Metaphysical

An overcoming tendency, or power, in man that brings forth thoughts that become 
active in freeing and restoring the Truth in consciousness ( conqueror ; Besai's "children" returned to 
Jerusalem with Zerubbabel from the captivity in Babylon) . There is a personal element in this 
overcoming thought or force, however, that needs further enlightenment and uplift.
