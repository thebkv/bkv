# Nahum (in A.V., Luke 3:25, Naum)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Luke 3:25, Naum), na'-hum

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of Amos and son of Esli, named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:25). b "The 
Elkoshite," one of the minor prophets of the Bible, writer of The Book of Nahum (Nah. 1:1).

## Metaphysical

The adjusting, soothing, healing effect of the Holy Spirit's presence in 
consciousness (comfort, solace, ease). Since one of the men named Nahum was a prophet, the thought 
of the Holy Spirit as teacher, as well as comforter and healer, is brought out.
