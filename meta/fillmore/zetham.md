# Zetham

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze'-tham

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ladan, of the Gershonite Levites (I Chron. 23:8). I Chronicles 26: 22 states that he is the 
son of Jehieli, in descent from Ladan, or Libni.

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to the love faculty in the individual (a man of the tribe of 
Levi) that radiates the spirit of peace and prosperity (luminous essence, giving light, oil, shining, olive 
ZIBA

tree, olive, olives being representative of peace and of great abundance, while oil symbolizes the Holy 
Spirit).
