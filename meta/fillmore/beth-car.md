# Beth-car

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth'car

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place where the children of Israel quit pursuing the Philistines after putting them to flight (I 
Sam. 7:11).

## Metaphysical

That place where we consciously abide and rejoice abundantly in the overcoming 
power of Spirit and in a realization of omnipresent substance and life (house of exultation, house of 
leaping about house of lambs house of lambs pasture). This is a genuine milestone in our spiritual 
progress; we have conquered all the ground that our consciousness grasps of that which is real (Israel) in 
ourselves and in the universe as well.
