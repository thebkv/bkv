# Jebusite (in A. V., Joshua 18:16, 28, Jebusi; Genesis 15:21 Jebusites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- o/ or belonging to Jebus

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A name of the city Jebus (Josh. 18: 16, 28). b A nation in Canaan, that built and inhabited the 
city Jebus, afterward called Jerusalem (Gen. 15:21; Josh. 3:10).

## Metaphysical

A Jebusite signifies a thought belonging to the state of consciousness that is 
symbolized by Jebus. (See JEBUS.)
