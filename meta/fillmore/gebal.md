# Gebal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ge'-bal

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Phoenician city. It is mentioned with Edom, Moab, Ammon, and Amalek, in Psalms 83:6, 7, 
and with Tyre in Ezekiel 27:8, 9 (Tyre and Sidon were cities of Phoenicia). In Joshua 13:5 we read of 
the land of the Gebalites.

## Metaphysical

That in sense consciousness which marks the limits, the boundary line, of purely 
human conquest, capacity, and influence apart from the recognition and acknowledgment of God, Spirit, 
as the one source of all true dominion and power. (Phoenicia means land of palm trees; also purple and 
red. Palm trees refer to conquest, victory; purple signifies power; red typifies life activity.)
