# Ahiam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-hi'-am

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A captain in David's army (II Sam. 23:33)

## Metaphysical

A sense of the close union that n Truth exists between all men, universal 
relationship (father's brother ; mother's brother , uncle). This feeling of universal relationship is always in 
evidence where love (David) is, and it aids greatly in preparing the way for love (David) to rule in 
consciousness. It also helps to sustain the rule of love after it is established.
