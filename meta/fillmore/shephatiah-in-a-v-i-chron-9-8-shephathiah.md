# Shephatiah (in A. V., I Chron. 9:8, Shephathiah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Chron. 9:8, Shephathiah), sheph-a-ti-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of several Israelitish men (II Sam. 3:4; I Chron. 12:5; 27:16; II Chron. 21:2; Ezra 2:4, 
57; Jer. 38:1).

## Metaphysical

Divine judgment and adjustment active throughout the higher, truer thoughts and 
states of consciousness (Israelites) in the individual. The judgments of the Lord (ordinances of Jehovah) 
are not evil, but they are "true and righteous," and they abound in mercy and goodness (Jehovah sets 
upright, judgment of Jah, whom Jehovah defends).
