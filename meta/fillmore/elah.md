# Elah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-lah

## Meanings

- terebinth
- oak
- so called from its strength, hardiness, and size

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A valley in Judah, where Saul and the men of Israel encamped and fought against the 
Philistines at the time that David killed Goliath (I Sam. 17:2). b Son of Baasha and king of Israel, who 
was killed by Zimri while in the house of Arza drunk (I Kings 16:8-14). c A chief who was descended 
from Esau (Gen. 36:41). d Son of Caleb of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:15). e A Benjamite (I Chron. 
9:8).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of strength and protection (an oak stands for something very 
strong and protective). When this thought of strength and protection is based on God, sense errors (the 
Philistines) are overcome and all is well. When it is based on material beliefs, however, as would be the 
case with Elah, the drunken king of Israel who was assassinated, and the Elah who was descended from 
Esau (Esau signifies the physical in man), this thought of strength and protection would be limited; it 
would be likely to fail one at the very time that one needed it most.
