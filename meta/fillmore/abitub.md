# Abitub

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ab'-;-tub

## Meanings

- father (source) of good
- my father is goodness

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite, son of Shaharaim and Hushim (I Chron. 8:11).

## Metaphysical

The idea that God is good and that all goodness is from God and is divine and that 
the Father is bountiful in goodness (father, or source, of good; my father is goodness). Thoughts of 
wholeness and bounty enter into the significance of the meaning of this name.
