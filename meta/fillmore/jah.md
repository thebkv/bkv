# Jah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jSh

## Meanings

- unchangeable
- eternal
- everlasting
- Lord
- Jehovah
- He

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A shortened or poetical form of Jehovah (Psalms 68:4, margin). While Jah is accepted as a 
contraction of Jehovah, it is really one of the most ancient expressions attached to Deity.
