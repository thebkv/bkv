# Serah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** se'-rah

## Meanings

- poured forth
- diffused
- spread out
- extension
- redundance
- abundance

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

excess.

Daughter of Asher (Num. 26:46).

## Metaphysical

While Serah bespeaks a rich, broad, extensive phase of soul quality (extension, 
abundance, spread out), there is also a strong suggestion of waste of substance, lack of conservation 
(poured forth, diffused, excess).
