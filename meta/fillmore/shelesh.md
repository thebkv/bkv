# Shelesh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she'-lesh

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Helem of the tribe of Asher (I Chron. 7:35).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of strength, power, wholeness, and fulfillment of all good, 
multiplied threefold (strength, might, fulfillment, realization, holiness, solidarity, salvation, 
preservation, threefold).
