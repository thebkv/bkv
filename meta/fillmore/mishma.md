# Mishma

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mishO-mCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One of Ishmael's twelve sons (Gen. 25:14). b A descendant of Simeon (I Chron. 4:25).

## Metaphysical

A receptive, attentive, obedient attitude (hearing, obeying) in bath the inner and 
the outer consciousness of the individual (Mishma, a descendant of Simeon, refers to the religious and 
spiritual realm of mind in man, while the other Mishma, a son of Ishmael, refers to the outer or sense 
consciousness); also the result of this attitude of mind, a result that is the receiving and expressing of a 
message, a revelation, a truth (report, rumor, fame, declaration ).
