# Obed

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** o'-bed

## Meanings

- laboring
- working
- making
- serving
- worshipping

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Boaz and Ruth, and grandfather of David (Ruth 4:21). b There were other Israelites by 
this name, also (I Chron. 2:37; 11:47; 26:7; II Chron. 23:1).

## Metaphysical

An active thought, in the spiritual consciousness of man, that pertains to service 
and worship. "God is spirit: and they that worship him must worship in spirit and truth" (John 4:24; first 
clause, as given in margin). In other words, God is Mind; and they that worship Him must worship 
through thinking, by their thoughts. Mind, in its highest aspect, and Spirit are synonymous.
