# Beelzebub

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** be-el'-ze-bub

## Meanings

- Baal of the flies
- lord of the flies

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Sovereign of evil spirits; a heathen deity, believed to be the head over evil spirits (Luke 11:15- 
19); the "prince of the powers of the air, of the spirit that now worketh in the sons of disobedience"

(Eph. 2:2).

## Metaphysical

You are crying "Beelzebub" whenever you say "crank" of the one who has caught 
sight of the spiritual mountain tops now glistening in the sun of the new age, just as they called Jesus 
Beelzebub because He presented and demonstrated Truth ahead of the age in which He lived. (See 
BAAL-ZEBUB.)
