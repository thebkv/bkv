# Rinnah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** rin-nah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shimon, a man of Judah (I Chron. 4:20).

## Metaphysical

Power expressing through the voice (tremulous vibration, strident sound, vibrant 
voice, song of praise, shout of joy, roaring torrent). Rinnah may also be said to signify an inner reaching 
after God. In the consciousness that is not yet poised in understanding, this inner urge and longing may 
take on characteristics either of positive affirmation and declaration of good as already received, or a 
negative form of prayer, that of sorrowful supplication (mournful cry, wailing moan).
