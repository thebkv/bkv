# twelve, the number

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

Twelve always refers to spiritual fulfillment.

The twelve stones, in I Kings 18:31, represent the twelve most important nerve centers in the 
body. A11 material things represent spiritual realities. The I AM (Elijah) in this demonstration must 
understand and affirm the spiritual reality of ah seemingly material organs and functions.
