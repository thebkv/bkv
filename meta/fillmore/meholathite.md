# Meholathite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- the dancing
- the mirthful
- the joyful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An inhabitant of Abel-meholah. Saul gave his elder daughter Merab as wife to Adriel the 
Meholathite. (I Sam. 18:19; see II Sam. 21:8 also.)

## Metaphysical

A thought springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Abel- 
meholah signifies. (See ABEL MEHOLAH.)
