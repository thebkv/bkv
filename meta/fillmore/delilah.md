# Delilah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** de-h'-lah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Philistine woman of Sorek, whom Samson loved; she was instrumental in bringing about his 
downfall (Judg. 16: 4-18).

## Metaphysical

Sensuality. (See SAMSON.)
