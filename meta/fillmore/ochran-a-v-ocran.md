# Ochran (A.V., Ocran)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Ocran), och'-ran

## Meanings

- troubled
- confused
- muddled
- disturbed
- afflicted

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Pagiel, who was a chief or prince of the tribe of Asher, in the wilderness (Num. 1:13;

2:27).

## Metaphysical

A thought that belongs to the religious thoughts of man but is out of harmony 
(troubled, disturbed, afflicted ) with Truth; it is not clear (muddled confused).
