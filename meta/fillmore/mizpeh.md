# Mizpeh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mizO-peh

## Meanings

- watchtower
- same as Mizpah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city in the lowlands of Judah (Josh. 15:38). b A valley (Josh. 11:8). c A town of Moab (I 
Sam. 22:3). d The Mizpeh of Joshua 18:26 is spelled Mizpah in other places (see Judg. 20:1; 21:1, and I 
Kings 15:22).

## Metaphysical

See MIZPAH.

Mizraim, mizO-r(E-;m (Heb .[—Egypt; Egyptians; circumscription; limitation; bondage; 
affliction; tribulation; straitness; distress.

Son of Ham (Gen. 10:6).

Metaphysical Ham, the father of Mizraim, typifies the physical in man, given over to sensuality. 
Mizraim signifies the sense belief that the life as well as the organism of man is bound in materiality, 
and that man is subject to sorrows and to all forms of errors that hinder him from receiving good 
(limitation, bondage, tribulation).
