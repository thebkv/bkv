# Joiarib

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** joi'-a-rib

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A teacher ("one who had understanding," margin) whom Ezra sent for while encamped by the 
river Ahava, that he might accompany him, and those with him, on their journey from Babylon to 
Jerusalem (Ezra 8:16). (See Nehemiah 11:5, 10; 12:19.) Joiarib is short for Jehoiarib.

## Metaphysical

Like Jehoiarib, an inner realization of God, through Christ, Jehovah, I AM, as our 
protection and defense; also an idea of spiritual increase (Jehovah will increase, Jah will multiply).
