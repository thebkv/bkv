# Parmenas

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** pSr'-me-nas

## Meanings

- steadfast
- constant
- abiding
- enduring
- permanent
- faithful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the seven men who were chosen to attend to the distribution of supply among the 
Christians in Jerusalem, that none might be overlooked (Acts 6:5).

## Metaphysical

A faithful, steadfast, abiding thought in the higher, spiritual consciousness of the 
individual— a thought that is resolute and firm in the Truth (steadfast, faithful, permanent, enduring; see 
Acts 6:3 for an insight into the character of the man Parmenas).
