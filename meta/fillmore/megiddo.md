# Megiddo

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me-gidO-do

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A royal city of the Canaanites that was captured by Joshua (Josh. 12:21). It was in Taanach by 
the waters of Megiddo that Sisera was overthrown, according to Judges 5:19. It was at Megiddo that 
King Ahaziah of Judah died, after he had been smitten by Jehu (II Kings 9:27); and it was there that 
Pharaoh-necoh, king of Egypt, slew Josiah, king of Judah (II Kings 23:29).

## Metaphysical

The gathering together of the hordes of error thoughts in consciousness 
MEHETABEL

(which sometimes seem to be countless) to make war against the truer and higher thoughts and ideals of 
the individual (place of many organisms, place of troops, i. e., for predatory incursions, rendezvous). If 
the higher thoughts are not true to their spiritual ideals they are sometimes overcome by these errors, as 
is suggested by the significance of the kings of Judah who were slain at Megiddo, yet it is really a most 
fortunate place, because it affords an excellent opportunity for the individual to obtain a great and 
sweeping victory over error (Sisera and his hosts were defeated there), and thus to bring forth precious 
fruit (place of great abundance) for growth in righteousness and Truth.
