# Geshan (A.V., Gesham)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Gesham), ge'-shan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jahdi, a descendant of Caleb's (I Chron. 2:47).

## Metaphysical

Geshan suggests two conditions that can follow the uniting of that in 
consciousness for which Caleb and Ephah stand. If the Caleb thought predominates to the clearing up of 
the Ephah thought, greater firmness and 
GESHURTTES

strength will result. On the other hand, if the darkness and gloom of the sense soul thought (Ephah, the 
concubine of Caleb) remains in evidence and becomes strengthened in its sense darkness instead of 
being overcome by its union with faith, enthusiasm, and courage (Caleb), greater uncleanness and 
impurity (unclean, filthy, dirty) would be expressed than would have been expressed had the union not 
taken place.

A thought of deeper and more thorough cleansing and purification should be held at every fresh 
flow of new life, energy, and faith into consciousness; otherwise, errors are likely to become 
strengthened in one instead of being put out of consciousness.
