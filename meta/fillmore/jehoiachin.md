# Jehoiachin

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-hoi'-a-chin

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jehoiakim, and next to the last king of Judah. He had reigned only a very short time when 
he was captured by Nebuchadnezzar and taken to Babylon (II Kings 24:6-15). Jeconiah and Coniah are 
contracted forms of Jehoiachin.

## Metaphysical

Jehovah, I AM, working in the consciousness of the individual to establish the 
will in spiritual uprightness, stability, assurance, faith.
