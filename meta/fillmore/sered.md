# Sered

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** se'-red

## Meanings

- fear
- trembling
- flight
- escape

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Zebulun (Gen. 46: 14).

## Metaphysical

Fearfulness, extreme unrest (fear, trembling) existing in the order faculty in the 
individual consciousness (Sered was a son of Zebulun, and Zebulun represents order). Back of this 
fearful, seemingly unstable thought that Sered signifies, however, deep within the truth that the order 
faculty is of spiritual origin and is abidingly perfect, there is that to which this thought can flee for 
refuge and escape from all sense of fear and its results (flight, escape).
