# Lud

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** lud

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shem, and grandson of Noah (Gen. 10:22; see also Isaiah 66:19 and Ezekiel 27:10). Lud 
is thought to have founded the kingdom of Lydia in Asia Minor; from him the Lydians get their name.

## Metaphysical

Shem is the son of Noah, who typifies the spiritual in man. Lud, son of Shem, is 
the beginning of man's concept of the truth that he is the offspring of God; that he came from Spirit 
instead of flesh (nativity). Thus a desire to bring forth on a higher plane is aroused within him. Because 
of his seemingly very material state, however, much travail of soul attends his efforts to bring to birth 
the higher ideals that have been conceived in him. In his first attempts to establish his higher, religious 
ideals he may strive and contend in a very material way. Later he learns the way of love and peace, and 
attains truer success.
