# waterpots

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

The "six waterpots of stone" (John 2:6, 7) are the six nerve centers in the body, 
which are filled with the water of life, or nerve fluid.

The symbol represents the abundance of vital energy that may be generated from a union of I 
AM spiritual thought with the water of life, or nerve fluid, in the various centers of the organism. With 
every thought we are setting this nerve fluid into a state of action, and it rushes to any part of the body to 
which our attention draws it.
