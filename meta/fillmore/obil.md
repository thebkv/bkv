# Obil

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** o'-bil

## Meanings

- chief of the camels
- keeper of camels

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Obil the Ishmaelite was "over the camels" in David's reign (I Chron. 27:30).

## Metaphysical

The camel, in its native country, is a beast of burden.

As pertaining to the individual, hard work, the bearing of burdens, are known only by the 
physical and seemingly mortal phase of his being. An Ishmaelite is a thought that is the fruit of the 
thoughts of the natural man at work in the flesh, Obil the Ishmaelite, meaning keeper of camels, is a

thought activity belonging to the outer consciousness of man. Having joined itself to the ruling love 
(David) faculty of the individual, it comes into dominion over such of his forces and tendencies as are 
symbolized by the camel— the bearing of burdens also great steadfastness and endurance— which are 
traits peculiar to camels and at times seem to develop into obstinacy. (See CAMEL.)

object of man's existence-The mortal concept of the object of man's existence is personal 
pleasure and profit. The spiritual concept is to express God.
