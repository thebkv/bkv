# Pamphylia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pam-phyl'-l-CE

## Meanings

- heterogeneous peoples
- of every race
- mixture of nations

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A seacoast district in Asia Minor. Paul preached in Perga, a city in Pamphylia (Acts 13:13; 
14:24, 25). It was in Pamphylia that Mark left Paul and Barnabas on his first trip with them (Acts 
15:38).

## Metaphysical

A mixed state of thought (mixture of nations, of every race).
