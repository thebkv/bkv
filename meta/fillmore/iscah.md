# Iscah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** is'-cah

## Meanings

- who looks upon
- scans abroad
- discerns
- watchful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Daughter of Haran, who was the brother of Abram (Gen. 1 1:29). Some think that Iscah is the 
same person as Sarah, Abram's wife.

## Metaphysical

The soul attentive to the things of Spirit (watchful, who looks upon). (See 
SARAH.)
