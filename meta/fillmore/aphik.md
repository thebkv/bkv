# Aphik

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-ph;k

## Meanings

- a fortress
- a .strong place

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in Canaan belonging to the Israelitish tribe of Asher, but from which the Asherites failed 
to drive the Canaanites (Judg. 1:31). Aphek is the same name.
