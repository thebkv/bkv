# Zoar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zo'-ar

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the cities of the plain, to which Lot escaped when Sodom and Gomorrah were destroyed 
(Gen. 13:10; 19:22). It was the same city as Bela (Gen. 14:2). In Isaiah 15:5 and Jeremiah 48:34 Zoar is 
mentioned as being a city of Moab.

## Metaphysical

Inferiority (smallness, littleness, one of the wicked cities of the plain, and 
belonging to Moab, or carnal mind).
