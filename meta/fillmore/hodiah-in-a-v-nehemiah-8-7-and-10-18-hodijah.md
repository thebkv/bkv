# Hodiah (in A.V., Nehemiah 8:7 and 10:18, Hodijah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Nehemiah 8:7 and 10:18, Hodijah), ho-dl'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A descendant of Judah (I Chron. 4: 19). b One who helped the people to understand the law 
when Ezra read it to them (Neh. 8:7). c One of those who joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant (Neh. 
10:18).

## Metaphysical

That in the religious and spiritual nature of man (Israelites) which realizes that 
Jehovah (the indwelling Christ) is man's true splendor, might, power of dominion, joy, and praise— the 
source of all that is lasting and good to him.
