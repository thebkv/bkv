# Joiada

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** joi'-a-dCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Eliashib, and his successor as high priest of Judah, under Nehemiah (Neh. 12:10). b A 
son of Paseah, one who repaired "the old gate," or "the gate of the old city," in the rebuilding of the wall 
of Jerusalem (Neh. 3:6 with margin). Joiada is a contraction of the name Jehoiada.

## Metaphysical

The inner spiritual knowing. (See JEHOIADA.)
