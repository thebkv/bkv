# Jeberechiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-ber-e-chi-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Zechariah (Isa. 8:2).

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of Berechiah. (See BERECHIAH.) 
As man reverences and praises Truth, gives earnest attention to that which is of Spirit, he becomes 
conscious of the divine blessings in his own life and world.
