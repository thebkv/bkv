# Jerah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je'-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Joktan, a descendant of Shem, and the founder of an Arabian tribe (Gen. 10:26).

## Metaphysical

Jerah (companionate radiation; he will breathe, he will become inspired, moon, 
lunar month) refers to the light (understanding) of the inspired intellect, or to the fact that the intellect of 
man can and will become illumined by Spirit, and will radiate the light of Spirit— divine understanding.
