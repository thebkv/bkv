# Gehenna

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A valley south of Jerusalem where the refuse of the city was burned. (The word Gehenna is not 
used in the American Standard Version, but it is the same as the Valley of Hinnom.)

## Metaphysical

The purifying fires of the soul are symbolized by the fires of Gehenna. (See 
HELL, and HINNOM, for further explanation.)
