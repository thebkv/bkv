# Ahava

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-h a'-va

## Meanings

- stream
- water

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A river, beside which the Jewish captives gathered when returning to Jerusalem under Ezra (Ezra

8:15).

## Metaphysical

A current of cleansing thought (stream) where the waters of denial (fasting, see 
Ezra 8:21) wash away fearful thoughts and material beliefs, thus enabling our religious thoughts (Jews) 
to proceed on their way to Jerusalem (spiritual consciousness) in safety.
