# Carcas

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** car'-cas

## Meanings

- severe
- lamb ’s covering

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of "the seven chamberlains that ministered in the presence of Ahasuerus the king" (Esth.

1 : 10 ).

## Metaphysical

A harsh, devouring, wolfish thought (severe) in sheep's clothing; that is, having 
outwardly an appearance of mildness and obedience (lamb's covering), thereby being of a deceptive 
character. This thought serves the puffed up, ambitious personal will (Ahasuerus) .
