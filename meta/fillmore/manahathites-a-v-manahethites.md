# Manahathites (A. V., Manahethites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Manahath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Inhabitants of Manahath that are named in the genealogy of Judah (I Chron. 2:54).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Manahath 
signifies. (See MANAHATH.)
