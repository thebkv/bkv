# Jeriel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je'-ri-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Tola, of the sons of Issachar. He was one of those who were "heads of their father's 
houses" (I Chron. 7:2).

## Metaphysical

A strong, spiritually enlightened, directive, cherishing (vision of God, God points 
the way, God scatters the early rain ), ruling thought belonging to the zeal faculty in individual 
consciousness (a chief man of Issachar), that has its foundation in Spirit and receives its impetus from 
Spirit ( founded by God, constituted by God).
