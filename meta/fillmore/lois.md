# Lois

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** lo'-is

## Metaphysical

The very free, unbound, pleasing state, and high, superior quality (better), of the 
soul that is established in faith and Truth. (The "unfeigned faith" that Paul recognized in Timothy dwelt 
first in his grandmother Lois, and in his mother Eunice.)
