# Hodesh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ho'-desh

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Wife of Shaharaim, of the tribe of Benjamin (I Chron. 8:9).

## Metaphysical

Shaharaim ( double dawn, double morning) represents the breaking of new light in 
consciousness, doubly strong. Hodesh (new moon, or month, day of the new moon ) suggests the 
reflection of this light in the soul while it is being carried into the body consciousness, for its renewal 
( make new). (The moon symbolizes reflected understanding, since it shines by borrowed light.)
