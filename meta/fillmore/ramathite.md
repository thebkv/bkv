# Ramathite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Ramah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An inhabitant of Ramah. Shimei the Ramathite was in charge of the vineyards, in David's reign 
(I Chron. 27:27).

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to that in consciousness which Ramah signifies. (See 
RAMAH and SHIMEI.)
