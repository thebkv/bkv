# Meremoth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mer'-e-moth

## Meanings

- elevations
- heights
- elations
- exaltations
- powers
- glories

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Uriah the priest. Meremoth was given charge of the silver and gold and the vessels in 
the house of God, after the return from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 8:33); he helped rebuild the wall 
of Jerusalem (Neh. 3:4, 21). b A man named Meremoth joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant (Neh. 
10:5).

## Metaphysical

Exalted spiritual thoughts, thoughts of power and might, in man's higher and truer 
realm of consciousness (Israelites). These thoughts minister in the house of God (body temple). They do 
much toward establishing the true 
MERIBAH

worship of God in the consciousness of the individual who gives them place, and they work toward 
building the whole organism in Truth, that it may be renewed and become abiding.
