# Ahasbai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-has'-bai

## Meanings

- I have taken refuge in Jehovah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of one of David's mighty men, a Maacathite (II Sam. 23:34).

## Metaphysical

An idea of faith and trust in Jehovah, the true, inner spiritual I AM (I have taken 
refuge in Jehovah ).
