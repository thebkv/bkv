# Gabbai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ga'-hai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Name of a Benjamite family, or of the head man of a family of Benjamites, who lived in 
Jerusalem after the return from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 11:8).

## Metaphysical

A thought or group of thoughts that attracts. It attracts to itself, by its active 
exercise of faith (Benjamin), other thoughts of a faith character, as well as much substance and energy, 
that a harvest time of good may be brought about.
