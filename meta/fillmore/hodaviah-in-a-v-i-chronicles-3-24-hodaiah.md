# Hodaviah (in A.V., I Chronicles 3:24, Hodaiah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Chronicles 3:24, Hodaiah), hod-a-vi'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A man of Manasseh; one of those who were the "heads of their fathers' houses" (I Chron. :24). 
b A Benjamite of note (I Chron. 9:7). c A Levite whose descendants came up out of the Babylonian 
captivity with Zerubbabel (Ezra 2:40)

## Metaphysical

Leading thoughts (men who were "heads of their fathers' houses") from our 
understanding faculty (Manasseh), from our active faith consciousness (Benjamin), and from our love 
faculty (Levi) as expressed by our natural religious tendencies (Levites), giving praise and honor to 
Jehovah (the indwelling Christ or I AM) and being blessed and highly esteemed by Jehovah.
