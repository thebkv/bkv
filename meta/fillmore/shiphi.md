# Shiphi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shi-phi

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Ziza and son of Allon, chiefs of the Simeonites (I Chron. 4:37).

## Metaphysical

A prosperity idea; a rich, influential thought of great increase, founded in Spirit 
(Jah is fullness, abundant, wealth, multitude of Jehovah), the result of an attentive, obedient attitude 
(Shiphi was a chief of the Simeonites, and Simeon the son of Jacob, from whom the Simeonites were 
descended, means hearing; hearing relates to attentiveness and obedience).
