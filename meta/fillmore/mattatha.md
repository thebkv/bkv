# Mattatha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** mat'-ta-thCE

## Meanings

- gift of Jehovah
- donation ofJah
- present of Jehovah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Menna and son of Nathan, in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:31)

## Metaphysical

Virtually the same as Mattaniah: the higher religious thoughts of man awakening 
to a realization of his divine possibilities of obtaining fullness of life and of all good, through Jehovah, I 
AM.
