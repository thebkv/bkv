# Eliathah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-h'-a-thah

## Meanings

- God has come
- God is come
- to whom God comes

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Heman, a singer or musician during David's reign. He with his sons and brothers, twelve 
in number, had the twentieth course in serving in the house of God (I Chron. 25:4, 27).

## Metaphysical

A realization and expression of the presence of God as divine harmony ( God is 
come; a musician or singer).
