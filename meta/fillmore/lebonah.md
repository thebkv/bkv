# Lebonah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** le-bo'-nah

## Meanings

- whiteness
- purity
- brilliance
- frankincense
- the moon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place north of Bethel and Shiloh (Judg. 21:19).

## Metaphysical

Like Lebana, Lebonah signifies the refining, beautifying effect of pure thoughts, 
even though these thoughts may be of the intellect, or partly so (frankincense, whiteness, purity, moon).
