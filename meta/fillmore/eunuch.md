# eunuch

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

A thought from which the capacity to increase life and its forms has been 
eliminated.

"The prince of the eunuchs" (Dan. 1:10) is that which stands highest in rank in the pure, 
illuminating spiritual consciousness, from which thought of sex has been excluded.
