# Lucius

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Lat
- **Pronunciation:** lu'-cius

## Meanings

- light
- light bearer, illuminative
- born of light

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A prophet or teacher in the church at Antioch (Acts 13:1). In Romans 16:21 he is mentioned as 
being Paul's kinsman, and some think him to be the same person as Luke.

## Metaphysical

Lucius (light, illuminative ) pertains to spiritual understanding and its expression 
in the awakening consciousness of man. The five prophets and teachers at Antioch, one of whom was 
Lucius of Cyrene, are the interpreters of Spirit to the outer or sense consciousness. (See LUKE.)
