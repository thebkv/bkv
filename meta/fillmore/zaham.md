# Zaham

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** za'-ham

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of Rehoboam's sons (IlChron. 11 :19).

## Metaphysical

Rehoboam represents that in man's consciousness which exalts the senses; it is the 
fruit of many sensuous and insane thought habits. (See REHOBOAM.) Zaham (offensive, impure, 
unclean, leprous, loathsome, hateful), son of Rehoboam, signifies the offensiveness of extreme 
uncleanness of thought and habit.
