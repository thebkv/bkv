# Shemaah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she-ma'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Gibeathite of the tribe of Benjamin, and father of Ahiezer and Joash who joined David at 
Ziklag while he was in hiding from Saul (I Chron. 12:3).

## Metaphysical

An attentive, receptive, obedient attitude (hearing, hearkening, obeying) in the 
active faith faculty in individual consciousness (Benjamin signifies active faith); also the understanding, 
the inspirations, of Truth that become current throughout the consciousness of one who is receptive and 
attentive to the things of Spirit (understanding, report, rumor, fame, annunciation).
