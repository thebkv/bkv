# Cos (A.V., Coos)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Coos), cos

## Meanings

- summit

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A small island in the ®gean Sea, or Grecian Archipelago (Acts 21:1); Paul stopped there for a 
day while on one of his missionary journeys.

## Metaphysical

Cos means summit; Rhodes, roses; Phoenicia, land of palm trees; Cyprus, 
fairness. These places symbolize certain pleasant phases of consciousness in the individual who is 
seeking the highest, but they are not, strictly speaking, on the spiritual plane.
