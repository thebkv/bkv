# Helbon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hel'-bon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The "wine of Helbon" is mentioned in Ezekiel 27: 18.

## Metaphysical

The rich substance of life, with all its possibilities of unfoldment and fruitfulness.

Tyre and Damascus, places that are mentioned in conjunction with Helbon, belong in the realm 
of sensation and material thought, in individual consciousness-therefore the rich life substance that is 
signified by Helbon is being used for the promotion of the "mind of the flesh" instead of being utilized

in spiritual ways to the uplift and regeneration of the whole man. The natural outcome of this waste of 
substance in sense ways is dullness, coarseness, and stupidity.
