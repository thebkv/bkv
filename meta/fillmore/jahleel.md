# Jahleel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jSh’-le-el

## Meanings

- waiting on God
- awaiting God
- hoping in God
- hope of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Zebulun (Gen. 46: 14).

## Metaphysical

The significance of this name is revealed in Psalms 62:5:

"My soul, wait thou in silence for God only;

For my expectation is from him."

Jahleelites, jSh’-le-el-ites (fr. Heb.).

Descendants of Jahleel the son of Zebulun (Num. 26:26).

Metaphysical. Thoughts in consciousness springing from and belonging to that which Jahleel 
signifies. (See JAHLEEL.)
