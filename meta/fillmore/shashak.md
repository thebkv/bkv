# Shashak

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sha'-shak

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

SHAVEH

Son of Beriah of the tribe of Benjamin (I Chron. 8:14).

## Metaphysical

An intense desire for good, but unfulfilled because of running to and fro in the 
outer, because of seeking in the outer only. That which Shashak signifies lacks poise, self-mastery, 
concentration. It does not seek the Spirit of truth in quietness and confidence. Violence enters into the 
activity of this great desire in its efforts to attain the fulfillment of the good that it is seeking; 
understanding is needed.

## Preserved OCR Source

Shashak, sha'-shak (Heb.)— running to and fro; pedestrian; seeker; prayer; assaulter; eagerness;
