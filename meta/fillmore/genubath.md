# Genubath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ge-nu'-bath

## Meanings

- theft
- things stolen

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Hadad, a prince of Edom. Genubath's mother was a sister of Tahpenes, wife of the king of 
Egypt. Genubath was reared in Pharaoh's home and became a member of Pharaoh's household (I Kings 
11 : 20 .

## Metaphysical

The stealthy, thieving method that the sensate, earthly consciousness in man 
employs in combating his more religious and spiritual nature.
