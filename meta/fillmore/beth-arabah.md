# Beth-arabah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-ar'-a-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city in the wilderness, near the Dead Sea, on the border of Judah (Josh. 15:6, 61). b A city of 
the tribe of Benjamin (Josh. 18:22).

## Metaphysical

The "arabah" is the desert, or places in the earthly, physical ideas of substance that 
have been separated from the indwelling vitality. The cities of Beth-arabah represent places of this kind 
in consciousness, that need to be lifted into fruitfulness by means of faith (Benjamin) and praise (Judah).
