# Rhegium

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** rhe'-gi-um

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A seaport of southern Italy. The boat on which Paul was being taken a prisoner to Rome stopped 
at this place (Acts 28: 13).

## Metaphysical

A breaking through ( breached ) from one phase of thought to another.
