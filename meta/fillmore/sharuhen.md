# Sharuhen

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sha-ru'-hen

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Simeon that had first been allotted to Judah (Josh. 19:6). The children of Simeon 
received their inheritance "in the midst of the inheritance of the children of Judah." In Joshua 15:32, this 
city is called Shilhim, and in I Chronicles 4:31, Shaaraim.

## Metaphysical

Loosening or awakening of love and Truth within one, this bringing one into an 
abiding consciousness of grace and joy and all good— a sense of satisfaction such as is suggested in 
Psalms 37:4: "Delight thyself also in Jehovah;

And he will give thee the desire of thy heart."
