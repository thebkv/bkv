# Pethuel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe-thu'-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Joel the prophet (Joel 1:1).

## Metaphysical

The understanding of the individual opened toward God, and thus broadened and 
deepened (enlarged of God) into a comprehension of man as being a spiritual being, a divine creation, 
through which God, the Father-Mind, expresses and works (open mouth of God). This enlarged 
understanding brings about deliverance from lesser error ideas and their limited results ( God delivers).
