# beka (A. V., bekah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bekah), be'-ka

## Meanings

- portion
- half
- cleft
- remnant
- half shekel
- half measure

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One half of a shekel (Exod. 38:26). A shekel was a certain weight of either silver or gold. A gold 
shekel was worth about $5.50. A silver shekel was worth about 75 cents.

## Metaphysical

A degree of recognition ( portion , half cleft) of the rich and everywhere present 
substance of Spirit. (Money is a symbol of substance.)
