# Seraiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** se-ra'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There were several Israelites by this name (II Sam. 8:17; II Kings 25:18, 23; I Chron. 4:13, 35; 
Ezra 2:2, and others).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts of dominion, of conquest and victory, based on the overcoming power 
of the indwelling Christ, I AM, Jehovah (Jehovah hath prevailed, Jah is prince, warrior of Jehovah, 
Israelitish men).

seraphim- Ideas of purity; the cleansing power of exalted ideas.
