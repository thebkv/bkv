# Horites (A. V., Genesis 36:30, Hori; Deuteronomy 2:12, Horims)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- troglodytes
- cave dwellers
- dwellers in black holes

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The Horites were very closely connected with the Edomites, the descendants of Esau. They were 
the inhabitants of the land of Mount Seir, before they were overcome by Esau. After that they lived in 
the land with the Edomites (Gen. 14:6). (See also Genesis 36.)

## Metaphysical

The Horites, like the Edomites, have their seat of action in the physical organism. 
The Horites refer more especially to the deep-seated, subconscious (cave dwellers), error, fleshly

tendencies and activities of the physical in man, while the Edomites refer more to the outer or body 
consciousness.
