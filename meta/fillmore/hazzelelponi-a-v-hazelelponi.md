# Hazzelelponi (A.V., Hazelelponi)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Hazelelponi), hazze-lel-po'-nl

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelitish woman of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:3).

## Preserved OCR Source

Hazzelelponi (A.V., Hazelelponi), hazze-lel-po'-nl (Heb.)— the shadow looking back upon me; 
the shadow of my face, i.e., my reflected image; shadowed countenance, mine, i. e. , sorrowful 
countenance; facing the approaching shades; protection of God looking on me.

An Israelitish woman of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:3).
