# Belial

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-h-al

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Any man or woman who was considered wicked, worthless, lawless, or licentious might be 
called a son or daughter of Belial. (See Deut. 13:13; Judg. 19:22; I Sam. 1:16, and II Sam. 16:7; in these 
texts "Belial" is used in the A. V.) Belial is not the name of any individual, except that in the New 
Testament it is used as an appellative of Satan (II Cor. 6: 15).

## Metaphysical

Belial refers to the Adversary, or (in our interpretation) to the adverse 
consciousness in man.
