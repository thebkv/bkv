# Marana tha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** mSr'-an-a' thCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An expression that Paul used in closing his first Epistle to the Corinthians (I Cor. 16:22).

## Metaphysical

A recognition of the Christ presence, and its ultimate manifestation.

Jesus Christ is here. The Christ also lives in us and will come into perfect expression and 
manifestation in our life, even as it did in Jesus. We shall be like Him and shall see Him as He is.
