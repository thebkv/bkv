# Pelethites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pel'-e-thites

## Meanings

- public runners
- messengers
- couriers
- swift horses

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Members of David's bodyguard, and among his most brave and valiant attendants, messengers, 
and warriors (II Sam. 8:18).

## Metaphysical

Love's messengers-swift, vital, spiritual thoughts that are in attendance upon our 
inner love (David) faculty when it is ruling in consciousness (public runners, couriers, swift horses; 
attendants, messengers, and warriors of David).
