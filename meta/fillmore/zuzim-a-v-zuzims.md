# Zuzim (A. V., Zuzims)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Zuzims), zu'-zlm

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A tribe of people, "in Ham," that was defeated by King Chedorlaomer and the other kings that 
were with him (Gen. 14:5). These people are thought to be

ZUZIM

the same as the Zamzummim of Deuteronomy 2:20.

## Metaphysical

The confusions, fears, unrestrained emotions, and general terrors of the physical 
consciousness, or "mind of the flesh," seemingly very prominent and flourishing at a certain stage in the 
evolution, or unfoldment, of the individual (sparkling, glittering, flowing out like rays, spouting, 
sprouting, flowering, nourishing, restless, moving about). (See ZAMZUMMIM.)
