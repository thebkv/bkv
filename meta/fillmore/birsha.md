# Birsha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bir'-sha

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

King of Gomorrah in Abraham's time (Gen. 14:2).

## Metaphysical

Gomorrah means material force, tyranny, oppression; it signifies a state of mind 
that is adverse to the law of Spirit. This state of mind has to do with the submerged or hidden, 
subconscious, sensual phase of man's life. Birsha is the ruling thought in this error state of consciousness 
in the individual (son of wickedness, son of godlessness fat with evil, full of wickedness).
