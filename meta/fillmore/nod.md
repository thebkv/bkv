# Nod

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** nod

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The land on the east of Eden, or in front of Eden, where Cain went out from the presence of 
Jehovah and dwelt, after he had killed Abel (Gen. 4: 16).

## Metaphysical

After any positive action of the mind there is always an apparent negative 
reaction. The "land of Nod," as we know it today, is the condition called sleep. Thus Nod, meaning

agitated flight, wandering with uncertainty, suggests the seemingly unguided activity of man's 
subconscious phase of mind during periods of sleep, or relaxation, or apparently negative reactions. The 
fundamental idea in Nod is that of uncertainty of mind, bewilderment.
