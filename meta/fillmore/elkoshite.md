# Elkoshite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el'-kosh-lte

## Meanings

- bowed of God
- my bow is God
- inclined of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A native of Elkosh (Nah. 1:1); "Nahum the Elkoshite."

## Metaphysical

My defense, might, power, are of God, Spirit (m bow is God; a bow represents the 
power of the will to act and to direct the consciousness). My willingness to do God's will is also the 
result of the activity of Spirit in my consciousness ( inclined of God).
