# Zephon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze'-phon

## Meanings

- watchman
- observer
- attender
- waiter
- expecter
- keeper of the high

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

watch.

The first-named of the sons of Gad (Num. 26:15). In Genesis 46:16, he is called Ziphion.

## Metaphysical

A realization of power (Gad, a son of Jacob, represents the power faculty in man), 
which is the result of a desire for, and a seeking after, power (watchman, observer, expecter, keeper of 
the high watch; the latter definition suggests prayer, an earnest desire for and expectation of something 
higher and better than that which relates purely to the mental and physical aspect of power and might).

Zephonites, ze'-phon-ites (fr. Heb.).

Descendants of Zephon, son of Gad (Num. 26 :15).

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from and like that in consciousness which Zephon signifies. 
(See ZEPHON’)
