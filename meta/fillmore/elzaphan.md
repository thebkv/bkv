# Elzaphan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el'-za-phan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Uzziel, and grandson of Kohath, who was one of Levi's sons (Exod. 6:22). Elizaphan is 
the same name.

## Metaphysical

Thoughts of divine protection and safety. (See ELIZAPHAN.)

## Preserved OCR Source
