# Elishua

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el-i-shu'-a

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of David (II Sam. 5:15); he is called Elishama in I Chronicles 3:6.

## Metaphysical

The understanding of love (David) that true riches are from Spirit; also that 
deliverance from all error, and help of every kind that pertains to salvation, are from the same source. 
(See ELISHAMA.)
