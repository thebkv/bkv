# east

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

In Scriptural symbology east represents the within. As used in Matthew 2:1 the 
word in the original is plural; the significance therefore is that from the regions of interior wisdom come 
thoughts of reverence and rich gifts of substance and understanding and every spiritual help for the holy 
Life, the Christ Child, that has begun its growth in the consciousness.
