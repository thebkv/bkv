# Mehujael

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me-hu'-jCE-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Irad and father of Methushael, descendants of Cain (Gen. 4:18).

## Metaphysical

The belief of the outer man that strength and power are purely physical. This 
belief leads to error manifestations and demonstrations of power and strength that always lead to trouble 
of some kind- and the outer, personal man usually attributes to God the afflictions and griefs that are the 
result of his own error activities.
