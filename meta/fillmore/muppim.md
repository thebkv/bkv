# Muppim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mup'-pim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Benjamin (Gen. 46:21). He is also called Shephupham, Shephuphan, and Shuppim.

## Metaphysical

Human or sense wisdom (serpents) that is very subtle, and is unsteady and 
unsettled in its reasonings and deductions (glidings, hiding under ground, wavings, "tossed to and fro 
and carried about with every wind of doctrine") and is not clear therefore (obscurities, darkenings), and 
does not lead the individual into the true light of spiritual understanding.

Muppim was a son of Benjamin the son of Jacob, and Benjamin represents an active faith quality 
in the spiritually awakening individual consciousness. At this stage of unfoldment these qualities that 
are innately spiritual are still expressing mostly on the natural or intellectual plane; the individual has 
not a clear recognition of his divine sonship, his spiritual reality.
