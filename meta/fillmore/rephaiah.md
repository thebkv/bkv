# Rephaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** re-pha'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A man of Judah (I Chron. 3:21). b A captain of the Simeonites who went to Mount Seir and 
smote the remaining Amalekites (I Chron. 4:42). c A man of Issachar (I Chron. 7:2). d A Benjamite, 
called Raphah in I Chronicles 8:37 (I Chron. 9:43). e One who helped repair the wall of Jerusalem under 
Nehemiah (Neh. 3:9)

## Metaphysical

The various men by this name, belonging to different Israelitish tribes or different 
faculties of mind, signify the growth— in man's consciousness— of knowledge of God, through his 
indwelling Christ, or I AM, as his healing, restoring power (Jah heeds, Jehovah makes whole).

Rephaim, reph'-(E-;m (Heb.)— bonds; glooms; terrors; feeirs; fearful ones; giants; strong ones.

a A people in Palestine that were to be driven out by the Israelites (Gen. 15:20). They were a 
people of great stature. Og, king of Bashan, was the only one left at the time Deuteronomy 3:11 was 
written, b A valley where the Philistines gathered to fight against Israel (II Sam. 5:18).

Metaphysical. The seeming strength of binding, fear-producing, opposing thoughts in 
consciousness, at a certain stage of man's unfoldment into Truth (bonds, terrors, giants, strong ones, 
enemies in the Promised Land; also a place where the Philistines gathered against Israel).
