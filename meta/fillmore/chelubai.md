# Chelubai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** che-lu'-bai

## Meanings

- clapper
- barker
- trapper
- basket weaver

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Hezron, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 2:9); he is thought to be the same person as Caleb, 
who is mentioned in verses 18 and 42. (See CALEB.)

## Metaphysical

A thought activity that works in the consciousness to put away error and establish 
good. The destructive tendency must be eliminated from this thought and the cleansing, redeeming spirit 
of the Christ must be wholly accepted, that it may do perfect work. (See CALEB,)
