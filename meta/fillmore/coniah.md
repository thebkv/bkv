# Coniah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** co-nl'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A kink of Judah who reigned about three months and was then taken captive to Babylon, while 
Zedekiah was made king in his stead (Jer. 37:1). Coniah was the son of Jehoiakim, and was an evil ruler, 
as his father had been before him.

## Metaphysical

Coniah is an abbreviated form of Jeconiah and Jehoiachim. (See JECONIAH and 
JEHOIACHIM . )
