# Ezbai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ez'-bai

## Meanings

- shining
- beautiful
- hyssoplike, i.e., fragrant

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Naarai, who was one of David's warriors (I Chron. 11:37).

## Metaphysical

The light of Truth as shining into individual consciousness, graciously (beautiful) 
and with fragrance (hyssoplike), ever aspiring to higher and better things. (Anything good connected 
with the sense of smelling bespeaks aspiration.)
