# Iru

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I'-ru.

## Meanings

- citizen
- watcher
- flux
- flowing together
- heat of the mind
- vigilance
- wrath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

anger.

Son of Caleb (I Chron. 4:16).

## Metaphysical

A citizen of, or thought belonging to, the same consciousness as Ir. (See IR.) It is 
that in us which is ever attentive to and zealous for what it understands to be Truth (watcher, heat of the 
mind, vigilance). This zealousness might easily turn into wrath or anger if the personal is too much in 
evidence in one's zeal.
