# Jeconiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jec-o-ni-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A king of Judah who was carried away captive to Babylon (I Chron. 3:16; Jer. 24:1). Jeconiah is 
a short form of Jehoiachin.

## Metaphysical

See JEHOIACHIN.
