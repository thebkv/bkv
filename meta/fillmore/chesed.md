# Chesed

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** che'-sed

## Meanings

- wisdom
- a savant
- an astrologer
- a magi
- an occultist

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Abraham's brother, Nahor, by his wife, Milcah (Gen. 22:22).

## Metaphysical

A degree of wisdom, a wisdom that is psychical in its nature, rather than spiritual 
(a savant, an astrologer, a magi, an occultist).
