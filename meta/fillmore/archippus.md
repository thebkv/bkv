# Archippus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ar-ch;p'-pus

## Meanings

- master of the horse
- chief of the horses
- ruler of horses

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Addressed by Paul as "our fellow soldier" (Philem. 2) .

## Metaphysical

Dominion over the vital forces of the organism, and the wisdom and ability to 
train and care for these forces wisely (master of the horse, chief of the horses, ruler of horses; horses 
refer to the vitality, or vital forces). In Colossians 4:17 Paul admonishes Archippus: "Take heed to the 
ministry which thou hast received in the Lord, that thou fulfil it."
