# Ahishar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-hl'-shar

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A prince over Solomon's household (I Kings 4:6).

## Metaphysical

The harmony and order (brother of song, brother of the upright, i. e., 
righteousness) that prevail in the consciousness when peace has been established by the union of 
wisdom and love, or, the blending of love (David) into wisdom (Solomon).
