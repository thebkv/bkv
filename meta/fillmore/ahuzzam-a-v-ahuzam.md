# Ahuzzam (A. V., Ahuzam)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Ahuzam), a-huz'-zam

## Meanings

- their possession
- holding fast
- possessions

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ashhur and Naarah, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:6).

## Metaphysical

The ability to perceive, acquire, and possess (their possession, holding fast, 
possessions), belonging to the Judah faculty of mind.
