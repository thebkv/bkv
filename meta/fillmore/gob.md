# Gob

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** gob

## Meanings

- hollowed out
- a cistern, a pit

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place where battles were fought between the Philistines and the Israelites (II Sam. 21:18, 19). 
In I Chronicles 20:4 this place is called Gezer.

## Metaphysical

Sense rule deeply established in the subjective life forces of the organism. (See 
GEZER.) (At least-two of the sons of the Philistine giant, Raphah, were slain at Gob by the Israelites.)
