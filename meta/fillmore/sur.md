# Sur

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** s r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A gate of the Temple (II Kings 1 1:6).

## Metaphysical

Elimination, as it pertains to the consciousness and the organism of man (going o, 
exit, removing, removed).

Susanna, su-san'-nCE (Heb white; shining; brilliant; bright-colored; lily; lily-like; ornamental; 
beautiful; pure.

One of the women who went with Jesus and His disciples and "ministered unto them of their 
substance" (Luke 8:3).

Metaphysical. Purity of soul (white, shining, lily, pure, a woman follower of Jesus, one of those 
who ministered to His needs).
