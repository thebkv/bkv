# Zebedee

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** zeb'-e-dee

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of James and John, who were two of the disciples of Jesus Christ (Matt. 4:21). This name 
is taken from Zabdi; Zebadiah is a form of the same name.

## Metaphysical

See ZABDI and ZEBADIAH for the significance of Zebedee.

Personal ambition trying to enter the kingdom is represented in Matthew 20: 20,21 by the 
attempt of the wife of Zebedee to gain the promise that her two sons, James and John (wisdom and 
love), should sit, one at Jesus' right hand and the other at Jesus' left, in the kingdom of heaven.

Ambition should not be ki lled out. It should be exalted. When thus transformed it becomes 
purely spiritual aspiration, or the desire for spiritual excellence.
