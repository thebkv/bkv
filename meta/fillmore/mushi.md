# Mushi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mu'-shi

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Merari, and grandson of Levi (Exod. 6:19).

## Metaphysical

A phase of the natural love in the human consciousness (a grandson of Levi; Levi 
signifies the first conscious development of the love faculty in man) that is very sensitive, susceptible, 
impressionable, touchy, quick to sense or feel conditions and to take offense. The individual who is 
dominated by this tendency of the love faculty expressing on the sense plane is likely to avoid direct 
contact with others (withdrawn) and to feel very negative and cut off from his associates, lonely and 
deserted, at times.
