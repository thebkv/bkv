# Nemuelites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Nemuel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The "family" or descendants, of Nemuel the son of Simeon (Num. 26:12).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and like to that in consciousness for which Nemuel 
stands. (See NEMUEL.)
