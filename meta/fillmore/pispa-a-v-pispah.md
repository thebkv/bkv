# Pispa (A.V., Pispah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Pispah), pis-pCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Jether, of the Israelitish tribe of Asher (I Chron. 7:38).

## Metaphysical

Jether, father of Pispa, represents an exalted thought and a thought of abundance. 
Asher means happy, and the significance points both to understanding and substance. Pispa (expansion, 
swelling, dispersion, profusion, abundance) bespeaks the enlarging, increasing, and spreading of the 
Jether and Asher phases of thought in consciousness.
