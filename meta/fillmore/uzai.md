# Uzai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u'-zai

## Meanings

- hasting of Jehovah
- Jah is refuge
- strong
- robust
- powerful
- mighty

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Palal, who helped in repairing Jerusalem's wall after the Babylonian captivity (Neh.

3:25).

## Metaphysical

Uzai bespeaks strength, health, power, might, divine protection, and earnest, 
zealous, spiritual activity toward perfection (hasting of Jehovah, Jah is refuge, strong, robust, powerful, 
mighty).
