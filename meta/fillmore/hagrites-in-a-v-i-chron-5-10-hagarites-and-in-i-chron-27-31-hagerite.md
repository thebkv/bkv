# Hagrites (in A.V., I Chron. 5:10, Hagarites, and in I Chron. 27:31, Hagerite)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Hagar

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Hagar, a tribe of Ishmaelites (I Chron. 5:10; Psalms 83:6, margin; in text, 
Hagarenes). Jaziz the Hagrite was overseer of the flocks in David's reign (I Chron. 27:30).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the Hagar and Ishmael phases of consciousness in man. 
(See HAGAR and ISHMAEL.)
