# Media

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A country in Asia that is always mentioned with Persia in the Bible. The Medes were descended 
from Madai, son of Japheth (Esth. 1:3).

## Metaphysical

The idea of sufficiency enters into the significance of Media and Madai only as the 
psychical in man is raised to spiritual understanding and realization. (See MADAI.)
