# Rhodes

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** rhodes

## Meanings

- rose
- roses
- a place of rosebushes

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a An island in the Mediterranean Sea. b A city on the island. Paul stopped at Rhodes on his way 
to Jerusalem (Acts 21:1).

## Metaphysical

The places mentioned in Acts 21:1-3,— places through which Paul passed on his 
way to Jerusalem, whence he was going against the repeated warnings of Spirit— with the exception of 
Patara, indicate pleasant phases of consciousness in the individual who is seeking the highest. These 
pleasant phases of consciousness are not, however, really on the spiritual plane. Rhodes (roses) 
symbolizes a phase of love or human affection, the rose being a flower that stands especially for the love 
thought in man.
