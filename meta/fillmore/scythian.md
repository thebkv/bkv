# Scythian

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** s yth'-i-an

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A name generally applied to the wandering, barbarous tribes of people north of the Black and 
Caspian seas (Col. 3:11).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts of a very low, ignorant, uncultivated, and seemingly warring character 
(rough, barbarous, rude, ignorant, fierce-looking, degraded). When the "old man" has been put off, 
however, and the "new man" fully put on, there will be no more Scythians in consciousness, because all 
one's thoughts will then have been raised to the Christ standard.
