# Kiriath-baal (A. V., Kirjath-baal)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjath-baal), kir'-i-ath-ba'-al

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The same place as Kiriath-jearim, a city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:60).

## Metaphysical

In Kiriath-baal the idea is given that, at this particular phase of unfoldment in the 
individual, this group of thoughts of dominion and abundance in consciousness still attributes power to 
things formed instead of understanding that all power and all reality exist in formless Spirit. (See 
BAAL; see KIRIATH-JEARIM also.)
