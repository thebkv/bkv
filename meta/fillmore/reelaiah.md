# Reelaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** re-el-a'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One who returned from the Babylonian captivity with Zerubbabel (Ezra 2:2). He is called 
Raamiah in Nehemiah 7:7.
