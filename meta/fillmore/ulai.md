# Ulai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u'-lai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A river near Shushan the palace, in Persia. It was there that Daniel saw his vision of a ram and a 
he-goat (Dan. 8:2).

## Metaphysical

A current of thought in the individual (a river), of a firm, strong, powerful, 
pushing, aggressive character more material than spiritual ( twisted together, strong, robust, powerful, 
foremost, ram's horn, leather bottle ). This current of thought may be one concerning the seemingly 
physical organism (leather bottle suggests this interpretation).
