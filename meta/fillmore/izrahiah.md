# Izrahiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** iz-ra-hiO-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Uzzi, of the tribe of Issachar (I Chron. 7:3).

## Metaphysical

The Christ mind, or spiritual understanding, shining brilliantly and clearly in and 
through the consciousness of the individual. This is made possible by an inner realization of divine 
strength (Uzzi, meaning Jah is strong) established in and actuated by a very active zeal (Issachar) for 
Truth, for the highest.
