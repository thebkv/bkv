# Salmai (A. V., Shalmai)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Shalmai), sal'-mai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the Nethinim whose descendants returned from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 7:48). He 
is called Shamlai in Ezra 2:46.

## Metaphysical

The serving thought in consciousness that rejoices greatly, gives praise and 
thanksgiving to God, because it perceives that all good comes from or through Jehovah. It is learning 
that all service should be done as to the Lord and that He is the compensator, not man. (Jah is 
recompenser, my thanks, my garment, the garment here being the praise and thanksgiving with which 
one clothes himself. "Put on thy beautiful garments, O Jerusalem." "All thy garments smell of myrrh, 
and aloes, end cassia.")
