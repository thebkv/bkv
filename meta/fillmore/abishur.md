# Abishur

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-bi'-shur

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man of the tribe of Judah; son of Shammai (I Chron. 2:28).

## Metaphysical

A perception, or recognition, of God as a wall of strength and of protection about 
one and within one; a stronghold; a fortification against error and seeming weakness (father of a strong 
wall, father of fortitude, my father is a wall of strength).
