# Zenan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze'-nan

## Meanings

- rich in flocks
- place of the flocks

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town in the lowland of Judah (Josh. 15 :37). It is thought to be the same place as Zaanan of 
Micah 1:11.

## Metaphysical

Abundant substance and vitality (rich in flocks, place of flocks; see ZAANAN).
