# Paulus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Lat
- **Pronunciation:** pau'-lus

## Meanings

- restrained
- constrained
- reduced, lessened, made small
- little

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The surname of Sergius, the proconsul at Paphos on the island of Cyprus (Acts 13:7)

## Metaphysical

See SERGIUS PAULUS.

peace-Harmony and tranquillity derived from awareness of the Christ consciousness. "Peace I 
leave with you; my peace I give unto you" (John 14:27). Steadfast affirmations of peace will harmonize 
the whole body structure and open the way to attainment of health conditions in mind and body.

Until world peace is based on the divine law of love and this law incorporated into the pact of 
peace as well as into the minds of those who sign the pact, there will be no permanent peace.
