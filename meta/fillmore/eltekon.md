# Eltekon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el'-te-kon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:59).

## Metaphysical

An aggregation of thoughts in consciousness that is based upon divine principle 
(founded by God). This group of thoughts is centered in the assurance that Principle, God, is perfectly 
upright, correct, just, unchangeable, and that Spirit constantly works in the universe and man to establish 
in order, to make straight, all persons, all things (God makes straight, God sets upright, i.e., establishes 
in order).
