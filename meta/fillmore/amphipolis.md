# Amphipolis

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** am-phlp'-o-l;s

## Meanings

- around the city
- about the city
- surrounded city

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Macedonian city through which Paul and Silas passed while Paul was on one of his missionary 
journeys (Acts 17:1).

## Metaphysical

Truth passes through several stages in entering the mind: doubt, then examination, 
then great desire for the Truth, then acceptance. These are represented by the Gentile cities, Amphipolis, 
Apollonia, and Thessalonica, through which Paul passed, and Beroea, where "he received the word with 
all readiness of mind."
