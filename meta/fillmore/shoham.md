# Shoham

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sho'-ham

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a The name of a very precious stone. It is thought to be either the beryl or the onyx, and is 
translated as such in various places in the Bible (see Gen. 2:12; Exod. 28:9; 35:9, 27; Job 28:16; Rev. 
21:20; see both text and margin), b Son of Jaaziah, of the Merari Levites, in the time that David was 
king of Israel (I Chron. 24:27).

## Metaphysical

A very highly refined idea and expression of substance in individual 
consciousness, or spiritual substance being revealed in all its purity, beauty, and priceless value to the 
consciousness of the spiritually awakened individual (universally sublimated, quickened, sardonyx, 
onyx, beryl, the precious stones here bespeaking substance).
