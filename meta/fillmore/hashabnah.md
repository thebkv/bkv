# Hashabnah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha-shab'-nah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One who joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant (Neh. 10:25).

## Metaphysical

The meaning is the same as that of Hashabiah; that in us which knows Jehovah 
within us to be our friend who cares lovingly and watchfully for us. In our silent communion with God 
this truth is borne in upon our souls and we realize our oneness with the Christ.
