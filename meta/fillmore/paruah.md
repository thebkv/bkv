# Paruah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pa-ru'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His son Jehoshaphat was the officer of King Solomon's who gathered provisions out of Issachar 
sufficient to keep the king's household for one month in each year (I Kings 4: 17).

## Metaphysical

The idea of spiritual substance and supply coming forth from within and 
increasing and abounding in the consciousness (breaking forth, budding, blossoming, being born, 
flourishing, increase, progeny). Paruah's son Jehoshaphat signifies judgment's being developed in 
consciousness; thus a right distribution and handling of this substance and supply would be made 
possible.
