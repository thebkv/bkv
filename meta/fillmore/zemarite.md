# Zemarite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A tribe of people who were descended from Canaan, son of Ham (Gen. 10: 18).

## Metaphysical

Rebellious, tyrannical, despotic thoughts and desires belonging to the "mind of the 
flesh" phase of consciousness in unredeemed man; the outer man seeking the dominion that belongs 
alone to the inner, or spiritual, man. (See HAM and CANAAN.)
