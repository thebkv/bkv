# Jehud

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je'-hud

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city, or town, allotted to Dan (Josh. 19:45)

## Metaphysical

The attitude of praise and thanksgiving operative in and through the judgment 
faculty (Dan) in individual consciousness. Thus a higher and truer idea of divine justice is established.
