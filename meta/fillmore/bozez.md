# Bozez

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bo'-zez

## Meanings

- shining
- glittering
- height

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A rocky crag or tooth on one side of the pass by which Jonathan and his armor-bearer went over 
into the garrison of the Philistines and put them to flight (I Sam. 14:4).

## Metaphysical

"The formidable tooth, 'I cannot.' (See SENEH, the name of the crag on the other 
side of the pass.)
