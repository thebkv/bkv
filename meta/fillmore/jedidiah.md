# Jedidiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jed-i-dl'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name that Nathan the prophet gave to Solomon (II Sam. 12:25).

## Metaphysical

Solomon means peaceful, and Jedidiah (beloved of Jehovah, friend of Jehovah) 
signifies the inner realization of divine love and the unifying quality of love in which alone true peace 
can be established.
