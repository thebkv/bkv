# Uzza

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** uz'-zCE

## Meanings

- strength
- might
- power
- firmness
- splendor
- majesty
- glory

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a The name of the man who owned the garden in which Manas seh and Amon, kings of Judah, 
were buried (II Kings 21:18, 26). b A Benjamite, brother of Ahihud (I Chron. 8:7). c An ancestor of 
some of the Nethinim who returned with Zerubbabel from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:49).
