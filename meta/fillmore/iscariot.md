# Iscariot

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** is-car'-i-ot

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The surname of Judas, the disciple of Jesus who betrayed Him (Matt. 10:4; 26:14).

## Metaphysical

Kerioth was a city of Moab against which Jeremiah prophesied (Jer.48:24, 41). 
Moab is the carnal mind. Judas Iscariot was the disciple of Jesus Christ who refers to the life faculty in 
man. Iscariot (man of Kerioth, man of hostile encounters), as the surname of Judas, signifies that the life 
faculty at this phase of overcoming is not fully redeemed from carnal thought and desire. (See JUDAS 
ISCARIOT.)
