## Meanings

- honeysweet
- sweetened with honey
- yielding

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

honey.

The ancient name for Malta, an island in the Mediterranean Sea. Paul and his companions landed 
upon this island when they were shipwrecked, while on their voyage to Rome (Acts 28: 1).

## Metaphysical

Here Melita refers to the sweetness (honey), joy, and agreeable, pleasant feeling 
that are sensed deeply by the individual when in his overcoming he has experienced some great 
deliverance from error and has entered into the peace, content, and satisfaction that follow such an 
experience in overcoming.
