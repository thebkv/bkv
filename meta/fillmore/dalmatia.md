# Dalmatia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** dal-ma'-ti-a

## Meanings

- cloaked
- covered
- concealed
- deceitful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A European province, on the eastern side of the Adriatic Sea, in what is now known as Turkey; it 
was to this place that Paul sent Titus to preach the gospel of Christ (II Tim. 4:10).

## Metaphysical

A double-minded, misleading, deceptive state of consciousness in the individual 
(cloaked, covered, concealed, deceptive; a province). If given way to, it causes one to reason falsely and 
to think and act without sincerity.
