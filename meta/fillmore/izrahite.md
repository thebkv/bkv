# Izrahite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of ox belonging to Zerah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Shamhuth the Izrahite was captain of the course of twenty-four thousand that served King David 
in the fifth month of each year (I Chron. 27:8). An Izrahite was a descendant of Judah's son Zerah.

## Metaphysical

A thought springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Zerah 
signifies. (See ZERAH and SHAMHUTH.)
