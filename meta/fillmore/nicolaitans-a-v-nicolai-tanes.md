# Nicolaitans (A.V., Nicolai tanes)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Nicolai tanes), nic-o-la'-l-tans

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An apparently religious people whose teaching and works are condemned in Revelation 2:6, 15.

## Metaphysical

Mixed thoughts. In Revelation 2:14, 15 the teaching of the Nicolaitans is likened 
to that of Balaam, by which the Israelites were taught to eat things sacrificed to idols and to commit 
fornication. To commit fornication typifies the mixing of one's thoughts with error.

The "sword of my mouth," Revelation 2:16, is the word of absolute Truth. The word of Truth 
sets the law into action, and thus makes war against the Nicolaitans (mixed thoughts).

Unless we keep our eyes on our first love, Spirit, we are apt to make wrong combinations of 
thoughts. This is where the Adversary gets his power even when we do not like him. See Revelation 2:4- 
6 .
