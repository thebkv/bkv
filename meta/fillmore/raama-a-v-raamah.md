# Raama (A.V., Raamah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Raamah), ra'-a-mCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Cush, and grandson of Ham, who was Noah's son (I Chron. 1:9). The name is also spelled 
Raamah in the same verse and in Genesis 10:7.
