# Nebaioth (in A.V., Genesis 25:13, Nebajoth)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Genesis 25:13, Nebajoth), ne-ba'-ioth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Eldest son of Ishmael (Gen. 25: 1316). b The name of the tribe of people that was descended 
from him, and the land in which these people lived (Isa. 60:7).

## Metaphysical

The outer, sensate, or material consciousness, reflecting the light of the inner, true 
ideals that are born of Spirit (heights), and realizing the possibility of bringing forth abundant good 
(cultivation of the soil, fruitfulness, germinations) through the power of the word of understanding 
(prophetic utterances, inspired words).
