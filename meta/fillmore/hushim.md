# Hushim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hu'-shim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Dan (Gen. 46:23). b A Benjamite (I Chron. 7:12). c The wife of Shaharaim, a Benjamite 
(IChron. 8:8).

## Metaphysical

An acceleration of thought activity (hasters, people of haste) in the judgment 
faculty in man (Dan) and in the active faith faculty (Benjamin). In the former, this accelerating of 
thought activity takes place in the reasoning or masculine phase of the justice consciousness only 
(Hushim of Dan was a man), while the latter is of the soul, feminine, as well as of the masculine (one of 
the Hushims of Benjamin was a woman). In all three the tendency is toward the carnal or physical. A 
good antidote for a hurried thought is to be found in Isaiah 28:16: "He that believeth shall not be in 
haste."
