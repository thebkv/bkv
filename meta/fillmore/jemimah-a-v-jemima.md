# Jemimah (A. V., Jemima)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jemima), je-ml'-mah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Eldest of the three daughters of Job who were born after his restoration to health, wealth, and 
power (Job 42:14).

## Metaphysical

The soul purified from fear, contention, and self-justification, and made gentle, 
peaceful, loving, fertile for greater acceptance and realization of good.
