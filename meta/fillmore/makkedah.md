# Makkedah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mak-ke'-dah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Five kings were fleeing from Joshua. They hid in the cave at Makkedah, and were killed and 
buried there by Joshua (Josh. 10:16-28).

## Metaphysical

The consciousness perceiving through discrimination, judgment, the true spiritual 
quality of man's inner life, strength, and substance (marking, selecting, i. c., for excellence, place of 
herdsmen, place of shepherds). Thus the subconscious life and strength, or seemingly animal forces, are 
lifted up by the I AM, and transmuted into higher and more spiritual expression, while the Carnal, error 
thoughts that have ruled them are put away (Joshua killed the five heathen kings and buried them in a 
cave at Makkedah).
