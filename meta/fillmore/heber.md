# Heber

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** he'-ber

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Grandson of Asher (Gen. 46: 17). b Heber the Kenite was the husband of Jael, who killed 
Sisera, captain of the hosts of Jabin, king of Canaan (Judg. 4:17). c A Judahite (I Chron. 4:18). d A 
Benjamite (I Chron. 8:17).

## Metaphysical

A passing over from the purely sensate, physical, earthly thought to a higher 
concept of religious Truth. This is the beginning of a conscious alliance with the mind of Spirit, and it 
will culminate in man and God's becoming companions, associates, friends, and, in a sense, equals: "Ye 
therefore shall be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect" (Matt. 5:48); "No longer do I call you 
servants... but I have called you friends" (John 15:15).

The names Heber and Eber in the Bible seem to be the same. (See EBER.) There is an idea held 
among the Jewish people that the term Hebrew is derived from the Eber of Genesis 10:24, who is 
believed to be an ancestor of Abraham (in the Authorized Version, in Luke 3: 35, the name is written 
Heber).
