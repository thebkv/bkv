# Higgaion

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hig-ga'-ion

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A word or name mentioned in Psalms 9: 16. In the Authorized Version it is mentioned also in the 
margin of Psalms 92:3.

## Metaphysical

To think deeply along any specific line of Truth that has just been presented to 
consciousness; contemplation; meditation; also the bringing of the whole being into harmony with one's 
deep inner thought (hum, murmur, a low vibrant sound).
