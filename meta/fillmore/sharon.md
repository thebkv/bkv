# Sharon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sh%or'-on

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A level tract of country in Palestine, bordering on the Mediterranean Sea, and extending from 
Joppa to C%sarea (I Chron. 27:29; Isa. 35:2; 65:10). b A place mentioned as being in the inheritance of 
Gad, on the east of the Jordan (I Chron. 5:16).

## Metaphysical

The rich substance of Spirit established in body consciousness (fruitful, 
prosperous a tract of country in Palestine that was noted for its rich pasture lands). See LASSHARON 
for the significance of plain. The thoughts of justice, honor, poise, and harmony are also symbolized in 
this name.

Sharonite, sh%or'-on-ite (fr. Heb.).

A native of Sharon (I Chron. 27:29). The name is applied here to Shitrai, who had charge of 
David's herds that fed in Sharon.

Metaphysical. A thought activity springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which 
Sharon signifies. (See SHARON and LASSHARON.)
