# Addi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-dl

## Meanings

- ornament
- adorned

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:28).

## Metaphysical

The thought that the Spirit of truth is the ornament that enriches the soul of man, 
giving him true grace and beauty, is suggested in this name ( ornament , adorned).
