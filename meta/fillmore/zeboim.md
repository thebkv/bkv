# Zeboim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze-bo'-im

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A valley in Benjamin (I Sam. 13 : 1 8). b A town that was occupied by Benjamites after the 
return from the Captivity (Neh. 11 :34).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts, deep within the life forces of man, that is given over to the 
expression of his beastly and sensual nature, appetite, and passion (place of ravenous beasts, ravenous 
appetites, bestiality; see ZEBOIIM). This thought center, at the stage of individual unfoldment signified 
in I Samuel 13:18 and Nehemiah 1 1 :34, has come under the directive power of man's active faith in 
God (Benjamin), for upliftment.
