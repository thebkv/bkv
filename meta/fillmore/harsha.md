# Harsha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hSr'-sha

## Meanings

- artificer
- craftsman
- worker
- magician
- enchanter
- deaf
- dumb
- silent

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

He was of the Nethinim. His descendants returned from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:52).

## Metaphysical

A religious thought in man that tends to the psychical yet seeks to serve God. It is 
not really receptive to spiritual Truth; it is deaf— meaning not receptive, not responsive. It works in the 
psychical, or soul, realm; thus it expresses much that seems to be occult, magical, supernatural 
(magician, artificer, enchanter). This thought belongs to the bond-servant class (Nethinim) in 
consciousness, and must give way to the true spiritual understanding that comes with the knowledge of 
divine sonship (see Gal. 4).
