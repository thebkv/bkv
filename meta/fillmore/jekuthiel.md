# Jekuthiel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-ku'-thi-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ezrah, by "his wife the Jewess," and founder of Zanoah. He was of the tribe of Judah (I 
Chron. 4:18).

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to the Judah (inner praise and life) consciousness that is very 
obedient and earnest in its devotion to Truth (obedience toward God, piety toward God). Its trust is in 
the divine law, in Spirit, as being its all-sufficient strength and might (hope of God, strength of God, 
mighty is God).
