# Ishma

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ish-mCE

## Meanings

- high
- elevated
- upright
- brilliant
- marvelous
- laid waste
- desolation

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

ruin.

A descendant of Judah (I Chron. 4:3). He was a son of the "father" or founder of Etam.

## Metaphysical

Ishma reveals the outcome of Truth expressed in one's consciousness and life; it 
also reveals the result of giving way to thoughts and activities such as those which are symbolized by 
Etam. (See ETAM.)
