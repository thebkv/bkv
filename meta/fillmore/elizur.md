# Elizur

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-h'-zur

## Meanings

- God is a rock
- God is a refuge, i.e., stable, sure, a bulwark of strength

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shedeur of the tribe of Reuben (Num. 1:5). He helped to number the Israelites while they 
were in the wilderness on their way to the Promised Land.

## Metaphysical

The spiritual insight that discerns God as a rock, a sure, immovable foundation of 
strength, protection, and Truth, upon which man builds his spiritual consciousness, to eternal life and 
peace. Reuben is faith in its aspect of discernment, sight; Shedeur, father of Elizur, means shedder of 
light.)
