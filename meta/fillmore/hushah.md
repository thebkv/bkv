# Hushah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hu'-shah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:4).

## Metaphysical

The swiftness and vehemence of unrepressed desire. In its expression this desire 
becomes good or evil according to the thoughts by which it is directed.
