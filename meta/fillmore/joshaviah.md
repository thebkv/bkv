# Joshaviah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** josh-a-viO-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of David's guard, son of Elnaam (I Chron. 1 1:46).

## Metaphysical

Establishing in consciousness (seat of Jehovah, dwelling of Jah) the spiritual idea 
of justice, fair-mindedness, equality (Jehovah is equality), and the all-sufficiency of God, Spirit, through 
I AM, Jehovah, to meet one's every need (sufficiency of Jah).
