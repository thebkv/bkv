# people

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

Strongly organized thoughts of energy and power in the subconscious realm of 
mind in man. When freed from carnal desires and warring tendencies, these thoughts will work mightily 
for the good of the individual, but in their unredeemed or error-possessed state they are very violent and 
destructive. (Upon coming into the land of the Gadarenes, Jesus was met by "two possessed with 
demons, coming forth out of the tombs, exceeding fierce, so that no man could pass by that way"— see 
Mark 5:1 also— and Jesus cast the demons out of the men and healed them.)
