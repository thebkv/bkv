# Pas-dammim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pas-dam'-mim

## Meanings

- end of bloodshed
- boundary of blood
- limit of blood

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Judah where the Philistines gathered to battle against Israel, and were defeated (I 
Chron. 11:13). In I Samuel 17:1 it is called Ephes-dammim.
