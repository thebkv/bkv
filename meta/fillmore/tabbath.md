# Tabbath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tab '-bath

## Meanings

- spreading out
- extending
- extension
- good report
- famous
- celebrated

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place mentioned in the account of the fleeing of the hosts of Midian from Gideon and his three 
hundred picked men (Judg. 7:22).

## Metaphysical

The broadening and deepening of peace, joy, and good thoughts in consciousness 
(extension, good report) and of spiritual excellence and true worth (famous, celebrated) that result from 
the overcoming of contention and strife. (The Midianites who fled from Gideon and his men signify 
strife and contention).
