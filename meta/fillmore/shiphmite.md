# Shiphmite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or pertaining to Shepham

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Thought to be a native of Shepham. "Zabdi the Shiphmite" was "over the increase of the 
vineyards for the wine cellars" in David's reign (I Chron. 27:27).

## Metaphysical

A thought that brings forth abundantly of glad, joyous life and good. (See 
SHEPHAM.)
