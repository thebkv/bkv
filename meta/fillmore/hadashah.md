# Hadashah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** had'-a-shah

## Meanings

- new
- make new
- make anew
- repair
- restore
- renew

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the lowland of Judah, mentioned as between Zenan and Migdal-gad (Josh. 15:37).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts (city) in the subconscious mind (lowland of Judah) that is 
established in a power that works for perpetual youth (new, make new, restore, renew ) in the individual. 
"If any man is in Christ, he is a new creature: the old things are passed away; behold, they are become 
new" (II Cor. 5:17). This group of thoughts, or state of consciousness, is closely associated with 
abundant substance, vitality, power, and clearness of vision (Zenan, rich in flocks, and Migdal-gad, 
tower of Gad, Gad referring to the power faculty in man and also meaning seer).
