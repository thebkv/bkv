# Magadan (A. V., Magdala)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Magdala), mag'-a-dan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A village on the west shore of the Sea of Galilee (Matt. 15:39). It is thought to have been the 
birthplace of Mary Magdalene, and is probably the same place as Migdal-el, of Naphtali (Josh. 9:38).

## Metaphysical

Exalting and magnifying strength in consciousness (a village of Naphtali, and 
meaning elevation, tower, castle, fortress, greatness).
