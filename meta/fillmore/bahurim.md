# Bahurim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba-hu'-rlm

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite town near Jerusalem, on the road to the Jordan (II Sam. 3:16). It was there that 
Shimei, of the family of Saul, cursed David and threw stones and dust at him when he was fleeing from 
Absalom (II Sam. 16:5-8); it was in that place that a woman hid in a well the messengers (Jonathan and 
Ahimaaz) of David, who were being pursued while they were on their way to David to tell him of 
Absalom's plans (II Sam. 17:18).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of physical strength and power ( choice young men, , young 
warriors). Such a consciousness is excellent, but if it is not established in love and wisdom the result is 
strife and a tendency to do unwise things, for which one repents afterward; this is typified in the case of 
Shimei. One may also do noble acts (saving David's messengers by hiding them in a well).
