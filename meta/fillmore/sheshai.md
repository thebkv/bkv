# Sheshai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she'-shai

## Meanings

- whitish
- wearer of fine linen
- free
- noble
- princely

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Anak the giant. He was driven from Hebron and was killed by Caleb and the men of 
Judah (Josh. 15:14; Judg. 1:10).

## Metaphysical

The high esteem in which the reasonings of the intellect functioning in sensate 
thought are held by the individual who is unawakened spiritually (free, noble, princely, whitish). (See 
ANAK, AHIMAN, and TALMAI.)
