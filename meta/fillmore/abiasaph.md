# Abiasaph

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-bl'-a-saph

## Meanings

- father of gathering

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Levi (Exod. 6:24). He is called Ebiasaph in I Chronicles 6: 37.

## Metaphysical

A thought in consciousness, of a kind that gathers together, or draws together 
(father of gathering). Such thoughts belong to the love (Levi) nature in man. They are of a harmonizing 
character and they help to hold the organism together.
