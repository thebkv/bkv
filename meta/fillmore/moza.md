# Moza

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mo'-zCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Caleb and his concubine Ephah (I Chron. 2:46). b Son of Zimri and father of Binea, of 
the tribe of Benjamin (I Chron. 8:36, 37).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the active faith (Benjamin) and praise (Judah) 
consciousness in man. These thoughts have their origin or source in Spirit (the two men named Moza 
were Israelites). They go forth throughout the organism of the individual as a bubbling spring or 
fountain of life and wholeness, of all good. "Whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall

never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall become in him a well of water springing up unto 
eternal life" (John 4:14).

## Preserved OCR Source

mouth; vein. The name Moza relates to everything that goes forth from any source.
