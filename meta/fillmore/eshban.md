# Eshban

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** esh'-ban

## Meanings

- man of understanding
- thinking man
- man of wisdom
- wise man
- son of

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

fire.

Son of Dishon, or Dishan, a chief of the Horites (Gen. 36:26).

## Metaphysical

The belief, held by the outer man, that wisdom and understanding come through 
the perception and reasonings of the senses. (If Eshban had been an Israelite instead of a Horite, the 
significance of the name would be that of true understanding.)
