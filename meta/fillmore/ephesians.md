# Ephesians

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- belonging to Ephesus

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Inhabitants of Ephesus (Acts 19:28; also the book of Ephesians).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts that belong to the Ephesus state of consciousness in man. The Ephesians 
to whom the Epistle of Paul was written signify the enlightened and partially enlightened thoughts that 
belong to this state of consciousness. (See EPHESUS.)
