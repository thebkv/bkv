# Socoh (in A. V., I Kings 4:10, Sochoh; I Sam. 17:1, Shochoh)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Kings 4:10, Sochoh; I Sam. 17:1, Shochoh), so'-coh

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of two cities of Judah, a one in the lowlands, and b the other in the hill country (Josh. 
15:35, 48). It was at Socoh in the lowlands of Judah that the Philistines gathered for battle against the

Israelites under Saul (I Sam. 17:1). Socoh is also named in the district whence Benhesed obtained 
victuals for King Solomon's household for one month in each year (I Kings 4:10).
