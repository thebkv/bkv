# Enan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-nan

## Meanings

- lowing forth
- eyed, i. e . , having eyes
- a natural spring
- a fountain

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Ahira, of the tribe of Naphtali (Num. 1:15).

## Metaphysical

The great strength and energy of the cleansing Christ life in consciousness 
(Naphtali refers to strength, and a fountain and flowing forth suggest life and cleansing). This cleansing 
fountain of life is allied with divine intelligence, sight, understanding (having eyes). 
end, of world. (See Matt. 13:49.)
