# Jehdeiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jeh-de'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Levite (I Chron. 24:20). b "The Meronothite," one who had charge of the asses, in David's 
reign (I Chron. 27:30).

## Metaphysical

Ideas of divine unity, or oneness with the inner Christ, Jehovah, the perfect-man 
idea in Divine Mind, or our true I AM. This gives joy, makes glad the heart, and enables us to direct 
aright, control, and care for even the stubborn animal nature in us that the word "asses" signifies.
