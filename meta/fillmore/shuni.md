# Shuni

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shu-ni

## Meanings

- rest
- quiet
- calm
- peace
- tranquillity
- ease

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Gad (Gen. 46:16).

## Metaphysical

A tranquil, poised, peaceful attitude of thought (rest, calm, peace, quiet) existing 
in the power faculty in the individual (Gad, son of Jacob, signifies the power faculty in man, and Shuni 
was a son of Gad).

This restful attitude that Shuni represents can be carried so far, because of the love of ease, as to 
bring about inertia or deadness, which is anything but good.

Shunites, shu-nites (fr. Heb.).

Descendants of Shuni, son of Gad (Num. 26:15).

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from and in character like that in consciousness which Shuni 
signifies. (See SHUNI).

Shuphamites, shu'-pham-ites (fr. Heb.).

The descendants of Shephupham, son or grandson of Benjamin (Num. 26:39).

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from and of a character like that in consciousness which 
Shephuphan signifies. (See SHEPHUPHAN.)
