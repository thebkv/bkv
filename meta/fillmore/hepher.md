# Hepher

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** he'-pher

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A man of the tribe of Manasseh, the head of the family of the Hepherites (Num. 26:32). b Son 
of Ashhur of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:6). c One of David's guard (I Chron. 1 1:36). d A city of 
Canaan that was conquered by Joshua (Josh. 12:17).

## Metaphysical

Understanding, that which opens (a digging, a well, a search) the consciousness 
to recognition and realization of the great life forces of the organism. (See MANASSEH, GILEAD, and 
ASHHUR.)
