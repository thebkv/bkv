# Aiah (in A. V., Gen. 36:24, Ajah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Gen. 36:24, Ajah), a'-iah

## Meanings

- a cry
- a clamor
- a hawk
- vulture

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of Saul's concubine, Rizpah, whose sons were delivered by David to the Gideonites to 
be hanged (II Sam. 3:7; 21:8). b A descendant of Seir the Horite (Gen. 36:24).

## Metaphysical

Destructive, devouring thoughts pertaining to the animal consciousness and to 
what should be the higher thought realm in the individual (a cry, a clamor, a hawk, a vulture; an 
Israelite and a Horite) .
