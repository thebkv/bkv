# Tarpelites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- plunderers, ravishers
- despoilers

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Assyrian colonists in Samaria (Ezra 4:9).

## Metaphysical

The violent and destructive tendency (ravishers) of the phase of intellectual 
thought that is represented by the Assyrians. (See ASSYRIA and ASSYRIANS.)
