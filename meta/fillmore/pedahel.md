# Pedahel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ped'-a-hel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ammihud and prince of the tribe of Naphtali. He was chosen to help divide Canaan 
among the Israelitish tribes (Num. 34:28).

## Metaphysical

A very influential thought in the strength faculty of man (a prince of Naphtali) 
that is established in the idea of divine deliverance from bondage to limitation and error (whom God 
delivers, God redeems, God frees from servitude).
