# Regem - melech

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** re'-gem - me'-lech

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One who was sent by the captive Jews in Babylon to Jerusalem to inquire of the priests there 
concerning fasting, and other things (Zech. 7:2).

## Metaphysical

A thought of power that is related to and in association with the will faculty in the 
individual (friend ofMelech, associate of the king, regal color, i. e., purple, purple representing power 
and the king representing the will). This thought perceives a higher ruling power for man than the 
human will, even the divine law, and it seeks association with that also (royal friend, one who was sent 
to Jerusalem from the Jews in Babylon to find out more about the statutes of Jehovah).
