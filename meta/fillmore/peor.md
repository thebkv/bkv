# Peor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe'-™r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A mountain in Moab. Balak, king of Moab, took Balaam to the top of this mountain in an effort 
to induce him to curse Israel, but Balaam was forced to bless Israel instead (Num. 23:28—24:9). b A 
Moabitish idol (Num. 25:18; Josh. 22:17).
