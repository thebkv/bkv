# Bethuel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be-thu'-el

## Meanings

- dweller in God
- dweller in God or abode of God
- filiation of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A nephew of Abraham, and father of Rebecca, Isaac's wife (Gen. 22:22, 23); he was also the 
father of Laban, whose daughters became Jacob's wives (Gen. 28:2, 5). b A city of Simeon (I Chron. 
4:30).
