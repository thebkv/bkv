## Metaphysical

A ruling thought in the sense-reasoning plane of consciousness in man. This is not 
the true source of understanding; it is head of the realm of thought represented by the Assyrians (a king 
of the Assyrians; see ASSYRIA). Though this ruling thought multiplies error in the consciousness that 
gives it place (Sin, moon god, has increased the brothers ), it comes to an end (bramble of destruction; 
the king was killed and the Assyrians were overcome).
