# Pekod

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe'-kod

## Meanings

- visitation
- punishment

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Babylonia (or a name that stands for Babylon) against which both Jeremiah and 
Ezekiel prophesied (Jer. 50:21; Ezek. 23:23).

## Metaphysical

The harvest time of error.

There is a law of sowing and reaping. When error thoughts, fears, and beliefs have been sown in 
our consciousness they must— unless we discern the Truth and root out the errors by means of our true 
thoughts, words, and acts— come to fruition, and the harvest time will bring inharmonious experiences of 
some kind. Under the old thought this harvest time of error was looked upon as a visitation or 
punishment from the Lord. We now understand it to be but the outworking of some phase of divine law 
that we ourselves have set into action destructively, through our ignorance, and have left to work out 
into inharmony in our life.
