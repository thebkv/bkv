# Adino

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-l-no

## Meanings

- his ornament
- his pleasure

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The Eznite," one of David's mighty men, and "chief of the captains" (II Sam. 23:8); Josheb- 
basshebeth or Jashobeam, a Tahchemonite, and Adino are the same man. He is called Jashobeam in I 
Chronicles 11:11.

See JASHOBEAM and EZNITE.
