# Epanetus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ep-a-ne'-tus

## Meanings

- approbation
- commendation
- praise
- praise -worthy

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Christian at Rome. He was a very dear friend of Paul's: "Salute Ep%netus my beloved, who is 
the firstfruits of Asia unto Christ" (Rom. 16:5).

## Metaphysical

The first acceptance of the Christ, or love teaching of the heart, by the hardened, 
seemingly material, warring thoughts of the head (Rome), in individual consciousness. This is very 
praiseworthy and it causes great rejoicing in the realm of the word of Truth (Paul).
