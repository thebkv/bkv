# Korahites (in A. V., Num. 26:58, Korathites; Exod. 6:24, Korhites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Levites descended from Korah, great-grandson of Levi (Exod. 6:24).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Korah, the 
great-grandson of Levi, signifies. (See KORAH.) Clear, crystal, definitions of Korah, bespeak a 
comprehension that would aid in raising these Korah thoughts to the love and productiveness that 
belong to their true nature.
