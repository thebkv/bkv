# Shammua

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sham-mu'-CE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Zaccur, the man who was chosen from the tribe of Reuben to help spy out the land of 
Canaan preparatory to the Israelites' going over to possess it (Num. 13:4). b A son of David (II Sam. 
5:14); he is called Shimea in I Chronicles 3:5. c A Levite and a priest (Neh. 11:17; 12:18).

## Metaphysical

Common truth; thoughts of man's higher religious state of mind that are attentive 
and receptive to only such truth as is commonly and widely known and accepted (what is heard, report, 
fame, obedience ). They would tend to make light of and scorn the deep inspirations of Spirit, or 
anything not generally understood as Truth.
