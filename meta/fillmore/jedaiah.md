# Jedaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-da'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a An Israelite of the tribe of Simeon (I Chron. 4:37). b Israelitish priests (I Chron. 9:10; 24:7; 
Neh. 11:10; 12:7). c One who helped to rebuild Jerusalem's wall (Neh. 3:10).

## Metaphysical

The Israelitish men by this name signify one's high spiritual thoughts of greatly 
increased executive power, or the ability to decide matters and to do things, in the manifest as well as in 
the unmanifest or consciousness ( hand of Jehovah, executive power of Jehovah), by acknowledgment of 
one's true, spiritual I AM, Jehovah, or Christ ( confess Jehovah, profession of Jehovah, praise Jehovah ).
