# Pirathonite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Pirathon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An inhabitant of Pirathon (Judg. 12:13; II Sam. 23:30). Abdon the son of Hillel, a judge of Israel, 
and Benaiah, one of David's mighty men, were Pirathonites.

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to that in consciousness that Pirathon signifies. (See 
PIRATHON.)
