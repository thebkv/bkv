# Adina

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-;-na

## Meanings

- ornament
- slender
- delicate

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shiza the Reubenite; a chief of the Reubenites, and one of David's captains (I Chron.

11:42).

## Metaphysical

A perceptive, discerning, seeing thought of the mind (ornament, .slender; a chief 
man of the Reubenites, and Reuben refers to the sense of sight). This thought is of a harmonizing, 
attracting nature because the loving idea (Shiza) is back of it.
