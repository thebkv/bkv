# Elonites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-lon-ltes

## Meanings

- belonging to Elon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Elon, who was a son of Zebulun and grandson of Jacob (Num. 26:26).

## Metaphysical

A powerful protective state of consciousness in man that is brought about by his 
fully trusting in God. (See ELON.)
