# Azrikam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** az'-r;-kam

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of several Israelitish men (I Chron. 3:23; 8:38; 9:14; II Chron. 28:7; Neh. 11:15).

Metaphysical, Firmness in standing for Truth, resulting from lifting up in consciousness thoughts 
of the reality and power of Spirit as one's sure helper and sustainer.
