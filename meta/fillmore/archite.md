# Archite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ar'-chlte

## Meanings

- a man of Erech
- prolonged
- lengthened
- extended
- adapted

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Hushai, a friend of David's (II Sam. 15:32; I Chron. 27:33). Archite and Archevite are supposed 
to be the same.

See ARCHEVITES and HUSHAI.
