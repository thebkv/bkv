# Jerimoth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jer'-i-moth

## Meanings

- elevations
- heights
- exaltations
- terror of death
- rejection of death

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of several prominent Israelites, some of the tribe of Benjamin, some Levites, and one 
a son of David, though not named in the general list of his sons (I Chron. 7:7; 12:5; 24:30; 25:4; II 
Chron. 11:18; 31:13).
