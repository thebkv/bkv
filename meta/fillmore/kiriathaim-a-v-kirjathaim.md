# Kiriathaim (A. V., Kirjathaim)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjathaim), kirO-a-tha'-im

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city east of the Jordan that Moses gave to the tribe of Reuben (Josh. 13:19). b A city of 
Naphtali that was given over to the Gershomite Levites (I Chron. 6:76). This city is thought to be the 
same place as Kartan.

## Metaphysical

Kiriathaim of the tribe of Reuben would belong to the seeing, discerning faculty 
in individual consciousness, instead of the strength faculty. (See KARTAN.)
