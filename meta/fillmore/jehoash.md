# Jehoash

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-ho'-ash

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Ahaziah. He became ruler of Judah in place of his grandmother Athaliah (II Kings 
11:21; 12:1). He is called Joash in II Kings 11:2, and 13:1. b King of Israel in Samaria (II Kings 13:10).
