# Kedesh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ke'-desh

## Meanings

- holy place
- sacred
- consecrated
- sanctuary
- holiness
- cleanliness

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

purity

a A city of Judah, toward the border of Edom in the south (Josh. 15:23). b A city in Galilee, in 
the hill country of Naphtali, that was set apart for a city of refuge (Josh. 20:7). c A city of Issachar, that 
was given over to the Gershomite Levites (I Chron. 6:72). This city is thought to be the same place that 
is mentioned in Joshua 12:22. Kadesh is the same name.

## Metaphysical

The divine presence within the individual consciousness. (See KADESH.)

"keep my word"— (John 8:51). Treasure the words of Jesus as a saving balm in time of need.

"keys of the kingdom of heaven"— (Matt. 16:19). Affirmation and denial, (see affirmation; denial)
