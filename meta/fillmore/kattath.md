# Kattath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** kat'-tath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A border city of Zebulun (Josh, 19: 15). In Judges 1:30 it is called Kitron. Kattath is thought to be 
the same place as Kartah of Joshua 21:34.

## Metaphysical

The idea expressed in Kattath is that of cutting off, lessening, reducing to 
exceeding smallness and insignificance (cut off, made smaller, very small, insignificant). This no doubt 
refers to putting away error, the limitations of sense thought and belief. Kattath was a border city of 
Zebulun. The Zebulunites failed to drive out the Canaanitish inhabitants, but reduced them to slavery, 
made them "subject to taskwork" (Judg. 1:30)
