# Joktheel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jok'-the-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city in the lowlands of Judah (Josh. 15:38). b A place in Edom called Sela, or the rock or 
cliff, a stronghold of Edom, that Amaziah, king of Judah, took and named Joktheel (II Kings 14:7).

## Metaphysical

Edom, or Esau, typifies the animal, physical, or sense consciousness in man. Sela, 
the rock or cliff, the capital of Edom, represents sense man's trust in physical strength and power. 
Amaziah's conquering this city and renaming it Joktheel signifies the transferring of one's faith and trust 
from outer personal strength and power to the inner spiritual ideas of strength and power. This brings 
about a consciousness of divine protection (protection of God), and a victory over error thoughts and 
activities. Thus the sensate comes into subjection to the spiritual (subdued of God), and great good for 
the whole man is the result (God's reward, i. e.,for victory).
