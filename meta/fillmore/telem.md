# Telem

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te'-lem

## Meanings

- overshadowing
- oppression
- wrongdoing

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A southern city of Judah (Josh. 15:24). b A porter in the Temple. He had married a foreign 
woman, but gave her up at the command of Ezra (Ezra 10:24).

## Metaphysical

The higher thought and life obscured, overshadowed for the time being, by the 
seeming reality and power of some carnal belief in error (overshadowing, oppression, wrongdoing).
