# Aphek

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-phek

## Meanings

- strong
- holding firm
- tenacious
- fortress

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of three or four fortified cities in Israel (Josh. 13:4; 19:30;

Kings 20:26; II Kings 13: 17). They were places that belonged to the enemies of the Israelites, or where 
these enemies —the Assyrians, Philistines, and others —encamped against Israel in battle, time after time.

## Metaphysical

The apparent ability of error states of consciousness in man to stay with him.

From a sense standpoint the thoughts and states of consciousness that comprise the carnal mind are very 
deep-seated and are strongly fortified. It is only by the positive power of the I AM or Christ dominion 
that they can be overcome.
