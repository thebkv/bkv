# Nemuel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** nem'-u-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Eliab, who was a son of Pallu and grandson of Reuben (Num. 26:9). b A son of Simeon 
(Num. 26:12). In Genesis 46:10 and Exodus 6:15 this latter Nemuel is called Jemuel.

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of JEMUEL, which see. Nemuel also 
suggests an increase of light throughout the consciousness ( God is spreading, luminous manifestation of 
God).
