# Elioenai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-li-o-e'-nai

## Meanings

- to Jah are mine eyes
- toward Jehovah are my eyes

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Becher and grandson of Benjamin (I Chron. 7:8). b The name of other Israelites (I 
Chron. 3:23; 4:36); a form of Eliehoenai.
