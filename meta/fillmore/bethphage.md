# Bethphage

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** beth'-pha-ge

## Meanings

- house of figs
- house of unripe figs

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A small town, or village, near Bethany, at the Mount of Olives (Mark 11:1).

## Metaphysical

A place in consciousness where grace is realized. It is a place of unripe fruit 
(house of figs, or house of unripe figs) .
