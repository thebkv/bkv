# Aharhel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-har'-hel

## Meanings

- behind the intrenchment
- behind the breastwork
- after might

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Harum, a descendant of Judah (I Chron. 4:8).

## Metaphysical

A belief in protection and might as coming from God and not emanating from the 
outer man.
