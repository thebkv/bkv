# Azaniah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** az-a-nl'-ah

## Meanings

- Jehovah hears
- whom Jehovah hears
- hearing Jah
- hearing the

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Lord.

Father of Jeshua, a Levite (Neh. 10:9).

## Metaphysical

A natural religious tendency in man that is receptive and obedient to spiritual 
Truth and guidance (hearing Jah, hearing, the Lord). This spirit of receptivity and obedience to Jehovah 
and the divine law is recognized by the true I AM in consciousness, the Christ, or Jehovah (whom 
Jehovah hears); and from it springs a perception of salvation (Jeshua, a form of Joshua, and meaning 
savior, Jehovah is help, or salvation ).
