# Abimelech

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-bim'-e-lech

## Meanings

- father of the king
- Melek (the king) is father
- a royal father

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Kings of Gerar in the time of Abraham and Isaac (Gen. 20: 2-18; 26: 1). Abimelech was the 
name of a line of Philistine kings, b A son of Gideon, or Jerubbaal, by his concubine in Shechem (Judg.

8:31; 9:1). This Abimelech slew all his brothers, except one who escaped, and made himself ruler after 
his father's death, c A priest in the time of David (I Chron. 18:16).

## Metaphysical

The will. Most of the Bible characters of this name stand for some phase of the 
unregenerate will, but Abimelech of I Chronicles 18: 16, a priest of Israel in David's time, is an 
exception. In his case spiritual rulership is suggested, or at least a higher form of intellectual rulership 
than that of the unregenerate will. This Abimelech stands for the intellectual thought or tendency that 
accepts and acknowledges God as the supreme ruler in man's consciousness.
