# Giddalti

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** g;d-dal'-ti

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Heman. He and his father and his brothers served as musicians in the "house of God" 
during the reign of David (I Chron. 25:4-6).

## Metaphysical

Love (Levite) magnifying God (spiritual Truth), thus producing rhythm and 
harmony in the body temple and bringing the organism into tune with the Infinite. This leads to true 
greatness and strength.
