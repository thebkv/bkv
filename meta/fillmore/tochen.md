# Tochen

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** to'-chen

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Simeon (I Chron. 4:32).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts in the attentive, obedient quality of mind that the Israelitish 
tribe of Simeon signifies (a city of Simeon), which is of an examining, weighing, discriminating, 
adjusting, evening character ( made even, leveled, poised, weighed out, proved, tried, adjusted by plumb 
or level, meted, made equcd). Established and set up point to something made firm and stable. This is 
good, so long as the thought of limitation is not allowed to enter (limitation may be suggested in 
portion ).
