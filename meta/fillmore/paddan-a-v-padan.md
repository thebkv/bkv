# Paddan (A.V., Padan)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Padan), pad'-dan

## Meanings

- field
- open plain
- table-land

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The same place as PADDAN-ARAM, which see (Gen. 48:7).

## Metaphysical

A place of substance in the consciousness and the organism of the individual (a 
field, field relating to ground, substance).
