# Ishmerai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ish'-me-rai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A chief man of Benjamin, one of those who were "heads of fathers' houses throughout their 
generations" (I Chron. 8:18, 28).

## Metaphysical

Active, obedient faith (observance; a chief man of Benjamin) as a saving, 
protecting, sustaining quality (preservative, whom Jehovah keeps) in individual consciousness.
