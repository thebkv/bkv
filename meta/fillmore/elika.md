# Elika

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el'-l-ka

## Meanings

- God is rejecter
- God his rejecter
- rejected of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Harodite who belonged to David's guard, named in II Samuel 23: 25. He is omitted in the 
same list of men given in I Chron. 1 1:26-47.

## Metaphysical

Elimination of fear (a Harodite is a native of Harod, or one belonging to Harod, 
and Harod means fear, terror , trembling). It is fear that is rejected of God, or in other words is 
eliminated by Truth.
