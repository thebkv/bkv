# Zuar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zu'-ar

## Meanings

- reduced
- lessened
- made little
- smallness
- littleness
- brought low

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Nethanel, a prince of the tribe of Issachar, who was chosen from that tribe to help 
Moses number the Children of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai, by command of Jehovah 
(Num. 1 :8).

## Metaphysical

Humility (made little, smallness). Nethanel, son of Zuar, signifies the grace that 
God imparts to His children. This grace is realized by those who are small in their own eyes, in so far as 
the lesser self is concerned. "God . . . giveth grace to the humble" (James 4:6; I Pet. 5:5); "whosoever 
shall humble himself shall be exalted" (Matt. 23: 12); "Blessed are the meek : for they shall inherit the 
earth" (Matt. 5:5). (See NETHANEL.)
