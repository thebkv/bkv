# En-gannim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** en-gan'-nlm

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city in the lowland of Judah (Josh. 15:34). b A city of Issachar (Josh. 19:21).

## Metaphysical

Spiritual life and fruitfulness, inherent in and restricted to the consciousness of the 
individual ( fountain of gardens, fount of organic inclosures, fountain of the hedged fields); thus the 
expression and manifestation may be very limited or very broad and abundant.
