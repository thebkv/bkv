# Arab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-rab

## Meanings

- ambuscade
- a lurking place
- a snare, - ambush

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Judah, in the hill country (Josh. 15:52).

## Metaphysical

The reasoning faculty (this city was near Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, and 
belongs therefore to the front brain, or seat of conscious thought— see HEBRON) in man used as a snare 
to entangle (ambuscade) the enemies (error thoughts and beliefs) of Judah (praise) and Israel (true 
thoughts).

Arabah, ar'-a-bah, (Web.) -an arid tract, a sterile region- a desert; burnt up. (In A. V., II Kings 
14:25, sea of the plain; Amos 6:14, wilderness; Deut. 1:1, 2:8, plain; Deut. 11:30, champaign; Ezek. 47: 
8, desert.)

A place in Palestine on the border of the allotment of Benjamin (Josh. 18:18).

Metaphysical. The empty, barren phases of thought and feeling that arise from the earthly ideas 
of substance in man, which have been separated from the indwelling vitality (an arid tract; a desert).
