# Crispus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Lat
- **Pronunciation:** cris'-lus

## Meanings

- curled
- curly-headed, wrinkled

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man at Corinth who was converted to Christianity and was baptized by Paul. He was ruler of 
the synagogue of Corinth (Acts 18:8; I Cor. 1:14).

## Metaphysical

Crispus (curled), the ruler of the synagogue, who believed with all his house, 
indicates that Truth really encompasses the whole man. The consolation and the encouragement that 
came from the Lord in a vision to Paul (Acts 18:9) indicate the fulfillment of the law in this movement 
of Truth in its work of regeneration.

cross (John 19:17).
