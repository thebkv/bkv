# Shelomoth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shel'-o-moth

## Meanings

- pacifications
- peacemaking
- peacefulness
- love of peace

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A descendant of Levi (I Chron. 24:22). He is called Shelomith in I Chronicles 23:18. b Other 
Levites (I Chron. 23:9; 26:25).

## Metaphysical

The same as Shelomith: the love for and establishment of peace in consciousness; 
at least the awakening to an understanding of the value of peace. "And the work of righteousness shall 
be peace; and the effect of righteousness, quietness and confidence for ever" (Isa. 32:17).
