# Pontus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** pon'-tus

## Meanings

- deep
- sea
- wave

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A district in the northeastern part of Asia Minor, and bordering on the sea, whence it received its 
name (Acts 2:9).

## Metaphysical

Universal mind (deep, sea; see AQUILLA, who was a man of Pontus).
