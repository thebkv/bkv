# Libni

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** libO-ni

## Meanings

- whiteness
- purity
- distinguished
- unto the son mine
- for building up

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Gershon, who was a son of Levi (Exod. 6: 17). b A grandson of Merari, son of Levi (I 
Chron. 6:29). These two men are thought to be the same person, some error having been made in I 
Chron. 6:29.

## Metaphysical

Constructive thoughts (for building up), in the love consciousness (descendants of 
Levi) of the awakening individual, that are distinguished by their purity, whiteness, by their being 
unadulterated by personal, limited, carnal, and material ideas.
