# Meshach

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me'-shach

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A name given to Mishael, one of Daniel's three companions, by the prince of the eunuchs, in 
Babylon. Meshach was one of the three who were saved out of the fiery furnace (Dan. 1:7; 2:49; 3:12- 
30).

MESHEZABEL

## Metaphysical

Love. Love is the guest of the mighty one that we all seek to entertain 
continuously throughout our being— the never failing love of God. Love is the attracting power of the 
universe; it is that which draws to us all good; it is irresistible.
