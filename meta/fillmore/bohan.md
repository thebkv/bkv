# Bohan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bo'-han

## Meanings

- thumb
- stumpy

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The son of Reuben"; the stone of Bohan was used as a mark on the boundary line between 
Judah and Benjamin, in Canaan (Josh. 15:6; 18:17).

## Metaphysical

Determination, strength of thought, firmness of purpose (thumb, stumpy; a stone).

bondage-Undue attachment to thoughts in personal consciousness, such as race, class, sex, age, 
or personality. "Whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven and whatsoever thou shalt 
loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven" (Matt. 16:19).

book, of the law, or "roll" that the king burned (Jer. 36:13, 23).

Metaphysical. The intuitive perception of the progressive trend of all things. The burning of the 
roll represents a denial of the spiritual thought that is working in consciousness.

booths (Lev. 23: 39-43) .

Metaphysical. Temporary abiding places, as contrasted with permanent houses, or places of 
abode. When man comes out of Egypt (mental bondage) into the light of freedom, he should keep green 
in memory, by thought and act, the idea of liberty. Young persons like to camp in the woods. This liking 
is a natural result of innate desire for freedom from the restraints of fixed thoughts.
