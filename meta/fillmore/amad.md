# Amad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-mad

## Meanings

- people of duration
- enduring
- a station

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A border town in the allotment of Asher near Allammelech (Josh. 19:26).

## Metaphysical

An aggregation of thoughts of an established, enduring, sustaining character, 
founded in Truth principles (a station, people of duration, enduring).
