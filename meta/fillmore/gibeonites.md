# Gibeonites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Gibeon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The Gibeonites of II Samuel 2 9 refer to the Canaanitish inhabitants of Gibeon, with whom the 
Israelites made a covenant of peace; they do not refer to the Benjamites and Levites who later inhabited 
the city of Gibeon.

## Metaphysical

Thoughts of the sensate or carnal phase of the subjective consciousness that aspire 
to higher and more spiritual ideals and try to reach these ideals by making an alliance with man's real, 
true thoughts (Israel).
