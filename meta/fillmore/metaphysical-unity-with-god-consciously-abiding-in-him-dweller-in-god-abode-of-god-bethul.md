## Metaphysical

Unity with God; consciously abiding in Him (dweller in God, abode of God) . 
Bethul, be'-thul (Heb.)— dweller in God; house of God.

A contracted form of Bethuel; a city of Simeon (Josh. 19:4); it is thought to have been the same 
place as the Bethel of Joshua 12: 16.

Metaphysical. See BETHEL and BETHUEL.
