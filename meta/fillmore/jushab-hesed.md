# Jushab-hesed

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ju'-shab-he'-sed

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Zerubbabel, descended from David (I Chron. 3:20).

## Metaphysical

The individual consciousness, realizing and expressing toward God, toward itself 
and its fellow men, toward all creation, the same perfection of love and kindness that it has come to 
know exists in God toward man and toward all the universe.
