# Haggai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hag'-ga-l

## Meanings

- festive
- festal
- feast
- festival
- joyous
- rejoicing

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the minor prophets, who prophesied at the time the Jews returned from the Babylonian 
captivity. (See Ezra 6: 14 and The Book of Haggai.)

## Metaphysical

A realization of good as taking the place of seeming evil. That spiritual insight in 
man which heralds joyous, full, free deliverance from oppression, and abundance of rich substance and 
life for mind and body; it feasts upon the Truth daily, and foresees and foretells the working out of good.
