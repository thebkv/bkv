# Rhesa

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** rhe'-sCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Mentioned in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:27).

## Metaphysical

The will taking the lead, or first place, in consciousness (will, head, chief, first, 
foremost).

Rhesa expresses that which rules or enjoys by virtue of its own inherent movement; hence, the 
will. Taken in a negative sense this same independent movement will also characterize a venomous 
principle, evil, insidious desire, the source of evil, venom. Rhesa, taken in its negative sense, is the head 
of the serpent of Genesis 3:15.
