# Naham

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** na'-ham

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A brother of the wife of Hodiah, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4: 19).

## Metaphysical

The individual's becoming conscious of the Holy Spirit as comforter ( comfort, 
consolation, solace) when he has whole-heartedly turned away from his errors in thought and act and 
has laid hold of Truth (repentance means a turning from error to Truth).
