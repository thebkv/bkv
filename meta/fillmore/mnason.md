# Mnason

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** mna'-son— pronounced na'-son

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A disciple, a resident of Cyprus, with whom Paul and his companions lodged at Jerusalem (Acts

21:16).

## Metaphysical

That in the spiritually awakening consciousness of the individual which causes 
him to remember, to be mindful of the word of Truth (Paul) and all the higher thoughts and ideals that 
pertain to the Christ mind. Mnason would be equivalent to the Holy Spirit, in significance.
