# Ezem (A.V., Joshua 15:29 and 19:3, Azem)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Joshua 15:29 and 19:3, Azem), e'-zem

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of "the uttermost cities of the tribe of the children of Judah toward the border of Edom in the 
South" (Josh. 15:21, 29). In Joshua 19:3 and I Chronicles 4: 29 Ezem is named as a city of Simeon.

## Metaphysical

An aggregation of thoughts in consciousness that are unified in their development 
in strength, stability, and power (collective growth bespeaks unity in development and bone bespeaks 
stability).
