# Shemida (in A. V., I Chronicles 7:19, Shemidah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Chronicles 7:19, Shemidah), she-mi'-dCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Gilead of the tribe of Manasseh (Josh. 17:2).

## Metaphysical

Spiritual understanding, discernment, a perceiving, knowing attitude of thought, 
true knowledge and wisdom (upright sight, righteous perception, brilliant discernment, renowned 
knowledge, splendor of wisdom, comprehension of the name).

Shemidaites, she-mi-da-ites (fr. Heb.).

Descendants of Shemida, son of Gilead of the Israelitish tribe of Manasseh (Num. 26:32).

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from that in consciousness which Shemida signifies. (See 
SHEMIDA.)
