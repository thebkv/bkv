# Tema

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te'-mCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One of Ishmael's twelve sons (Gen. 25:15). b A people, c A land (Job 6:19).

## Metaphysical

Abundant substance and life, firmness, faithfulness, and Truth, stored in the 
subconsciousness (south, southern quarter, on the right side, i. e., when facing east, sunny, good fortune, 
prosperity, abundance, good faith, firm, faithful, a person, a people, and aland). "The inhabitants of the 
land of Tema did meet the fugitives with their bread" (Isaiah 21:14).
