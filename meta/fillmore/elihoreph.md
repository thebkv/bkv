# Elihoreph

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el-l-ho'-reph

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shisha (I Kings 4:3). He was a scribe in Solomon's reign over Israel.

## Metaphysical

God is the power that brings our thoughts to fruition. He is the source of our 
bountiful supply and the power by which our good is gathered to us— is made manifest for our use.
