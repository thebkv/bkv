# Zaccai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zae'-eai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Seven hundred and sixty of his descendants returned from the Babylonian captivity with 
Zerubbabel (Ezra 2:9).

## Metaphysical

A very influential, or fruitful, thought belonging to the spiritual phase of 
consciousness in man (an Israelite, who had numerous descendants who returned to Judea from the 
Captivity) that is true, honest, upright, and unadulterated by materiality or by error 
(cleansed, purified, just, guileless, righteous).
