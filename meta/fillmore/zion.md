# Zion

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zi'-on

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A fortified hill that David took from the Jebusites. David built his palace there (II Sam. 5:7), 
and the place was called the city of David. The tent of meeting and the Ark of the Covenant were on

Zion during David's reign (I Kings 8: 1); this i9 why it was called the "holy hill" (Psalms 2 :6). Later 
Zion became a part of the city of Jerusalem, b Zion as mentioned in many places in the Bible refers to 
the whole city of Jerusalem (as in Isa. 33 :20). c Zion is sometimes used as figurative of the Holy City, 
the New Jerusalem (Heb. 12:22).

## Metaphysical

Love's abode in the phase of the subjective consciousness where high, holy 
thoughts and ideals abide (clear, i. e., unobstructed, sunshine, sunny, set up, monument, fortress, 
landmark, a fortified hill, which later became a part of Jerusalem).

In referring to the Holy City, the New Jerusalem, Zion symbolizes spiritual consciousness.
