# Naamathite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Naamah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Zophar the Naamathite was one of Job's three friends (Job 2:11).

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to the group of thoughts that the town of Naamah signifies.
