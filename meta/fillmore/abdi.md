# Abdi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ab'-dl

## Meanings

- my sen’cmt
- serx’ant of Jah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Malluch (I Chron. 6:44) and father of Kish, of the Levites (II Chron. 29: 12). b A son of 
Elam (Ezra 10:26).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts that serve Jehovah or are subject to Jehovah (my servant, sen’ant of 
Jah). The first mentioned Abdi signifies a thought that springs from counseling with Spirit and 
meditating on divine law. The father of this Abdi was named Malluch, which means counselor.

Abdi, the son of Elam, represents a thought of service to God that is established in an idea of 
youth and strength (Elam means /w//y developed, a young man) but is united to a physical or carnal soul 
quality (he was married to a foreign wife). This carnal idea has to be given up. The Israelites had to 
separate themselves from their foreign wives and from the children (mixed thoughts) that had come of 
union with them.
