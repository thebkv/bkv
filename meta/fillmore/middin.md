# Middin

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** midO-din

## Meanings

- extended
- lengthened
- stretched out
- meted
- measured

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the wilderness of Judah (Josh. 15:61).

## Metaphysical

An extending or enlarging (extended, lengthened, stretched out ) of one's inner 
spiritual capacity, in what seems to be the wilderness phase of one's Judah or praise consciousness, that 
all limitation (meted, measured) may be done away. "For he giveth not the Spirit by measure" (John 
3:34).
