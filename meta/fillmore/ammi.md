# Ammi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** am'-ml

## Meanings

- my people
- my flock
- my troop
- my serx’ants

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A symbolical name given to the redeemed people of Israel (Hos. 2:1).

## Metaphysical

The acknowledgment by Jehovah, the Christ or true self in us, that our Israel 
thoughts have become obedient to the true ideas of the mind of Spirit (m?l people). Formerly, they had 
given themselves over to belief in a formal religion, to belief in materiality.
