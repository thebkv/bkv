# Zibia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zib'-;-a

## Meanings

- beauty
- gracefulness
- antelope
- gazelle
- roe
- deer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Benjamite, son of Shaharaim and Hodesh (I Chron. 8:9). b A woman of Beersheba. She was 
the mother of Jehoash, king of Judah, and her name is spelled Zibiah (II Kings 12 : 1).

## Metaphysical

A swift, graceful, harmonious thought activity in the faith faculty in consciousness 
(beauty, gracefulness, gazelle, roe, deer; a man of Benjamin), also 
ZIHA

in the spiritual phase of the soul (a woman of Beersheba, and mother of Jehoash, king of Judah).

Zibiah. (See ZIBIA, b.)
