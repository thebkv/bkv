# Manoah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma-no'-ah

## Meanings

- resting
- quieting
- restoring
- giving
- leaving
- forsaking

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Samson. He was a man of Zorah, of the family of the Danites (Judg. 13:2-24).

## Metaphysical

The significance of Manoah is given in Isaiah 40:31: "They that wait for Jehovah 
shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; 
they shall walk, and not faint." In the Authorized Version the first clause reads, "They that wait upon the 
Lord shall renew their strength." Manoah-meaning resting, restoring, forsaking, and being the father of 
Samson, who represents strength— signifies this inner waiting upon the Lord, or resting in the 
consciousness of our oneness with the Father, Jehovah, within us. By this inner worship something of 
the limited in thought and belief is put away and a great increase in strength and might is experienced.
