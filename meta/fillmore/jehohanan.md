# Jehohanan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-ho-ha'-nan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There are several Israelites by this name (I Chron. 26:3; II Chron. 17:15; 23:1; Ezra 10:28; Neh. 
6:18; 12:13,42).

## Metaphysical

Love, mercy, and good will as qualities of Being and as becoming known to man 
and expressed in him through his spiritual I AM, or Jehovah (whom Jehovah bestows, compassion of 
Jah, grace of the Lord).
