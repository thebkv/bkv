# Netophathites (in A.V., Nehemiah 12:28, Netophathi)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Inhabitants of Netophah (Neh. 12:28; Jer. 40:8). Two of David's warriors, Maharai, and Heleb 
the son of Baanah, were Netophathites (II Sam. 23:28, 29). The Netophathites are mentioned in I 
Chronicles 2:54 as being "sons of Salma."

## Metaphysical

Thoughts in consciousness pertaining to that which Netophah indicates. (See 
NETOPHAH.)
