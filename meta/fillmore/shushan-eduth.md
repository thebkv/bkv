# Shushan Eduth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shu.'-shan e'-duth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Instruction to the choirmaster or leader of the Temple music, as to the tune, tempo, quality, or 
instruments to be used as accompaniments (Psalms 60, title).
