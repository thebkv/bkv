# Ishbi-benob

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ish'-bi-be'-nob

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Raphah, the giant; he tried to kill David, and it looked as though he were going to 
succeed, but Abishai came to David's help and killed Ishbi-benob (II Sam. 21:16).
