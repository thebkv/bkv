# Eleadah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-le-a'-dah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son, or descendant, of Ephraim (I Chron. 7:20).

## Metaphysical

The individual will (Ephraim signifies the will) fully unified with and expressing 
the divine, having the quality of Godlikeness, and being eternal even as God is eternal. (The name 
Eleadah is prophetic, as many of the names of the Old Testament are, inasmuch as the Truth for which it 
stands was not fulfilled then but is to be established perfectly in individual consciousness when man 
unfolds spiritually to the place where this is possible.)

## Preserved OCR Source

Eleadah, e'-le-a'-dah (Heb.)— whom God puts on, i.e., fills and dons with Himself; whom God 
adorns, i.e., brings to time of fullness of perfection; eternity of God; precept of God; ordained of God; 
witness of God.
