# Ahlai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ah'-lai

## Meanings

- Oh that! would God ! an intense wish
- ornamental

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Daughter of Sheshan. Sheshan had no sons; his lineage was preserved through his daughters (I 
Chron. 2:31-35). The Ahlai of I Chronicles 11:41 may have been this same woman, since in I Chronicles 
2:36 we see that Zabad was descended from Ahlai the daughter of Sheshan .

## Metaphysical

An inner or soul desire for a more positive (masculine) expression of truth ( Oh 
that ! would God ! an intense wish; ornamental; an Israelitish woman). This desire was realized in due 
time. Attai, the son of Ahlai, means timely, opportune, seasonable.
