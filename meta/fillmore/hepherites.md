# Hepherites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Hepher

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The descendants of Hepher of Manasseh (Num. 26:32).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts of understanding and life, springing from that in consciousness for 
which Hepher stands.
