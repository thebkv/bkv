# Cushite (in A.V., Num. 12:1, Ethiopian; in II Samuel 18:21, Cushi)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Num. 12:1, Ethiopian; in II Samuel 18:21, Cushi), cushOte

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Moses' wife was a Cushite woman, and Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because he had 
married a woman of that race (Num. 12:1). It was a Cushite also whom Joab sent to David to tell him of

the overthrow and death of Absalom (II Sam. 18: 21). A Cushite was a native of the land of Cush, or 
Ethiopia.

## Metaphysical

A thought that belongs to the state of consciousness that Cush typifies. (See 
CUSH.)
