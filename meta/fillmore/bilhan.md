# Bilhan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** b;l'-han

## Meanings

- bashful
- modest
- confused
- timid
- weak
- tender

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Ezer, a Horite of the land of Seir (Gen. 36:27). b A son of Jediael, a Benjamite (I Chron.

7:10).

## Metaphysical

Jediael means knowledge of God, known of God. "The wisdom that is from above 
is first pure then peaceable, gentle, easy to be entreated, full of mercy" (James 3:17); this is the 
consciousness that is signified by Bilhan, son of Jediael (tender, modest). It is compassionate, tender, 
modest— not bold, presumptuous, or hypocritical; it is bom of a true knowledge of God.

The Bilhan who was a Horite partakes more of a confused state of mind (confused), and a lack of 
self-assertion (weak); these are products of a giving way to the fulfillment of the thoughts and desires of 
one's unredeemed subconsciousness.
