# Baal-meon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-al-me'-on

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of the Reubenites; it was built by them in the land that had been taken from Sihon, king of 
the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan (Num. 32:38).

## Metaphysical

Reuben means behold a son, vision of a son, and signifies understanding. Baal- 
meon (lord of habitations, possessor of the houses, lord of dwellings), a city of Reuben, represents 
understanding centered in the outer consciousness, understanding that still looks upon the manifest 
world and outer possessions as being real and true of themselves; this limited understanding does not see 
back of the manifest to spiritual reality.
