# Kiriath-jearim (A. V., Kirjath-jearim)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjath-jearim), kirO-i-ath-jeO-a-rim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Gibeon (Josh. 9: 17). The Ark remained in this city for twenty years after the 
Philistines sent it back to Israel, until David had it returned to its place in Jerusalem (I Sam. 7:1,2).

## Preserved OCR Source

Kiriath-jearim (A. V., Kirjath-jearim), kirO-i-ath-jeO-a-rim (Heb.)— city of redundant growths; 
city of dense thickets; city of forests.

A city of Gibeon (Josh. 9: 17). The Ark remained in this city for twenty years after the 
Philistines sent it back to Israel, until David had it returned to its place in Jerusalem (I Sam. 7:1,2).
