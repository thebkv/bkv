# Hakkatan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hak'-ka-tan

## Meanings

- the little one
- the smaller
- the lowly
- the inferior
- the younger
- the

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

junior.

Father of Johanan, and descended from Azgad. Johanan with one hundred and ten other male 
descendants of Azgad returned with Ezra from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 8:12).

## Metaphysical

The thought that Hakkatan symbolizes is clearly expressed in Jesus' words 
recorded in Matthew 18:3, "Except ye turn, and become as little children, ye shall in no wise enter into 
the kingdom of heaven," and in Mark 10: 15, "Verily I say unto you, Whosoever shall not receive the 
kingdom of God as a little child he shall in no wise enter therein." It is that humble, lowly attitude of 
mind and that youthful thought of the child— full of hope and willing to unfold and to learn new things, 
willing to change the mind and to accept the true Christ ideas— which will set one free from all sense 
bondage and confusion (Babylon).
