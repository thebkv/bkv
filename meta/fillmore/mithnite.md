# Mithnite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- loin
- strength
- firmness
- liberality
- gift

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Joshaphat the Mithnite was one of David's warriors (I Chron. 11:43).

## Metaphysical

The increase of strength, faithfulness, Truth, and broadness of vision in 
consciousness (loin, strength, firmness, liberality; "she girdeth her loins with strength"; "faithfulness the

girdle of his loins"; "having girded your loins with truth." This great increase of good, or grace (gift of 
God), comes as the result of the development of the idea of judgment, or good judgment, clear 
discrimination, a quality in the Christ mind (Joshaphat, meaning whom Jehovah judgeth). "Wherefore 
girding up the loins of your mind ... set your hope ... on the grace that is to be brought unto you at the 
revelation of Jesus Christ" (I Pet. 1:13).
