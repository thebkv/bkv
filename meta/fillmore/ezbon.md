# Ezbon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ez'-bon

## Meanings

- pushing to understanding
- hastening to understand
- splendor
- bright

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Gad (Gen. 46:16). He is called Ozni in Num. 26: 16. b A son of Bela and grandson of 
Benjamin (I Chron. 7:7).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts that come into the light, into the brightness and glory of Truth, because 
of being attentive to the things of Spirit. Because these thoughts have a receptive ear to hear the Truth, 
they understand quickly. (See OZNI.)
