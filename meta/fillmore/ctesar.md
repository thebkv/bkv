# CTEsar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Lat
- **Pronunciation:** y/E'-sar

## Meanings

- hairy
- sharp
- cutting edge
- keen

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A title belonging to several of the Roman emperors (Matt. 22:17).

## Metaphysical

In the individual consciousness rulership implies the joint activity of reason and 
will. CTEsar, the Roman emperor, therefore refers to the personal seifs ruling, through reason and will 
(unmodified by spiritual love, mercy, and justice), both mind and body, as indicated by the "world" 
(Luke 2:1), and forcing all the faculties and powers to pay tribute in slavish carnal service. This is the 
natural attitude of the unspiritualized or Adam consciousness.

In our individual consciousness we must learn to "render unto C/Esar the things that are CTEsar's, 
and unto God the things that are God's" (Luke 20:25) . The outer man, as well as the inner, must be 
given proper attention and care. Each must have its share of the sustenance of life. We even must keep 
harmony with the unenlightened human will, until the spiritual grows wise enough, loving enough, and 
strong enough in our consciousness to take its rightful dominion throughout our entire being.

By the stamp that is upon them we always shall know the thoughts and tendencies in ourselves 
that belong to CTEsar and those that belong to God. That which is but a symbol of the true substance, the 
outer reasoning husk of the sensate thought of life and its resources, goes to C/Esar. The real substance 
of life, love, understanding, and Truth, goes to build up and to sustain the spiritual consciousness in 
mind and body.

C/Esarca, c/Es-a-re'-a (Gk.)~m honor of Caesar; for C/Esar.

A principal city and seaport of Palestine; it was built by Herod the Great (Acts 8:40). C/Esarca 
was the political capital of Palestine during the time that the country was ruled by the Romans. The 
Roman ruler of Palestine— the head administrator for the Roman emperor— always lived at C/Esarca.

Metaphysical. C/Esarca, dominant world power, symbolizes the intellect.
