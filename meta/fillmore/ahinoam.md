# Ahinoam

- **Corpus:** MBD

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

## Metaphysical

discord (my brother is delight, pleasant brother, gracious). The wives of David represent the substance 
side of thought, which must enter into the demonstration of our rule in divine love. The first step is
