# Jotbah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jot'-bah

## Meanings

- goodness
- healthfulness
- beauty
- comeliness
- pleasantness
- excellence

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The home of Haruz, father of Meshullemeth, the wife of Manasseh and mother of Amon, two 
kings of Judah (II Kings 21:19). Jotbah is thought to be the same place as Jotbathah of Numbers 33:33 
and Deuteronomy 10:7.

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of the refreshing waters of life and Truth; this is indeed pleasant 
and good and healthful.
