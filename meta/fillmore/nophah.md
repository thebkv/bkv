# Nophah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** no'-phah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Moab (Num. 21:30).

## Metaphysical

The personal mind (Moab), which is often boastful, noisy, showy, explosive, 
tempestuous, yet is empty of real Truth and power (puffing, blowing, blustering, a windy place). Back of 
this phase of the carnal mind that Nophah represents is the very breath of life (Nophah comes from the 
same root as that used in Genesis 2:7, in connection with the "breath of life"). Under spiritual 
understanding and direction the expression will take on a very different aspect.
