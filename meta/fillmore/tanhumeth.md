# Tanhumeth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tan'-hu-meth

## Meanings

- rest
- quiet
- comfort
- consolation

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Seraiah, who came to Gedaliah to Mizpah. In II Kings 25:23 he is called "the 
Netophathite," but according to Jeremiah 40:8 this appellative belongs to Ephai instead of Tanhumeth.

## Metaphysical

A peaceful, helpful, cheering,

TARALAH

strengthening thought activity in the higher, more spiritual phase of man's consciousness (rest, quiet, 
comfort, consolation, an Israelite).
