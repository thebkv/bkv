# Jerubbaal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jer-ub-ba'-al

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name that Joash gave his son Gideon when Gideon had destroyed the altar of Baal and the 
Asherah, and had built an altar to Jehovah and offered up burnt offerings on it. The people wanted Joash 
to have Gideon put to death for cutting down the altar of Baal and the Asherah, but Joash said of Baal, 
"If he be a god, let him contend for himself, because one hath broken down his altar. Therefore on that 
day he called him Jerubbaal, saying, Let Baal contend against him" (Judg. 6:31, 32). Jerubbaal, or 
Gideon, defeated the Midianites, delivered Israel, and judged Israel for forty years (Judg. 7:1 to 8:32). 
JERUSALEM

## Metaphysical

At this time the Israelites had so lost their consciousness of the one true God that 
they were worshipping Baal, or materiality. For them to be conducted out of darkness it was necessary 
that some capable leader be raised up. This leader was found in the person of Gideon or Jerubbaal. 
Jerubbaal is a family name, meaning contender with Bacd. The name shows that he came from a family 
naturally opposed to the rule of materiality. This idea found its culmination in Gideon, which means 
destroyer. (See GIDEON.)

Jerubbesheth, je-rub'-be-sheth (Heb.)~n7zo contends with idolatry; contender with shame; strife 
with ignominy; let the idol of disgrace defend itself.

A name of Gideon, a form of the name Jerubbaal (II Sam. 11:21); in Judges 6:32, Jerubbaal.

Metaphysical. See JERUBBAAL.
