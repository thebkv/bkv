# Zebidah (A. V., Zebudah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Zebudah), ze-bi'-dah

## Meanings

- gift outright
- bestowal
- dowry
- endowment

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

ZEBOIM

Daughter of Pedaiah of Rumah, and mother of Jehoiakim, king of Judah (II Kings 23:36).

## Metaphysical

The soul of man's recognizing the Truth that is signified by Zabbud (the masculine 
form of Zebidah). Zebidah, being the name of a woman, signifies the soul or feminine phase of that for 
which the masculine form of the name stands. (See ZABBUD.)
