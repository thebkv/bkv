# Elizaphan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-l;z'-a-phan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Uzziel, a prince of the Kohathites (Num. 3:30). b Son of Parnach and a prince of the 
tribe of Zebulun (Num. 34:25). c The father of Shimri and Jeuel, Levites who aided in cleansing the 
"house of Jehovah" (II Chron. 29:13, 15).

## Metaphysical

The inner assurance of divine protection and safety through putting away error 
and doing right (cleansing the Temple).
