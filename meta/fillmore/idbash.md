# Idbash

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** id' -bash

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Judah. He was a son of the "father," or founder, of Etam (I Chron. 4:3).

## Metaphysical

A thought of love (honey, sweetness), pliability (kneadable), and substance (fatty, 
corpulent), more of the physical than of spiritual consciousness.
