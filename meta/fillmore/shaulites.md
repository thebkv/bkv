# Shaulites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or pertaining to Shaul

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Shaul the son of Simeon (Num. 26:13).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from that in consciousness which Shaul the son of Simeon 
signifies. (See SHAUL.)
