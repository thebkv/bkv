# Beth-anath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-a'-nath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Canaan that was allotted to Naphtali (Josh. 19:38). The men of Naphtali did not drive 
out the Canaanitish inhabitants of this city, but made servants of them (Judg. 1:33).

## Metaphysical

A thought center in man that, to a degree at least, realizes the response of Spirit to 
the cry of the soul (see ANATH) for a greater realization of strength (house of answers, i. e., to prayers, 
house of reply; Naphtali is the Israelitish tribe that represents strength) .

Naphtali failed to overcome the Canaanitish inhabitants of Beth-anath completely, but made 
them subject to task work. This incident symbolizes a drawing on the elemental life forces of the 
organism (Canaanites) for increased strength and vigor, while retaining more or less the old material 
belief concerning these forces. We must know that life is spiritual, immortal, abiding, unlimited, 
unchanging, and pure, before we can realize and express it in its fullness.
