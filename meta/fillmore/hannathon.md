# Hannathon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** han'-na-thon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Zebulun, on the northern boundary (Josh. 19: 14).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts in which order (Zebulun) is the central idea. This group of 
thoughts is consecrated to the purpose of promoting, realizing, establishing a consciousness of the all- 
sufficiency of God, divine grace (dedicated to grace).
