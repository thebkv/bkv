# Bariah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba-rl'-ah

## Meanings

- fugitive

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shemaiah, and descended from Solomon (I Chron. 3:22).

## Metaphysical

A quality of mind that is bom into consciousness through the activity of divine 
inspiration (the name of his father, Shemaiah, means Jehovah hears, or harkening unto Jah), but is not 
established, as yet-it is transient (fugitive).
