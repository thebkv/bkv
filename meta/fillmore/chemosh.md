# Chemosh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** che'-mosh

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A national god of the Moabites (I Kings 11:7,33).

## Metaphysical

The seeming strength and subtlety of error, of the "mind of the flesh" in man. (See 
CARCHEMISH.)
