# Geba (A.V

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Joshua 18:24, Ezra 2:26, and Nehemiah 7:30, Gaba, ge'-ba

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Benjamin that was given over to the children of Aaron the priest (Josh. 2 1: 17).

## Metaphysical

A high state of consciousness, the result of an active faith (Benjamin) combined 
with an executive, or practical, working understanding of divine law (Aaron).
