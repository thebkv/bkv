# Ben-geber

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ben-ge'-ber

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of Solomon's twelve commissaries (I Kings 4:13).

## Metaphysical

A realization of great strength, courage, nobility, and conquering power; a high 
spiritual thought; a thought of divine sonship.
