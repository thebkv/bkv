# Perga

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** per'-gCE

## Meanings

- very earthy

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Pamphylia, in Asia Minor, where Mark parted from Paul and his company, and 
returned to Jerusalem (Acts 13:13). Later Paul preached there (Acts 14:25).

## Metaphysical

Very earthy, material thoughts. (See PAMPHYLIA.)
