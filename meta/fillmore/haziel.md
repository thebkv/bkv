# Haziel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-zi-el

## Meanings

- vision of God
- whom God sees
- meditation of God
- beholding of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shimei, of the Gershonite Levites (I Chron. 23: 9).

## Metaphysical

Spiritual seeing, seeing or understanding with the eye (or mind) of Spirit.
