# Zebina

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze-bi'-na

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Nebo, an Israelite. He had taken a foreign wife after the return from the Babylonian 
captivity, but gave her up at Ezra's command (Ezra 10 :43).

## Metaphysical

Acquisition, getting for oneself. This is good, so long as one does not let the spirit 
of acquisition degenerate into selfishness and dishonesty, so long as one exercises it in acquiring that 
which tends to Christlikeness, to one's eternal good (precious possession), and does not draw to oneself 
error, limitation, bondage.
