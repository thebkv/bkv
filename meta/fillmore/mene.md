# mene

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Chald
- **Pronunciation:** me'-ne

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The first word of the warning to King 
MEONENIM

Belshazzar of Babylon. It appeared in the form of handwriting on the wall (Dan. 5:25, 26): "MENE;

God hath numbered thy kingdom, and brought it to an end."

## Metaphysical

The assurance that the ruling error phase of will and consciousness that 
Belshazzar and his kingdom represent has been taken account of ( numbered ) by the divine judge, the 
Spirit of truth, and that its end is at hand.
