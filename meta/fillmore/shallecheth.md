# Shallecheth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shal'-le- heth

## Meanings

- felling
- overthrowing
- casting out
- thrown down

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A gate of the house of God, in Jerusalem, in David's reign (I Chron. 26:16, "Casting forth, " 
margin).

## Metaphysical

The word of denial; putting away out of consciousness that which is not 
acceptable to it; the casting forth of error by denial (casting out, felling, overthrowing, thrown down).

Shallecheth, the gate at the west of the Temple (west signifying the without), is "at the causeway 
that goeth up, watch against watch." This symbolizes a raised way or highway that has been built by the 
individual, by means of true thoughts and words; it leads step by step to higher realms of expression and 
manifestation in the outer, to the west. Shuppim and Hosah, who were given charge of this gate, bespeak 
a degree of wisdom in the outer man and a trustful, hopeful spirit that becomes a real haven to him while 
he is passing through his overcoming experiences.
