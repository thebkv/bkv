# Raphu

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ra'-phu

## Meanings

- repaired mended
- healed
- restored
- made whole, wholesome

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Palti, of the tribe of Benjamin. Palti was chosen to assist in spying out the land of 
Canaan preparatory to the Israelites' going over and possessing it (Num. 13:9).
