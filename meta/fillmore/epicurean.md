# Epicurean

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ep-l-eu-re'-an

## Meanings

- followers of Epicurus

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A sect of philosophers, founded by Epicurus (Acts 1:18). These philosophers believed that the 
soul dies with the body and that there is no future, either good or bad, for man beyond death. Their 
doctrine was one of pleasure seeking and ease; very naturally it led to sensuality and error.

## Metaphysical

The character of the thoughts that Epicurean signifies is revealed in Jesus' parable 
of Luke 12: 16-21; see the 19th verse, especially the last part of the verse.
