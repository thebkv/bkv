# Iim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I'-im

## Meanings

- overthrown, subversions
- heaps of ruins
- heaps of rubbish

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Judah (Josh. 15:29). It is the same place as Iyim and Iye-abarim.

## Metaphysical

Overthrow and destruction of, and dominion over, some error seeming.
