# Sarsechim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sSr-se'-chim

## Meanings

- prince of the eunuchs
- chief eunuch

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A prince of the king of Babylon, evidently one of the commanders of his army that came up 
against Jerusalem and conquered the city (Jer. 39:3).
