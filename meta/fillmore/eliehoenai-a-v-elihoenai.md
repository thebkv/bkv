# Eliehoenai (A.V., Elihoenai)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Elihoenai), el'-ieho-e'-na-i

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite, a descendant of Pahath-moab. He returned under Ezra from the Babylonian 
captivity (Ezra 8:4).

## Metaphysical

The turning of the attention from the outer world of manifestation to Jehovah, the 
Christ, or Father within one, as the power that delivers from sense bondage and saves to the uttermost— 
brings again to the Promised Land.
