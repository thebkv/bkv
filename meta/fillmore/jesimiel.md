# Jesimiel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-simO-i-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man named in the genealogy of Simeon (I Chron. 4:36), a chief, or prince.

## Metaphysical

An influential thought in the spiritually receptive, attentive, obedient attitude of 
mind in man (Simeon), which acknowledges God, Spirit, as the power that not only created man 
(builded of God, Gocl-made ), but that also establishes him in his rightful place in Being (whom God has 
set up), and gives him his true character (naming of God).
