# Beeliada

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be-e-h'-a-da

## Meanings

- whom the Lord knows
- the Lord knows
- known of Baal

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of David (I Chron. 14:7); he is called Eliada in II Samuel 5:16.

## Metaphysical

The idea that Baal (a personal god, or a being with form and shape) knows things 
and is the source of knowledge. The other form of the name, Eliada, comes nearer suggesting the truth. 
(See ELIADA.)
