# Uel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u'-el

## Meanings

- will of God
- desire of God
- wish of God
- desiring of God
- God's will

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the sons of Bani. He had taken a foreign wife, but gave her up at the command of Ezra 
(Ezra 10:34).

## Metaphysical

An awakening in man to his true desire, which is for God and is caused by God's 
seeking greater expression in him. (Uel means will of God, desire of God, wish of God, God's will. At its 
root every desire of man is God's seeking greater and truer expression in and through him, though by the 
time these desires reach man's outer consciousness he often interprets them falsely, and according to the 
ignorance of his sense or carnal mind.
