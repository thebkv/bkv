# Elihu

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-l'-hu

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Barachel the Buzite, one of Job's friends (Job 32:2-6). b An ancestor of Samuel's (I 
Sam. 1:1). c The name of other Israelitish men (I Chron. 12:20; 26:7; 27:18).

## Metaphysical

Elihu of the book of Job represents the Holy Spirit. The name Elihu also signifies 
the recognition by man that his true inner self is Spirit.
