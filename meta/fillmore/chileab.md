# Chileab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** eh;l'-e-ab

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of David, by Abigail, who had been the wife of Nabal the Carmelite (II Sam. 3:3). In I 
Chronicles 3:1 this son of David is called Daniel instead of Chileab.

## Metaphysical

Daniel signifies spiritual judgment. Chileab here refers to love (like into the 
father; David symbolizing love) established in judgment.
