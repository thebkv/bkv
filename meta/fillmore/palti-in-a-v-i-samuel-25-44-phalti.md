# Palti (in A.V., I Samuel 25:44, Phalti)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Samuel 25:44, Phalti), pal'-ti

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Raphu; the man chosen from the tribe of Benjamin to help spy out the land of Canaan 
preparatory to the Israelites' going over and possessing the land (Num. 13:9). b The son of Laish, of 
Gallim, to whom Saul, king of Israel, gave Michal his daughter for a wife, after she had first been given 
to David (I Sam. 25:44). This latter Palti is called Paltiel in II Samuel 3:15.

## Metaphysical

A belief in or thoughts of deliverance through faith. (Jah fully delivers is one 
meaning of Palti. Both these men named Palti were Benjamites; a Benjamite signifies an active faith 
thought in consciousness. The second- mentioned Palti was from Gallim, and Gallim is thought to have 
been a town of Benjamin.)
