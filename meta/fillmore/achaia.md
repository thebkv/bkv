# Achaia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** a-cha'-ja

## Meanings

- land of Achaicus
- trouble

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A part of, or a province in, Greece (Acts 18:12; 19:21).

## Metaphysical

Greece represents the intellect of man, and so Achaia pertains to the intellect. The 
reasonings of the intellect, apart from the inspiration and guidance of the Holy Spirit, lead to trouble. 
Divine Mind, not human consciousness, is the one source of true joy and good.
