# Rephah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** re'-phah

## Meanings

- riches
- wealth
- abundance

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Beriah, an Ephraimite from whom Joshua was descended (I Chron. 7:25).

## Metaphysical

The will in man laying hold of the idea of abundant substance (riches, wealth, 
abundance, a man of Ephraim— Ephraim representing the will faculty in individual consciousness).
