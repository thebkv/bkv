# Giddel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** g;d'-del

## Meanings

- very great
- gigantic
- greater magnitude, i.e., of size or number

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A man belonging to the Nethinim (Ezra 2:47). b A servant of Solomon's (Ezra 2:56). 
Descendants of both of these men returned from the Babylonian captivity under Zerubbabel.

## Metaphysical

The exaltation of service.
