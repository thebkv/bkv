# Erastus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** e-ras'-tus

## Meanings

- beloved
- amiable

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A native of Corinth, a Christian worker with Paul. Paul sent him with Timothy into Macedonia 
(Acts 19:22; II Tim. 4:20). b A treasurer of the city of Corinth, whence Paul's Epistle to the Romans was 
sent (Rom. 16:23). Though the texts that are given may all refer to the same man, authorities believe that 
there were two men by this name.

## Metaphysical

The harmonizing quality of love. (See CORINTH.)
