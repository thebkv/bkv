# Joahaz

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jo'-a-haz

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The recorder" in the reign of Josiah king of Judah, and the father of Joah, one of those who 
were sent to repair the house of Jehovah (II Chron. 34:8).

## Metaphysical

A phase of man's memory in his higher realm of consciousness, in which is kept a 
record of all true, helpful, spiritual thoughts and experiences (who lays hold of Jehovah, who adheres to 
Jah).
