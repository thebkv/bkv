# Semachiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sem-a-chi-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shemaiah, and grandson of Obed-edom (I Chron. 26:7).

## Metaphysical

One who is consciously joined to Jehovah and leans upon the Lord; who is 
supported and sustained by Jehovah, the indwelling Christ or Father (Jehovah sustains him, whom Jah 
upholds).
