# Uzzielites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Uzziel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Uzziel, son of Kohath, of the Israelitish tribe of Levi (Num. 3:27).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and of character like that in consciousness which Uzziel 
signifies. (See UZZIEL.)
