# Tormah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** t™r'-mah

## Meanings

- deception fraud
- betrayal

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place that is mentioned in the margin of Judges 9:31. There the messengers of Zebul found 
Abimelech.

## Metaphysical

A crafty, treacherous, deceptive state of thought (deception, fraud; "craftily" is 
used in the text, in place of "in Tormah").
