# Hamathite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Hamath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Canaan the son of Ham (Gen. 10:18). It is thought that descendants of Canaan 
settled in Hamath; thus a Hamathite would also be an inhabitant of Hamath.

## Metaphysical

A thought or tendency in man that belongs to the body consciousness (Canaan), or 
to the Hamath consciousness (confidence in materiality, and its results).
