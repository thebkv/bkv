# Beth-haran

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-ha'-ran

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A fortified city that was built by the Israelitish tribe of Gad, beyond the Jordan (Num. 32:36). It 
is thought to be the same city as Beth-haram.

## Metaphysical

A strong, dominating, conquering phase of thought ( house of might, house of 
strength, place of overcoming, i. e., getting the upper hand).

## Preserved OCR Source
