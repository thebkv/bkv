# Obadiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** o-ba-dl'-ah

## Meanings

- serx’ant of Jehovah
- service ofJah
- worshiper of Jehovah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One who was "over the household" of King Ahab. Obadiah feared the Lord, and so he hid 
five hundred prophets in caves and fed them, when Jezebel "cut off the prophets of Jehovah" (I Kings

18:3, 4). b There were several other Israelites by this name also (I Chron. 7:3; 8:38; 9:16; 12:9; 27:19; II 
Chron. 17:7, 34:12; Ezra 8:9).

## Metaphysical

The higher thoughts in man that serve and worship God through the I AM, 
Jehovah, indwelling Christ. They are faithful and true. They are thoughts that are obedient to spiritual 
ideals, that hear and heed the voice of Spirit.
