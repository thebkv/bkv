# Ginnethoi (A.V., Ginnetho and Ginnethon)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Ginnetho and Ginnethon), g;n'-ne-thoi

## Meanings

- gardener
- great protection

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One or more priests who returned from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 12:4); called Ginnethon 
in Nehemiah 10: 6; 12: 16.

GITTAIM

## Metaphysical

A strong assurance of divine protection and that God the Father is caretaker, the 
power by which one is enabled to overcome that in one which falls short of perfection. (A gardener is 
one who tends a garden, sows the seed, prunes the vines, pulls the weeds, sees that the ground is well 
fertilized, watered, and worked. We are the garden of God: "My Father is the husbandman," Jesus said.)
