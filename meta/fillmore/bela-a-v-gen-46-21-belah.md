# Bela (A. V., Gen. 46:21, Belah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Gen. 46:21, Belah), be'-la

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a The city called Zoar (Gen. 14:2). b A king of Edom (Gen. 36:32). c The eldest son of Benjamin 
(Gen. 46:21). d A son of Azaz— a Reubenite (I Chron. 5:8).

## Metaphysical

Destructive tendencies in consciousness (destroy, utterly consume, destruction). 
The city of Bela symbolizes a group of destroying, consuming thoughts. Bela, the eldest son of 
Benjamin, suggests the destroying or letting go of error by denial, an absorption (swallow up) of error by 
Truth, or of darkness by light, thus doing away with the error. (See BECHER, Bela's brother.)
