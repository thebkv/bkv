# Pithon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pi-thon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Micah, a Benjamite, and descended from Saul through Jonathan (I Chron. 8:35; 9:41).

## Metaphysical

An open-minded, receptive, childlike phase of thought. If guided by Spirit and 
thus led unresistingly into Truth, it is good. If guided by personal willfulness and selfishness, however, 
(Pithon was descended from Saul, who represents the personal will), delusion and error will be the 
result.
