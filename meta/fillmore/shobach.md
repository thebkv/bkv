# Shobach

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sho'-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Captain of the host of Hadarezer, king of Syria. David defeated this host at Helam, and Shobach 
was killed (II Sam. 10:16, 18). He is called Shophach in I Chronicles 19:16.

## Metaphysical

The dissipation of one's mental and bodily forces by unwise and riotous 
expression in thinking, talking, and acting (poured out, profusely expended, copiously extended); also 
the result of this dissipation or lack of righteous conservation. This result is exhaustion, lack of strength, 
lack of substance, lack of executive ability (exhausted, weakened).
