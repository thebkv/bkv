# Mattithiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mat-t;-thi'-ah

## Meanings

- gift of Jehovah
- gracious gift ofJah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There are five Israelites mentioned by this name. At least three of them were Levites (I Chron. 
9:31; 15:18; 25:3; Ezra 10:43; Neh. 8:4).

## Metaphysical

Man's high, true, spiritual thoughts (Israelites) awakening to his divine 
possibilities of attaining perfection, fullness of abiding life, wholeness, peace, and all good, through I 
AM, Jehovah.
