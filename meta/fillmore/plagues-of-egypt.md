# plagues of Egypt

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

It is right and proper to recognize the vital center in the organism, the generative 
or life center, as having a place in the divine economy, but spiritual man should never become involved 
in the mere animal processes of life generation through sex. This is what brings the plagues of Egypt 
(disease) upon the human family.
