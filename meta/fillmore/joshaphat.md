# Joshaphat

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** josh'-a-phat

## Meanings

- whom Jehovah judges
- whom Jah sustains
- Jah defends

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of David's mighty warriors, and a member of his guard (I Chron. 1 1 :43). The name is a form 
of Jehoshaphat.

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of Jehoshaphat: the development in 
consciousness of the idea of judgment and of the sustaining power of I AM. (See JEHOSHAPHAT.)
