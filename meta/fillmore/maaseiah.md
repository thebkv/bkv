# Maaseiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma-a-se'-iah

## Meanings

- work of Jehovah
- Jehovah's creation
- fruits of J ah's labor

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There were several Israelites by this name. Among them were priests, chiefs, and princes (I 
Chron. 15:18, 20; II Chron. 23:1; 26:11; 28:7; 34:8; Ezra 10:18, 21; Neh. 11:5).
