# Tebaliah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** teb-a-h'-ah

## Meanings

- dipping of Jehovah
- Jehovah immerses
- Jah purifies
- cleansing of

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Jah.

Third son of "Hosah, of the children of Merari" (I Chron. 26: 1 1).

## Metaphysical

A thought activity of inner spiritual cleansing and purifying through Christ, 
Jehovah, or spiritual I AM (Jehovah immerses, Jah purifies, cleansing of Jah)
