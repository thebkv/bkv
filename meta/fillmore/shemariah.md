# Shemariah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shem-a-ri-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One of "Saul's brethren of Benjamin" who came to David at Ziklag (I Chron. 12:5). b Son of 
Rehoboam (II Chron. 11:19). c Two men of Israel who had married foreign women, but put them away 
at the command of Ezra (Ezra 0:32, 41).

## Metaphysical

Faith in the keeping, protecting, sustaining power of God, through Jehovah, the 
indwelling Christ (Jehovah keeps guard, whom Jehovah keeps, safekeeping of the Lord), being 
awakened and established in the phases or faculties of man's being that are represented by the different 
tribes of Israel (Benjamin and Judah) to which the men named Shemariah belonged.
