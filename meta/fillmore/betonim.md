# Betonim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bet'-o-nlm

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Canaan that was allotted to the Israelitish tribe of Gad (Josh. 13: 26).

## Metaphysical

Spasmodic attempts to lift up the consciousness (pistachio nuts, mounds, humps) 
guided by impulse, or by one's feelings, apart from real, orderly, consistent Truth thinking (the inmost 
parts, i. e., where one thinks and feels, bellies, hollows). A sense of order and power is gained from that 
which Gad signifies (this Canaanitish city was allotted to Gad) . (See GAD .)
