# Zalaph

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** za'-laph

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Hanun, who helped to repair the wall of Jerusalem after the return from the Babylonian 
captivity (Neh. 3 :30)

## Metaphysical

A breaking forth from, or breaking up of, errors in consciousness, that good may 
be established. Seeming sorrow and travail in one's being often accompany a breaking up and passing 
away of established error thoughts, habits and conditions (fracturing, bruising,

wounding, hurting), but the outcome is always joy, peace, and greater good: "A woman when she is in 
travail hath sorrow, because her hour is come: but when she is delivered of the child, she remembereth 
no more the anguish, for the joy that a man is born into the world."
