# Amana

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** am'-a-na

## Meanings

- constant
- permanent
- integrity
- a seal of verity
- a covenant

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A high mount, or summit, of the Antilibanus range (Song of Sol. 4:8).

## Metaphysical

A high place in consciousness where we enter into the realization of a deeper 
union with our indwelling Christ —our bridegroom— than we have known before. We get a clearer 
understanding of His promises (covenant,) and the Christ becomes to us such a source of constancy and 
integrity (constant, permanent, integrity, a seal of verity) that we experience the joy and the rapture that 
are symbolized in the 4th chapter of the Song of Solomon.
