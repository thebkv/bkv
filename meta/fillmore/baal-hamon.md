# Baal-hamon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-al-ha'-mon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place where Solomon had a vineyard (Song of Sol. 8: 1 1). It is thought by some to be the same 
place as Baal-gad.

## Metaphysical

The significance is much the same as that of Baal-gad, though Solomon's vineyard 
gives the added thought of abundant life. While both life and all increase of substance and supply come 
from Spirit, Baal gives a suggestion of the mistaken belief that is still so prevalent among people today, 
the belief that the source of life and substance is in the outer world of form and activity. Man needs to 
learn that the true source of all people, as well as the source of all supply and of every real expression 
and manifestation, is unformed Spirit; having learned that, he will get away from Baal worship entirely. 
(See BAAL.)
