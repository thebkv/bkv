# En-haddah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** en-had'-dah

## Meanings

- swift fountain
- fount of vehemence
- sharp fountain
- fountain of

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

zeal.

A city of Canaan that was allotted to Issachar (Josh. 19:21).

## Metaphysical

The springing up of life into consciousness, quickened into greater activity and 
energy, and lifted to a higher level by the influence of the faculty of zeal (Issachar).
