# Tizite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Joha, one of David's mighty men, was called "the Tizite" (I Chron. 11:45).

## Metaphysical

A spreading and enlarging of the idea of the omnipresence and abidingness of 
divine life and love throughout the consciousness (germinating, scattering, going forth, extending, 
publishing; see JOHA).
