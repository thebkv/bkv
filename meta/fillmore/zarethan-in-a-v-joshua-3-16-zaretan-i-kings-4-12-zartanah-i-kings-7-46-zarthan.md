# Zarethan (in A. V., Joshua 3:16, Zaretan; I Kings 4 : 12, Zartanah; I Kings 7 :46, Zarthan)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Joshua 3:16, Zaretan; I Kings 4 : 12, Zartanah; I Kings 7 :46, Zarthan), 
zar'-e-than

## Meanings

- piercing
- penetrating
- cooling

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place near which the Israelites crossed the river Jordan when they entered the Promised Land 
(Josh. 3 : 16). In the clay ground between Succoth and Zarethan the vessels that were made by Hiram, 
king of Tyre, for Solomon's Temple were "cast" or formed, as in a mold (see I Kings 7 :46).
