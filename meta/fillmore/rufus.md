# Rufus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Lat
- **Pronunciation:** ru'-fus

## Meanings

- red
- reddish
- ruddy

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Simon of Cyrene. This Simon was compelled to bear Jesus' cross to Calvary (Mark 
15:21). b A Christian at Rome to whom Paul sent salutation in his letter to the Romans (Rom. 16:13).

## Metaphysical

Life activity in body consciousness (red, ruddy).
