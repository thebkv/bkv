# Ephlal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** eph'-lal

## Meanings

- judgment
- equity
- judging i.e., coloring with ones thoughts

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Judah (I Chron. 2:37)

## Metaphysical

The dawning concept in the individual that he has power to name, or give 
character to, whatever within himself or his world of affairs he passes judgment upon (judgment judging 
i.e., coloring with one s thoughts. See Gen. 2:19, 20.) By right judgment, thinking the truth about 
himself and all that he contacts, man can establish equity harmony, balance in his life.
