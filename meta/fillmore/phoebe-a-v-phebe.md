# Phoebe (A.V., Phebe)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- brilliant
- radiant

A "servant" or "deaconess" (margin) of the church at Cenchre% in Corinth, to whom Paul sent 
salutation in his letter to the Romans (Rom. 16:1).
