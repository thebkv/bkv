# Zabbai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zab'-bai

## Meanings

- washed
- cleansed
- clean
- clear
- limpid
- pure
- innocent

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One of the sons of Bebai. He had taken a foreign wife, but agreed to give her up when his error 
was made clear to him by Ezra (Ezra 10 :28). b The father of a Jewish man named Baruch, who helped 
to repair the wall of Jerusalem after the return from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 3:20).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the high, spiritual phase of consciousness in man 
(Israelites), which tend to purity and Truth (washed, cleansed, clean, pure) and to clearness of 
comprehension (limpid, clear).
