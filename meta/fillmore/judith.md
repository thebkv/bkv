# Judith

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ju'-dith

## Meanings

- feminine form of Judah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Wife of Esau and daughter of Beeri the Hittite (Gen. 26:34).

## Metaphysical

Judith, feminine of Judah, signifies the outer-sense soul (wife of Esau and 
daughter of Beeri the Hittite, both referring to the physical or sense man) or feminine aspect of praise- 
and-prayer thoughts in consciousness.
