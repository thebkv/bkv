# Laodicea

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ICE-od-i- e'-CE

## Meanings

- justice of the people
- judgment of the people

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Phrygia, in Asia Minor, about forty miles from Ephesus. There was an assembly of 
Christians at Laodicea (Col. 2:1; Rev. 1 :] 1).

## Metaphysical

A phase of the judgment faculty in the individual, expressing in the personal. It is 
that phase of judgment which bases its understanding, its decisions, on outer seemings and intellectual 
reasonings.

Laodiceans, ICE-od-T- e'-ans (fr. Gk.)

People who lived in Laodicea. It was to the church in Laodicea that Paul and John wrote (Col. 
4:16; Rev. 3:14).

Metaphysical. Thoughts belonging to that in consciousness for which Laodicea stands. (See 
LAODICEA.)

Lappidoth (A. V., Lapidoth), lap'-pi-doth {Heb.)— light radiations; spreading abroad of lights; 
lamps; torches; flames; enlightened; enlighteners; instructors.

Husband of the prophetess Deborah (Judg. 4:4).

Metaphysical. Wisdom expressing, radiating (light radiations, lamps, enlighteners ). (See 
DEBORAH.)

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet. And light unto my path."

"The spirit of man is the lamp of Jehovah,

Searching all his innermost parts."
