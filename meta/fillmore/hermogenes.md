# Hermogenes

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** her-mog'-e-nes

## Meanings

- born of Hermes
- Mercury-bom

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Christian in Asia who turned away from Paul (II Tim. 1:15).

## Metaphysical

The outer, verbal expression of one's Christian principles. This outer expression is 
very likely to be cut off for a time when the inner word of Truth (Paul) is limited in its activities by the 
dominating force of the unspiritualized will and intellect (is imprisoned in Rome). Firm establishment in 
Truth is necessary that one may remain faithful in one's outer thoughts, words, and acts when going 
through some serious conflict within oneself.
