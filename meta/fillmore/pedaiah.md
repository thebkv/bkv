# Pedaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe-da'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There were several Israelitish men by this name (II Kings 23:36; I Chron. 3:18, 27:20; Neh.

3:25; 8:4; 11:7).

## Metaphysical

The symbolism here is that of redemption, overcoming power, salvation, from 
error and its effects to manifest Christlikeness, by means of Jehovah, I AM, the indwelling Christ.
