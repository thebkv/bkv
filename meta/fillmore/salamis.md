# Salamis

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** sal'-a-mis

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city on the island of Cyprus. Paul preached in the Jewish synagogues in Salamis (Acts 13:5).

## Metaphysical

A state of thought that is protective, preservative, uplifting, fertile, harmonious, 
and wise-or at least is receptive to these qualities (salt, savor, fertility, concord, wisdom).

## Preserved OCR Source

Salamis, sal'-a-mis (Gk.)— possibly, tossing; shaking. More likely, salt; savor; fertility; arable 
land; concord; wisdom; grace of speech.
