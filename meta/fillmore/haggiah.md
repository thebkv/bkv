# Haggiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hag-gl'-ah

## Meanings

- Jehovah's feast
- festival of Jehovah
- feast of the Lord
- joy of the

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Lord.

A Levite, descended from Merari (I Chron. 6: 30).

## Metaphysical

The Christ of God (Jehovah) feasting with man upon the joyous occasion of man's 
opening the door of his consciousness to the Christ rule of love. "Behold, I stand at the door and knock: 
if any man hear my voice and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with 
me" (Rev. 3:20).
