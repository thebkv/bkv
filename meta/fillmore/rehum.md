# Rehum

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** re'-hum

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One who returned from the captivity with Zerubbabel (Ezra 2:2); in Nehemiah 7:7, he is called 
Nehum. b Other men by this name are mentioned in Nehemiah 3:17, 10:25, and 12:3.

## Metaphysical

Rehum suggests the tender, brooding, mother love of God, which soothes the 
whole being of man and through which new understanding and realization of Truth are germinated in 
consciousness and grow and increase to perfect expression and demonstration.
