# Tolad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** to'-lad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Simeon (I Chron. 4:29). It is called Eltolad in Joshua 15:30, where it is named as a city 
of Judah (see the latter half of Joshua 19:1).

## Metaphysical

The originating, forming, and bringing into expression of a new state of 
consciousness (generation, begetting, conceiving, bringing forth, birth, a city of Simeon; see 
ELTOLAD).

Tolaites, to'-la-tes (fr. Heb.).

Descendants of Tola, son of Issachar (Num. 26:23).

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from that in man which Tola signifies. (See TOLA.)
