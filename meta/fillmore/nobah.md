# Nobah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** no'-bah

## Meanings

- barking
- striking
- pulsating
- prominent
- vehement of voice

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A man of Manasseh. b A city, or district. "And Nobah went and took Kenath, and the villages 
thereof, and called it Nobah, after his own name (Num. 32:42). c A city near which Gideon smote the 
host of Zebah and Zalmunna, kings of Midian (Judg. 8: 1 1).

## Metaphysical

Positive affirmations of Truth (vehement of voice). While these affirmations and 
thoughts of Truth are of a high character (prominent), there is something of personal force, or a fighting 
attitude of mind, in declaring them, as is signified by barking and striking.
