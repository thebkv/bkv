# Ahiram

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-hl'-ram

## Meanings

- brother of height, i. e., high, lofty
- exalted brother
- (my) brother is

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

high.
