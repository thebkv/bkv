# Tahrea

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tSh-re'-CE

## Meanings

- quick
- fleet
- flight
- adroitness
- adroit
- crafty
- cunning

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Micah, who was a grandson of Jonathan son of Saul (I Chron. 9:41). He is called Tarea in 
I Chronicles 8:35.

## Metaphysical

The quickness of movement in consciousness, and very great skill, perception, and 
judgment, of spiritual faith (quick, fleet, adroit, cunning, a Benjamite). To the extent that this thought 
belongs to the outer consciousness and partakes of guile (crafty, cunning), its results will be 
unsatisfactory and inharmonious.
