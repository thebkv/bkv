# Shelumiel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she-lu'-mi-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Zurishaddai and prince of the tribe of Simeon. He was chosen to be head over this tribe 
during its joumeyings in the wilderness (Num. 1:6; 2:12).

## Metaphysical

A sense of companionship with God (friend of God) and of spiritual peace and 
wholeness (peace of God, God makes complete ). This comes about by attentiveness and obedience, or 
consecration, to higher ideals (Simeon, meaning hearing) and by becoming established in the staying 
qualities of Spirit (Zurishaddai, father of Shelumiel, meaning my rock is the Almighty).
