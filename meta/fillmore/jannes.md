# Jannes

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** jan'-nes

## Meanings

- mockery
- deception
- poverty
- rebellion

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man, supposedly an Egyptian magician, who opposed Moses when Moses was seeking from 
Pharaoh the release of the Israelites (II Tim. 3:8). This same adverse spirit worked in some of the 
professing Christians of Paul's day.

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of Jambres. The quality of seeming 
wisdom in consciousness for which these men stand is actuated and governed by the adverse, carnal 
mind, and cannot compare with the wisdom and power of true understanding. It is deceptive and is 
altogether lacking in reverence and respect for anything higher than itself. It cannot abide, therefore, but 
will be overthrown with the Egyptian obscurity and darkness to which it belongs.
