# Jehubbah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-hub'-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shemer, of the tribe of Asher (I Chron. 7:34).

## Metaphysical

A thought of watchfulness and caution (Shemer, father of Jehubbah) carried to 
such an extreme by the individual that it causes a fearfulness that tends to hide the newer and higher 
ideas of Spirit from his consciousness, and also binds or limits him in his expression of Truth.
