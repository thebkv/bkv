# Tadmor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tad'-m™r

## Meanings

- Tamar
- Palmyra
- palms
- city of palms

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city built in the wilderness by King Solomon (II Chron. 8:4). In I Kings 9: 18 it is called Tamar.

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of victory (palms, which denote victory).

Tahan, ta'-han (Heb bent down; bowed down; inclined; settling down; pitching one's tent; tent 
place; encampment; graciousness; entreaty; supplication; prayer.

a A descendant of Ephraim, perhaps a son (Num. 26:35). b A son of Telah an Ephraimite (I 
Chron. 7:25).

Metaphysical. Humble, gracious, prayerful, earnestly desirous of good, abiding thoughts relating 
to the will faculty in man (bowed down, graciousness, encampment, settling down, entreaty, prayer, men 
of the Israelitish tribe of Ephraim, which represents the will in individual consciousness).

Tahanites, ta'-han-ites (fr. Heb.).

The "family" of Tahan of the "sons" of Ephraim (Num. 26:35).

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from and of a character like that in consciousness which is 
signified by Tahan. (See TAHAN.)
