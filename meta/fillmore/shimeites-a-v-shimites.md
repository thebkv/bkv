# Shimeites (A. V., Shimites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Shimei

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Shimei son of Gershon, of the Levites (Num. 3:21).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and in close relation to that in consciousness signified by 
Shimei. (See SHIMEI.)
