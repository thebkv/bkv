# Gudgodah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** gud'-go-dah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in which the Israelites camped while in the wilderness, on their way to the Promised 
Land (Deut. 10:7).

## Metaphysical

A further cutting, or cleaving away from consciousness, of the remains of 
Egyptian darkness and ignorance, that more abundant vitality and wholeness (well of much water, 
signifying the life force) and happiness may be realized by the individual who has started out on his 
conscious journey toward perfection.
