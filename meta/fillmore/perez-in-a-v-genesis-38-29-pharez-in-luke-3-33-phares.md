# Perez (in A.V., Genesis 38:29, Pharez; in Luke, 3:33, Phares)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Genesis 38:29, Pharez; in Luke, 3:33, Phares), pe'-rez

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Judah by his daughter-in-law Tamar (Gen. 38:29; see I Chronicles 27:3 also).

## Metaphysical

Victory through praise, or making a way out of apparent limitation and error and 
predominating over them by means of prayer and praise. Perez means broken through, torn asunder, 
dispersed. Judah, father of Perez, signifies praise; his mother, Tamar, meaning palm tree, erect, 
represents uprightness and victory. Judah said of her, "She is more righteous than I" (Gen. 38:26).
