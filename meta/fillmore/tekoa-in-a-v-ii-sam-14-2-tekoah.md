# Tekoa (in A. V., II Sam. 14:2, Tekoah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** II Sam. 14:2, Tekoah), te-ko'-CE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Judah, founded by Ashhur (I Chron. 2:24). It was fortified by Rehoboam (II Chron. 
11:6). Amos was "among the herdsmen of Tekoa" (Amos 1:1). The "wise woman" whom Joab sent to 
David to intercede for Absalom lived in Tekoa (II Sam. 14:2).

## Metaphysical

The establishing in the individual of a more firm and abiding idea regarding his 
body (pitching a tent, fixing (of tents), fastening, securing, confirming).

Tekoite, te-ko'-ite (fr. Heb.).

A native of Tekoa. Here the reference is to "Ira the son of Ikk esh the Tekoite" (II Sam. 23:26).

Metaphysical. A thought like and belonging to the group of thoughts that Tekoa signifies. (See 
TEKOA; also IRA and IKKESH.)
