# Beriites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be-rl'-ltes

## Meanings

- misfortune

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Beriah, the son of Asher (Num. 26:44).

## Metaphysical

The thoughts that belong to the Beriah phase of consciousness. They suggest a 
belief in evil that causes one to behold the seeming error of an experience instead of the good that is 
attained in every instance wherein some of the self is crossed out that the spiritual may come into fuller 
and freer expression .
