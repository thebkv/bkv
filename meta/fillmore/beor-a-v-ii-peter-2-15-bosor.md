# Beor (A. V., II Peter 2:15, Bosor)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** II Peter 2:15, Bosor), be'-or

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of Bela, who was a king of Edom (Gen. 36:32). b The prophet Baalam's father (Num.

22:5).

## Metaphysical

An intuitive understanding belonging to the outer man of sense (a torch, a lamp). 
Since this light (understanding) is not established consciously in Spirit nor guided by Spirit, but by 
material beliefs and desires, it is not a true shepherd to the pure natural life of the organism (a flock), 
instead it works unwisely and destructively (an eating flame, brutish, foolish).
