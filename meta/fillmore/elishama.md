# Elishama

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-l;sh'-a-ma

## Meanings

- whom God hears
- God of hearing, i. e. , understanding
- God is

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

hearer.

a A son of David (II Sam. 5:16). b A priest during the reign of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah (II 
Chron. 17:8). c The name of other Israelitish men (Num. 1:10; I Chron. 2:41).

## Metaphysical

The inner assurance that God hears and answers the true desires of His people, 
and that Spirit is the source of true understanding.
