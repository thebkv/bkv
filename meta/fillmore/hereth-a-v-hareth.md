# Hereth (A.V., Hareth)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Hareth), he'-reth

## Meanings

- thick wood
- thicket
- dense forest

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A forest in the land of Judah where David went when he was hiding from Saul (I Sam. 22:5).

## Metaphysical

A state of mind that cannot be penetrated by error or force. By resorting to praise 
and prayer (Judah), love (David) builds up a state of spiritual thought to which the will functioning in 
the selfishness of personality (Saul) cannot possibly gain access. This is Hereth, a thicket, a dense 
growth of shrubbery and trees, in Judah, to which David fled from Saul.
