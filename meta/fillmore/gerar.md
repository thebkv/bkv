# Gerar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ge'-rSr

## Meanings

- a sojourn
- a lodging place
- an encampment
- a halting place

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Philistine place, near Gaza (Gen. 10:19). Abraham sojourned in Gerar (Gen. 20:1), and Isaac 
also went to Gerar when there was a famine in the land where he had formerly lived. Both Abraham and 
Isaac at this place told that their wives were their sisters, and came near getting into serious trouble 
because of their deception. It was here that Isaac's herdsmen and the herdsmen of Gerar contended over 
the wells that Isaac's servants dug (Gen. 26).

## Metaphysical

Subjective substance and life. In the beginning of man's journey spiritward this 
substance and life are in the possession of the sense nature (Philistines), and the ruling ego of the sense 
nature lives in the region of this place (Isaac went to Abimelech, king of the Philistines, "unto Gerar"). 
The awakening intellectual and spiritual man must draw upon this substance and life for sustenance 
(faith, Abraham, must be established in substance) and sooner or later, when the spiritual in man has 
gained sufficient understanding, strength,

GERSHOM

and power, it must take entire possession of this phase of man's being.
