# Josech (A. V., Joseph)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Joseph), jo'-sech

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:26).

## Metaphysical

The significance is the same as that of Joseph: the imagination, the imaging, 
increasing power of the mind.

## Preserved OCR Source

Josech (A. V., Joseph), jo'-sech (Gk. fr. Heb.)— whom Jehovah will add to; Jehovah shall 
increase; he shall increase progressively, i. e., to perfection.
