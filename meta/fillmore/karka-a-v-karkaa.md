# Karka (A. V., Karkaa)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Karkaa), kSr’-kCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place mentioned as being on the south border of Judah (Josh. 15:3).

## Metaphysical

Karka (ground floor, bottom, a place on the south border of Judah) signifies a 
group of thoughts pertaining to the very bottom, or foundation principle, of man's subconscious life 
forces. This group of thoughts is deeply rooted in substance (deep ground).
