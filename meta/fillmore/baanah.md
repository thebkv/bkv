# Baanah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-a-nah

## Meanings

- son of affliction
- son of grief

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There were different men by this name (II Sam. 4:2; 23:29; Ezra 2:2); it is a form of Baana.

## Metaphysical

From the Bible history of the men named Baanah, we are shown how giving way 
to seeming affliction and sorrow works out greater evil in one's life, as in the case of the Baanah of II 
Samuel 4, who with his brother killed Ishbosheth the son of Saul, and was slain himself because of this 
act. On the other hand we can cause even suffering, and that which appears evil, to work out for our 
good; we can become more firmly established in strength, love, and Truth by refusing to be daunted by 
seeming error conditions and by rising out of them through exercise of the Christ power in ourselves. 
(One of the men named Baanah was a mighty man of David's; another was an officer of King 
Solomon's; still another was one of those who returned with Zerubbahel from the Babylonian captivity.)
