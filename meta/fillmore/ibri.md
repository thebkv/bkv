# Ibri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ib'-ri

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Merari, of the tribe of Levi (I Chron. 24:27).

## Metaphysical

A passing over from the bitter, unhappy frame of mind and conditions that result 
from love expressed negatively, to a true comprehension and expression of the love faculty. (Levi 
signifies love, and Merari, the father of Ibri and son of Levi, signifies the bitterness and sorrow that 
results when the power of love is not established in a right understanding of Truth, but is directed by 
false notions such as a belief in righteous indignation and wrath, "an eye for an eye, and a tooth for a 
tooth," and "love thy neighbor, and hate thine enemy.") (See HEBREW and MERARI. See HEBER and 
EBER also, since the word Ibri has virtually the same meaning.)
