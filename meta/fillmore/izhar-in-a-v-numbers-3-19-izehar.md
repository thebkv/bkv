# Izhar (in A. V., Numbers 3:19, Izehar)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Numbers 3:19, Izehar), izO-hSr

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Kohath, who was one of Levi's sons (Exod. 6: 18).

## Metaphysical

Spiritual understanding manifesting as true understanding in individual 
consciousness, and tending to happiness and prosperity, true riches.

Izharites (in A. V., Numbers 3:27, Izeharites), izO-hSr-ites (fr. Heb.).

Levites, descendants of Izhar (I Chron. 26:23). "Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were 
for the outward business over Israel, for officers and judges" (I Chron. 26:29); this was in David's reign.

Metaphysical. Thoughts that spring from and belong to that in consciousness which Izhar 
represents. (See IZHAR.)
