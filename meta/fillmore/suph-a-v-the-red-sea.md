# Suph (A. V., the Red Sea)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** the Red Sea), suph

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A part (the Gulf of Arabia, because it abounds in seaweed), if not all, of the Red Sea (Deut. 1:1).

## Metaphysical

See the RED SEA for the significance of this name.

Seaweed here gives the idea of obstruction of the pure life flow in the organism of man because 
of material thoughts, weeds, tares.
