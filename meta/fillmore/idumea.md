# Idumea

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** id-u-mea'-CE

## Meanings

- territory of Edom

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The Greek name for Edom, Idumeaa is the land of Edom (Mark 3:8). In the American Standard 
Version, Edom is given instead of Idumea, in most places.
