# Maath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-ath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Mattathias, in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:26).

## Metaphysical

A refining process (smooth, polished, sharpened) in consciousness, by which 
seeming error, the personal and carnal in the individual, is removed, ( scraped off) and becomes very 
small, very insignificant.
