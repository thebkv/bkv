# Imnah (in A. V., Genesis 46:17, Jimnah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Genesis 46:17, Jimnah), im'-nah

## Meanings

- good fortune
- prosperity

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Asher (I Chron. 7:30). b A Levite (II Chron. 31:14).

## Metaphysical

A strong belief in and realization of abundant prosperity and every good as being 
man's true inheritance and the Father's will for him. (Kore, the son of the second Imnah that is 
mentioned, "was over the freewill-offerings of God, to distribute the oblations of Jehovah, and the most 
holy things." This was in the Temple, in Hezekiah's reign.)
