# Puthites (A.V., Puhites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A family of Kiriath-jearim (I Chron. 2:53)

## Metaphysical

Divided thoughts, thoughts of a deceptive character, that have found a place in the 
phase of consciousness signified by Kiriath-jearim. (See KIRIATH-JEARIM.)

Putiel, pu'-t;-el (Heb.) afflicted of God; stifled of God.

Father-in-law of Eleazar who was Aaron's son (Exod. 6:25).

Metaphysical. The mistaken belief that God limits His people, withholds good from them, and 
afflicts them, for some purpose of His own— presumably for their higher good (afflicted of God, stifled of 
God). This belief exists in the old-established religious thoughts of man, but is not of the Spirit of truth 
(Putiel evidently was an Israelite).
