# Shaphat

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sha'-phat

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

(E Son of Hori of the tribe of Simeon, one of the twelve spies (Num. 13:5). b Father of Elisha the 
prophet (I Kings 19:16). c Others of the same name (I Chron. 3:22; 5:12; 27:29).

## Metaphysical

These men by the name of Shaphat (to judge) signify the development of the 
discerning, judging faculty throughout the consciousness, or the judgment idea's becoming operative 
throughout the various faculties of mind that are represented by the tribes of Israel to whom the men by 
this name belonged.
