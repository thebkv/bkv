# Elimelech

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-l;m'-e-leeh

## Meanings

- God is king
- God the king
- my God is king

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite of Beth-lehem-judah, and husband of Naomi (Ruth 1:2). Because of a famine in the 
land of Judah he took his wife and two sons to live in the country of Moab, and he died there.

## Metaphysical

The consciousness of the soul (Naomi) that God is king. (See NAOMI and 
CHILION.)
