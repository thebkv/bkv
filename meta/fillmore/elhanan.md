# Elhanan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el-ha'-nan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Beth-lehemite warrior in the time of David (II Sam. 21: 19; I Chron. 20:5). He killed the 
brother of Goliath the Gittite. b One of David's guard of thirty valiant men (II Sam. 2:24); he also was of 
Beth-lehem.

## Metaphysical

A gracious, kindly, loving attitude of mind that emanates from God, Spirit, and is 
established in substance (Beth-lehem). This gives courage and fearlessness in combating seeming error.
