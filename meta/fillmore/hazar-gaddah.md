# Hazar-gaddah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-zSr-gad'-dah

## Meanings

- village of Gad
- court of fortune
- court of troops

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A southern city of Judah (Josh 15:27).

## Metaphysical

Strongly organized thoughts of abundant substance and power, in the 
subconsciousness (south means below, and here it represents the subconsciousness; fortune refers to 
abundant supply; court of troops bespeaks strongly organized thoughts, Gad suggests power).
