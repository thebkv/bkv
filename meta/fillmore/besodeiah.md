# Besodeiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bes-o-de'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His son Meshullam helped to repair the walls of Jerusalem after returning from the Babylonian 
captivity (Neh. 3:6).

## Metaphysical

A knowledge of man's true being, of his spiritual nature and sonship. This is the 
Lord's secret, and that in us which perceives it is in close association with Jehovah (in familiar converse 
with Jehovah, in the intimacy ofJah, in counsel with Jehovah ). This knowledge generates, in the 
individual who has entered into a consciousness of his oneness with the Christ within him, a devotion to 
spiritual things, a unification with Spirit, and a companionship with true ideas that does much toward 
rebuilding his mind and body in enduring life, substance, and Truth. (Rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem 
refers to the rebuilding of the organism into its true spiritual likeness and durability.)
