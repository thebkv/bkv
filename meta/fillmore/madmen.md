# Madmen

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mad'-men

## Meanings

- dunghill
- manure heap

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Moab that Jeremiah mentions in his prophecy of the destruction of Moab (Jer. 48:2).

## Metaphysical

The very low opinion (dunghill) that the carnal mind in man (Moab) entertains 
concerning man's true, higher, religious or spiritual, consciousness (Israel). This opinion must be cast 
out by the word of Truth (sword). "Thou also, O Madmen, shalt be brought to silence; the sword shall 
pursue thee."
