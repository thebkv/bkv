# Euphrates

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_addendum

## Preserved OCR Source

Euphrates. The fourth river, Euphrates, means "fructifying," or "that which is the fructifying 
cause." ly, it represents the blood stream. The circulatory system receives and distributes the nutrients 
contained in the food we eat. The blood stream is charged with the food substance for bone, muscle, 
brain, teeth, and hair. Every part of the organism is supplied with substance through this wonderful river 
Euphrates.
