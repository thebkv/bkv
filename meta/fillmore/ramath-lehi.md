# Ramath-lehi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The place where Samson cast from his hand the jawbone of the ass with which he had just killed 
a thousand Philistines (Judg. 15:17, in margin, "The hill of the jawbone").

## Metaphysical

A lifting up, in consciousness, of the qualities that the jawbone of the ass 
signifies: determination, endurance, vigorous strength, and so forth. (See LEHI.)
