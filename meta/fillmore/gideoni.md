# Gideoni

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** gld-e-o'-nl

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Abidan. Abidan was the man whom God chose from the tribe of Benjamin to stand 
with Moses when Moses was numbering the men of Israel in the wilderness (Num. 1:11).

## Metaphysical

Denial, working through the activity of faith (a Benjamite) to destroy error and to 
bring about discrimination, good judgment (Abidan).
