# Telaim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te-la'-im

## Meanings

- young lambs
- lambs
- infants
- youths

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The place where Saul, king of Israel, gathered his people together and numbered them, 
preparatory to going to war against the Amalekites (I Sam. 15:4).

## Metaphysical

A thought center in consciousness that is of the nature of active life, youth, 
freshness, and newness (young lambs, infants, youths). In other words; fresh, new, alive ideas.
