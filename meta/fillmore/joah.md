# Joah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jo'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Asaph, the recorder, in the reign of Hezekiah king of Judah (II Kings 18: 18). b A 
Gershomite Levite (I Chron. 6:21), called Ethan in verse 42. c Son of Obed-edom, of the Korathite 
Levites, doorkeepers (I Chron. 26:4). d A son of Joahaz the recorder, in Josiah's reign, who was sent to 
help repair the house of Jehovah (II Chron. 34:8).

## Metaphysical

Man's divine relationship to his / AM, or indwelling Christ (Jehovah); an 
awakening by man to his close friendship, kinship, fellowship, with his true, higher self— brotherhood, 
by right of divine origin, or birth. Man's conscious union with his true, inner, spiritual self.
