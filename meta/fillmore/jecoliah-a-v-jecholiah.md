# Jecoliah (A. V., Jecholiah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jecholiah), jec-o-li-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Wife of Amaziah and mother of Azariah, or Uzziah, kings of Judah (II Kings 15:2). She is called 
Jechiliah in II Chronicles 26:3.
