# Eshton

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** esh'-ton

## Meanings

- womanish
- uxorious
- weak
- restful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Mehir, a Judahite (I Chron. 4: 1 1).

## Metaphysical

A thought of or tendency toward love of ease; also a weak, or negative, 
submissiveness to the emotions and desires of the human soul ( uxorious means "excessively or dotingly 
fond of, or submissive to, a wife").
