# En-dor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** en'-dor

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Manasseh from which the Manassites failed to drive the Canaanites (Josh. 17:11). This 
city was the home of the woman who had a familiar spirit, to whom Saul sent for advice (I Sam. 28:7). 
According to Psalms 83:9, 10, En-dor seems also to have been the seat of the overthrow of Sisera, and of 
Jabin, king of Canaan.

## Metaphysical

The life faculty in man unified with and activated by generative and psychic sense 
thought (fount of the habitation, fount of the generation, the home of a woman who had a familiar 
spirit). As the Israelites, the true, higher thoughts of the individual, grow stronger in the regenerative

idea, the Canaanites are gradually driven out; the old beliefs, desires, and habits give up their substance 
to the establishing of the uplifting, purifying, and restorative ideas of the Christ mind.
