# name

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

Spiritual understanding, divine insight into spiritual Truth (face of God, vision of 
God, understanding of God); also the inner realization of the divine presence, of having met God face to 
face and of having succeeded, through prayer, in attaining the divine favor and blessing that has been 
sought ( turned toward God, within the presence of God, beholden of God). (See PENUEL and 
PENIEL.)
