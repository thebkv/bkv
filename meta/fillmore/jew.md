# Jew

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Judea

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man of the tribe of Judah; more broadly speaking, a Hebrew. Mordecai, the Jew (Esth. 2:5), 
was a Benjamite.

Metaphysical As a descendant of Judah, son of Jacob, and as a member of the tribe or kingdom 
of Judah, a Jew signifies a thought springing from and belonging to the praise and inner-life 
consciousness of the individual.

As a Hebrew, which a Jew is in the broader sense, the significance is the same as that of 
HEBREW, which see.

For the symbology of Mordecai the Jew, see MORDECAI. See JEWS also.

inner-life consciousness of the individual.
