# Hararite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-ra-rite

## Meanings

- of or belonging to the mountain
- a mountaineer
- strong
- elevated

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

exalted.

Shammah, the son of Agee, a Hararite, was one of David's warriors (II Sam. 23:11). In I 
Chronicles 1 1:34, 35 two of David's mighty men are mentioned as being Hararites.

## Metaphysical

High aspirations; strong, noble, uplifting thoughts.
