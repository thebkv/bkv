# Azmon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** az'-mon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town at the south border of Judah and of Canaan (Num. 34:4; Josh. 15:4); it was near the 
"brook of Egypt," and so was near the western extremity of the south boundary line of Canaan.
