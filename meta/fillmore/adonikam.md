# Adonikam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-don'-;-kam

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite whose descendants, to the number of six hundred and sixty-six, returned from the 
Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:13).

## Metaphysical

A resurrecting, uplifting, firmly based spiritual belief (whom the Lord establishes, 
my Lord is raised, the Lord stands) that, even though it resided in sense confusion (Babylon), brought 
forth a company of thoughts (the descendants of Adonikam) that returned to spiritual consciousness 
(Jerusalem), their true abiding place. Every true thought in man aids in the resurrection of the whole 
man into a consciousness of life and wholeness.
