# Eshtemoa

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** esh-te-mo'-a

## Meanings

- hearing
- hearkening
- understanding
- obedience
- submission

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city of Judah that was given over to the priests (Josh. 21: 14). b A man, a Judahite of the city 
of Maachah (I Chron. 4:19).

## Metaphysical

The spirit of receptivity, understanding, and obedience, which causes one to give 
attention to spiritual guidance and to obey its leadings.
