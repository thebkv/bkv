# Kishi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** kishO-1

## Meanings

- bow of Jehovah
- strength ofJah
- power of Jehovah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Ethan, who was one of the musicians in the tabernacle in David's reign (I Chron. 6:44). 
He is called Kushaiah in I Chronicles 15:17.
