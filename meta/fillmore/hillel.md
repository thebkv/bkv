# Hillel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hilO-lel

## Meanings

- praise
- rejoice
- celebrate
- shout for joy
- shine forth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Pirathonite. His son Abdon was a judge of Israel (Judg. 12:13). He was of the tribe of 
Ephraim.
