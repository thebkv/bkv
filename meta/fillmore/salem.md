# Salem

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sa'-lem

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The city of Melchizedek. It is thought to have been the same city that was later called Jerusalem 
(Gen. 14:18; Heb. 7:1,2).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of spiritual peace, wholeness, and perfection (whole, summit, 
peace, complete, perfect; see JERUSALEM).
