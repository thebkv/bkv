# Jeremoth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jer'-e-moth

## Meanings

- elevations
- heights
- exaltations
- terror of death
- rejection of death

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A chief of Benjamin, who lived in Jerusalem (I Chron. 8: 14). b Several other Israelitish men (I 
Chron. 23:23; 25:22; Ezra 10:26, 27).

## Metaphysical

Lofty, constructive thoughts in the true religious consciousness of man. Such 
thoughts aid in putting away death.
