# Elisheba

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-llsh'-e-ba

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Aaron's wife, and daughter of Amminadab (Exod. 6:23). Elisabeth is the same name.

## Metaphysical

The full assurance in the soul or feminine, love consciousness, that God is a God 
of Truth and keeps His promises to man; in other words, the inner conviction of the soul that the 
working out of the law of life and Truth in man's consciousness is inevitable and sure.
