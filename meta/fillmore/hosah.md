# Hosah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ho'-sah

## Meanings

- refuge
- shelter
- protection
- trust
- hope
- confidence

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city on the border of Asher (Josh. 19:29). b One of the doorkeepers of the Temple during 
David's reign (I Chron. 16:38); his place of watch was "westward, by the gate of Shallecheth" (I Chron. 
26:16).

## Metaphysical

A trusting, hopeful spirit in man that becomes a true haven (refuge) to him at all 
times, and especially when he is passing through some overcoming experiences in which the error side 
of things appears to be triumphing over the true.
