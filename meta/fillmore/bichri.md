# Bichri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** blch'-rl

## Meanings

- youthful', youth
- first-born

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite, father of Sheba, a base fellow who started a rebellion against David after Absalom 
had been defeated and killed (II Sam. 20: 1-22).

## Metaphysical

An idea of life, force, and action that is new and young in consciousness (youthful, 
youth, first-born), and has as yet no true concept of the value and power of love (David).
