# Maon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-on

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:55). b Son of Shammai and founder of Beth-zur, of 
the tribe and cities of Judah (I Chron. 2:45).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of abidingness, of stability, of continuity, of oneness with God, 
within the very center of one's being.
