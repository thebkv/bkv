# Tyrannus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ty-ran'-nus

## Meanings

- tyrant
- sovereign
- ruling prince

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man of Ephesus, in whose school Paul was "reasoning daily" for two years after they of the 
synagogue turned away from the Truth (Acts 19:9, 10).

## Metaphysical

The absolute ruling power of desire in the individual. Desire knows no restraint by 
law or custom, it works irresistibly toward expression (tyrant, sovereign, ruling prince, a man of 
Ephesus). Ephesus signifies desire, which is the central building faculty of the consciousness.

After Paul was forced to leave off preaching in the synagogue because of the resistance of the 
Jews, he continued for two years to reason daily in the school of Tyrannus. This signifies that the word 
of Truth continues to work in the spiritually awakening individual through his inner desires that truly 
long for the things of Spirit, even though the individual may for a time outwardly repudiate the higher 
messages of Truth because of his preconceived, formal, established religious beliefs' still holding so 
great a place in his thought.
