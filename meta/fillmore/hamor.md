# Hamor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-m™r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A prince of the Hivites, from whom Jacob bought the land on which he built

the altar El-Elohe-Israel (Gen. 33:19). Hamor was the father of Shechem, who defiled JacobOs daughter 
Dinah and wanted to marry her. Both father and son were slain by Jacob's sons, Simeon and Levi (Gen. 
34:2-26).

## Metaphysical

The ruling or central thought in the Hivite state of consciousness in man (see 
HIVITE), stubborn foolishness and unreasonableness (ass); also the transitory and material (clay) 
character of man's sensual, worldly mind.
