# Edrei

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ed'-re-l

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city of Bashan, where the remnant of the Rephaim lived; this place was conquered by the 
Israelites under Moses and was given to Manasseh for an inheritance (Josh 12:4-6). b A fortified city of 
Canaan that was allotted to Naphtali (Josh. 19:37).

## Metaphysical

A fixed state of consciousness, or aggregation of nerve cells, established in life 
and substance (see BASHAN), whose dominant idea is strength and might.
