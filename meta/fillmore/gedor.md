# Gedor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ge'-d™r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:58). b The Gedor of I Chronicles 4:39 is thought to 
be the same place as the Geder of Joshua 12:13. c A Benjamite town, the home of Jeroham, whose sons 
Joelah and Zebadiah joined David at Ziklag when David was in hiding from Saul (I Chron. 12:7). d An 
Israelitish man, a Benjamite (I Chron. 8:31; 9:37). e The name Gedor occurs in I Chronicles 4:4, 18 in 
the genealogy of Judah.

## Metaphysical

Man's innate yet exalted belief in divine strength and protection active in 
consciousness. (See GEDER and BETH— GADER.)
