# Imrah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** im'-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Zophah, of the tribe of Asher (I Chron. 7:36).

## Metaphysical

The Adversary, or personal ambition and selfishness, the determination (perhaps 
almost unconscious) to exalt self, working in man's higher religious understanding.
