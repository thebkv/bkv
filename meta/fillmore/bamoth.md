# Bamoth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-moth

## Meanings

- high places
- heights
- sepulchral mound
- hill chapels

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in which the Israelites camped while they were on their way to the Promised Land 
(Num. 21:19). It was a city of the Moabites, situated on the river Arnon, or near it, and was allotted to 
the tribe of Reuben; it is called Bamoth-baal in Joshua 13:17, and "the high places of the Amon" in 
Numbers 21:28. (See margin also.)

## Metaphysical

Amon refers to the life current in man, and Bamoth, or Bamoth-baal (the height or 
sanctuary of Baal) signifies the exaltation of carnality. In other words, Bamoth symbolizes the honor and 
the veneration (worship) that the carnal mind (Moab) gives to the life forces for use in sensuality. No 
idol is served more persistently by man in sense consciousness than this one. Bamah must be lifted up 
and redeemed by Reuben, who represents sight, the seeing or perceiving mind. This clear seeing reveals 
the truth about life and its right functioning in man for his eternal good.
