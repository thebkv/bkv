# Hamath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-math

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A small Syrian kingdom on the northern border of Palestine (Num. 34:8).

## Metaphysical

Confidence in material conditions rather than trust in God; also the result of the 
material thought and belief— an unpoised, unbalanced state of mind. (See ASHIMA.)
