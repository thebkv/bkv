# Tebah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te'-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Nahor's son by his concubine, Reumah (Gen. 22:24).

## Metaphysical

An active thought of or strong belief in self-defense (life guard, bodyguard) that is 
very destructive (slaughter, i. e., of animals or of persons, killing, slaying, execution, butchering).
