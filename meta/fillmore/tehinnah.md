# Tehinnah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te-hin-nah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Eshton of the tribe of Judah, and the father of Ir-nahash (I Chron. 4:12).

## Metaphysical

One's praying earnestly but in a supplicating, entreating manner, as though mercy 
and grace would come from without, instead of having confidence in the inner Source of all overcoming 
power and positively affirming one's good until it comes into manifestation (prayer, entreaty, 
supplication, imploring mercy, disposed with favor, mercy, compassion).
