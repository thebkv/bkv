# Dan-jaan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** dan-ja'-an

## Meanings

- Dan of the forest
- Dan of the woods

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place to which Joab and the captains of the host came when they were numbering Israel at 
David's command (II Sam. 24:6).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts of a rich, prosperous, redundant nature, expressing in 
conjunction with the judgment faculty (Dan of the forest; Dan signifies judgment in the beginning of its 
expression in individual consciousness and forest, in the meaning of jaan, is developed from the idea of 
redundance, exuberance, luxuriance).
