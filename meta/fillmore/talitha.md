# Talitha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** tal'-i-thCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The words that Jesus spoke to the daughter of the ruler of the synagogue when He restored her to 
life. "And taking the child by the hand, he saith unto her, Talitha cumi; which is, being interpreted, 
Damsel, I say unto thee, Arise" (Mark 5:41).

## Metaphysical

Talitha (fresh, young, new, damsel, maiden) refers here to the soul, which is being 
awakened to a new, higher, and fuller realization of life by the I AM or Christ consciousness in the 
individual.
