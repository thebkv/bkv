# Jeruel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-ru'-el

## Meanings

- foundation of Gocl
- founded of God
- God points the way
- vision of Gocl

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A wilderness, where Jehoshaphat met and defeated the children of Ammon and Moab. This was 
the battle wherein Jehoshaphat was told that the battle was not his but God's and that he would not have 
to fight in it; he was simply to stand still, fearlessly, and see the salvation of Jehovah (II Chron. 20:16).
