# Matred

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-tred

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Daughter of Mezahab, and mother of Mehetabel the wife of Hadar. This Hadar was king of 
Edom (Gen. 36:39).

## Metaphysical

Matred and Mehetabel, women of Edom, pertain to activities in the soul's progress 
toward spiritual perfection. Even the phase of the soul that pertains to Edom, or the outer physical man, 
has its impulses that are uplifting and are ever moving toward the more perfect understanding and 
expression of Being.
