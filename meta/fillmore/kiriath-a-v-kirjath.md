# Kiriath (A. V., Kirjath)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjath), kirO-i-ath

## Meanings

- meeting
- joining
- uniting
- building
- city
- town

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Benjamin (Josh. 18:28).

## Metaphysical

A city represents a group of thoughts, or a thought and nerve center, in the 
individual consciousness and body. Kiriath (meaning meeting, uniting, joining, building, city, and being 
a city of Benjamin) signifies a group of attracting, unifying, constructive thoughts whose central and 
dominating idea is an active, accomplishing faith (Benjamin).
