# Lysanias

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ly-sa'-ni-as

## Meanings

- loosening of bonds
- ending of sadness
- driving away sorrow

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Tetrarch of Abilene when John the Baptist began his preaching to prepare the way for Jesus 
Christ (Luke 3:1).

## Metaphysical

The ruling thought in that which Abilene signifies (see ABILENE), at the time of 
one's first, or intellectual (John the Baptist), understanding of the Christ Truth, when one seeks to 
cleanse one's thoughts and habits and to make oneself a fit conscious dwelling place for the Christ. Thus 
Lysanias becomes in one an aid to the ending of sadness and to the driving away of sorrow.
