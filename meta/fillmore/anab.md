# Anab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-nab

## Meanings

- cluster of grapes
- grape town

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:50). It was formerly a city of the Anakim, who were 
overcome by Joshua (Josh. 11:21).

## Metaphysical

A state of thought that magnifies the life idea in man, thus increasing his 
consciousness of life and making union with the one Source of all life. Grapes suggest wine, and wine

symbolizes life; Judah (praise) gives the thought of increase of life. A cluster of grapes bespeaks a 
conjunction, a grouping, or joining of that which grapes suggest. Anab was a city in the hill country of 
Judah, and hills represent high, exalted states of mind.
