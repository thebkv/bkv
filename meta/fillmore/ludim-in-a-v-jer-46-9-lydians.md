# Ludim (in A. V., Jer. 46:9, Lydians)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jer. 46:9, Lydians), la'-dim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Mizraim, of the sons of Ham. b A people who were descended from Ludim (Gen. 
10:13). The Ludim should not be identified with the Lydians, who were descended from Lud the son of 
Shem.

## Metaphysical

Man's material beliefs regarding his origination and the continuation of the race; 
also the expression of these beliefs. Such thoughts belong to the outer, mortal consciousness. (Ludim 
was a grandson of Ham, and Ham, one of Noah's three sons, signifies the physical in man.)
