# Japheth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ja'-pheth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of Noah's three sons (Gen. 5:32).

## Metaphysical

The intellect or reason, the mental realm. To extend without limitation it would 
have to enter into the spiritual. Noah's three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, refer to the spiritual, the 
physical, and the mental in man.
