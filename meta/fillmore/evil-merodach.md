# Evil-merodach

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-vl-me-ro'-dach

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

King of Babylon, son of Nebuchadnezzar (II Kings 25:27).

## Metaphysical

A central, ruling thought in the Babylon state of consciousness in man. This 
thought is foolish in that it looks upon worldly pride, pomp, and power as worthy of one's effort to 
attain; it also believes in outer, limited, error conditions as real. Persons who behold the outer constantly, 
and believe in error seemings, bring about strife and confusion in mind, body, and affairs; they are 
foolish, and grind bitterly, in the end, (See BABYLON, BERODACH-BALLADAN, and 
MERODACH.)
