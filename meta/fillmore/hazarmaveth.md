# Hazarmaveth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha-zSr-ma'-veth

## Meanings

- village of death
- court of death

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Joktan, or a place in Arabia where some of Joktan's descendants settled (Gen. 10:26).

## Metaphysical

A central thought or group of thoughts belonging to the sense mind of man, 
having as its ruling idea a strong belief in death and in that which leads to disintegration, corruption. Its 
conception of justice (court) is always on the negative, condemnatory, and destructive side.
