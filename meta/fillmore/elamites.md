# Elamites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-lam-ltes

## Meanings

- of or pertaining to Elam

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Shem, and inhabitants of the country of Elam, who were settled in Samaria by 
the Assyrian king after he had carried the Israelites away captive (Ezra 4: 9; Acts 2: 9).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts, beliefs, and activities belonging to the Elam consciousness in man, and 
dominated by error views concerning life and Truth. (See ELAM.)
