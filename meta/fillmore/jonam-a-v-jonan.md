# Jonam (A. V., Jonan)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Jonan), jo'-nam

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Eliakim in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:30).

## Metaphysical

The Spirit of love, mercy, and peace becoming known and established in 
consciousness, through Jehovah, I AM.
