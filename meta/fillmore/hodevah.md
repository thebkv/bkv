# Hodevah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ho-de'-vah

## Meanings

- praise of Jehovah
- Jah is honor
- strength of Jehovah
- praise the

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Lord.

A Levite. Seventy-four of his descendants came out of the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 7:43). In 
Ezra 2:40, Hodaviah.

## Metaphysical

Virtually the same as Hodaviah: thoughts and activities of the love consciousness 
as expressed by our natural religious tendencies (Levites), giving praise, honor, and glory to Jehovah 
(the indwelling Christ or I AM), and being blessed and highly esteemed by Jehovah.
