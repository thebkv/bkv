# Kenizzite (in A. V., Genesis 15:19, Kenizzites; Numbers 32:12, Kenezite)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Canaanitish tribe (Gen. 15:19). The Kenizzites are thought to have been a tribe that descended 
from Kenaz, grandson of Esau. Persons from this tribe became closely associated with the Israelites by 
marriage, and we find Jephunneh, the father of Caleb of the Israelitish tribe of Judah, called a Kenizzite 
(Num. 32:12). One of his sons and one of his descendants through Caleb are named Kenaz.

## Metaphysical

A thought springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Kenaz 
represents. (See KENAZ.)
