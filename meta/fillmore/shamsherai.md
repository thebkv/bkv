# Shamsherai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sham'-she-rai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite, son of Jeroham, and a chief man who lived in Jerusalem (I Chron. 8:26).

## Metaphysical

An understanding faith, strong, joyous, fearless, which retains and radiates the 
light of Spirit (keeper of brilliance, hero, heroic, radiance, a Benjamite, Benjamin representing the 
active faith faculty in man).
