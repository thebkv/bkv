# Beth-peor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-pe'-or

## Meanings

- house of opening
- temple ofPeor, i. e., Baal-peor

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Moabitish city that was allotted to the Israelitish tribe of Reuben (Josh. 13:20). This city was 
noted for its worship of Baal-peor. Moses was buried "in the valley in the land of Moab over against 
Beth-peor" (Deut. 34:6).

## Metaphysical

See BAAL-PEOR. Beth-peor represents a corresponding state of consciousness 
(house ofPeor).
