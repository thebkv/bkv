# Diblaim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** d;b'-la-lm

## Meanings

- double cakes
- twin cakes
- two fig cakes
- cakes

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A parent (whether mother or father is not known) of Hosea's wife (Hos. 1:3). The wife of Hosea 
was a prostitute; she was taken by Hosea at the command of Jehovah, to signify the adultery of the 
Jewish nation in departing from Jehovah.
