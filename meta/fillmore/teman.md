# Teman

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te'-man

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Eliphaz who was a son of Esau (Gen. 36:11). b A place in Edom (Jer. 49:7).

## Metaphysical

Teman, like Tema, refers to the realm of the subconsciousness with its inherently 
rich stores of substance and good. Here this realm is under the influence of the Esau-and-Edom phase of 
mind in the individual; in other words, under material thought. (See ESAU and EDOM.)

Temanites (in A. V., Genesis 36:34, Temani), te'-man-ites (fr. Heb.).

Descendants of Teman, or inhabitants of the place named Teman (Gen. 36:34). In Job 2:11 the 
reference is to one of Job's three friends, Eliphaz the Temanite.

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Teman 
signifies. (See ELIPHAZ the Temanite; also TEMAN.)
