# Jokshan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jok'-shan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Second son of Abraham by his wife Keturah (Gen. 25:2).

## Metaphysical

A sly, treacherous, deceitful tendency that often exists in the sense mind of man 
(snarer, fowler, insidious); also the results that follow the activity of this tendency (offensive, hard), and 
the difficult situations in which it places the individual who gives way to it. (See ISHBAK.)
