# Zeri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze'-ri

## Meanings

- running
- flowing
- distilling
- mastic
- balsam
- balm

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Jeduthun, who played the harp in the Temple worship during David's reign (I Chron. 25 
:3). In I Chronicles 25 :1 1, he is called Izri.

## Metaphysical

The very essence of Spirit entering into the consciousness and organism of man as 
a harmonizing, healing, renewing energy and power (running, flowing, distilling, mastic, balsam, balm, 
an Israelitish musician in David's reign). (See IZRI.)
