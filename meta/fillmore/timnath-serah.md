# Timnath - serah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tim-nath - se'-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The same city as Timnath-heres. It was the inheritance of Joshua, in the mountains of Ephraim, 
in the land of Canaan or Palestine (Josh. 19:50).

## Metaphysical

See TIMNATH-HERES.

Timnite, tim-nite (fr. Heb.).

A native of Timnah. Here the reference is to Samson's father-in-law, who lived in Timnah (Judg.

15:6).

Metaphysical. A thought that springs from and belongs to that in consciousness which Timnah 
signifies. (See TIMNAH.)
