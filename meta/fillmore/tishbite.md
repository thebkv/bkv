# Tishbite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A native of the town of Tishbi, or Thisbe, of Naphtali; Elijah the prophet is called a Tishbite (I 
Kings 17:1).

## Metaphysical

Elijah the prophet signifies the spiritual I AM of man's consciousness. This I AM, 
when in activity in one's higher thoughts (Israel), is a Tishbite, one that makes captive, brings into 
subjection to Spirit (appropriating, captivating, leading captive ), and in time brings to the Christ 
standard, all one's thoughts and activities.
