# Hauran

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hau'-ran

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place on the northeast border of the Promised Land (Ezek. 47:16).

## Metaphysical

A state of consciousness in man that belongs to the subconscious realm of mind, 
or has its root in and is sustained by the subconscious mind (holed, caved, cave land). It belongs to the 
seemingly mortal, material, limited, impure, and obscure ( black hole, filthy prison), though it is close to 
the high place in consciousness that Gilead stands for (Ezekiel mentions Hauran with Gilead, as well as 
with Damascus). By holding to the light of Truth that shines upon it from Gilead, this thought can be 
made free from the seemingly material and corruptible (liberty) and established in true spiritual 
understanding and purity ( enlightened , whiteness).
