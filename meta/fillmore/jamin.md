# Jamin

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ja'-min

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One of Simeon's sons (Gen. 46:10). b Son of "Ram the first-bom of Jerahmeel," of the tribe of 
Judah (I Chron. 2:27). C One of those who "caused the people to understand the law," when Ezra read it 
to them (Neh. 8:7).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts pertaining to divine order; also to executive ability, strength, power, 
skill (right hand, right place, dexterity). When such thoughts become active in consciousness they lead 
one to happiness, prosperity, abundant good.
