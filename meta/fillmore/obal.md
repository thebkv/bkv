# Obal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** o'-bal

## Meanings

- extreme attenuation of matter
- uncovered
- stripped
- bare
- naked
- barren

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Joktan (Gen. 10:28). In I Chronicles 1:22 he is called Ebal.

## Metaphysical

The bareness, nakedness, nothingness of all that is not founded in Truth. Those 
who hold to material beliefs and do not enter into understanding and practice of spiritual Truth must 
experience age, decay, deterioration of consciousness and body; they enter into a seemingly very barren 
state of existence. (See EBAL.)
