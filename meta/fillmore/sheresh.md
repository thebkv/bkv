# Sheresh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she'-resh

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Machir, and grandson of Manasseh (I Chron. 7:16).

## Metaphysical

Sheresh, in its highest sense, signifies the fact that the root, the beginning, or 
foundation (base, seat) of all understanding, of all expression and manifestation, is Spirit. In Isaiah 
11:10 this same word is used figuratively of the Messiah, as the "root" of Jesse.
