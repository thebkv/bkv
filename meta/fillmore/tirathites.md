# Tirathites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A family of scribes that lived at Jabez (I Chron. 2:55).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts that stand at the entrance to the inner consciousness (openings, doors, 
gatekeepers) to receive and transcribe upon the memory, the subconscious mind, all ideas relating to that 
which Jabez signifies (a family of scribes that lived in Jabez. See SCRIBES and JABEZ.)
