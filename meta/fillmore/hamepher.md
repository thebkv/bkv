# Hamepher

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hSr'-ne-pher

## Meanings

- snoring
- snorting
- panting
- hard breathing, i. e. , through the

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

nose.

Son of Zophah, of the tribe of Asher (I Chron. 7:36).

## Metaphysical

Difficulty in realizing spiritual inspiration (breathing is symbolical of receiving 
inspiration, while panting is a shortened or labored breathing) because of the prevalence of outer 
activities of the personal and animal in consciousness (this latter is suggested in snoring, snorting).
