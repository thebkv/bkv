# Deuel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** deu'-el

## Meanings

- knowledge of God
- God knoweth
- known of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Eliasaph of the tribe of Gad (Num. 1:14). In Numbers 2:14, he is called Reuel.
