# Abel - meholah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-bel - me-ho'-lah

## Meanings

- meadow of the dance
- dancemeadow

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town that is mentioned in connection with Gideon's victory over the host of Midian (Judg.

7:22; see also I Kings 4:12; 19:16). This town is supposed to have been in the Jordan valley.

## Metaphysical

An aggregation of joyous, harmonious thoughts of substance activity, or of the 
activity of substance.
