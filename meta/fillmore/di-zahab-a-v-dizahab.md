# Di-zahab (A.V., Dizahab)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Dizahab), di'-za-hah

## Meanings

- auriferous
- place rich in gold
- abundance of gold

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

golden.

A place in the wilderness; it was near this place that Moses made one of his talks to the children 
of Israel (Deut. 1:1).

## Metaphysical

A realization of divine wisdom and substance in the wilderness phase of 
consciousness in man.
