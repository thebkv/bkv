# Shammah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sham'-mah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Reuel, one of Esau's sons, and a chief of Edom (Gen. 36: 13). b A son of Jesse and 
brother of David (I Sam. 16:9). Shammah, son of Jesse, is called Shimeah in II Samuel 13:3, and 
Shimea in I Chronicles 2:13. c There were others by this name also (II Sam. 23:11, 25, 33).
