# Ibneiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ib-ne'-iah

## Meanings

- Jehovah will build
- Jah will erect
- Jehovah will construct
- Jah will

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

rebuild.

Son of Jeroham, a Benjamite (I Chron 9:8).

## Metaphysical

The understanding or belief that the I AM, Jehovah, the indwelling Christ, 
established in love (Jeroham, father of Ibneiah, meaning he will cherish, he will love) and an active faith 
(Ibneiah was a Benjamite), works constructively in man's consciousness and will build up his organism 
in full expression or manifestation of spiritual Truth.
