# Pathrusim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** path-ru'-sim

## Meanings

- Egyptians of the upper kingdom

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Mizraim son of Ham, and the inhabitants of Pathros in Egypt (Gen. 10: 14).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the state of consciousness in man that Pathros signifies. 
(See PATHROS.)
