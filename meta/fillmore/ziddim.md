# Ziddim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zid'-dim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A fortified city of Naphtali (Josh. 19: 35).

## Metaphysical

Strength, exalted very highly in the consciousness of the spiritually awakening 
individual, but expressed in a downward, destructive way because of the outer, material beliefs that he 
still holds regarding this important faculty of mind (sides, declivities, steeps, oppressions, destructions, a 
fortified city of Naphtali).
