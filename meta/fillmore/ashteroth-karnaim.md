# Ashteroth-karnaim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ash'-te-roth-kama'-;m

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Bashan (Gen. 14:5), the home of the Rephaim, or giants. Og, king of Bashan, was the 
last of these giants to be overcome.

## Metaphysical

A state of consciousness in man that attributes double honor, authority, exaltation, 
and power to purely intellectual understanding and capacity. In this state of thought man does not 
recognize that God instead of intellect is the source of intelligence. The intellect borrows all its real light 
from Spirit, just as the moon, which has no light of its own, reflects light from the sun. (Ashteroth refers 
to the moon, or intellect, while kamaim-Avo horns or peaks - suggests exaltation and power.)
