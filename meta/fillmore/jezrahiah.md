# Jezrahiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jez-ra-hiO-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An "overseer" of the singers at the dedication of Jerusalem's walls after they had been rebuilt, in 
the time of Nehemiah (Neh. 12:42).

## Metaphysical

The light of Truth shining forth, radiating in and from the redeemed 
consciousness of man (the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls signifies the renewing and spiritualizing of 
man's organism).
