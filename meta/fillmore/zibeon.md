# Zibeon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zib'-e-on

## Meanings

- immersed
- dipped in
- dyed
- tinged
- variegated
- ravenous preyer
- wild

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

robber.

a A Hivite. His granddaughter, Oholibamah, was one of Esau's wives (Gen. 36:2). b A son of 
Seir the Horite, and probably the same person as the former (Gen. 36 :20).

## Metaphysical

Hivites represent thoughts of opposition and resistance in the carnal phase of 
consciousness in unredeemed man. Zibeon (wild robber, ravenous preyer, immersed, dipped in, 
variegated, dyed) symbolizes a wild, lawless sense thought that has the capacity of adapting itself to the 
varying ideas and moods of the individual; thus it remains in his consciousness (until it is cast forth by 
Truth) and robs his body of its energy and substance.
