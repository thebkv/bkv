# Hod

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hod

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Asherite. He is named among those who were "heads of the fathers' houses, choice and 
mighty men of valor, chief of the princes" (I Chron. 7:37, 40).

## Metaphysical

A ruling, conquering thought in the Asher consciousness in man. (See ASHER.) It 
is the lifting up and exalting (majesty, glory, splendor, praise ) of the joy of true spiritual understanding 
(see Prov. 3:13-18).
