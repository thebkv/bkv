# Beth-shittah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** betli-shlt'-tah

## Meanings

- place of acacia
- house of the acacia

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place that is mentioned in the account of Gideon's overthrow and pursuit of the hosts of 
Midian (Judg. 7:22).
