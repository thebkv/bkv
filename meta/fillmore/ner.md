# Ner

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ner

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Grandfather of Saul (I Chron. 8:33). He was the father of Abner, Saul's uncle and captain of his 
host (I Sam. 14:50).

## Metaphysical

Ner, l ik e Abner, signifies the illumined reason or intellect. (See ABNER.)
