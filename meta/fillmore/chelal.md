# Chelal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** che'-lal

## Meanings

- perfection
- completion
- whole
- finished
- crowned

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite who had a foreign wife after the return from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 10:30); 
he is named as one of the sons of Pahath-moab.

## Metaphysical

The perfection or completion that Chelal signifies cannot refer to spiritual 
wholeness and perfection; if it did, he could not have taken a foreign wife— made union with the 
unredeemed affectional soul nature. Chelal must refer more to the rounding out of the outer man 
preparatory to a higher spiritual understanding and attainment. He put away his foreign wife when his 
mistake was revealed to him by Ezra.
