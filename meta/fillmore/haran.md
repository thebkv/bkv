# Haran

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha' -ran

## Meanings

- strong
- elevated
- exalted
- mountaineer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Brother of Abram, and the father of Lot (Gen. 1 1:27). b A place where Abram and his father 
lived after they left Ur of the Chaldees to go into the land of Canaan (Gen. 1 1:31). It was here that 
Terah, Abram's father, died (Gen. 11:32). c A son of Shimei of the Gershonite Levites (I Chron. 23:9).

## Metaphysical

An exalted state of mind, wherein Truth is lifted up in consciousness and the 
individual is strengthened in his determination to go on toward fuller spiritual enlightenment and 
upliftment.
