# Machirites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Machir

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Machir the son of Manasseh (Num. 26:29).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Machir the 
son of Manasseh represents. (See MACHIR.)
