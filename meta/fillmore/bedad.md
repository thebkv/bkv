# Bedad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-dad

## Meanings

- son of Acladl
- separation
- alone

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Hadad, king of Edom (Gen. 36:35)

## Metaphysical

The belief in separation, or apartness, from God that exists in the sense man's 
thought of himself, and especially of his body ( separation , alone; the Edomites were descendants of 
Esau; they pertain to the physical-the outer body consciousness).
