# Carpus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** car’-pus

## Meanings

- harvest, i. e., ingathering of crops
- fruitfulness
- products
- fruits

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A friend of Paul's at Troas, with whom Paul left his cloak and some of his books (II Tim. 4:13).

## Metaphysical

The good effect (fruits) of the word of Truth that has been declared (Paul's work) 
in the Troas consciousness in the individual. (See TROAS.)
