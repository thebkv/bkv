# Adbeel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-be-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ishmael, and the founder of an Arabian tribe (Gen. 25:13).

## Metaphysical

A thought that seemingly springs from the sense or flesh mind, but in reality is 
inspired by Spirit. It longs for God, for something higher and better than it has heretofore experienced 
(yearning for God; languishing for God) . This yearning will bring forth fruit in time, when it has been 
subjected to the necessary education and training (disciplined of ox for, God). Though not always 
recognized as coming from God, this discipline is brought about by the working of the divine law.
