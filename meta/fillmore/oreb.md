# Oreb

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** o'-reb

## Meanings

- jet blackness
- western darkness
- rapacious
- voracious
- ravenous
- raven

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A prince of Midian, and b the name of a rock where this prince was slain by the Ephraimites 
under Gideon (Judg. 7:25)

## Metaphysical

A greedy, rapacious ruling thought in unredeemed man (raven, meaning rapine, 
rapacity; and a prince of Midian, Midian meaning strife, contention). If the individual gives this thought 
a place it robs him of his peace of mind and consciousness of good. It must therefore be overcome by 
denial of the error (Gideon) backed up by the individual will (Ephraimites).
