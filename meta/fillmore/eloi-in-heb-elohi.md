# Eloi (in Heb., Elohi)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Aram
- **Pronunciation:** Elohi), e-lo'-l

## Meanings

- my God
- God
- in Aramaic also a contraction of Elias, or

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Elijah.

Part of the exclamation that Jesus made on the cross, and meaning my God (Mark 15:34). In 
Matthew 27:'16 the word is spelled Eli.

## Metaphysical

The soul in great stress crying out for God.
