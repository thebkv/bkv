# Joram

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jo'-ram

## Meanings

- whom Jehovah has set on high
- Jehovah has exalted
- Jehovah is exalted

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Ahab king of Israel (II Kings 8:25). He was killed by Jehu (II Kings 9:24). b King of 
Judah (II Kings 8:20-24). These kings are also called Jehoram (II Chron. 22:1, 5).

## Metaphysical

See JEHORAM.

The kings, Jehoram of Judah, and Jehoram or Joram of Israel, in their individual characters and 
reigns, were not true to that which their name implies. Seeming evil came to them instead of the good 
that should have been their lot.
