# Hurai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hu'-rai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"Hurai of the brooks of Gaash" was one of David's mighty men (I Chron. 1 1:32). In II Samuel 
23:30 he is called Hiddai.

## Metaphysical

A freeing, ennobling power that works in the spiritual qualities of discrimination, 
intuition, and love, to bring about an orderly interblending of the true thoughts and ideals in 
consciousness (weaver of fine linen, linen worker, whitener, purifier, splendid); thus the individual 
becomes purified and clothed with the garment of light and wholeness. (See Isaiah 52:1; 61:10; see 
HIDDAI.)
