# Horam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ho'-ram

## Meanings

- high
- elevated
- lofty
- exalted
- mountaineer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

King of Gezer, who helped Lachish against the Israelites, but was overcome and destroyed, with 
all his people, by Joshua (Josh. 10:33).

## Metaphysical

The lifting up and exalting (lofty, elevated, high) in consciousness of the sense 
beliefs and activities that Gezer symbolizes. (See GEZER.)
