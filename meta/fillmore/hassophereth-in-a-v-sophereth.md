# Hassophereth (in A.V., Sophereth)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Sophereth), has-soh'-e-reth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A servant of Solomon's, whose descendants returned from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:55). 
He is called Sophereth in Nehemiah 7: 57.

## Metaphysical

That in man which inscribes, engraves, records, or establishes in his memory or 
subconscious mind all past understanding and events as he goes on to new ideas, new thoughts, and new 
experiences.
