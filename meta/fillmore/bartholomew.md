# Bartholomew

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** bar-thol'-o-mew

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the twelve apostles of Jesus Christ (Matt. 10:3).

## Metaphysical

The faculty of imagination. He is called Nathanael, in the 1st chapter of John, 
where Jesus saw him under the fig tree. The inference here is that Jesus discerned Nathanael's presence 
before the latter came into visibility.
