# Hara

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-ra

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the places to which Pul and Tilgath-pilneser, kings of Assyria, carried the Reubenites, 
Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh captive (I Chron. 5:26).

## Metaphysical

Darkness piles up like mountains before the religious thoughts of man (Israelites) 
when he gives himself over to the rule of the intellect guided by selfishness and sense (Assyria). He may 
think that true spiritual knowing and peace are to be attained through intellectual understanding and 
reason, but he is mistaken. True light comes only from Spirit.
