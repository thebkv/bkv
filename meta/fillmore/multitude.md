# multitude

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

The "great multitude" of John 6:5 is composed of our own hungry thoughts. They 
want an influx of the truths of Spirit into our consciousness. Man does not live by bread alone, but by 
every word proceeding out of the mouth of God.
