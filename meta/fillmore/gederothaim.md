# Gederothaim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ged-e-ro-tha'-im

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the lowlands in the allotment of Judah; it is mentioned with Gederah (Josh. 15:36).

## Metaphysical

The Geder and Gederah consciousness doubled in strength. See GEDER and 
GEDERAH.) It is just possible, however, that this may refer rather to the double-minded attitude of man 
toward this Gederah consciousness— to his material concept of it as well as his spiritual understanding of 
it.
