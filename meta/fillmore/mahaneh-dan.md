# Mahaneh-dan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-ha-neh-dan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place just back of Kiriath-jearim, where the Danite warriors camped after they started out to 
takeLaish (Judg. 18:12).

## Metaphysical

The judgment faculty (Dan) in temporary session ( camp of judgment, inclined 
unto judgment) in Kiriath-jearim of Judah, or illumination and praise, preparatory to a further 
establishment of the power of discernment and discrimination (judgment) throughout the consciousness.
