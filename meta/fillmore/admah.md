# Admah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-mah

## Meanings

- dumb
- silent
- unrelenting
- unmerciful
- menacing
- a fortress
- a tomb

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of "the cities of the Plain." Its king, and later the city itself, were destroyed (Gen. 10: 19;

14:2, 8; Deut. 29: 23) .

## Metaphysical

The seeming strength and merciless sureness of the death thought and condition 
that enter into man's experience as the result of his carnal, material, adverse thoughts and activities. (See 
SODOM and GOMORRAH.)
