# Peninnah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe-nin-nah

## Meanings

- pearl
- red coral
- precious stone
- gem
- jewel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the wives of Elkanah the Ephraimite. His other wife was Hannah, mother of Samuel the 
prophet (I Sam. 1:2).

## Metaphysical

The soul lifted up in individual consciousness and unified with the understanding 
that man's inheritance is from God; that while he does not own anything selfishly, yet all that the Father 
has is his (Elkanah). The union of the soul with this understanding makes of the soul, or causes the 
individual to realize that the soul is, a precious jewel (pearl, precious stone); thus there is brought forth 
increase of good in thoughts and ideas (Peninnah had sons and daughters).
