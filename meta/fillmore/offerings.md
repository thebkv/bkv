# offerings

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

The burnt offerings of bullocks and sheep on the altar (I Kings 3:4; II Chron. 
30:24) represent the transmutation of the physical forces to the next higher plane of action. This is a 
process of body refinement to be accomplished by those who follow Jesus in the regeneration.
