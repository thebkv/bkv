# Anamim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** an'-a-mlm

## Meanings

- statues
- rockmen
- fountains

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Mizraim, son of Ham (Gen. 10: 13); his descendants are supposed to have inhabited a 
portion of Egypt. The name Mizraim in the Bible is in meaning identical with Egypt.

## Metaphysical

Hard, material thoughts ( rockmen ) about life (fountains). Material thoughts about 
life aid in building corruptible bodies, mere statues in so far as being truly alive through union with the 
Source of all life is concerned.
