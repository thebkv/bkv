# Anaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-na'-iah

## Meanings

- Jah answers
- Jah has answered
- whom Jehovah answers

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A priest who stood at the right hand of Ezra the scribe when Ezra read the book of the law to 
the people of Israel (Neh. 8:4). b One who joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant (Neh. 10:22).

## Metaphysical

A positive assurance in the consciousness of awakened man that God hears and 
answers when man seeks to understand and obey the divine law (Jah answers Jah has answered).
