# Telassar (in A. V., II Kings 19:12, Thelasar)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** II Kings 19:12, Thelasar), te-las'-sSr

## Meanings

- hill ofAsshur
- Assyrian hill

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Mesopotamia, in which "the children of Eden" lived. (Isa. 37:12).

## Metaphysical

A lifting, exalting, of the sense reasoning or intellectual plane of thought in 
individual consciousness (hill ofAsshur, Assyrian hill, Asshur and Assyria typify the mental, reasoning 
plane in man).
