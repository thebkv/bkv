# Mahalalel (A. V., Mahalaleel)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Mahalaleel), ma-haO-la-lel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Kenan, and father of Jared (Gen. 5:12-17). b A man of Judah (Neh. 11:4).

## Metaphysical

Like Mahalaleel, Mahalalel signifies that in man which praises and blesses and 
glorifies God, the good. It is the higher Judah attitude, the attitude of praise and prayer in expression.
