# Erech

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-rech

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city that Nimrod built in the land of Shinar (Gen. 10: 10).

## Metaphysical

The truth about the natural, inherent wholeness and goodness of man (because of 
his being made in the likeness of God), reduced in his consciousness to material thinking and, because 
of long and extended material thinking, bringing about the disastrous results of error in his body and 
affairs. (See ARCHEVITES.)
