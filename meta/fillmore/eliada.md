# Eliada

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-ll'-a-da

## Meanings

- God knows
- God of knowledge
- power of understanding
- known of

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

God.

a A son of David (II Sam. 5:16); he is called Beeliada in I Chronicles 14:7. b A Benjamite, a 
mighty man of valor in the reign of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah (II Chron. 17:17).

## Metaphysical

Ian's awakening, through the consciousness of love and active faith (David and 
Benjamin), to the understanding that God, Divine Mind, is the source of all knowing, of all knowledge. 
The loving (David) thought in this awakening bespeaks the idea that God cares, as well as knows.
