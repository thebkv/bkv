# Kiriath-sannah (A. V., Kirjath-sannah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjath-sannah), kirO-i-ath-san'-nah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the hill country of Judah. It is the same city as Debir (Josh. 15:49) and Kiriath-sepher.

## Metaphysical

The central idea of the group of thoughts that Kiriath-sannah signifies is 
cleanliness, purity, victory over that which is impure and unclean, over that which is of a divided mind, 
by casting out the seeming error (city of palm branch, city of broom, city of palms, bristling city). (See 
KIRIATH-SEPHER and DEBIR.)
