# Metaphysical

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_addendum

## Metaphysical

An idea is the mode of expression of the mind. Ideas may be thought of as 
offspring or children of the mind. Since all minds are part of the one Divine Mind, ideas are children of 
God.
