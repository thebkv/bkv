# Bath-shua

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bath'-shu-a

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Another translation of Bath-sheba, David's wife and the mother of Solomon I Chron. 3:5).

## Metaphysical

This form of the name Bath-sheba signifies that the fulfilling (accomplishing or 
carrying out in thought and practice) of love in consciousness is directly related to richly prosperous 
conditions in mind and affairs. Owing to the softening of the sounds, Bath-shua takes on a more spiritual 
quality than Bath-sheba. Where sheba signifies an oath of fulfillment, slum represents its 
accomplishment.
