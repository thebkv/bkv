# Adlai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-lai

## Meanings

- justice of God
- Jehovah's justice
- my witness
- lax
- weary

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite; father of Shaphat (I Chron. 27:29) .

## Metaphysical

A just, discerning thought in consciousness, which is based on divine justice 
(justice of God, Jehovah's justice). Lax, weary, suggests a need of a more definite purpose on the part of 
this thought.
