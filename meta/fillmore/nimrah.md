# Nimrah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** nim'-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Gad, on the east of the Jordan (Num. 32:3). In the 36th verse it is called Beth-nimrah.
