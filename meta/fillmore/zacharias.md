# Zacharias

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** zach-a-ri'-as

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of John the Baptist (Fuke 1 :567). Zechariah and Zachariah are the same name.

## Metaphysical

Spiritual consciousness (Jehovah has penetrated, Jehovah has remembered).
