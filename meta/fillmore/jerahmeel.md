# Jerahmeel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-rSh’-me-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Hezron, who was a grandson of Judah (I Chron. 2:9). b Son of Kish, of the tribe of Levi 
(I Chron. 24:29). c "The king's son," one of those who were sent to arrest Baruch and Jeremiah (Jer. 
36:26).

## Metaphysical

The true idea of God as a loving and compassionate father, mother, friend, and 
almighty resource, and not as an angry, revengeful deity. (When the personal man gets hold of this idea 
he is likely to carry it to an extreme by thinking that he can do anything that he wishes to do, even to the 
point of working directly against the divine law, and still reap only good.)
