# Euphrates

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** eu-phra'-te,s

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the four rivers of Eden (Gen. 2: 14). It is a large river in western Asia (II Sam. 8:3, with 
margin; II Kings 23:29; Rev. 16:12).
