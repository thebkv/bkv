# Bithynia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gr
- **Pronunciation:** b;-thyn'-;-a

## Meanings

- violent precipitation

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A province of Asia Minor (I Pet. 1:1). When Paul and Silas "were come over against Mysia, they 
assayed to go into Bithynia; and the Spirit of Jesus suffered them not" (Acts 16:7).

## Metaphysical

Mysia means criminal, and Bithynia means violent precipitation. Under the 
guidance of the Lord the redeeming power of Spirit is not set into action in these dark states of 
consciousness until neighboring thought centers that are more or less awakened to Truth have been 
spiritually strengthened.

This dark state of consciousness that Bithynia represents is characterized by its strong tendency 
to act hastily and impulsively, in a headlong manner, without any forethought, with undue vehemence, 
force, and lack of reverence and respect ( violent precipitation). Peter names the "elect" who are 
sojourners in Bithynia as some of those to whom his first epistle was written. This shows that later the 
Truth must have been preached in that place, or state of consciousness, and must have been accepted by 
some of its inhabitants (thought people) .
