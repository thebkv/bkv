# Jattir

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jat'-tir

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:48).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts (city) belonging to the praise element, or positive pole of life 
(Judah), in man, which is very high, broad, and fruitful because of its insight into and constant eulogy of 
spiritual life and Truth (excellent, preeminent, redundant ).
