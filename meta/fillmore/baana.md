# Baana

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-a-na

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Two men, officers of King Solomon's, who were commissioned to gather victuals for the king's 
household (I Kings 4: 12,16). b Father of Zadok; Zadok worked with Nehemiah in rebuilding the wall of 
Jerusalem after the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 3:4).
