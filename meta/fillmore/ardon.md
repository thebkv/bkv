# Ardon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ar'-don

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Caleb (I Chron. 2:18).

## Metaphysical

A bewildered state of mind. A stirring up of one's thoughts and a somewhat "lost" 
feeling are very apt to follow bold, zealous, impetuous denials of error and declarations of Truth by faith 
(Caleb —faith, bold, impetuous, fearless, father of Ardon). The reason for this is that the individual is 
changing his base. He has become released from certain errors that he had trusted in formerly, but he has 
not yet entered into spiritual understanding and become established in the Christ consciousness, or 
consciousness of Truth. Thus he becomes, for the time being, like a ship without a rudder; he scarcely 
knows what to think or do.
