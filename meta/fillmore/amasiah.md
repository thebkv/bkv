# Amasiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** am-a-sl'-ah

## Meanings

- Jehovah bears
- Jah has strength (to bear)
- whom Jehovah bears

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Zichri, "who willingly offered himself unto Jehovah" (II Chron. 17: 16). He was captain 
over "two hundred thousand mighty men of valor" during Jehoshaphat's reign.

## Metaphysical

Sustaining strength, through the inner Christ (whom Jehovah bears , Jah has 
strength to bear). Amasiah was of the tribe of Judah. By means of praise (Judah) our strength is 
increased and all our inner faculties and powers are greatly increased in consciousness.
