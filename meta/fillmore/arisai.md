# Arisai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Pers
- **Pronunciation:** a-rl'-sai

## Meanings

- arrow ofAriAi,
- arrow ofVishnii
- excellence

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the ten sons of Haman the Agagite; he was hanged with his brothers (Esth. 9:9).

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of Aridai and Aridatha, which see. 
Arisai also bespeaks the swiftness of the arrow and its ability to pierce, to penetrate. These qualities are 
attributed to the errors that this name signifies.

Aristarchus, ar-ls-tar'-ehus (Gk.)~ best ruling; the best ruler; best leader; supreme beginning; 
first principle.

A Christian man from Thessalonica, a city in Macedonia. He was a companion of Paul's on 
missionary journeys, and was Paul's fellow prisoner at Rome (Acts 19:29; Col. 4:10).
