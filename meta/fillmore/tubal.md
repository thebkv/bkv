# Tubal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tu'-bal

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A son of Japheth and grandson of Noah (Gen. 10:2); also b a people descended from him 
(Ezek. 27:13).

## Metaphysical

Noah's three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, represent the threefold being of man: 
the spiritual, the physical, and the mental. Japheth signifies the intellect or reason, the mental realm. 
Tubal, a son of Japheth (meaning diffusibility, flowing forth, welling, flowing copiously, leading, 
conducting, bringing forth, jubilation, triumphal song), bespeaks the expanding possibilities of the 
consciousness of man, with the joy and the good that result from increased understanding. (See 
JAPHETH.)
