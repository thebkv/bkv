# Misham

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ml'-sham

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Elpaal, a Benjamite (I Chron. 8: 12).

## Metaphysical

One of the results of a great increase of the activity of faith in consciousness (a 
son of Elpaal; see ELPAAL). This result is a quick purification from error and an establishment of the 
good and true.
