# Pelonite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- a separation
- a distinction
- definite
- distinct

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Designation of Helez and Ahijah, two of David's warriors and members of his "guard" or 
"council" (I Chron. 11:27, 36). In II Samuel 23:26 Helez is called "the Paltite." Helez the Pelonite was 
the seventh captain for the seventh month, over the Ephraimites (I Chron. 27:10).

## Metaphysical

The thought activities that are symbolized by Helez and Ahijah set apart to do a 
specific work in the consciousness in behalf of the rule of divine love— David (a separation, a 
distinction, a certain one, definite, distinct; see HELEZ and AHIJAH).
