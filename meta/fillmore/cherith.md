# Cherith

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** che'-rith

## Meanings

- cutting
- separating
- dividing
- slaying
- a trench
- a gorge
- a wound

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The brook Cherith, that is before the Jordan" (I Kings 17:3); a place in Palestine— a brook, or 
river, where Elijah hid in obedience to the word of Jehovah, and was fed by ravens.

## Metaphysical

Subjective life currents in individual consciousness.
