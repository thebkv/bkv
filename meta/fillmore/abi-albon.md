# Abi-albon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-bl-al'-bon

## Meanings

- Jather of strength
- father of valiance
- father of prevailing

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of David's mighty men (II Sam. 23:31); he is called Abiel in I Chronicles 1 1:32.

## Metaphysical

A mighty thought of conquest.
