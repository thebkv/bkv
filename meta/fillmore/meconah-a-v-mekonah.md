# Meconah (A.V., Mekonah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Mekonah), me-coO-nah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Judah to which some of the Israelites returned after the Babylonian captivity (Neh.

11:28).

## Metaphysical

An established faith in God. This is a firm, steady, secure place in consciousness, 
an inner assurance on which the individual can stand and rest (foundation, base, or standing place).
