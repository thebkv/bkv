# Shubael

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shuO-bCE-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Levite, son of Amram and father of Jehdeiah (I Chron. 24:20). In 
Chronicles 23:16 the name is Shebuel. b Another Levite, a singer in the house of God in David's reign (I 
Chron. 25:20).
