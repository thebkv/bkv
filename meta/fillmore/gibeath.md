# Gibeath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** g;b'-e-ath

## Meanings

- heights
- hills
- eminences
- distinctions

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town of Benjamin (Josh. 18:28). Gibeath is the plural form of the word Gibeah.

## Metaphysical

See GIBEAH.

Gibeathite. glb'-e-ath-lte (fr. Heb.)~o/or belonging to Gibeah.

A native or inhabitant of Gibeah; "the sons of Shemaah the Gibeathite" (I Chron. 12:3).

Metaphysical. A thought belonging to the Gibeah consciousness. (See GIBEAH.)
