# Eliel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-ll'-el

## Meanings

- my God is God
- God of gods
- power of God
- exaltation God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of several Israelitish men (I Chron 5:24; 6:34; 8:20, 22; 11:46, 47; 12:11; 15:9; II 
Chron. 31:13).

## Metaphysical

The strength, the power, the almightiness of God.
