# Ahzai (A. V., Ahasai)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Ahasai), ah'-zai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A priest of Israel (Neh. 1 1 : 13); a form of the name Ahaziah.

## Metaphysical

The Israelitish priests represent our so-called "natural religious tendencies." Some 
of these may be spiritual, and others may be simply of the intellect. Ahzai signifies just what the name 
means— that Jehovah upholds, sustains, and protects the true religious nature of man as it is unfolding 
into spiritual consciousness. (See AHAZIAH.)
