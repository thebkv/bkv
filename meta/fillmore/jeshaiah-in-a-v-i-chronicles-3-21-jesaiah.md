# Jeshaiah (in A. V., I Chronicles 3:21, Jesaiah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Chronicles 3:21, Jesaiah), je-sha'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There were several Israelites by this name (I Chron. 3:21; 25:3, 15; 26:25; Ezra 8:7, 19).

## Metaphysical

This name is virtually the same as Isaiah, and its symbology is the same: the 
higher self, Jehovah delivers. Spiritual understanding in us discerns the reality, or the real character, of 
spiritual man, I AM, Jehovah, and receives divine help in our daily overcoming.
