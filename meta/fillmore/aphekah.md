# Aphekah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-phe'-kah

## Meanings

- the fortress
- the strong city

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Judah (Josh. 15:53). It is thought to be the same place as the Aphek of Joshua 12:18.
