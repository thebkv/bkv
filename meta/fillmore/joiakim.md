# Joiakim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** joi'-a-kim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jeshua, a high priest who returned from Babylon with Zerubbabel (Neh. 12:10). Joiakim 
is a contraction of the name Jehoiakim.

## Metaphysical

A ruling thought in man's religious or spiritual consciousness (high priest) that 
exalts Spirit, that perceives the possibility of establishing God in individual consciousness, and that 
works toward that end. (See JEHOIAKIM.)
