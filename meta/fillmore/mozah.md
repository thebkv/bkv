# Mozah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mo'-zah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Benjamin (Josh. 18:26).

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of Moza: the issuing forth of the 
waters of life, or thoughts of life and Truth, into the outer consciousness, the result of an active faith 
(Benjamin) that the one Source of all life (springhead) is the life of the outer man as well as of the inner 
man.
