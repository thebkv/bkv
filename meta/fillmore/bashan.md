# Bashan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-shan

## Meanings

- light, sandy soil
- smooth, fertile soil
- fruitful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A district east of Jordan, the land of Og, who was the Amoritish king (Deut. 3:1-11). It was noted 
for its fruitfulness and its herds (Deut. 32:14).

## Metaphysical

Life and substance, inexhaustible and limitless (smooth, fertile soil, fruitful) when 
the animal forces (herds) are properly directed and handled by the understanding (half tribe of Manasseh 
to whom the land of Bashan was assigned).
