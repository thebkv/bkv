# Jemuel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jem'-u-el

## Meanings

- God is light
- day of God
- infinite and universal luminous manifestation

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

of God.

Son of Simeon (Gen. 46:10). In Numbers 26:12 and I Chronicles 4:24 the name is Nemuel.

## Metaphysical

Jemuel (God is light, day of God) symbolizes the time in individual unfoldment 
when the light of Truth is accepted into consciousness and realized. The individual thus enters spiritual 
understanding, a comprehension of the light that is God (infinite and universal luminous manifestation 
of God). This comes about by the listening, attentive, obedient attitude of mind that Simeon, father of 
Jemuel, signifies.
