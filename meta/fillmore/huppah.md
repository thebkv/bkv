# Huppah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hup'-pah

## Meanings

- covering
- protection
- veil
- canopy
- curtain
- covert
- sanctuary

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A priest in David's reign, on whom the lot fell for the thirteenth course in the Temple service (I 
Chron. 24:13).

## Metaphysical

The spiritual clothing (a covering) and defense, refuge, preservation, protection, 
that love (David) and true religious ideals (priests) are to the consciousness of man.
