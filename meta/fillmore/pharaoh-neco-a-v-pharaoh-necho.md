# Pharaoh-neco (A.V., Pharaoh-necho)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Pharaoh-necho), pha'-raoh-ne'-co

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The same person as Neco, a king of Egypt when Josiah was king of Judah. Josiah was killed in 
waging battle against this king (Jer. 46:2).

See PHARAOH and NECO.
