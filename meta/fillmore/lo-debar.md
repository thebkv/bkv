# Lo-debar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** lo-de'-bSr

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Gilead to the east of the Jordan; it was the home of Machir the son of Ammiel. Machir 
cared for Jonathan's lame son, Mephibosheth, until David took him into his own house (II Sam. 9:4, 5; 
17:27).

## Metaphysical

The disorderly, undisciplined, barren, substanceless (without pasture), and 
unillumined (not the word or oracle ) state into which the consciousness of man comes when the 
personal will (Saul) has been exercised without restraint. (See MEPHIBOSHETH and MACHIR.)
