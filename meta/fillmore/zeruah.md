# Zeruah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze-ru. '-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A widow, the mother of Jeroboam (I Kings 1 1 :26).

## Metaphysical

A phase of the soul consciousness in man that is unclean and impure in thought 
and desire (leprous); also the troublous, fearful, stinging, error experiences that are the result of this 
condition of the soul ( heavily smitten, stricken, hornets, terror).
