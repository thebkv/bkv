# Zuriel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zu'-r;-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Abihail, and a prince of the Merari Levites, in the wilderness (Num. 3:35)

## Metaphysical

A realization of God, the indwelling Spirit of truth, as one's firm, unwavering 
faith, as one's steadfastness and defense (God is my rock, God is a refuge, strength of God; a Levite).
