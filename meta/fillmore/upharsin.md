# upharsin

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u-phSr'-sin

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the words of the handwriting that appeared on the wall during Belshazzar's feast (Dan.

5:25).

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of PERES. See PERES; see also the 
other words belonging to this handwriting on the wall: MENE and TEKEL

## Preserved OCR Source

upharsin, u-phSr'-sin (Heb.)— and shall be divided; and they divide; and shall be broken in 
fragments; and shall be sundered. It is a significant fact that par si, by a simple vocal change without any 
change in characters, means a Persian. So it seems that not only what was to take place, but also the 
force destined to accomplish it, is contained in the word UPHARSIN
