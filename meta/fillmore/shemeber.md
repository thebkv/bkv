# Shemeber

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shem-e'-ber

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

King of Zeboiim (Gen. 14:2).

## Metaphysical

The innate spiritual ideal, implanted in man from the beginning, that causes him to 
grow, unfold, and unceasingly to desire and seek to attain higher and better understanding and 
expression (superior brilliance, superior name, striving upward to the height). The fact that Shemeber 
was king of Zeboiim shows that the perfect-man idea of God is implanted in the seemingly physical 
being of man as well as in his more inner and spiritual consciousness. Because of ignorance, however, 
the spiritual impetus within him is misunderstood by the sense mind in man, and to the extent that sense 
mind rules an individual the inner urge of Spirit apparently serves only to. stimulate sense activity in 
him. (See ZEBOIIM.)
