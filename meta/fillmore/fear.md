# fear

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

The word fear as used in I Sam. 12:24 implies reverence or respect for the law of 
God. "All your heart" represents the whole of the inner consciousness or the inner source of life, which 
should be dedicated to the Lord.
