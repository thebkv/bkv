# Tebeth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te'-beth

## Meanings

- goodness
- returning good
- good

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Tenth month of the Jewish sacred year and the fourth month of their civil year, beginning with 
the new moon in January. It was the month in which Esther was taken to King Ahasuerus (Esth. 2:16).

## Metaphysical

A belief in divine, omnipresent goodness, in the allness of good, even when the 
seemingly cold, blighting, barren experience of winter is being felt in consciousness.
