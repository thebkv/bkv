# Nebuzaradan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** neb'-u-zSr-a'-dan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The captain of the guard, a servant of the king of Babylon" (II Kings 25:8; Jer. 39:9-13).

## Metaphysical

A thought activity that is the fruit of the purely mental, or soul, discernment and 
understanding that Nebo, the Babylonian god of learning, represents. (See NEBO.)

## Preserved OCR Source
