# Hilen

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hiO-len

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Levitical city of Judah (I Chron. 6:58). It is called Holon in Joshua 21:15.

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts in individual consciousness that is strongly fortified (strong 
place, fortress) in goodness and Truth (good place, place of virtue) and in praise and prayer (Judah). 
These thoughts are given over to the Levites (one's natural religious and spiritual tendencies).
