# Hasadiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** has-a-dl'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Zerubbabel, of the royal line of David (I Chron. 3:20).

## Metaphysical

Compassion, forgiveness, love, as characteristic of Jehovah, the Lord, or law of

Being.
