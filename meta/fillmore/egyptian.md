# Egyptian

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** e-gp'-tian

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Natives or inhabitants of Egypt; descendants of Mizraim, son of Ham (I Sam. 30: 1 1 !.

## Metaphysical

Egyptians signify sense thoughts, or thoughts that pertain to the subjective 
consciousness in its unawakened state. They belong to the Egypt consciousness. (See EGYPT.)

Ehi, e'-hi (Heb.) union; united; unity; brother; brotherly; friend; friend of Jehovah.

Son of Benjamin. In other places he is called Ahiram, Aher, and Aharah (Gen. 46:21).

Metaphysical. See AHIRAM, AHER, AHARAH, and add to the suggestions applied to them the 
thought of unity— conscious oneness with God and with mankind; thus you will get the import of Ehi.
