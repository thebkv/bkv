# Jezreel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jez'-re-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A town in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:56). b A border town of the "children of Issachar" 
(Josh. 19:18). c A Judahite, son of the "father" or founder of Etam (I Chron. 4:3). d Son of the prophet 
Hosea by his wife Gomer (Hos. 1:4). e A valley (Hos. 1:5).

## Metaphysical

Words of Truth (seed o~ God ) brought into man's conscious, thinking mind, or 
brought to his attention, by the inspiration of the Holy Spirit within him ( God scatters, whom God 
sows).
