# Bocheru

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** boch'-e-ru.

## Meanings

- the firstborn is he
- he is youthful
- he is early
- he is in season

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the six sons of Azel, a Benjamite descended from Saul (I Chron. 8: 38) .

## Metaphysical

The tendency to receive new ideas, Truth, in order, in season; the reference here 
may be to the Christ idea (the first-born is he, he is youthful, he is early, he is in season ).
