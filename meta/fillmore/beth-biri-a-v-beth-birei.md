# Beth-biri (A. V., Beth-birei)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Beth-birei), bethblr'-l

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Simeon, in the land of Canaan (I Chron. 4:31).

## Metaphysical

The truth that man's consciousness and body, established in substance, are the 
product of his own inner, true I AM (house of my creation, house of my begetting, house of fatness); also 
the truth that man's consciousness and body constitute the house, or temple, in which his Christ self, 
Jehovah God, or true I AM dwells, and through which it expresses (house of my coming forth, house of 
my Creator). Man comes into an understanding of this truth by hearing and obeying the divine law of 
Being as revealed by the Spirit of truth within him. (Simeon is one who hears and obeys; hearing is 
another word for receptivity, or a listening attitude of mind.)
