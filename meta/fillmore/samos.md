# Samos

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** sa'-mos

## Meanings

- height
- distinguished
- prominent

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An island in the ®gean Sea, near the coast of Lydia. The boat in which Paul traveled on his way 
to Jerusalem from one of his missionary trips "touched at Samos" (Acts 20: 15).

## Metaphysical

A high, noble state of thought (height, distinguished; see SAMOTHRACE).
