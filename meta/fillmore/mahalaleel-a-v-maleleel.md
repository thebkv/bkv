# Mahalaleel (A. V., Maleleel)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Maleleel), ma-ha'la-le-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Jared and son of Cainan, named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:37).

## Metaphysical

Man expressing his innate tendency to praise God, to hold to the light, to the 
good. To exalt God in consciousness is very uplifting to the individual.
