# Eleazar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-le-a'-z r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Aaron. He succeeded his father as high priest of Israel (Ex. 6:23; 20:25, 28). b There are 
several other Israelitish men by this name (I Sam. 7:1; II Sam. 23:9; I Chron. 23:21; Ezra 8:33; Neh. 
12:42; Matt. 1:15).

## Metaphysical

Spiritual strength through the individual's recognition of God as his supporting, 
sustaining power ( God is helper). This becomes the presiding, directive faculty of a new state of 
consciousness. (See AARON.)
