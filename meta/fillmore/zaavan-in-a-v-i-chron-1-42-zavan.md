# Zaavan (in A. V., I Chron. 1:42, Zavan)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Chron. 1:42, Zavan), za'-a-van

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ezer. Ezer was a Horite chief and a son of Seir (Gen. 36 :27).

## Metaphysical

A confused, fearful, unstable thought tendency in the outer or body consciousness 
in the individual (disquieted, disturbed, trembling- a man of Seir, or Edom).
