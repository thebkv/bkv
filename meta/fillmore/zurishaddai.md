# Zurishaddai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zu-ri-shad'-dai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Shelumiel, who was the prince chosen from the tribe of Simeon to stand with Moses 
and Aaron in numbering the Children of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai (Num. 1:6).

## Metaphysical

Like Zuriel, the knowledge or realization of God as unchanging principle, as one's 
staying power, defense, the 
ZUZIM 706

sure foundation of one's abiding perfection and good ( the Almighty is a rock, my rock is the Almighty).
