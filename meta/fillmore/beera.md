# Beera

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be-e'-ra, Beerah

## Meanings

- well
- fountain
- expounder
- declarer
- explainer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Zophah, an Asherite (I Chron. 7:37). b A Reubenite who was carried away captive by 
Tilgathpilneser, king of Assyria (I Chron. 5:6); the name here is Beerah.

## Metaphysical

That in us which interprets the truth about life ( expounder ; a well) and makes this 
truth clear to our consciousness.
