# Adoni-zedek

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-don'-;-ze'-dek

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Canaanitish or Amoritish king of Jerusalem. He was defeated and slain by the Israelites under 
Joshua, when they began to take possession of the Promised Land (Josh. 10: 1-27).

## Metaphysical

The sensual or carnal idea of what is just and right having dominion over the life 
forces of the individual and ruling according to carnal affections and desires. Adoni-zedek cannot 
represent spiritual justice and righteousness, since he was not an Israelite but was an enemy to the 
Israelites. Though he was king of Jerusalem— the spiritual center in consciousness— he was an Amorite, 
and the Amorites symbolize carnal, sensual thoughts and desires.
