# Mibhar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mibO-hSr

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Hagri, and one of David's mighty men (I Chron. 1 1:38).

## Metaphysical

A new, high, discriminating,

MICA

superior thought in consciousness (approved, chosen, selected, best); a thought of the vitality and vigor 
of youth, that allies itself to the ruling David, or love faculty, and becomes one of its strong supporters.
