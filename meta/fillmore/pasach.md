# Pasach

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pa'-sach

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Japhlet and descendant of Asher (I Chron. 7:33).

## Metaphysical

A deeply analytical and positive tendency of thought (cutting up, considering 
decisively, dividing), but more from a human or intellectual than from a spiritual standpoint. The result 
therefore is not a perfect deduction, or perfect understanding. (Halting, limping, a lameness in walking, 
signifies some kind of flaw in the phase of one's understanding that comes into contact with the outer 
conditions of life.)
