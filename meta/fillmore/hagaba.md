# Hagaba

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hag'-a-ba

## Meanings

- a locust

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His descendants were of the Nethinim who returned from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 7:48). 
The name is spelled Hagabah in Ezra 2:45.

## Metaphysical

This name signifies, like Hagab, a thought of service to God, in man's religious 
consciousness, yet with a destructive tendency. (The Nethinim were servants in the Temple.)

Hagar, ha'-gSr (Heb. (--flight; to flee one's country; fugitive; wanderer; stranger.

Sarai's handmaid, the mother of Ishmael by Abraham (Gen. 16:1-16).

Metaphysical. The natural soul. It is a stranger to the awakened spiritual phase of the soul, in 
that its thoughts and emotions are sensual and are likely to be selfish and unholy, thus producing fear 
and uncertainty (wanderer). The sensual must give way to the spiritual; it cannot stand in the presence of 
the Christ truth, but flees (flight). "Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are children of promise. But as then 
he that was born after the flesh persecuted him that was born after the Spirit, so also it is now. Howbeit 
what saith the scripture? Cast out the handmaid and her son: for the son of the handmaid shall not inherit 
with the son of the freewoman. Wherefore, brethren, we are not children of a handmaid, but of the 
freewoman" (Gal. 4:28-31). (See also verses 21 to 27 of Galatians 4, and compare the significance of 
Hagar and Ishmael with that of Sarah and Isaac.)
