# Eliphal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el'-i-phal

## Meanings

- whom God judges
- judgment of God
- God is my judge

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ur (I Chron. 1 1:35). He was one of David's guard of valiant men, and is called Eliphelet 
the son of Ahasbai, in II Samuel 23:34.

## Metaphysical

The recognition by love (David) that God is the true judge of His people-that true 
judgment, discernment, discrimination, and justice are spiritual and lead to salvation always. (See 
ELIPHELET.)
