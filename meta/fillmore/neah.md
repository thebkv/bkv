# Neah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ne'-ah

## Meanings

- moving
- shaking
- trembling
- agitating
- staggering

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

NEBAIOTH

waving; wandering; disturbing; sifting; shaking down; settling.

A city of Zebulun, on the northeast border (Josh. 19: 13).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts of a wandering, changeable, uncertain, unsettled, fearful 
tendency (shaking, moving, trembling, agitating, disturbing) coming under the dominion of the faculty 
of order (a city allotted to Zebulun, Zebulun representing the order faculty in man), that it may be made 
firm, steady, reliable, and become established (shaking down, settling).
