# Rameses

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Egypt where Joseph placed his father and his brothers, and gave them possessions 
(Gen. 47:11). It was a city in the land of Goshen, and the name was sometimes given to the whole 
province.
