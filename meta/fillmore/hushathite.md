# Hushathite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- name for the dwellers in Hushah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Sibbecai the Hushathite was one of David's valiant men (II Sam. 21:18). He was appointed 
captain over twenty-four thousand who served King David in the eighth month of each year (I Chron.

27:11). In II Samuel 23:27 he is called Mebunnai; at least it is supposed that these two men are the 
same.

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to and springing from the Hushah thought and attitude of 
mind. (See HUSHAH.)
