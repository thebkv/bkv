# Jechiliah (A. V., Jecoliah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jecoliah), jech-i-li-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Wife of Amaziah and mother of Uzziah, kings of Judah (II Chron. 26:3).

## Metaphysical

The soul established in divine strength, in the firm conviction that enables one to 
say, "I can do all things in him that strengtheneth me" (Phil. 4:13).

Jechoniah (A. V., Jechonias; the Greek form), jech-o-ni-ah (Heb f—whom Jehovah has 
appointed; whom Jah has set upright; established by Jehovah; stability of Jah; Jehovah's creation.

Son of Josiah, at the time of the carrying away of the Israelites to Babylon (Matt. 1:11).

Metaphysical. The same as Jeconiah and Jehoiachin. (See JEHOIACHIN.)
