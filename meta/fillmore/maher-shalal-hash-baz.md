# Maher-shalal-hash-baz

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-her-shalO-al-hash'-baz

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A name symbolical of the very short time that was to elapse before Damascus and Samaria 
would be taken captive by the king of Assyria. This name was given by Isaiah to his son, symbolizing 
the fact that before the child was old enough to call his parents Father and Mother the spoiling of 
Samaria and Damascus would take place (Isa. 8:1).

## Metaphysical

The rapidity with which the law of cause and effect works. The two phases of the 
law are virtually one, certain causes being followed almost immediately by their corresponding effects, 
or results.
