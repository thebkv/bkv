# Mearah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me-a'-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place at the north of Palestine belonging to the Sidonians (Josh. 13:4). It was a portion of the 
"very much land" that remained to be possessed by the Israelites (see Josh. 13:1-7).

## Metaphysical

A deep-seated, subconscious belief in lack of both understanding and substance 
(cave, excavation, without sight, blind, making empty).
