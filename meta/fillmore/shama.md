# Shama

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sha'-mCE

## Meanings

- hearing
- hearkening
- obedient
- dutiful
- understanding

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Hotham the Aroerite, and one of David's mighty men (I Chron. 1 1 :44).

## Metaphysical

An obedient attitude of mind, one attentive to the higher things of Spirit 
(obedient, hearing, dutiful, an Israelite).
