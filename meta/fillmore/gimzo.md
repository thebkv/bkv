# Gimzo

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** g,lm'-zo

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the south part of the lowland of Judah. The Philistines took this city from Judah and 
lived in it (II Chron. 28:18).

## Metaphysical

Sense shrewdness, subtleness, and wisdom (one acute-minded sagacious; this city 
reverted to the Philistines). Jesus said in one of His parables: "The sons of this world are for their own 
generation wiser than the sons of the light" (Luke 16:8).
