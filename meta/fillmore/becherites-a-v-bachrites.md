# Becherites (A. V., Bachrites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Bachrites), be'eher-ltes

## Meanings

- belonging to Becher

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Becher, or Bered, son of Ephraim, was the head of the family of the Becherites (Num. 26:35). 
Metaphysical. Thoughts belonging to the Becher consciousness. (See BECHER.)
