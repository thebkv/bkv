# Sharai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sha'-rai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite. He had taken a foreign wife after the return from the Babylonian captivity, but gave 
her up at the command of Ezra (Ezra 10:40).

## Metaphysical

An awakening to understanding that from the inner Christ comes the solution to 
all life's problems and deliverance from all error (Jah is deliverer, beginning, i. e., opening up, solution, 
an Israelite).
