# Beth-marcaboth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-mar'-ea-both

## Meanings

- house of chariots
- place of chariots

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in Canaan that was allotted to Simeon (Josh. 19:5). It is thought to have been a stopping- 
place used in the chariot trade between Jerusalem and Egypt.

## Metaphysical

A place in consciousness where the body activities meet, and give themselves 
over to the guiding, directing light of Spirit (house or place of chariots). Chariots represent the body 
activities, and Simeon, to whom this city of Beth-marcaboth was allotted, means one who listens and 
obeys.
