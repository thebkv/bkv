# Darkon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** dSr'-kon

## Meanings

- hearer
- hastener
- scatterer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His "children" returned with Zerubbabel from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:56).

## Metaphysical

A thought that supports and maintains the real and the true in consciousness; this 
thought also tends to dispel and dissipate the confused and darkened beliefs and thoughts of the 
Babylonian state of mind.
