# Bel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bel

## Meanings

- lord

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The Aramaic form of Baal (Isa. 46.1). Bel is mentioned in connection with Babylon, and refers 
to the Babylonish god (Jer. 50:2; 51:44). It is not certain whether it was the planet Jupiter or the sun that 
was worshiped under the name of Bel. Bel, however, is virtually the same name as Baal.
