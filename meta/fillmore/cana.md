# Cana

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ca'-na

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town in Galilee where Jesus performed His first miracle by turning water into wine at the 
marriage feast (John 2: 1-11) .

## Metaphysical

Cana means place of reeds, reed, measuring rod, rule, balance. In the body it is 
symbolized by the larynx. Cana of Galilee is the power center in consciousness. The larynx originates 
and measures the volume and character of the sounds of the voice. Galilee (circle, a circuit, rolling 
energy) furnishes the vibratory force that acts through the place of reeds.
