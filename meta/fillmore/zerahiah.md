# Zerahiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zer-a-hi'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A priest who was descended from Aaron, through Eleazar, and was an ancestor of Ezra (Ezra 
7:4). b Father of Eliehoenai "of the sons of Pahath-moab." Descendants of his returned from the 
Babylonian captivity with Ezra (Ezra 8 :4).

## Metaphysical

The individual awakening to the 
ZERERAH

conscious recognition of his own indwelling Christ or I AM understanding and might (Jehovah has 
arisen, Jah shines forth with glory, Jah is appearing, brilliance of Jehovah, birth of Jehovah), and 
expressing this light and power constructively.

Zerahites (A. V., Zarhites), ze'-rah-ites (fr. Heb.).

Descendants of Zerah, son of Simeon (Num 26:13).

Metaphysical. Thoughts and activities springing from and in character like that in consciousness 
which is signified by Zerah, son of Simeon. (See ZERAH.)
