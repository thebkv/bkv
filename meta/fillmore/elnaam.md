# Elnaam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el'-na-am

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Jeribai and Joshaviah, two of David's mighty warriors (I Chron. 1 1:46).

## Metaphysical

The spiritual joy, satisfaction, delight, and fair-minded judgment that adhere to the 
will ruling in love (David). These qualities of mind aid in supporting and protecting the ruling love idea 
in consciousness and in causing it to be victorious over opposing sense thoughts and activities.

Elnathan, el-na'-than {Heb.)— bestowed of God; God gives; given of God; God's gift, i.e., 
conscience that God bestows.

a Grandfather of Jehoiachin, king of Judah, and son of Achbor (II Kings 24:8). He "made 
intercession to the king that he would not bum the roll" that contained Jeremiah's prophecies, but the 
king would not listen to him (Jer. 36:12, 25). b There were other Israelitish men by this name also (Ezra 
8: 16).

Metaphysical. The conscience that God bestows- the inner intuition, discrimination, and sense of 
judgment that are given of God to man to enable him to know the right course to pursue and also to 
know the result of willfulness and disobedience.
