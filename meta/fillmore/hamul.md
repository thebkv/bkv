# Hamul

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-mul

## Meanings

- spared
- pitied
- mildness
- gentleness
- compassion
- sympathy
- mercy

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Perez and grandson of Judah, by Tamar (Gen. 46:12).

## Metaphysical

Compassion, mercy, and forgiveness. These attitudes of mind are Godlike, and 
they pertain to the salvation of the individual who entertains them.
