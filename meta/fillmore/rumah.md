# Rumah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ru-mah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The home of Pedaiah, father of Zebidah. This Zebidah was the mother of Jehoiakim, king of 
Judah (II Kings 23:36).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts in the Israel consciousness of man; they are of a lofty, 
sublime nature (height, sublimity, exaltation). To the extent that personality enters in, haughtiness and 
pride result from them.
