# Pelaliah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pel-a-li'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelitish priest, son of Amzi (Neh. 11:12).

## Metaphysical

The significance of this name is that true judgment, inspiration, and prayer are 
spiritual, are of Jehovah, the Christ (Jah judges; Jah is thinker; Jah intercedes ). By earnest desire 
( entreating

the Lord ) and by thinking on Jehovah, man becomes spiritually discerning, discriminative, and divinely 
just in his judgment.
