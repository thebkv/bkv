# Misgab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** misO-gab

## Meanings

- height
- altitude
- a high rock
- refuge
- security
- an inaccessible place

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in Moab that was to be "put to shame and broken down," according to Jeremiah 48:1.

## Metaphysical

The carnal mind, or limited, personal man (Moab), exalting itself and seeking to 
justify and fortify itself in its human and sense limitations.
