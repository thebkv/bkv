# Sithri (A. V., Zithri)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Zithri), sith'-ri

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Uzziel, and grandson of Kohath, a son of Levi (Exod. 6:22).

## Metaphysical

A thought of divine care and protection (Jehovah conceals, protection of 
Jehovah). "Hide me under the shadow of thy wings" (Psalms 17:8; 91:4).
