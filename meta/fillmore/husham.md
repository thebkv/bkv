# Husham

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hu'-sham

## Meanings

- hasting
- quick
- fleeting
- vehement
- passionate
- ardorous

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A king of Edom. He was "of the land of the Temanites" (Gen. 36:34).

## Metaphysical

The hurried attitude of mind, relating to physical desire. This belongs to the outer 
consciousness and tends to confusion and disorder.
