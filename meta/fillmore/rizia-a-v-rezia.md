# Rizia (A. V., Rezia)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Rezia), ri-zi-'CE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ulla, of the tribe of Asher (I Chron. 7:39).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of Joy, satisfaction, because of fulfilled desire. The definitions of 
Rizia make us think of Psalms 37:4:

"Delight thyself also in Jehovah;

And he will give thee the desires of thy heart."
