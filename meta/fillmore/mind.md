# Mind

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

By the term Mind, we mean God-- the universal principle of causation, which 
includes all principles. Mathematics is a principle that in its field illustrates the exact and inexorable 
laws that are one with the manifestations of God, or Mind.
