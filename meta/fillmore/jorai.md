# Jorai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jo'-rai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A chief man of Gad (I Chron. 5:13).

## Metaphysical

A central thought of the power faculty (Gad) in individual consciousness, 
receiving spiritual inspiration, or spiritually inspired and guided (whom Jehovah teaches, directed by 
Jehovah).
