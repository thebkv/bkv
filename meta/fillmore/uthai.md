# Uthai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u'-thai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Ammihud, descended from Perez, son of Judah (I Chron. 9:4). b Son of Bigvai, who 
returned from the Babylonian captivity with Ezra (Ezra 8:4).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts of divine helpfulness, of divine aid in all the affairs of one's daily life 
(whom Jehovah sustains, Jah is help, comfort ofJah, Jehovah succors). The significance of this name is 
quite clearly revealed in Psalms 46:1 and in Philippians 4:13: "God is our refuge and strength, a very 
present help in trouble." "I can do all things in him that strengtheneth me." Divine assistance is realized 
by words of Truth declared in I AM, or Jehovah
