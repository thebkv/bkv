# Amariah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** am-a-rl'-ah

## Meanings

- Jah says
- word of Jehovah
- Jehovah enlightens

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Aaron, by his eldest son, Eleazar. He lived during the time that Eli and his sons 
were in possession of the priesthood, before it reverted to the rightful line of high priests— the 
descendants of Aaron through his eldest son (I Chron. 6:7, 52) . There are others by this name also.

## Metaphysical

An inner assurance of sonship, of oneness with God and dependence upon His 
word (Jah says, word of Jehovah), which stays with one when one seems incapable of outer spiritual 
expression for the time being; it is the light and assurance of the inner Word with its creative power to 
bring forth fruit in due season.
