# Ammizabad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** am-miz'-a-bad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Benaiah, who was a mighty man during David's reign (I Chron. "7:6).

## Metaphysical

The idea of God as the source of all, and man as the receiver and expresser of the 
faculties and powers of Being (people of endowment, my people is endowed, kindled of the giver).
