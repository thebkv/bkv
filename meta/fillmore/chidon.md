# Chidon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ehl'-don

## Meanings

- javelin
- spear
- calamity
- war
- destruction
- ruin

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

At "the threshing-floor of Chidon" Uzza put out his hand to steady the Ark and was killed (I 
Chron. 13:9); in II Samuel 6:6 it is called "the threshing floor of Nacon."
