# Jutah (A. V., Juttah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Juttah), ju'-tah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:55).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts (city) in the higher praise and prayer consciousness of man 
(the hill country of Judah) turning away (turned away, repulsed) from set forms and creeds of religious 
belief, that are binding and limiting to the consciousness, and reaching out, broadening out (extended), 
to a more truly spiritual understanding and standard of thought and act.
