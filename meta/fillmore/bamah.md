# Bamah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-mah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A word that is used figuratively by Ezekiel as the name of places in which Israel offered 
sacrifices to idols (Ezek. 20:29).

## Metaphysical

The mentality, or intellect, of man given over to exaltation (high place, height, 
stronghold, place of worship) of the world of sense and materiality.
