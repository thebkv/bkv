# Neco (A.V., Necho)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Necho), ne'-co

## Meanings

- beaten
- smitten
- afflicted
- smitten in the feet
- lame

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A king of Egypt at the time that Josiah was king of Judah. Josiah was killed in battle against this 
king (II Chron. 35:20-24).

## Metaphysical

A ruling thought of the Egyptian, or darkened, little understood phase of 
consciousness in man; it is greatly lacking in practical understanding (smitten in the feet, lame, the feet 
referring to the phase of the understanding that comes in touch with outer conditions).
