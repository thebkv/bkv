# Bar-Jonah (A. V., Barjona)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Barjona), bar-jo'nah

## Metaphysical

The inner sight (faith) acting in consciousness through peace and love. (Peter 
represents the faith faculty in man; Jonah means a dove, which signifies peace; John stands for love. In 
John 21:15-17 Jesus said three times to Peter, "Simon, son of John," and John is a form of Johanan. See 
PETER.)

## Preserved OCR Source

Bar-Jonah (A. V., Barjona), bar-jo'nah (Heb.)— son of Jonah; son of Johanan; son of Jonas.
