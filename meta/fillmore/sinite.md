# Sinite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sin-ite

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A people descended from Canaan, son of Ham (Gen. 10:17).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and in character like that which Canaan, the son of Ham, 
and the Canaanites signify. (See CANAAN and CANAANTTES.) See SIN (a wilderness and a city) for 
the specific characteristics of these thoughts.
