# Tahchemonite (A. V., Tachmonite)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Tachmonite), tah-che-mo-nite

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Josheb-basshebeth a Tahchemonite was one of David's mighty men, and "chief of the captain" (II 
Sam. 23:8). In I Chronicles 11:11 he is called "Jashobeam, the son of a Hachmonite."

## Metaphysical

A very wise, keen, discerning thought belonging to the wisdom center in 
consciousness (wise, knowing, judicious, sagacious). (See HACHMONITE and HACHMONI for further 
signification.)
