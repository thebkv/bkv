# Bithiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** b;-thl'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A daughter of Pharaoh who became the wife of a Jewish man (I Chron. 4: 18).

## Metaphysical

A phase of the soul consciousness that, though it belongs to the force that rules 
man in his material state (Pharaoh, king of Egypt) yet has set its love upon the Truth, and worships 
Jehovah; that is, it gives its substance to building up the real Christ self in the individual (daughter of 
Jehovah; worshiper of Jehovah).
