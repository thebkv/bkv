# Jahdai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jSh’-dai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Judahite, descended from Caleb (I Chron. 2:47).

## Metaphysical

The consciousness of man awakening to divine guidance. It is by exercising faith 
(faithfulness to God and to the Truth that one has perceived) and enthusiasm (Caleb), coupled with 
praise and prayer (Judah), that man comes to realize his true inner leader and guide— Jehovah, the Christ 
Spirit. Thus he begins to receive conscious direction in his daily life and affairs, from the spiritual realm 
within him.
