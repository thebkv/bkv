# Tidal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ti'-dal

## Meanings

- splendor
- renown
- veneration
- awe
- fear

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

King of Goiim. He joined Chedorlaomer, king of Elam, in battle against the five kings of the 
plain, in the vale of Siddim (Gen. 14:1, 9).

## Metaphysical

The very large and prominent place (splendor, renown, veneration) that sensuality 
has in the sense, material, and carnal states of consciousness that belong to the outer, animal man; also 
the fearfulness that results from sense expression (fear). (See GOIIM and CHEDORLAOMER.)
