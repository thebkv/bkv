# Zemirah (A. V., Zemira)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Zemira), ze-ml'-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

First-named son of Becher, who was one of Benjamin's sons (I Chron. 7 :8).

## Metaphysical

Harmonious expression in joy, praise, and triumph over error (striking the harp, 
music, hymn of praise, song of triumph). This comes about by means of an active faith (Benjamin).
