# Theophilus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** the-oph'-i-lus

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The person to whom Luke wrote his letters that later became our New Testament books of Luke 
and The Acts (Luke 1:3; Acts 1:1).
