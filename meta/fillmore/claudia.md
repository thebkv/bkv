# Claudia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Lat
- **Pronunciation:** clau'-di-a

## Meanings

- broken
- lame
- lamentable

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Christian woman at Rome (II Tim. 4:21). She was a friend to Paul and Timothy.

## Metaphysical

A soul quality that loves the truth of man's divine sonship (believes on Jesus 
Christ); this soul quality is somewhat limited or hindered in its practical understanding and expression 
( broken , lame ) because of its relation to the head in its sensate reasoning and carnal personal will (Rome 
and the Roman ruling powers). Claudia is supposed to have been a Briton by birth, and to have come to 
Rome with the wife of the Roman leader in Britain. She no doubt changed her name to a Roman one, 
since Claudia is the feminine form of Claudius. (See CLAUDIUS.)
