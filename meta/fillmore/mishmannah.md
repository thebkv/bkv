# Mishmannah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mish-man'-nah

## Meanings

- fatness
- sumptuous
- strength
- vigor
- fertility

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Gadite who came to David in his stronghold in the wilderness (I Chron. 12:10)

## Metaphysical

A strong, active thought of power, vitality, substance, strength, that allies itself to 
the love faculty (David) and aids it in coming into its rightful place of rulership in consciousness.
