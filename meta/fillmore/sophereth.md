# Sophereth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** soph'-e-reth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A servant of Solomon's, or a descendant of one of Solomon's servants. His descendants returned 
from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 7:57). He is called Hassophereth in Ezra 2:55.

## Metaphysical

See HASSOPHERETH.

In the significance of this name we find also the idea of an orderly gathering together, a directing 
and instructing of thoughts stressed (military scribe, muster master, governor, learned ).
