# Asnah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** as'-nah

## Meanings

- bramble
- dweller in the thorn bush
- thorn bush

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man whose children belonged to the Nethinim, who came up from the captivity with 
Zerubbabel (Ezra 2:50). The Nethinim were servants in the Temple. They were set apart to wait on the 
priests and to do the menial work in the Temple and in the Temple worship. After their return from the 
captivity their position was regarded as more honorable than it had been before.

## Metaphysical

The belief in servitude to God; a belief that, when it dominates the mind of a 
Christian, makes him really a slave. The life of such a one is full of trials and harassing experiences and 
hard places ( bramble , thorn bush, dweller in the thorn bush). His path is thorny indeed, until he 
recognizes that he is a son of God, free-born; then he enters into the joy and the freedom of sonship. His 
Christian life is no longer a burden, nor is his sendee given from the standpoint of duty. He lovingly and 
happily expresses his spiritual faculties and powers in serving God and his fellow men; even the most 
lowly sendee is a joy to him.
