# Ahoah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-ho'-ah

## Meanings

- brother's reed
- brotherly
- living brother
- brother, i. e., friend ofJah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Benjamin through his son Bela (I Chron. 8:4). He is called Ahijah in I 
Chronicles 8:7. Ahi and Ahiah are also different forms of the same name.
