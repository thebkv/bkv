# Hadadrimmon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha-dad-r;m'-mon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place in the valley of Megiddon where all Judah and Jerusalem mourned for King Josiah, who 
was slain in a battle with Neco, king of Egypt, in this valley (Zech. 12:11; see II Chron. 35: 20-26 also). 
Hadad and Rimmon are both names of Syrian deities.

## Metaphysical

Attributing joy, fruitfulness, abundance to the efforts of that which Hadad 
represents in man (see HADAD); or the putting away of true fruitfulness (rending of the pomegranate) 
from consciousness by ceasing to be loyal to spiritual Truth (Josiah, whose death was mourned at 
Hadadrimmon, is representative of loyalty to Truth) because of putting one's trust in and magnifying the 
outer or intellectual sense reasonings. (Syria pertains to the unawakened intellect, guided by the senses.)

Hadar. See HADAD.
