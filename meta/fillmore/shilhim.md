# Shilhim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shil-h'im

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A southern city of Judah (Josh. 15:32). This city is called Sharuhen in Joshua 19:6, and Shaaraim 
in I Chronicles 4:31.

## Metaphysical

A phase of thought in the subconsciousness, close to the body consciousness (one 
of the uttermost cities of Judah toward the border of Edom in the south), that tends to look to the without 
for good and for ill; that resists outward expressions and manifestations of error instead of looking 
within. This phase of thought needs to know that one's good comes from one's inner Christ self and that 
all of one's overcoming is to be done within. It might be termed a phase of thought that is very active in 
outer reform, in setting persons and conditions right in the outer.
