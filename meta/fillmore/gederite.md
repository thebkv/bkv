# Gederite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Geder
- a native of Geder

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Baal-hanan the Gederite was "over the olive-trees and the sycamore-trees that were in the 
lowland," during the reign of David (I Chron. 27:28).

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to the Geder consciousness in man. (See GEDER and 
BETH-GADER.)
