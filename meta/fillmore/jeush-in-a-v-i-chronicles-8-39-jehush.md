# Jeush (in A. V., I Chronicles 8:39, Jehush)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** I Chronicles 8:39, Jehush), je'-ush

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Esau by his wife Oholibamah (Gen. 36:5). b Son of Bilhan, a Benjamite (I Chron. 7:10). 
c son of Shimei of the Gershonite Levites (I Chron. "3:10). d A son of Rehoboam, Solomon's son (II 
Chron. 11:19).

## Metaphysical

Strong, unifying, attracting, accumulating thoughts in consciousness (he will 
collect, he will assemble, he will bring together). The thought of Cod, in the meaning of this name, to 
whom He (God) hastens, he will make haste, with the idea of bringing together, bespeaks the final lifting 
up and unifying of the entire man in spiritual life and wholeness.
