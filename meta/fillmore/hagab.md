# Hagab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-gab

## Meanings

- a locust
- hiding
- covering
- leaping
- bending

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His descendants were among the Nethinim who returned from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra

2:46).

## Metaphysical

A serving thought in man's religious consciousness, but with a somewhat biased, 
darkening, and destructive (locust) tendency. (See Revelation 9:310 and Exodus 10:12-15 for an insight 
into the destructive thought force that locusts symbolize.) Likewise a strong belief in evil works 
destructively in one's consciousness, even if with the belief in evil or with fear of it there is a desire and 
a willingness to serve God.

To serve God (good) fully and fruitfully one must learn to give all of one's thought substance to 
the good; one must quit building a destructive current in oneself by magnifying evil through believing in 
it, or by thinking about it as having any power to perpetuate itself.
