# Beno

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-no

## Meanings

- his son

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Levite, son of Jaaziah, and descended from Merari (I Chron. 24:27).

## Metaphysical

An idea of love (a Levite) that is working in man to bring about a realization of 
his divine sonship (his son). As yet, however, the individual has not made -conscious union with this 
love idea or claimed his sonship. The inner forces of Spirit are working, though, to bring about the 
acknowledgment and consciousness of the truth of man's being. (Jaaziah, father of Beno, means Jah will 
determine, or Jah will strengthen.) Before he understands and realizes the fact of his divine sonship, 
man often has bitter experiences and is not centered in the poise, peace, and abidingness of Spirit. 
(Merari, the name of the Levite chief from whom Beno was descended, means bitter, unhappy)
