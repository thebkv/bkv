# Azzan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** az'-zan

## Meanings

- very strong
- sharp
- a thorn

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man of the Israelitish tribe of Issachar. He was father of Paltiel, who was chosen by Jehovah as 
prince over this tribe when the land of Canaan was divided among the Israelites (Num. 34: 26) .

## Metaphysical

The attitude of mind in us that is definite, unwavering, and to the point. Both 
strong and sharp it does not quibble over sense feelings or appearances; it is keen in its discernment, 
comes quickly to a well-defined decision, and abides by it.
