# Bar-Jesus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** bar-je'-s us

## Meanings

- son of Jesus
- son of Joshua

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Jewish man who was a sorcerer, a false prophet (Acts 13:6). He stood against Paul and 
Barnabas, and tried to turn the proconsul, Sergius Paulus, from the faith; he was smitten with blindness 
for a season, in consequence. His Arabic name was Elymas.
