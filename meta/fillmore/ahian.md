# Ahian

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-hi'-an

## Meanings

- brotherly

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shemida, a Manassite (I Chron. 7: 19) .

## Metaphysical

The significance here is that true understanding expressing in the consciousness of 
an individual establishes a sense of union with and brotherly kindness toward all mankind and all 
creation. (Shemida, father of Ahian, is interpreted splendor of wisdom, renowned knowledge, while 
Manasseh refers to the understanding faculty in man, from the negative standpoint, the standpoint of 
denial.)
