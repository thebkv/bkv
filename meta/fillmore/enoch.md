# Enoch

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'noch

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A son of Cain, b A city that Cain built and named after his son (Gen. 4:17). c Son of Jared and 
father of Methuselah (Gen. 5:18-24). This latter Enoch was the man who walked with God and was 
translated, who did not die (Heb. 11:5).

## Metaphysical

Entrance into and instruction in a new state of thought, of understanding. In the 
case of the Enoch who walked with God, the new state of thought would be spiritual consciousness, the 
new life in Christ.
