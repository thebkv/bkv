# Zephath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze'-phath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of the Canaanites. It was captured by Judah and Simeon and its name was changed to 
Hormah (Judg. 1:17).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts of a high, wide-awake, watchful character (looking 
ZEPHATHAH 694

about, attending, mountain watch, watchtower, bending forward to behold) that has become a bearer of 
light (beacon) to the phase of consciousness in which the city of Zephath belongs (Canaanites, Canaan, 
lowland, signifying the body consciousness). The Canaanites signify the elemental life forces in the 
subconscious realm of mind in man. Therefore Zephath, a city of the Canaanites, would pertain to the 
seemingly mortal or physical phase of man's being, which is apparently far removed from that which is 
really true of man (viewing from a distance).

Because of its high, watchful, awakened character, however, this group of thoughts that Zephath 
signifies passes into the possession of the more spiritual qualities in the individual (Zephath was 
captured by Judah and Simeon) and it becomes a fortress and a sanctuary for many inner thoughts and 
ideals during times of adjustment in consciousness— the name of this city was changed to Hormah. (See 
HORMAH.)
