# Be-eshterah (A. V., Beesh-terah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Beesh-terah), beesh'-te-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in Manasseh that was given to the Gershonite Levites (Josh. 21:27); it is the same as 
Ashtaroth of I Chronicles 6:71, and is a form of Beth-ashtaroth, meaning the "temple of Ashtoreth."

## Metaphysical

A state of consciousness in man that houses (shelters and protects) intellectual 
beliefs in the reality of the outer formed world, and in carnal sensations and pleasures. This city, being 
allotted to Manasseh (understanding) and given over to Levi (love), signifies that by means of an 
awakening to a degree of understanding of and love for Truth ideals the Be-eshterah state of thought is 
being lifted to a higher and truer level.
