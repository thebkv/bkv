# Lahmi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ISh’-ml

## Meanings

- Bethlehemite of ox belonging to bread
- my bread
- my substance

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Brother of Goliath the Gittite, a Philistine warrior (I Chron. 20:5).

## Metaphysical

The mistaken belief of the sense mind (Philistines) in individual consciousness 
that substance, prosperity, man's bountiful supply, can be obtained only by personal ownership, by 
means of competition, oppression, defense, and by fighting for one's rights (Bethlehemite, of ox 
belonging to bread, m~ bread, my substance; a Philistine warrior, brother of the giant Goliath).
