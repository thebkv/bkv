# Neri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ne'-ri

## Meanings

- Jehovah spreads light abroad
- Jah is a light
- lamp of Jehovah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:27); thought to be the same as Neriah.

## Metaphysical

Spiritual understanding quickened in individual consciousness (Jehovah spreads 
light abroad, Jah is a light, lamp of Jehovah).
