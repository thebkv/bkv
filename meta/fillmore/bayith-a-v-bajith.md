# Bayith (A. V., Bajith)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Bajith), ba'-y;th

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A temple of the gods of Moab (Isa. 15:2); in the margin it is translated "the temple.

## Metaphysical

The carnal mind's conception, or consciousness, of the body temple (house; a 
temple of the gods of Moab).
