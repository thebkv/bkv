# Man must be true to the Spirit within him

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_addendum

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

God has promised all His children perfect health, prosperity, peace, light, and happiness, but it is 
necessary for us to abide by the spiritual law if we would gain the blessings of Spirit. We must keep 
faith in God and seek Him diligently. Then His good will be ours. We must not follow after lesser goods 
or gods. We must keep faith in our own spirituality if we hope to use our spiritual powers and handle 
spiritual substance.

## Preserved OCR Source

Man must be true to the Spirit within him. If a man follows false gods, that is, material things, he 
will find these to be powerless to satisfy him, and he will fail to enter into the spiritual state that God has 
promised him. A man must be true to his I AM nature if he would reap the benefits of the I AM 
consciousness.
