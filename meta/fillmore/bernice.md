# Bernice

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ber-ni'-§e

## Meanings

- victorious
- bringing .victory

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Eldest daughter of Herod Agrippa, and a sister of the Agrippa of Acts 25 and 26 (Acts 25:13).

## Metaphysical

Bernice, with Drusilla, the wife of Felix, represents the voluptuous side of the 
sense life. That is not true victory which is successful only in feeding and building up for a time the 
outer or sense man in his carnal state.
