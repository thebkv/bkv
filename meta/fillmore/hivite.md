# Hivite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Canaan the son of Ham (Gen. 10:17). The Hivites were a nation in the land of 
Canaan that was to be overcome and destroyed by the Israelites (Exod. 3:8; Josh. 3:10).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the carnal consciousness in man. The Hivites were 
descended from Canaan, son of Ham, and refer to the physical and carnal in the individual.
