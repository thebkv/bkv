# Jahziel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jSh'-zi-el

## Meanings

- whom God apportions
- allotment of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Naphtali (I Chron. 7:13). He is called Jahzeel in Genesis 46:24 and in Numbers 26:48.

## Metaphysical

See JAHZEEL.

jailor (Acts 16:27-34).

Metaphysical. That in us which controls the body in its physical aspect. It wants to conform to 
the new relation and is open to the baptism of spiritual life, which is imparted by the word of Truth 
(Paul).
