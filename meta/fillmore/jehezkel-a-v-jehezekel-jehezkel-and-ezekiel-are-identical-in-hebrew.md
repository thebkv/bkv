# Jehezkel (A. V., Jehezekel, Jehezkel, and Ezekiel are identical in Hebrew)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jehezekel, Jehezkel, and Ezekiel are identical in Hebrew), je-hez'-kel

## Meanings

- whom God makes strong
- whom God holds fast
- God will strengthen
- cleave unto God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A priest to whom the twentieth lot fell for service in the Temple, in David's reign (I Chron.

24:16).

## Metaphysical

Like Ezekiel, Jehezkel signifies that in us which holds to Spirit and relies on Spirit 
as our all-sufficient strength. (See EZEKIEL.)
