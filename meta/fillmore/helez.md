# Helez

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** he'-lez

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Helez the Paltite was one of David's guard of valiant men (II Sam. 23:26). b Son of Azariah 
and father of Eleasah, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 2:39).

## Metaphysical

Putting off human belief in limitation and weakness, and becoming established in 
divine energy, deliverance, and strength.
