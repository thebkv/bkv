# Jathniel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jath'-ni-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Meshelemiah, who was a doorkeeper in the Temple and a descendant of Asaph (I Chron.

26:2).

## Metaphysical

Conscious acceptance of the truth that life comes from God and is continued by 
God (gift of God, God is everlasting), is everlasting because God— our life— is everlasting. "The free gift 
of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord" (Rom. 6:23). (A doorkeeper of the Temple signifies a 
thought that guards the door of our consciousness, admitting certain thoughts and ideas and refusing 
admittance to others.)
