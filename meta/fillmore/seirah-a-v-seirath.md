# Seirah (A. V., Seirath)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Seirath), se'-i-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Ehud escaped to this place after he had killed Eglon, king of Moab, and there Ehud gathered the 
Children of Israel to fight against Moab (Judg. 3:26).

## Metaphysical

Seirah, being the feminine form of Seir, refers to the soul, receptive, or feminine 
phase of that which Seir signifies.
