# Chesalon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ches'-a-lon

## Meanings

- loin
- fleshy
- fat
- strength
- great hope
- sure confidence
- fertile

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place on the border of Judah, in the land of Canaan (Josh. 15:10). It is mentioned as being on 
"the side of mount Jearim on the north." Mount Jearim is a mountain on the north border of Judah, a few 
miles west of Jerusalem.

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of physical strength (loin; fleshy; fat; strength). Under the 
influence of the higher ideals that Judah signifies, this consciousness of strength will be raised to the 
understanding that all true strength is founded in Spirit and works out great good to the individual who 
realizes God as his unfailing, abiding strength (great hope, pure confidence, fertile).
