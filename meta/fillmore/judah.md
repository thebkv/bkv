# Judah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

A hill always refers to a high, elevated thought, desire, aspiration, or state of 
consciousness. This may not always be spiritual; it may be of the intellect or of the soul, but it is an 
aspiring to higher things. The meaning of Gibea is virtually the same as that of Gibeah. (See GIBEAH.)
