# Naarai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** na'-a-rai

## Meanings

- of the young man
- boyish
- youthful
- bridegroom
- my young man- my

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

youth.

"The son of Ezbai," one of David's mighty men (I Chron. 11:37). In II Samuel 23:35 he is called 
Paarai the Arbite.

## Metaphysical

The youthful thought and ideal that are born anew into consciousness as a result 
of Truth's shining into the individual mind and creating aspirations for higher things (Ezbai, father of 
Naarai). (See PAARAI.)
