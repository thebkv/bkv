# Ishvi (in A. V., Genesis 46:17, Isui; in Numbers 26:44, Jesui; in I Chronicles 7:30, Ishuai; in I 
Sam. 14:49, Ishui)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Genesis 46:17, Isui; in Numbers 26:44, Jesui; in I Chronicles 7:30, Ishuai; in I 
Sam. 14:49, Ishui), ish'-vi

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Asher (Gen. 46: 17). b Son of Saul (I Sam. 14:49).

## Metaphysical

Virtually the same as ISHVAH, which see. As a son of Saul, Ishvi signifies a 
sense of equilibrium and poise (evenness, tranquillity, quietness) in man, but more personal and limited 
in its character than it is spiritual (Saul, father of Ishvi, and first king of Israel, represents the will's 
functioning in personality rather than in Spirit). Self-satisfaction in this name would tend to egotism.
