# Kadmiel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** kad'-ml-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Levite, who, with his "children," returned from the Babylonian captivity to Jerusalem and 
helped in rebuilding the Temple (Ezra 2:40; 3:9). A man by this name, possibly the same man, is 
mentioned several times in the book of Nehemiah; he joined in sealing the covenant (Neh. 10:9).

## Metaphysical

An inherently spiritual ideal in consciousness, founded on the innate knowledge 
of God, divine principle, as being from the beginning of time (God aforetimes, primeval God), serving 
God or ministering to the individual who gives it place (before God, i. e., servant or minister).

Since God is from everlasting to everlasting (God eternal), has always existed, and is the Source 
of all, man and the creation came from Him and are constantly being sustained and blessed by Him. Not 
only were all things made by Him but by Him all things continue to exist. The universe and man would 
pass quickly out of existence could they be separated in ever so small a degree from the omnipresent 
life, intelligence, and substance that are God, and with which man comes into conscious touch within his 
own soul (God of the east, east referring to the innermost of man's being).
