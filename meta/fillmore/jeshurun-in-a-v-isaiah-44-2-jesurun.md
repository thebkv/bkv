# Jeshurun (in A. V., Isaiah 44:2, Jesurun)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Isaiah 44:2, Jesurun), jesh'-u-run

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A symbolical name applied to Israel (Deut. 32:15; 33:5, 26; Isa. 44:2).

## Metaphysical

The happy state (blessedness) of our real, religious thoughts (Israel), especially 
when they have become up right according to the true spiritual standard of righteousness, which includes 
love, mercy, good judgment, order, and the activity of all the faculties of the mind raised to spiritual 
consciousness.
