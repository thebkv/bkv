# Areli

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-re'-li

## Meanings

- lionlike
- valiant
- heroic

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Gad and grandson of Jacob (Gen. 46:16).

## Metaphysical

Courage to abide by that which one believes to be right and best; also boldness 
and fearlessness in making one's ideas practical (lionlike, valiant, heroic).
