# Naamites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Naaman

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The "family" that descended from Naaman, son of Bela and grandson of Benjamin (Num.

26:40).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Naaman, 
grandson of Benjamin, represents. (See NAAMAN.)
