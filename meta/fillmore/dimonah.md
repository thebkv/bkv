# Dimonah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** dl-mo'-nah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A southern city of Judah (Josh. 15: 22); it is thought to he the same place as the Dibon of 
Nehemiah 11:25.

## Metaphysical

The significance is much the same as that of Dibon; Dimonah refers to the 
denying, letting go, or wearing away of error beliefs, that one may become established in Truth ideals 
and practices. (See DIBON.)
