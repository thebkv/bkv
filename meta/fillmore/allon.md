# Allon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** al'-lon

## Meanings

- oak
- an oak

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite, descended from Simeon (I Chron. 4:37). The "oak" of Joshua 19:33 is called Allon 
in the Authorized Version.

## Metaphysical

A realization of protection and strength (oak).
