# Shihor (in A. V., Josh. 13:3; Isa. 23:3; Jer. 2:18, Sihor)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Josh. 13:3; Isa. 23:3; Jer. 2:18, Sihor), shi'-h™r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a The river Nile (Isa. 23:3; Jer. 2:18). b "The brook of Egypt" (I Chron. 13:5; Josh. 13:3, see 
margin) may also refer to the Nile, though some think it to be the present Wady el Arish.

## Metaphysical

The great subconscious current of life, but seemingly dark and corrupt with 
ignorant, material thought (the river Nile of Egypt, dark, turbid, clouded, blackness).
