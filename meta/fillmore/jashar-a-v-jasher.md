# Jashar (A. V., Jasher)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jasher), ja'-shSr

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A book (Josh. 10:13; II Sam. 1:18). Fallows says, "As the word Jasher signifies just ox upright, 
by which word it is rendered in the margin of our Bibles, this book has been generally considered to 
have been so entitled as containing a history of just men. "

## Metaphysical

The phase of our memory that retains thoughts that are just, righteous, and true.
