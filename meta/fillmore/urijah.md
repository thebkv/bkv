# Urijah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u-ri-jah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A priest in the time of King Ahaz of Judah (II Kings 16:10). Urijah is the same name as Uriah.

## Metaphysical

Uriel, Uriah, and Urijah have virtually the same meaning. (See URIAH and 
URIEL.)
