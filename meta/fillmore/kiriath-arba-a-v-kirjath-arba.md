# Kiriath-arba (A. V., Kirjath-arba)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjath-arba), kirO-i-ath-Sr'-bCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The old name of Hebron; it was called Kiriath-arba after Arba, who was the greatest man 
among the Anakim (Gen. 23:2; Josh. 14:15 with marginal note).

## Metaphysical

Attributing to carnal reason the perfection that belongs to and comes forth from 
spiritual understanding only; attributing strength, power, knowledge, and greatness to the outer formed 
world, instead of knowing that all power and reality exist in Spirit— in the unformed ideas of the one 
Mind. (See ARBA; see HEBRON also.)
