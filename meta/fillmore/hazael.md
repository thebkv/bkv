# Hazael

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** haz'-a-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An officer of Benhadad, king of Syria. Elijah was told by Jehovah to anoint Hazael as king of 
Syria (I Kings 19: 15). Benhadad sent him to Elisha to find out whether he would recover from his 
sickness. Hazael murdered Benhadah and became king in his stead (II Kings 8: 7-15)

## Metaphysical

To anoint Hazael (all-seeing eye) king of Syria (confusion of thought) means to 
restore order in the confused consciousness.
