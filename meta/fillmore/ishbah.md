# Ishbah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ish'-bah

## Meanings

- he will praise
- he will laud
- he will soothe
- he will appease
- he will still

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"Father of Eshtemoa," of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:17).

## Metaphysical

The activity of the spirit of praise in man's consciousness (he will praise ). The 
habit of praising God in times of seeming stress or trial brings one into a calm and quiet state of mind 
very quickly and causes one to sense an inner peace and satisfaction (he will appease, he will soothe, he 
will still).
