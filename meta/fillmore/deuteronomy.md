# Deuteronomy

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** deu'-ter-on'-o-my

## Meanings

- repetition of the law
- restatement of the law

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The fifth book of the Bible, and supposed to have been written by Moses; authorities, however, 
disagree as to this. It is named Deuteronomy because it rehearses, or retells, the law.
