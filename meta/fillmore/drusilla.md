# Drusilla

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** dru-sll'-la

## Meanings

- bedewed
- watered by dew

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Wife of Felix, who was the Roman governor of Judea at the time that Paul was tried and was sent 
to Rome to appear before C%sar (Acts 24:24).

## Metaphysical

The voluptuous side of the sense life. (See BERNICE.)
