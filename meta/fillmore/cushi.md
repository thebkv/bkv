# Cushi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** cu'-shl

## Meanings

- firelike
- burned
- black
- combustible
- an Ethiopian

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of the prophet Zephaniah (Zeph. 1:1). b The great-grandfather of Jehudi. Jehudi read the 
roll, written by Baruch as given him by Jeremiah, to King Jehoiakim, who burnt it (Jer. 36:14).

## Metaphysical

The darkness and denseness of material, sensual thought and belief (firelike, 
burned, black, combustible, an Ethiopian). The fact that the men by this name were Israelites shows that 
the religious tendencies and desires and the spiritual aspirations of the individual are piercing this 
darkness; the gloom is giving way to the light of Truth, which will establish spiritual thought and belief.
