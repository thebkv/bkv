# an

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hem'-dan

## Meanings

- desirable
- pleasant
- grateful
- precious

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Dishon, who was a son of Seir the Horite (Gen. 36:26); in I Chronicles 1:41, Hamran.

## Metaphysical

The seemingly desirable and pleasant aspect of the sense beliefs and activities of 
one that is not awakened spiritually. This pleasure, however, is but transitory and soon turns to dust and 
ashes, to vanity and vexation of spirit. (See DISHON, SEIR, and HORITE.)
