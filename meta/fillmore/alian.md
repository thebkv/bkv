# Alian

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

A lofty concept, of man's seemingly material consciousness, concerning the 
apparently sensual or physical phase of his organism— a glimpse of the truth that even the outer man has 
his origin in Spirit. (See SHOBAL.)
