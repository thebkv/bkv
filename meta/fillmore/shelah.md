# Shelah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she'-lah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Judah, by the daughter of Shua the Canaanite. He was bom at Chezib (Gen. 38:5). b Son 
of Arpachshad and grandson of Shem, who was one of Noah's three sons (I Chron. 1:18).

## Metaphysical

A sense of peace, harmony, security that has come about by prayer, affirmation, 
desire, centered in that which is good and true (prayer, petition, request, demand ).

Shelanites, she'-lan-ites (fr. Heb.).

The "family" of Shelah, the son of Judah (Num. 26:20).

Metaphysical. Thoughts springing from and like to that in consciousness which is signified by 
Shelah. (See SHELAH.)
