# Eznite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- strong
- firm
- sharp
- a spear

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Adino the Eznite is the first named of David's mighty warriors, as given in II Samuel 23:8.

## Metaphysical

A perceptive, discerning, piercing thought in man. This thought is very strong, 
steadfast, and active in consciousness, in its defense of the ruling love ego (David).
