# Beth-aven

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-a'-ven

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city, or town, near Bethel (Josh. 7:2; Hos. 4:15; 10:5, 8). Bethel is also said to have been 
nicknamed Beth-aven after Jeroboam had made it the chief seat of the worship of the golden calf. (See I 
Kings 12:25-13:10.)

## Metaphysical

See AVEN for the significance of Beth-aven, since apparently they are the same 
place. Beth-aven, in the case of Bethel's being called by this name, refers to the vanity of worshipping 
gold, money, or the outer, material thought about substance.
