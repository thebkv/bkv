# Tartan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tSr'-tan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Tartan is not a proper name, but is the title of an Assyrian officer who was sent by the king of 
Assyria with a great army against Jerusalem (II Kings 18:17).

## Metaphysical

The executive activity, or seeming power of execution, that belongs to the ruling 
sense-reasoning phase of the will in man that is signified by Sennacherib, king of Assyria. (Tartan was a 
commander in chief ox general of this king, and was sent with the Assyrian army against Jerusalem. See 
SENNACHERIB and ASSYRIA.)
