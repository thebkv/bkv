# Jehiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-hiO-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the doorkeepers for the Ark of the Covenant, when David had it brought back to 
Jerusalem (I Chron. 15:24). Jehiel of verse 18 is another form of the same name.

## Metaphysical

That in us which recognizes that life is of Spirit and comes from Spirit; that in 
Spirit there is no death or destruction. As Jesus said to the Sadducees, "God is not the God of the dead, 
but of the living" (Matt. 22:32). This recognition of life only, in regard to God and to His will for us, as 
expressed through our I AM, Jehovah, or indwelling Christ, helps to guard our inner divine spark, which 
the Ark of the Covenant signifies.
