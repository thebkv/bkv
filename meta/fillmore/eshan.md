# Eshan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-shan

## Meanings

- couch
- support
- prop

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the hill country of Judah, in Canaan (Josh. 15:52).

## Metaphysical

An aggregation of thoughts in consciousness that is of a supporting, sustaining, 
restful character (couch, support). This aggregation of strengthening, sustaining thoughts is the product 
of, and belongs to, the praise-and-prayer consciousness (Judah).
