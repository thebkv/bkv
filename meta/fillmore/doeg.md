# Doeg

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** do'-eg

## Meanings

- fearful
- anxious
- timid

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A servant of King Saul's, one of his chief herdsmen (I Sam. 21:7). Doeg told Saul that 
Ahimelech the priest had befriended David, and he slew the priests at Saul's command (I Sam. 22:9, 18).

## Metaphysical

Fearfulness, anxiety, and timidity, in individual consciousness. This is a result of 
believing in the impossibility of lifting the physical or outer consciousness into spirituality and 
establishing it in the Truth. This belief when in control of the vital forces in one (Doeg was Saul's chief 
herdsman) does much to destroy, or to bring into inactivity, one's spiritual qualities (Ahimelech and the 
other priests whom Doeg slew). See AHIMELECH.
