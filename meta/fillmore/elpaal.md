# Elpaal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** el-pa'-al

## Meanings

- God works
- God's work
- wages of God
- God's reward

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite. His mother's name was Hushim, and his father was Shaharaim (I Chron. 8:11).

## Metaphysical

The activity of faith greatly increased in consciousness, and its good results. 
(Benjamites represent the active faith quality in man, which is the work of God; the wages or reward is a 
great increase of faith and its activity, and the good that man experiences by acting upon his faith in 
God, the good.) "Jesus answered and said unto them, This is the work of God, that ye believe on him 
whom he hath sent" (John 6:29).
