# Ish-bosheth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ish-bo'-sheth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Saul. He was made king over a portion of Israel by Abner, but his rule did not last long 
(II Sam. 2:8). He is called Esh-baal in I Chronicles 8:33.

## Metaphysical

A remembrance of past errors remaining as a subconscious remorse or shame in 
mind. He rules part of the consciousness, but he is a weakling and his kingdom is insignificant. We 
should always be on the alert to put down this accusing conscience and place divine love (David) in its 
stead.
