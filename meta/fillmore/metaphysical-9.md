# Metaphysical

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_addendum

## Metaphysical

The rod represents spiritual power. Men have always considered a rod to be a 
symbol of power. The wizard waves a wand and the king carries a scepter to show his power. The staff 
or club is probably man's earliest tool. Through the ages it has assisted him in 
his rise to mastery.
