# Ismachiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** is-ma-chi-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Levite; he was an overseer in the Temple service, during the reign of Hezekiah (II Chron. 
31:13). He was one of the overseers over the oblations, tithes, and dedicated things that were brought by 
the people of Judah to the Temple.

## Preserved OCR Source

Jehovah; who lays hold of Jah; cleaving to the Lord.
