# Ucal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u'-cal

## Meanings

- I am strong
- I shall be nourished
- lam power
- lam mighty
- I shall prevail

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the two men to whom Agur seems to have addressed his words of the 30th chapter of 
Proverbs (Prov. 30:1).

## Metaphysical

An inner consciousness of strength, power, and might. This consciousness is the 
result of feeding upon, receiving, affirming, realizing, assimilating, and being nourished by words of 
Truth (I am strong, / shall be nourished, lam power, lam might, l shall prevail).
