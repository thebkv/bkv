# Cainan (more properly Kenan)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ca-i'nan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Named twice in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:36, 37); the second Cainan in this 
genealogy is called Kenan in Genesis 5:9.

## Metaphysical

Cainan, named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ, and meaning possessor, 
centralizer, one who fixes and establishes his center, symbolizes the establishing of one's outer 
consciousness and organism in the truth that the whole man, even the body, is in essence spiritual. Thus 
does one prepare oneself for the new or Christ birth. Through this new birth, even the very outermost 
phase of man's organism undergoes a change from seeming material to spiritual expression and 
manifestation— the body takes on its real spiritual nature.
