# Mibzar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mibO-zSr

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A chief of Edom, descended from Esau (Gen. 36:42).

## Metaphysical

A ruling belief of the carnal mind in man that the things of Spirit, of God, are hard 
to understand, that they are so far removed from the apparently finite mind of man that they are 
unattainable.
