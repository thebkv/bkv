# Omega

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** o-me'-gCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The last letter of the Greek alphabet, "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the 
beginning and the end" (Rev. 22:13).

## Metaphysical

The last, the end, or the fulfillment. Christ, being the Alpha and the Omega, the 
first and the last, the beginning and the end, signifies the allness of Truth- through Christ, the ideal 
creation of God, or Divine Mind, all things came into existence in the first place and all things will 
finally come to the Christ likeness, which is the goal of perfection toward which the whole creation is 
unfolding. Christ is the fulfillment of all good to everybody and to everything.
