# Gerizim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ger'-l-zim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The mount of blessing in the land of Canaan (Deut. 11: 29; Josh. 8:33).

## Metaphysical

The working of the law in blessings and good to those who think and act in 
harmony with divine principle. It is that in us which takes cognizance of the working out of life, health, 
peace, joy, plenty, and every good in our life because of our understanding of and obedience to the law 
of God. This phase of the activity of the law builds up the seemingly waste places; it cuts out the error 
and establishes the good. (See EBAL, the mount of cursing.)
