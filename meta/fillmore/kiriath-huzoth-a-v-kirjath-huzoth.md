# Kiriath-huzoth (A. V., Kirjath-huzoth)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjath-huzoth), kirO-i-ath-hu'-zoth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Moab, where Balak brought Balaam and offered up oxen and sheep as sacrifices, 
when he wanted Balaam to curse Israel (Num. 22:39).

## Metaphysical

Divided thoughts, thoughts of good and evil, God and self ( city of divisions, city 
of two parts), expressing in and through the psychic realm in individual consciousness (middle city, that 
which exists between the inner and the outer), that the carnal mind (Moab) would have one believe to be 
ways of Spirit. Thus the carnal mind would defeat the purpose of the real, true thoughts of the 
consciousness (Israelites), would bring the individual into deep trouble and confusion, and would 
perpetuate its own sensate activities.
