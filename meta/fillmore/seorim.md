## Metaphysical

Seorim is a form of the words SEIR and SEIRAH, which see. Since Seorim was 
the name of an Israelitish priest who served in the Temple in David's reign, we see in it a suggestion of 
elevation and transmutation of the sense or physical consciousness to higher and more spiritual 
expression.
