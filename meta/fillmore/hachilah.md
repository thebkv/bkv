# Hachilah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** haeh'-i-lah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A hill in the wilderness of Ziph where David was in hiding from Saul, and where Saul encamped 
when he went to hunt for David (I Sam. 23:19; 26:3).

## Metaphysical

Hope, expectation of good, in the face of seeming darkness and lack; light, in the 
midst of apparent darkness.
