# Chaldea

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** chal-de'-a

## Meanings

- savant
- astrologer
- magi

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Asiatic country lying on both sides of the Euphrates River (Jer. 50:10; Ezek. 11:24). It was a 
portion of Babylon and, in a general sense, included all of Babylonia.

## Metaphysical

The psychic realm in man (savant, astrologer, magi) posing as the true spiritual 
realm, thus deceiving the individual and robbing him of the spiritual contact and good that he is seeking.
