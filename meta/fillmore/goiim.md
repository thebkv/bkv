# Goiim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** goi'-lm

## Meanings

- Gentiles
- nations
- peoples, especially foreign
- bodies
- corporealities

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Tidal, king of Goiim, was one of the four kings who went to war with the kings of Sodom and 
Gomorrah, and defeated them. Later these four kings were overthrown and destroyed by Abraham (Gen. 
14:1).

## Metaphysical

The carnal, material thoughts and state of consciousness that belong to the outer 
man (Gentiles, nations).
