# Aridatha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-rld'-a-tha

## Meanings

- strength
- worthy
- desen’ing

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of Haman's sons, who was hanged with his brothers (Esth. 9:8, 13, 14).

## Metaphysical

The darkened thought in man that calls evil good and that attributes strength to 
error. The truth is that error has no strength or power except that which man gives to it temporarily by 
believing in it. (See ARIDAI.)
