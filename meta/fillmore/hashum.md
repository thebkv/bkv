# Hashum

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** h a'-shum

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Two hundred and twenty-three of his descendants returned from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 
2:19). b A man by this name stood at Ezra's left hand when Ezra read the law of Moses to the people 
(Neh. 8:4). c A Hashum joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant (Neh. 10:18).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts in consciousness that have been enriched by union with high ideals.
