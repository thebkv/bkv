# Beeroth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be-e'-roth

## Meanings

- wells

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Many think Beeroth to be the same place as Beer, only given in the plural. It was a city of the 
Hivites that was assigned to the Israelitish tribe of Benjamin (Josh. 9:17; 18:25).

## Metaphysical

A congregation of thoughts about life and its increase in spiritual consciousness 
(wells, a city). Under the Hivites this city, or group of thoughts in consciousness, would be of a material 
character, but it would be elevated to a higher level in passing into the possession of the Benjamites 
(active faith and illumination).
