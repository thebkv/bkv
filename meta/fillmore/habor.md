# Habor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-b™r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Either a river in Media, a tributary of the Euphrates, or a place situated on that river (II Kings

17:6).

## Metaphysical

A forced seeming union made between the religious and spiritual thoughts of man 
(Israelites) and his intellectual sense reasonings (Assyrians). (The Israelites were taken captive by the 
Assyrians, and Habor was one of the places where they were placed.)
