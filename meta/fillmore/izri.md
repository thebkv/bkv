# Izri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Tz'-ri

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Leader of the fourth course of singers for service in the Temple during David's reign (I Chron. 
25:1 1; in the 3d verse of the same chapter he is called Zeri.)

## Metaphysical

Thought, imagination, based upon Principle ( Creator), upon spiritual Truth and 
harmony, as the power that makes all forms, that brings the outer man into existence as a manifest being 
(fashioning, framing, making; thought is the formative power in man, and all thinking should be based 
upon Principle, Truth). (See ZERI.)
