# Huppim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hup'-pim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite (I Chron. 7:12). In Numbers 26:39 he is called Hupham. He was a son of Benjamin, 
according to Genesis 46:21.

## Metaphysical

Virtually the same as that of Hupham. (See HUPHAM.)
