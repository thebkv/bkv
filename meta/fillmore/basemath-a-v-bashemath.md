# Basemath (A. V., Bashemath)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Bashemath), bas'-emath

## Meanings

- fragrant
- aromatic
- spicy
- sweet
- pleasant

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Wife of Esau (Gen. 26:34). b Daughter of Solomon and wife of Ahimaaz in Naphtali (I Kings

4:15).

## Metaphysical

The soul, or feminine, in man, in its fineness of ability to perceive, or to receive 
intuitively (fragrant, aromatic, pleasant; the sense of smell bespeaks perception, discrimination, 
intuition), spiritual understanding and guidance. Even the soul in its seemingly material aspect is 
intuitive, and is much quicker to feel instinctively and know the truth about things than is the outer 
reasoning quality, the masculine, in the individual.
