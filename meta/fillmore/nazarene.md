# Nazarene

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** naz-a-rene'

## Meanings

- patronymic for inhabitants of Nazareth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An inhabitant of the town of Nazareth. Jesus and His followers were called Nazarenes, because 
Jesus' home was in Nazareth (Matt. 2:23; Acts 24:6).

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to that in consciousness which Nazareth signifies. (See 
NAZARETH.)
