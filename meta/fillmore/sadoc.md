# Sadoc

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** sa'-doc

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Matt. 1:14). Zadok is the same name in the Hebrew.

## Metaphysical

The consciousness awakening to and laying hold of the true idea of righteousness 
and justice. True righteousness and justice measure up to the Christ standard of right, and that standard

includes mercy; it involves the right relation of all one's thoughts, faculties, and powers to oneself as 
well as to people and the world without one.
