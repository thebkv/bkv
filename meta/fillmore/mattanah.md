# Mattanah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mat'-ta-nah

## Meanings

- gift
- present
- donation
- gratuity

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place where the Israelites camped while in the wilderness (Num. 21:18).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of the divine favor and blessing, of one's possibilities through 
Spirit, of eternal life through Christ the Son, which is God's gift of gifts to man.
