# Demas

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** de'-mas

## Meanings

- popular
- of the people

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One who worked with Paul in the Truth but later forsook him, "having loved this present world" 
(II Tim. 4:10). (See Col. 4:14 and Philem. 24.)

## Metaphysical

While the work of regeneration is going on, many old ideas are eliminated. We 
find Demas, whose name means popular, of the people, forsaking Paul and going to Thessalonica. His 
love for the material world separated him from the consciousness of Truth. The thoughts in us that 
believe in the material world as the source of our happiness and well-being cannot follow us in our 
spiritual development. Love of popularity, love of the approval of the world, and the popular beliefs 
about life cause one to fall short of the Truth standard.
