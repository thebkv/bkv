# Sharar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sha'-rSr

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The Ararite," father of Ahiam. This Ahiam was one of David's mighty men, and a member of 
his guard or council (II Sam. 23:33). He is called Sacar the Hararite in I Chronicles 1 1:35.

## Metaphysical

An active thought of strength, firmness, and order (strong, hard, firm, navel; the 
order faculty in the individual is located at the navel). The definitions of Sharar, as a whole, bespeak a 
great need of more love, cooperation, and harmony. These seem to be attained in the son Ahiam. (See 
AHIAM.)
