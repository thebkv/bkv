# Libyans

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- land of the Lubim
- dried
- parched
- thirsty

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

People of Libya (Dan. 1 1:43). Libya is that portion of Africa bordering on the Mediterranean Sea 
and lying west of Egypt. The Hebrew name for Libya is Put, or Phut, and the Libyans are thought to 
have been descendants of Put, the son of Ham. They are also identified with Lubim.
