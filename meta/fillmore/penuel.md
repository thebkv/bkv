# Penuel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe-nu'-el

## Meanings

- turned toward God
- face of God
- within the presence of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a The same place as Peniel (Gen. 32:31). b A man of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:4). c A son of 
Shashak of the tribe of Benjamin (I Chron. 8:25).
