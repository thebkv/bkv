# Beth-rapha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-ra'-pha

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The son of Eshton, a descendant of Judah (I Chron. 4:12).

## Metaphysical

With the exception of its application to a people of tallness, Beth-rapha in its 
various forms refers to healing, remission, upbuilding, restoring 
BETHS AIDA

(house of repairing, house of healing, place of relaxing). All fearfulness (place of fear) can be overcome 
by

the exercise of praise and thanksgiving (the Judah consciousness), established in true, abiding thoughts 
of wholeness.
