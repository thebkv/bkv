# veil, of the temple, rent in the midst

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

"The veil of the temple was rent in the midst" (Luke 23:45) means that the last 
step in regeneration is the giving up of the thought of the corporeal existence of the body temple. Then 
the veil of sense thought that conceals the spiritual body is rent, and man comes into consciousness of 
the body imperishable and eternal. (See RENT.)
