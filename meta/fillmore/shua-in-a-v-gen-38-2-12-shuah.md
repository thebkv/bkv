# Shua (in A. V., Gen. 38:2, 12, Shuah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Gen. 38:2, 12, Shuah), shu'-CE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of Judah's wife, a Canaanite (I Chron. 2:3). b Daughter of Heber, of the tribe of Asher (I 
Chron. 7:32).

## Metaphysical

Broad, rich thoughts of abundant substance, in the depths of the subconscious 
mind (ample, riches, wealth, prosperity, a Canaanitish man and an Israelitish woman; the Canaanites 
refer to the elemental life forces in the depths of the subconsciousness, and a woman signifies some 
phase of the soul). Shua signifies also a strong desire for the uplifting power of God, for something 
higher and better, emanating from the soul and from the subconscious elemental life forces (a cry for 
help, prayer, supplication), with the sure response of Spirit to the cry (saving, set free, delivered).
