# Shaveh-kiriathaim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sha'-veh-kir-i-atha'-im

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A plain near Kiriathaim, a city that later came into possession of the tribe of Reuben. It was a 
place where lived the Emim who were smitten in the time of Abraham (Gen. 14:5).

## Metaphysical

Shaveh means a plain, an equalized, poised state of mind and body; Kiriathaim 
means double city and signifies a twofold strength or supply of whatever the central idea of the group of 
thoughts may be. In Shaveh-kiriathaim, the significance is poise and equalization in the consciousness 
and the organism, doubly established and sure. (See SHAVEH and KIRIATHAIM.)
