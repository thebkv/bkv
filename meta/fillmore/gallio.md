# Gallio

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** gal'-ll-o

## Meanings

- one who lives on milk
- white as milk

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Roman proconsul of Achaia. He paid no attention when the Jews who opposed Christianity 
beat Sosthenes, the ruler of the synagogue at Corinth, and would have nothing to do with religious 
matters and differences (Acts 18:12-17).

## Metaphysical

A ruling thought that is entirely intellectual. It has no spiritual illumination, and 
no desire for anything of a spiritual nature. (The use of gala or galactos, milk, to designate the most 
simple and elemental of Christian truths would indicate that that which Gallio signifies is limited to the 
most elemental and material phases of consciousness.)
