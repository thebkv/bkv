# Beth-arbel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-ar'-bel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place that was destroyed by Shalman, or Shalmaneser, king of Assyria (Hos. 10:14). It was a 
fortified place, apparently, and belonged to the Israelites, but was overthrown because they did not 
remain true to their worship of the one God.

## Metaphysical

The working out of the divine law in a way seemingly adverse to the higher 
religious thoughts of the consciousness (Israelites), in that the light and joy of their oneness with God is 
lost to them for a time. (Beth-arbel was destroyed by Shalmaneser, king of Assyria; Shalmaneser 
signifies a very destructive tendency in man. A court is a place where justice is administered, and an 
ambush bespeaks an unseen peril or attack.)
