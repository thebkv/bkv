# Becorath (A.V., Bechorath)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Bechorath), be'-eorath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite from whom Saul was descended (I Sam. 9: 1).

## Metaphysical

A conception of sonship (first birth, first-born, birthright, primogeniture) .
