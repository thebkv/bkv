# Artemis

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ar'-te-mis

## Meanings

- Diana
- huntress
- goddess of night

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Artemis is another name for the goddess Diana of the Ephesians (Acts 19:24, margin). Fallows 
says: "The Diana of the Romans is a goddess known under various modifications, and with almost 
incompatible attributes. As the tutelary divinity of Ephesus, in which character alone she concerns us 
here, she was undoubtedly a representative of the same power presiding over conception and birth that 
was adored in Palestine under the name of Ashtoreth. She is therefore related to all the cognate deities of 
that Asiatic Juno-Venus, and partakes, at least, of their connection with the moon . . . The Arabic version 
of the Acts renders Artemis, in the chapter cited, by Az Zuharat, which is the Arabic name for the planet 
Venus."
