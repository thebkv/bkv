# Joel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jo'-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A son of Samuel (I Sam. 8:2). b Son of Pethuel, and prophet of Judah (Joel 1:1). c Also others 
(IChron. 6:36; 7:3; 11:38; 15:7; 27:20).

## Metaphysical

Joel bespeaks the I AM in dominion in the individual (Jah is his God, he who 
desires, he who wills, he who fulfills every desire ). In this consciousness all things are possible.
