# Gileadite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Gilead

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Gilead of the tribe of Manasseh (Num. 26:29). Jair and Jephthah, two of the 
judges of Israel, were Gileadites (Judg. 10:3; 11:1-40).

## Metaphysical

A discerning, discriminating, judging thought belonging to the Gilead 
consciousness in man. (See GILEAD.)
