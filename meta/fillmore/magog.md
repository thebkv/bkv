# Magog

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-gog

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Japheth, who was one of Noah's three sons (Gen. 10:2). A "land" or a nation against 
which Ezekiel prophesied (Ezek. 38:2). "Gog and Magog" are used symbolically in Revelation 20:8.
