# Gilboa

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** g;l-bo'-a

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A mountain in Palestine. There Israel under Saul encamped against the Philistines, and there Saul 
and his three sons were slain (I Sam. 28:4; 31:8).
