# Aman

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ar'-nan

## Meanings

- active
- nimble
- strong

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite mentioned in the genealogy of Zerubbabel (I Chron. 3:21).

## Metaphysical

A constructive, restoring, upbuilding thought in consciousness-a thought that is 
powerful, vigorous, alert, resourceful, and active in its nature (strong, nimble); it might be named a 
belief in efficiency.
