# Bezai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-zai

## Meanings

- shining
- high
- victory
- conqueror

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Jewish man whose descendants, to the number of three hundred and twenty-three, returned 
from the Babylonian captivity with Zerubbabel (Ezra 2: 17). b One who joined Nehemiah in sealing the 
covenant (Neh. 10:18).

## Metaphysical

An assurance of light and power to overcome error and remain true to Spirit 
(shining, high, victory, conqueror) .
