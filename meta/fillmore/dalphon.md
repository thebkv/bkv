# Dalphon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** dal'-phon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the ten sons of Haman. He was killed by the Jews (Esth. 9:7).

## Metaphysical

Hanman, the Jews' enemy, was an Agagite. The Agagites were descendants of 
Agag, who represents the Adversary in individual consciousness. Dalphon signifies a thought tendency 
that is very leaky (dropping, dripping, leaking), and not at all steadfast; it is very sympathetic in its 
nature, with a weak, human sympathy (flowing copiously, tears) that is of a destructive character. 
DAMASCUS

It belongs to the adverse, and it works against the higher or spiritual thoughts and activities of man's 
being (represented by the Jews).
