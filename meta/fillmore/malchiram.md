# Malchiram

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mal-chi-ram

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jeconiah (I Chron. 3:18).

## Metaphysical

Exalting the will in consciousness (my king is exalted, king of the heights), giving 
it a very high place, even attributing to it understanding and judgment (high counselor).
