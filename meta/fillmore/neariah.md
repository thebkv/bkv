# Neariah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ne-a-ri-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Shemaiah, descended from David (I Chron. 3:22). b Son of Ishi, a captain of the five 
hundred Simeonites who went to Mount Seir and "smote the remnant of the Amalekites that escaped, 
and have dwelt there unto this day" (I Chron. 4:42, 43).

## Metaphysical

True thoughts in consciousness; thoughts that are bom of God, the offspring of the 
divine idea of sonship; thoughts that serve Jehovah, the I AM, in the work of freeing the individual from 
sense errors and in unifying him consciously with spirit ( Jehovah shakes out, i. e., disperses, drives out, 
casts forth, liberates, child of Jah, servant of Jehovah, Jah's bride).
