# Haroeh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** har'-o-eh

## Meanings

- the perceiver
- the seer
- the prophet

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Shobal, who was the "father" or founder of Kiriath-jearim. He was of the tribe of Judah (I 
Chron. 2:52).

## Metaphysical

Clear- seeing, spiritual understanding and insight into things; also a knowledge of 
the law of being and its working, thus enabling us to foresee the outcome of our thoughts, words, and 
acts.
