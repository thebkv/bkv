# Reubenites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of ox belonging to Reuben

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Reuben, the eldest son of Jacob, members of the tribe of Reuben (Num. 26:7).

Thoughts springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Reuben 
signifies. (See REUBEN.)
