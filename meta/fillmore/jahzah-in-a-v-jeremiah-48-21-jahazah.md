# Jahzah (in A. V., Jeremiah 48:21, Jahazah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jeremiah 48:21, Jahazah), jSh’-zah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Reuben, on the east side of Jordan, that was given over to the Levites (I Chron. 6:78). It 
is the same city as Jahaz.

## Metaphysical

The same as Jahaz, only the thoughts that Jahzah typifies are in the phase of 
consciousness that Reuben signifies. (See JAHAZ and REUBEN.)
