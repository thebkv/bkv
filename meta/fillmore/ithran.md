# Ithran

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Tth'-ran

## Meanings

- redundant
- abundant
- excellent
- plenty
- pre ‘minent

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Dishon, who was a chief of the Horites (Gen. 36:26). b A man of the Israelitish tribe of 
Asher (I Chron. 7:37). This latter Ithran is thought to be the same person as the Jether of I Chronicles 
7:38.

## Metaphysical

Ideas of great excellence and of great value; thoughts that are superior to their 
fellows, working in both the seemingly deep-seated sense (Horite) consciousness in the individual and 
in his more spiritual phase of mind (the Israelites). These ideas have taken on abundant substance; they 
believe in bountiful supply, and they lead to plenty.
