# Toah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** to'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Levite, an ancestor of Samuel (I Chron. 6:34). He is called Nahath in I Chronicles 6:26. Tohu 
of I Samuel 1:1, is the same name, apparently, and the same lineage; here the men given in the lineage 
are said to be of the hill country of Ephraim.

## Metaphysical

A lowly, humble attitude of thought, to the point of negativeness and dejection 
( sunk down, bowed down, depressed, prostrated, downcast). The meek, humble attitude of mind is good. 
One must abide in the divine qualities of joy, strength, power, and assurance also, however, that one 
may avoid negative tendencies that are deteriorative (inferior, declining, weakness) instead of 
upbuilding and vitalizing.
