# Kitron

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** kitO-ron

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Zebulun. The Zebulunites did not drive out its Canaanitish inhabitants, "but the 
Canaanites dwelt among them, and became subject to taskwork" (Judg. 1:30). This city is named Kattath 
in Joshua 19:15.

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts in the Zebulun or order faculty in individual consciousness. 
Like Kattath, the ideas of this thought center are too small and savor too much of the belief in limitation 
( little and short); there is too little reality in them (figurative) and they believe the problem of putting 
away all error, sensate ideas (Canaanites) to be too knotty and hard a task for them to accomplish. They 
do not, therefore, at this phase of the unfoldment of the individual, make the attainment that they should. 
(See KATTATH.)
