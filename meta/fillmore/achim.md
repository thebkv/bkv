# Achim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** a'- chim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Sadoc, named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Matt. 1:14).

## Metaphysical

The work of the just and righteous (Sadoc) law in us in preparing the way for the 
establishing of the Christ or spiritual consciousness in our mind and our body.

## Preserved OCR Source

Achim, a'- chim (Gk.)— Greek phonetic equivalent of the Hebrew name Iachin (he whom God 
makes firm), which in turn is a contraction of Jehoiachin (whom Jehovah establishes).
