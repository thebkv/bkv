# Tobijah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** to-bi-jah

## Meanings

- Jehovah is good
- goodness of Jehovah
- Jah's goodness
- pleasing unto

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Jah.

a One of the Levites whom Jehoshaphat sent throughout Judah to teach the law to the people (II 
Chron. 17:8). b A Jewish captive from whom Zechariah was to take silver and gold to make a crown for 
Joshua the son of Jehozadak, the high priest (Zech. 6:10, 14).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts, belonging to the high and spiritual phase of man, that, having laid hold 
of the idea of divine goodness, aid in establishing the consciousness of the individual in wisdom and 
substance (Jehovah is good, goodness of Jah, Jah's goodness).
