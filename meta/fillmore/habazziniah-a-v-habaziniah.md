# Habazziniah (A.V., Habaziniah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Habaziniah), habaz-zl-nl'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Rechabite (Jer. 35:3).

## Metaphysical

True, spiritual understanding. The Rechabites stand in consciousness for logical 
reasoning, level-headedness, good judgment, and the faithfulness of the intellect to that which one 
believes to be right. This opens the consciousness to spiritual thoughts; thus the true light, 
understanding, shines in and is recognized by the individual.
