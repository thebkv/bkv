# Sabaoth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** sa-ba'-oth

## Meanings

- armies
- hosts

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The Greek form of the Hebrew word for armies or hosts; generally used with Jehovah, as, 
Jehovah of hosts, and meaning Jehovah as ruler over the whole earth and heaven (Rom. 9:29).

## Metaphysical

The significance of "Lord of Sabaoth," or "Jehovah of hosts," in individual 
consciousness is that the Jehovah, Christ, true I AM in one, is Lord of— has dominion over— all the host 
of thoughts, forces, and activities in one's whole organism, in mind (heaven) and body (earth).
