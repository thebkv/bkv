# Asherim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-she'-r;m

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Heathen, idolatrous goddesses or worship (Exod. 34:13; I Kings 14:23).

## Metaphysical

Human love with its animal propensities. Through Jehoshaphat's allegiance to 
Jehovah, the inner forces (Judah) are purified. "He took away . . . the Asherim out of Judah" (II Chron. 
17:6).

Asherites, ash'-er-ltes (Heb.) .

Men belonging to the tribe of Asher (Judg. 1:32). They were descendants of Asher, one of the 
sons of Jacob.

Metaphysical. Thoughts relating to uprightness, understanding, substance, and happiness. (See 
ASHER.)
