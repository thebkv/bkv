- **Pronunciation:** I Chronicles 12:4, Josabad), joz'-a-bad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

of Manasseh, who came to David at Ziklag (I Chron. 12:20). C A Levite (Ezra 8:33). Jozabad is a
