# Shihor - libnath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shi-h™r - lib-nath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A boundary of Asher, in Canaan (Josh. 19:26).

## Metaphysical

Shihor means black, dark, turbid; Libnath means to be white, to make white, 
cleanse, purify, clarify, transparent. The former signifies impure and ignorant thoughts and beliefs; the 
latter, purity and clearness of thought expressing. The two together, Shihor-libnath, the name of a small 
river on the border of Asher in the land of Canaan, bespeak a purifying current of thought that is doing a 
cleansing work in the body consciousness.
