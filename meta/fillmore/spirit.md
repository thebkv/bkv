# Spirit

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

A name for God. Spirit and Mind are synonymous; therefore we know God- 
Spirit— as Mind, the one Mind, or Intelligence, of the universe. (See SOUL.)

Spirit, Holy.
