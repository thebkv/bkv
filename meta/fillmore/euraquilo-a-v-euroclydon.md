# Euraquilo (A.V., Euroclydon)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Euroclydon), euraq'-ui-lo

## Meanings

- northeast wind

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A very strong and stormy northeast wind that is experienced at times in the eastern part of the 
Mediterranean Sea and countries that border on it. The storm that arose when the Roman soldiers were 
taking Paul to Rome to appear before C%sar was called the Euraquilo (Acts 27:14).

## Metaphysical

The tempest or brain storm that sometimes arises in one's mind because of the 
introduction of the word of Truth (Paul) into the material thoughts and beliefs of the intellectual 
consciousness. (The countries of Bible times that bordered on the eastern, southeastern, and northeastern 
parts of the Mediterranean Sea refer, for the greater part, to the intellect or head in man.)
