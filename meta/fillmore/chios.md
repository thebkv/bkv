# Chios

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** chl'-os

## Meanings

- wine
- fertility
- open

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An island in the ®gean Sea. Paul came "over against" this island while he was on one of his 
missionary journeys (Acts 20:15).

## Metaphysical

An open, unobstructed, fertile attitude of thought; this thought relates closely to 
Spirit and life (wine). When man opens his intellectual reasoning mind so that the word of Truth (Paul) 
may find access, he is "over against" Chios.
