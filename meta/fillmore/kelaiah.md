# Kelaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ke-la'-iah

## Meanings

- Jehovah is light
- swift Jehovah
- Jah is fleet

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Levite who had married a "foreign" wife (Ezra 10:2:3); "the same is Kelita."

## Metaphysical

The lightness, swiftness, fleetness, ethereality, of spiritual thought, going forth in 
the I AM or Christ consciousness and power (Jehovah is light, swift Jehovah, Jah is fleet).

When the personal prevails in one to the extent of causing one to give way to sense desires and 
to set love on the lesser things of life, the consciousness is always dwarfed and cannot grow so large, 
spiritual, broad, and truly useful as it must become in putting on the Christ likeness. (See KELITA.)
