# Meshullam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me-shul'-lam

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Gadite (I Chron. 5:13). b One 
MESOPOTAMIA

who stood at the left hand of Ezra when he read the book of the law to the people (Neh. 8:4). c There 
were several other Israelites by this name.

## Metaphysical

Thoughts in man's higher consciousness (Israelites) that are in accord with the 
Christ Spirit, with Truth (devoted).

These thoughts are, friends and associates of and are unified (allied) with Divine Mind; therefore 
that which is peaceable and perfect (peaceful, perfected) is their keynote.
