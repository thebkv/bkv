# Jeshishai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-shish-ai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A man of the Israelitish tribe of Gad (I Chron. 5:14).

## Metaphysical

The idea that wisdom, peace, and true purity of thought and life come by age, 
experience, worship or great veneration of age, of that which is old: relics, thoughts and beliefs of 
ancestors, and so forth. Man must be watchful not to let this idea have place in his consciousness to the 
extent that his mind becomes closed to new ideas, since holding to the old hinders growth and is a direct 
cause of old age (of or pertaining to age).
