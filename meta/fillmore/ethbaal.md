# Ethbaal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** eth'-ba-al

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

King of the Sidonians, and father of Jezebel, the wife of Ahab, king of Israel (I Kings 16:31).

## Metaphysical

A belief that is wholly given over to materiality; it is the ruling, or central thought 
of the Sidon state of consciousness. (See SIDONIAN.)

## Preserved OCR Source

Ethbaal, eth'-ba-al (Heb.)— with Baal, i.e., with the favor or help of Baal; Baal's similitude, i.e., 
likeness; self-sameness of Baal.
