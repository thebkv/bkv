# Abel-shittim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-bel-shit'-tlm

## Meanings

- meadow of the acacias
- place of acacias

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town "in the plains of Moab" (Num. 33:49). It is more often called Shittim. (See SHITTIM.)

## Metaphysical

A perception, or conception, of the substance, reality, and resurrecting power of 
the inner spiritual life. (Acacias refer to resurrection, life, and a meadow refers to substance).
