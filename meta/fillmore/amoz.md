# Amoz

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-moz

## Meanings

- vigorous
- strong
- courageous
- quick

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Isaiah the prophet (Isa. 1:1).

## Metaphysical

A firm, active trust in God, which is an encouragement and protection to the 
individual and is the forerunner of salvation (strong, vigorous, father of Isaiah— salvation of Jehovah, or 
God saves).
