# Isaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** i-sa'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Amoz; he was a prophet during the reign of Hezekiah, king of Judah (II Kings 19:20; II 
Kings 20; and The Book of Isaiah).

## Metaphysical

The higher self (Jehovah is salvation), that in us which discerns the reality, the 
real character, of spiritual man, and fearlessly proclaims it; spiritual understanding.

After the dominating physical strength has lost its hold on the organism (King Uzziah died), the 
higher self (Isaiah; Jehovah is salvation) begins the purification of the body (Isa. 6:1-8).

Isaiah also signifies understanding of the truth that deliverance, abundant supply, spirituality, 
and all other forms of good come to man through Jehovah, the Christ, his higher self or spiritual I AM.
