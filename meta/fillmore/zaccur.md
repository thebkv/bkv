# Zaccur

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zae'-cur

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of Shammua, who was the spy chosen from the tribe of Reuben (Num 13:4). b Other 
Israelites by this name are mentioned in I Chronicles 4:26; 24 :27; 25 :2; Nehemiah 3 :2; 10 :12; 13: 13.

## Metaphysical

The action that an idea sets up in entering the consciousness of an individual, 
producing thought, reflection, due consideration, and bringing forth a positive conclusion (pricking, 
penetrating, remembering, considering, reflecting, bearing a nude child).
