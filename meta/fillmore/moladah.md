# Moladah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mol'-a-dah

## Meanings

- begetting
- conception
- birth
- nativity
- offspring
- progeny

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Judah (Josh. 15:26); also mentioned as a city of Simeon (Josh. 19:2); it is mentioned 
again after the return from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 1 1:26).

## Metaphysical

New thoughts born into consciousness, thoughts of praise (Judah) and receptivity 
to Truth (Simeon).
