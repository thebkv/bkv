# Baal-gad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-al-gad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city on the northern border of that part of Palestine which was conquered by the Israelites 
under Joshua; it was "in the valley of Lebanon under mount Hermon" (Josh . 11:17) .

## Metaphysical

Lebanon means white, and represents pure thoughts. Mount Hermon (lofty, 
prominent, sacred mountain ) indicates a high, sublime state of mind. Baal-gad (Gad is lord, the god of

fortune is lord, lord of fortunes), a city in the valley of Lebanon under Mount Hermon, signifies a 
realization of substance and bounty, but with a strong tendency toward the belief that the source of this 
good is in the outer formed or psychic world instead of in the inner formless Spirit. (See BAAL.)
