# Machnadebai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mach-na-de'-bai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite named as one "of the sons of Bani"; he had taken a foreign wife, but put her away at 
the command of Ezra (Ezra 10:40).

## Metaphysical

The consciousness beginning to awaken to a perception of the liberality and 
nobility of Spirit; to the broadmindedness, generosity, bigness, and splendidness of spiritual 
understanding and Truth.
