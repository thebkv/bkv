# Asaiah (A. V., II Kings 22:12, 14, Asahiah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** II Kings 22:12, 14, Asahiah), a-sa'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of at least four different Israelitish men (I Chron. 4:36; 6:30; 9:5; 15:6, 11).

## Metaphysical

The innate truth of man's being, that he is spiritual, he came forth from God 
(whom Jah constituted, Jah is doer, Jehovah has made).
