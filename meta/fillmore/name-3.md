# name

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

The "name" in Acts 4:12 ("Neither is there any other name under heaven, that is 
given among men, wherein we must be saved") means authority, or that which unites the right and the 
power of control. Jesus Christ, by taking upon Himself the sins of the flesh and demonstrating over 
them, instilled within the heart of the race both the understanding and the force that are today leading 
mankind out of sin and darkness and death into purity, light, and life everlasting.
