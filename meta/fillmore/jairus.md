# Jairus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** jCE-l'-rus

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A ruler of the synagogue; it was his daughter whom Jesus raised to life (Mark 5:22).

## Metaphysical

Much the same as Jair; a spiritually illumined, ruling thought belonging to the 
established religious ideas (Jews of the New Testament) in individual consciousness. When truly 
enlightened this thought becomes a radiating center of light (he shall shine).
