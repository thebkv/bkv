# stone, that the builders rejected

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

In Luke 20:17 it is recorded that Jesus told the scribes and Pharisees that 
"The stone which the builders rejected, The same was made the head of the corner."

This stone refers to the indwelling Christ, which is the keystone of man's character.
