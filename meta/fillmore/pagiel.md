# Pagiel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pa'-g;-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ochran, and chief, or prince, of the tribe of Asher, in the wilderness (Num. 2:27).

## Metaphysical

A place in consciousness where God meets with the individual; where the 
individual enters into conscious unity with the Divine and is assured of his inheritance of good. This 
meeting place is in the devotional thought, or attitude of true prayer (prayer of God).
