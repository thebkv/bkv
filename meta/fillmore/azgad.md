# Azgad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** az'-gad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Judahite whose "children," to the number of 1,222, returned to their own land from the 
Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:12). b One of those who joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant (Neh. 
10:15).

## Metaphysical

Azgad, with his family, signifies a well-balanced mind in a well-balanced body 
(strong, organized troop or body). The thought of might, as well as that of sustenance and abundance, is 
also contained in that which Azgad signifies.
