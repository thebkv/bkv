# Asharelah (A. V., Asarelah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Asarelah), ash-a-re'lah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Asaph, and one of those who were set apart by David and the captains of his host to 
"prophesy with harps, and psalteries, and with cymbals" (I Chron. 25:2); in verse 14, Jesharelah.

## Preserved OCR Source

Asharelah (A. V., Asarelah), ash-a-re'lah (Heb.)— upright toward God; happiness of God; 
blessedness of God.

Son of Asaph, and one of those who were set apart by David and the captains of his host to 
"prophesy with harps, and psalteries, and with cymbals" (I Chron. 25:2); in verse 14, Jesharelah.
