# Bilshan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bll'-shan

## Meanings

- son of the tongue, i. e., eloquent
- searcher

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One who returned to Jerusalem with Zerubbabel from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:2).

## Metaphysical

A thought activity that gives itself to a diligent searching out of Truth (searcher); 
also the ability to express Truth clearly when it has once been thoroughly investigated and 
comprehended (eloquent). Zerubbabel is one who restores the worship of God.
