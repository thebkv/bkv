# Peruda

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe-ruO-dCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The same person as Perida, of Nehemiah 7:57 (Ezra 2:55).

## Metaphysical

See PERIDA. The thought of separation that is given in the meaning of these 
names suggests the ability to analyze a matter, to separate or disunite the parts of it, for the purpose of 
looking into it from different angles.
