# Neapolis

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** ne-ap'-o-lis

## Meanings

- young city
- youthful city
- new city
- restored city

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Macedonian seaport city, between Samothrace and Philippi. Paul came to this city on one of 
his missionary journeys (Acts 16:11).

## Metaphysical

The new city, or center of action. To make a straight course to Neapolis means 
that he who concentrates all his energies on the accomplishment of his purpose will surely achieve.
