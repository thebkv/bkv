# Shephuphan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she-phu'-phan

## Meanings

- creeping along
- smoothly gliding
- serpent- reptile

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Bela, one of Benjamin's sons (I Chron. 8:5). This name is written Shephupham in 
Numbers 26:39, Shuppim in I Chronicles 7:12, 15, and Muppim in Genesis 46:21.

## Metaphysical

A human or sense wisdom (serpent). See MUPPIM.
