# Baalah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ba'-al-ah

## Meanings

- lady
- mistress
- possessor
- guardian
- sorceress
- citizenship
- a citizen

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A border town of Judah. Kiriath-jearim is another name for this city (Josh. 15:9).

## Metaphysical

An innate consciousness of authority and ownership in man, a consciousness that 
pertains to the feminine or affectional nature, the soul (mistress, possessor), and is expressed in the 
psychic and material to the point of idolatry. (See BAAL.)
