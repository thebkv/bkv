# Sodomites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"Sodomites" as used in the Bible does not refer to the inhabitants of Sodom, but to men who 
committed the sins of which Sodom was guilty and for which it was destroyed (Deut. 23: 17; I Kings 
14:24). When women thus debased themselves they were called prostitutes.
