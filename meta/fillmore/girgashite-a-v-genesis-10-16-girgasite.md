# Girgashite (A.V., Genesis 10:16, Girgasite)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A primitive Canaanitish tribe of which almost nothing is now known. This tribe was descended 
from Canaan, one of the three sons of Noah (Gen. 10: 16), and was among the enemies, in the land of 
Canaan, that the Israelites were to drive out (Gen. 15:21; Josh. 3:10).

## Metaphysical

The very material (dense condensed) state of thought that unawakened man holds 
concerning himself and especially concerning his seemingly material organism. He thus builds up a 
consciousness that cannot abide, because it is not established in Truth.
