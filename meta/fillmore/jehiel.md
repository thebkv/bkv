# Jehiel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-hi-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of several Israelites, most of them Levites, of the priesthood (I Chron. 15:18; 23:8; 
27:32; II Chron. 21:2; 31:13; 35:8; Ezra 8:9; 10:2, 21).

## Metaphysical

Much the same as Jehiah: the true thoughts (Israelites) in us that recognize God as 
life and the author of life for man. God is life, and He cannot will, or be the cause of, anything less than 
the fullness of eternal life to His offspring— mankind. Jehiel refers more to this truth; Jehiah, taking on 
the form of "Jehovah," signifies expression of the truth for which the name stands.
