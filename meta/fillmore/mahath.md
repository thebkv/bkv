# Mahath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-hath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

MAHER- S H AL AL-H ASH-B AZ

Two Kohathite Levites (I Chron. 6:35; II Chron. 29: 12). The Mahath of I Chronicles 6:35 is 
thought to be the same as Ahimoth of the 25th verse.

## Metaphysical

Like Ahimoth, breaking up and disorganizing seeming error in consciousness 
(breaking in pieces, destruction, dissolution); also mentally laying hold of higher ideals (taking, 
grasping). Fire pan, censer, definitions of Mahath, suggest transmutation too.
