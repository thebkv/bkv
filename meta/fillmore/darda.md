# Darda

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** dSr'-da

## Meanings

- pearl of knowledge
- pearl of wisdom

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Mahol, but is no doubt the same person as Dara of I Chronicles 2:6. Darda was one of 
the wise men of Israel, but his wisdom was exceeded by that of Solomon (I Kings 4:31).

## Metaphysical

A perception by the individual of his true source of wisdom, Christ. As the 
realization is ahead of the perception of Truth, however, so Solomon exceeds Darda in that Solomon 
signifies the actual expressing and manifesting of the Christ wisdom. (See DARA.)
