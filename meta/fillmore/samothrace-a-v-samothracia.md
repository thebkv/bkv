# Samothrace (A. V., Samothracia)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Samothracia), sam'-o-thrace

## Meanings

- Samos of Thrace
- height of Thrace

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An island in the northern part of the ®gean Sea. Paul came to this island on his first missionary 
journey into Europe (Acts 16:11).

## Metaphysical

The interpretation is virtually the same as that of Samos, which see. The state of 
thought that Samos and Samothrace symbolize is intellectual rather than spiritual, since their location is 
in the Grecian Archipelago and Greece refers to the intellect.
