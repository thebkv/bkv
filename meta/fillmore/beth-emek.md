# Beth-emek

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-e'-mek

## Meanings

- house of the deep
- house of the unsearchable
- house of the

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

valley.

A boundary city of Asher (Josh. 19: 27).

## Metaphysical

The subconscious realm of mind in man, and the mistaken idea that the depths of 
the subconsciousness are past finding out (house of the deep; house of the unsearchable); also a 
depressed state of thought in the subconscious realm of man. This depressed state of mind can be built 
up and the nature of the thoughts in it can be changed to joy and gladness, strength and abundance, by 
the work of the Asher consciousness in their midst. (A valley always refers to a low place in 
consciousness-thoughts that need to be raised to a higher level.)
