# Madon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-don

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Canaan that was captured by Joshua. Its king was Jobab (Josh. 11:1; 12:19).

## Metaphysical

The established belief of the outer man that he must contend for his rights; that he 
will receive justice only by striving for it in a personal way (contention, strife, quarrel, from Dan, place 
of judgment). Madon's being conquered by Joshua bespeaks the introduction of a higher idea of justice 
into this thought realm, thus enlarging the vision and clothing the consciousness with true judgment 
(extension, vestment, garment).
