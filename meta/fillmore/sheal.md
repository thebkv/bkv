# Sheal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** she'-al

## Meanings

- digging out
- searching
- inquiring
- asking
- requesting
- desiring
- praying

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Bani. He had married a foreign woman after the return from the Babylonian captivity, 
but he gave her up at the command of Ezra (Ezra 10:29).

## Metaphysical

A thought activity in the higher consciousness of man (an Israelite) that 
SHEARIAH

is seeking earnestly after more of God (searching, inquiring, desiring, praying).
