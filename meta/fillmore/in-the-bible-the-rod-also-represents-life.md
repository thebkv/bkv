# In the Bible the rod also represents life

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_addendum

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The rod is a symbol of mastery, a symbol of our Christ dominion. When we have gained the 
Christ mastery, it is expressed in our thoughts as order and strength and in our body as life.

## Preserved OCR Source

In the Bible the rod also represents life. In this connection note that Aaron's rod burst into bloom 
and also turned into a serpent (an Oriental symbol of life). Aaron symbolizes the executive function of 
divine law. This power expressed itself through his rod. So the rod may rightly be considered a symbol 
of the power of divine life.
