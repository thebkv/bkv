# Moreh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mo'-reh

## Meanings

- teaching
- a teacher
- sage
- prophet
- importer of wisdom

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a An oak tree near Shechem where Abram came and built an altar to Jehovah, who appeared to 
him there (Gen. 12:6). It may have been a grove of oaks (Deut. 1 1:30). b A hill beside which the 
Midianites camped when Gideon went out against them (Judg. 7:1).

## Metaphysical

A state of mind receptive to Truth; a teachable state of mind. In a teachable state 
of mind the constructive methods that are always characteristic of the divine are revealed, and with this 
mind protection and strength (oak tree) are realized and victory is assured.
