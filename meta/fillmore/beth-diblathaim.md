# Beth-diblathaim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-dlb-la-tha'-im

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Moabitish town. It is thought to be the same place as Almon-diblathaim (Jer. 48:22).

## Metaphysical

An assemblage of thoughts of duality that have become established in man's 
consciousness; they are now ripe for judgment, or adjustment. (So we derive the meaning, house of 
assembly house of two fig cakes house of pressing together. Two fig cakes and pressing together give the 
suggestion that two kinds of thought, or a belief in both evil and good, are being carried in 
consciousness; and judgment is pronounced upon Beth-diblathaim, or this state of thought, by the 
prophet. See Jer. 48:21, 22). Moab stands for the carnal mind and the most external conditions of life, 
yet within man's inner consciousness the true idea of life and substance always abides. (See ALMON- 
DIB LATH AIM.)
