# Haruz

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-ruz

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"Haruz of Jotbah" was the father of Meshullemeth, who was the mother of Amon king of Judah 
(II Kings 21:19).

## Metaphysical

A thought that is active, analytical, persevering, serious, and vigilant in its 
character.
