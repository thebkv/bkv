# Shiza

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shi-zCE

## Meanings

- splendor
- lifting up
- captivating
- loving

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Adina, who was a chief of the Reubenites and one of David's mighty men (I Chron. 1

1:42).

## Metaphysical

The glory of true understanding working through love to the elevating of the 
consciousness (splendor, loving, lifting up, a Reubenite, Reuben referring to the seeing quality of the 
mind).
