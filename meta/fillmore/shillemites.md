# Shillemites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Shillem

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The "family" or descendants of Shillem, son of Naphtali (Num. 26:49).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and like that in consciousness which is signified by 
Shillem, son of Naphtali. (See SHILLEM.)
