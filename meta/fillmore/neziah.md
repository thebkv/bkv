# Neziah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ne-zi-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His "children" were among the Nethinim who returned from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra

2:54).

## Metaphysical

The idea of Christian service, lifted to its true, exalted place in consciousness 
(pre ‘minent, illustrious, perfected, not finite). Neziah was one of the Nethinim, and they were servants 
in the Temple. (See NETHINIM.)
