# Ithrite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Two men of David's guard were Ithrites, Ira and Gareb (II Sam. 23:38). (See I Chronicles 2:53.)

## Metaphysical

A thought springing from and resembling that in consciousness for which Jether 
stands. (See JETHER, ITHRA, and GAREB.)
