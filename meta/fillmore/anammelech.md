# Anammelech

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-nam'-me-lech

## Meanings

- king's statue
- king's rock
- kingly image

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The companion god of Adrammelech in the heathen worship of the Sepharites, and introduced 
into Samaria by them (II Kings 17:31).
