# Kohath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ko'-hath

## Meanings

- called together
- convocation
- assembly
- one who obeys a call
- an ally

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Second son of Levi, who was one of Jacob's twelve sons (Gen. 46:11).

## Metaphysical

The attracting, unifying, gathering element in love, and the power of love (Levi is 
the son of Jacob who represents love, and love is unifying in its nature).
