# Gadites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of Gad
- posterity of Gad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Gad, the son of Jacob (Deut. 3:12).

## Metaphysical

The assemblage of organized thoughts that belong to the power faculty in 
individual consciousness. These thoughts are presented as power because they are organized, assembled 
into troops or organs. The character "G" is the organic sign, associated with all organic ideas, 
conducting, leading, organizing.
