# Irad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** i'-rad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Enoch and grandson of Cain (Gen. 4:18). This was not the Enoch who walked with God.

## Metaphysical

Irad belongs to the outer physical consciousness and organism of man. He is the 
summing up of the stubborn, foolish, destructive, devouring— yet transitory and fleeting— emotions and 
desires that are the result of sense man's ignorant, confused thoughts and beliefs. (See ARAD; the two 
names seem to be identical, and the meanings are the same.)
