# Meshezabel (A.V., Meshezabeel)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Meshezabeel), me-shezO-a-bel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Named among Israelites who returned from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 3:4; 10:21; 11:24).

## Metaphysical

That in man's high, true thoughts (Israelites) which perceives and accepts divine 
deliverance from the confusion of mind signified by Babylon (God is deliverer, delivered by God).
