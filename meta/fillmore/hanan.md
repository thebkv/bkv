# Hanan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-nan

## Meanings

- favorably disposed
- gracious
- merciful
- compassionate

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There were several Israelitish men by this name; some of them were men of note (I Chron. 8:23, 
38; 11:43; Ezra 2: 46; Neh. 8:7; 10:22, 26; 13:13; Jer. 35:4).

## Metaphysical

Kindness, forgiveness, compassion. The religious and spiritual consciousness of 
man (the Israelitish nation) abounds, or should abound, in thoughts of this character. Thoughts of 
fullness of the grace, or power and all-sufficiency, of the divine presence also belong to man's highest 
spiritual concepts (Israelites).
