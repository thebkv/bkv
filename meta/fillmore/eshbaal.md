# Eshbaal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** esh'-ba-al

## Meanings

- man of Baal
- Baal's man
- fire of Baal
- will of the master
- dominant

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

will.

Son of Saul (I Chron. 8: 33); the same person as Ishbosheth.

## Metaphysical

Self-will and its disastrous end in the individual who gives way to it. (See 
ISHBOSHETH.)
