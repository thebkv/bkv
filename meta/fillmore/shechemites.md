# Shechemites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Shechem

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The family and descendants of Shechem son of Gilead, of Manasseh (Num. 26:31).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and partaking of the character of that in consciousness 
which Shechem signifies. (See SHECHEM.)
