# Dishan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** di'-shan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Seir the Horite, and a chief of the Horites (Gen. 36: 21).

DODANIM

## Metaphysical

A very active, rich, or fertile controlling thought belonging to the Horite 
consciousness in the individual. (See HORITES.)
