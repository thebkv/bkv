# Jaakobah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ja-a-ko'-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A prince of Simeon, an influential and prosperous man (I Chron. 4:36).

## Metaphysical

Jaakobah, like Jacob, pertains to the mental in man, which must supplant the 
animal, the fleshly, in consciousness, or, the spiritual, which must in turn take the place of the purely 
mental. (See JACOB.)
