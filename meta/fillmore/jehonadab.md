# Jehonadab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-hon'-a-dab

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Rechab (II Kings 10: 15). He is called Jonadab in Jeremiah 35:6.

## Metaphysical

The power (Rechab, meaning horseman ) of true thoughts in us actuated by I AM 
(whom Jehovah impels). This leads to a consciousness of the munificence and generosity with which 
spiritual gifts of the Father are imparted to His children. (See RECHAB and JONADAB.)

## Preserved OCR Source

Jehonadab, je-hon'-a-dab (Heb.)— whom Jehovah impels- Jehovah incites to follow freely; 
liberality of Jehovah; who gives voluntarily for Jah; munificence of Jehovah; freewill offering of Jah; 
abundance of Jehovah.
