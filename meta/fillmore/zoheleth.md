# Zoheleth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zo'-he-leth

## Meanings

- creeping
- crawling
- slippery
- sliding
- serpent
- timidity
- fearfulness

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A stone just outside Jerusalem, near the well of En-rogel, where the men who followed Adonijah 
gathered, and Adonijah sacrificed, sheep, oxen, and fatlings. This was when Adonijah attempted to 
make himself king of Israel in the place of his father David (I Kings 1:9).

## Metaphysical

The sense life, with its wisdom, which is of the outer man of carnality ( creeping, 
crawling, slippery, serpent). Adonijah's sacrificing sheep, oxen, and fatlings by this stone denotes the 
using of one's vitality, strength, and body substance to the furtherance of the sense life, or to the 
furtherance of the rule of the senses.
