# Neriah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ne-ri-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Baruch, to whom Jeremiah delivered the deed of the field in Anathoth that he bought 
from Hanamel his cousin (Jer. 32:12). Neriah is mentioned again, in Jeremiah 51:59, as the father of 
Seraiah.

## Metaphysical

The consciousness of the individual awakening to the truth that all true light or 
understanding is spiritual and exists in and radiates through the Christ mind in man, Jehovah (Jah is 
light, Jehovah enlightens, Jehovah spreads light abroad ).
