# Gishpa (A.V., Gispa)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Gispa), ,rlsh'-pa

## Meanings

- stroke
- caress
- fondle
- flattery

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An overseer of the Nethinim, who dwelt in Ophel after the return from the Babylonian captivity 
(Neh. 11:21).

## Metaphysical

Love for Truth or consideration of Truth, but directed more by sense thought than 
by spiritual thought.
