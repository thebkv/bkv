# Archevites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Chald
- **Pronunciation:** ar'-che-vltes

## Meanings

- belonging to Erech

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Supposed to have been inhabitants of Erech, a city of the kingdom founded by Nimrod in the 
land of Shinar (Ezra 4:9).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the state of consciousness that Erech signifies. (See 
ERECH.)
