# Aznoth-tabor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** az'-noth-ta'-bor

## Meanings

- peaks of Tabor
- ears or peaks of Tabor
- summits of

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Tabor.

A place on the western border of Naphtali (Josh. 19:34), evidently a mountain or mountains.

## Metaphysical

A twofold realization (peaks of Tabor, two mountain peaks; a mountain always 
represents a high plane of consciousness or a state of spiritual exaltation and realization) of strength 
(Naphtali) through the recognition that all strength is spiritual. When the material thought is removed 
from the strength faculty (which Naphtali signifies) it becomes more than doubled in consciousness in

the knowledge that it is founded in Spirit, God, and does not have its source or its sustaining power in 
the physical. There is therefore no limit to one's strength.
