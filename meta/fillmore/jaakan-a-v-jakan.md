# Jaakan (A. V., Jakan)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jakan), ja'-a-kan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ezer, a descendant of Seir (I Chron. 1:42). In Genesis 36:27 he is called Akan. He was 
the "father" of Bene -jaakan (Num. 33:1; Deut. 10:6).
