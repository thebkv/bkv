# Nogah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** no'-gah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of David, who was bom to him in Jerusalem (I Chron. 3:7).

## Metaphysical

Love ruling in consciousness does away with hate, prejudice, fear, narrow- 
mindedness, and the like, which darken and obscure the understanding. Thus the understanding becomes 
brighter and clearer, and shines with a truer, finer, purer light. David, king of Israel and father of Nogah, 
represents love as ruling in consciousness. Nogah is the clear, bright, shining, and unobstructed light of 
understanding.
