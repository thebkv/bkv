# Telah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** te'-lah

## Meanings

- breach
- rupture
- cleft
- fracture
- break

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Ephraim's from whom Joshua was descended (I Chron. 7:25).

## Metaphysical

A breaking-up, separating, putting-away tendency of the will (breach, rupture, 
cleft). This may pertain to a putting off of error or a breaking up of harmony and peace, according to 
whether the will is exercised in union with Truth or in personal consciousness. Ephraim refers to the 
will.
