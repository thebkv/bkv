# Geshem

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ge'-shem

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Arabian who scoffed at the Jews and opposed them in the rebuilding of the Temple and of 
Jerusalem's walls. He is the same man as Gashmu (Neh. 2:19; 6: 1).
