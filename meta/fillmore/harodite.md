# Harodite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Harod

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Two of David's warriors were Harodites (II Sam. 23:25); there must have been a town named 
Harod in connection with the spring of Harod.

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to the Harod consciousness in man. (See HAROD.) 
Shammah the Harodite bespeaks the desolation and destruction of fear, and Elika the Harodite 
symbolizes the elimination of fear. These two, therefore, do much toward keeping the ruling power of 
love (David) established in consciousness.
