# Lahad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** la'-had

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jahath, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:2).

## Metaphysical

Jahath, father of Lahad, represents the tearing down and the building up that 
attend every true overcoming of error in individual consciousness. (See JAHATH.) Ahumai, Lahad's

brother who is named with him, signifies a negative, denying, or letting-go state of thought, (See 
AHUMAI.) The negative meanings of Lahad (scorched, dark, oppression) do not belong to this man. He 
denotes the consciousness' coming up out of the seemingly negative attitude, into which it has been 
thrown in the process of putting away error, into a realization of conscious, joyous victory and strength 
(praising, glowing, bright, splendid, majestic).
