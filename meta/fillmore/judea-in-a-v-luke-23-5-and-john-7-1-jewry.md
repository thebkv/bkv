# Judea (in A. V., Luke 23:5 and John 7:1, Jewry)

- **Corpus:** MBD

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The southern division of Palestine; the land or province of Judah (Matt. 2:1).

## Metaphysical

A reference to the interpretation of Scriptural names shows Jud%a (now 
commonly spelled Judea) to mean "the praise of Jehovah." This then is a key to the mental attitude in 
which the Christ consciousness will be opened to us— while we are praising the Lord.

I AM (Jesus) dwelling in "praise of Jehovah" (Jud%a) identifies man with Christ thoughts and leads to 
greater expression of divine power. (See JUDAH, the tribe and kingdom.)
