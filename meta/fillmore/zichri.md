# Zichri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zich'-ri

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There were several Israelites by this name. They were of the tribes of Levi, Benjamin, Judah, and 
Ephraim (Exod. 6:21; I Chron. 8:19; II Chron. 17:16). Zichri of the tribe of Ephraim (II Chron. 28:7) is 
thought to be the same person as "the son of Tabeel" (Isa. 7:6).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts in man's higher, religious and spiritual consciousness (Israelites, some 
of them men of note) that keep calling his attention to the outworking of the law of Being, and to the

importance of taking cognizance of this law ( called to mind, remembered, recollected, remembrance, 
renowned ).
