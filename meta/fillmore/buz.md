# Buz

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** buz

## Meanings

- despicable
- contempt, despised

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Nahor, the brother of Abraham (Gen. 22:21). b A man of the Israelitish tribe of Dan (I 
Chron. 5:14). c Jeremiah mentions Buz as a people upon whom tribulations and judgments are to come 
(Jer. 25:23); the people of Buz are mentioned with Tema and Dedan, Arab tribes.

## Metaphysical

A scornful, scoffing state of thought; this is despicable in the light of Truth, and it 
cannot be productive of good.
