# Hadad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-dad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A king of Edom (Gen. 36:35).

HADAREZER

b A son of Ishmael (Gen. 25:15). C Hadad was the name of the chief Syrian deity and was the title of 
several of their kings, d An Edomite prince in Solomon's time (I Kings 11:14).

## Metaphysical

The setting up, as all-powerful, of the intellect in its spiritually unawakened state. 
Back of the intellect, however, back of every expression of intelligence or understanding, there exists the 
hidden principle of all light, all wisdom, all knowledge-God, Spirit.
