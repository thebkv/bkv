# Eliam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-ll'-am

## Meanings

- GW of the people
- God's people
- who are the people of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of Bathsheba, who was the wife of Uriah the Hittite. Bathsheba afterward became 
David's wife and the mother of Solomon (II Sam. 1 1:3). b One of David's mighty men, son of 
Ahithophel the Gilonite (II Sam. 23:34).

## Metaphysical

Our true thoughts are God's people. They are gathered together and established in 
consciousness by divine light and power.
