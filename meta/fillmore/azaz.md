# Azaz

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-zaz

## Meanings

- strong
- mighty powerful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite of the tribe of Reuben (I Chron. 5:8).

## Metaphysical

A realization and expression of strength and power (strong, mighty, powerful) . 
Strength and power are of spiritual origin. They can, however, be exercised either constructively or 
destructively, according to whether the thought held over them by the individual is spiritual or purely 
material and physical .
