# Haggites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Haggi

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Haggi, the son of Gad (Num. 26:15).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts pertaining to the realization of good that Haggi stands for in 
consciousness; thoughts that spring from this realization. (See HAGGAI.)
