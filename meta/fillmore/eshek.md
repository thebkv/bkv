# Eshek

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-shek

## Meanings

- oppression
- violence
- strife
- injury
- distress
- straits

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite who was descended from Saul (I Chron. 8:39).

## Metaphysical

The activity of the faith (Benjamin) thought in man, but directed and influenced 
by the selfish, oppressive, contentious dominance of the personal will (Saul). This brings about a very 
destructive attitude of mind. Faith must work by love in order to establish harmony, true freedom, and 
peace in man.
