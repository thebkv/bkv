# Nebat

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ne'-bat

## Meanings

- look
- behold
- regard
- see
- view
- perceive, i. e., with the eye

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Jeroboam, of the tribe of Ephraim (I Kings 1 1:26).

## Metaphysical

A willing or determining to look into matters, to perceive or understand (look, 
behold, regard, see, view, perceive, i. e., with the eye; the eye, or seeing, beholding, always relates to 
understanding).

Nebat was of the tribe of Ephraim (the will), and his son Jeroboam became the first king of Israel 
after the nation of Israel was divided into two kingdoms, Judah and Israel. Israel, the part that Jeroboam 
ruled, represents the objective consciousness in the individual.
