# Azariah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** az-a-rl'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Twenty-eight different persons of this name are mentioned in the Old Testament. In II Kings 15: 
1, Uzziah, king of Judah, see margin.

## Metaphysical

That in man which realizes Jehovah as a very present and efficient deliverer and 
help at all times, whatever the need may be ( help of Jehovah, whom Jah succors, whom Jehovah aids). 
In II Chronicles 26:8-21 Azariah signifies the spiritual quality of strength. (See UZZIAH.) In II 
Chronicles 31:13 the clause, "and Azariah the ruler of the house of God," refers to the I AM dominion 
within man, the Christ.
