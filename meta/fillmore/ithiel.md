# Ithiel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ith'-i-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Benjamite whose descendants returned from the Babylonian captivity (Neh. 1 1:7). b A 
name mentioned in Proverbs 30:1.

## Metaphysical

An awakening to the Christ consciousness of oneness with the Father: "Even as 
thou. Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be in us" (John 17:21).
