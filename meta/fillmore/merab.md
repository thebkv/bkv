# Merab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me'-rab

## Meanings

- increase
- enlargement
- greatness
- greatly multiplied
- myriad

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Eldest daughter of Saul (I Sam. 14:49). Saul promised her to David for his wife, but gave her to 
Adriel the Meholathite instead (I Sam. 18:17, 19).
