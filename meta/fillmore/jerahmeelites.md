# Jerahmeelites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Jerahmeel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Descendants of Jerahmeel, son of Hezron of Judah (I Sam. 27:10).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts springing from and belonging to that in consciousness which Jerahmeel 
signifies. (See JERAHMEEL.)
