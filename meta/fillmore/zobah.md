# Zobah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zo'-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Syrian kingdom that Saul and David both fought against (I Sam. 14 :47; II Sam. 10: 6).

## Metaphysical

A fixed error state of thought belonging to the sense intellectual realm of mind in 
man (settled, station, encampment, a Syrian kingdom in the time of Saul and David).
