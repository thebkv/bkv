# Nahalol

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** na'-ha-lol

## Meanings

- the same name as Nahalal. (See NAHALAL.)

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Canaan. It was allotted to Zebulun, but the Zebulunites did not drive the Canaanites out 
of this city (Judg. 1:30)

## Metaphysical

The significance is the same as that of NAHALAL, which see. That the 
Zebulunites failed to drive the Canaanites out of Nahalol suggests a sense rule over the elemental life 
forces of the organism that has not at this stage of individual unfoldment been fully lifted up.
