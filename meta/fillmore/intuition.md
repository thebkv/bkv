# intuition

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

There is a wisdom of the heart; it is called intuition. It is very much surer in 
guidance than the head. When one trusts Spirit and looks to it for understanding, a certain confidence in 
the invisible good develops in the soul. This faith awakens the so-called sixth sense, intuition or divine 
knowing.
