# moon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

Personal intelligence, the intellect. Its light is supplied by the sun, symbol of 
spiritual intelligence. This influx of spiritual intelligence has regular periods of flooding the 
consciousness, and there is a definite relation between the body consciousness and the moon.
