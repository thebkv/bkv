# Uzziel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** uz-zi-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A son of Kohath, and grandson of Levi (Exod. 6:18). b Son of Ishi, and a captain of the tribe of 
Simeon (I Chron. 4:42). c Son of Bela, and grandson of Benjamin (I Chron. 7:7). d There were others of 
this name.

## Metaphysical

Thoughts in the higher, more spiritual consciousness of man (Israelites) that 
recognize the qualities of strength and power as being not of material, but of divine, origin ( God is my 
strength, whom God strengthens, power of God, glory of God).
