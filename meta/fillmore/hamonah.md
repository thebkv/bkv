# Hamonah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ham-o'-nah

## Meanings

- host
- multitude
- noise
- tumult
- commotion of mind

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The prophetic name of a city that is mentioned in conjunction with Hamon-gog: "And Hamonah 
shall also be the name of a city. Thus shall they cleanse the land" (Ezek. 39:16).

## Metaphysical

The great multitude of true thoughts that shall be established in man's 
consciousness in place of the hosts of error, selfish, warring thoughts that are being overcome and put 
entirely out of existence, in so far as the individual is concerned. (See GOG and HAMON-GOG.)
