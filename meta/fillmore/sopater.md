# Sopater

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** sop'-a-ter

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Christian of Beroea, and son of Pyrrhus; he accompanied Paul as far as Asia on one of Paul's 
missionary trips (Acts 20:4).

## Metaphysical

The spiritually awakened or illumined understanding, that perceives the saving 
power of Spirit and defends the cause (as by argument, reasoning) of the true Source of all (the father 
who saves, savior or defender of the father).
