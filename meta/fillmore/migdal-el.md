# Migdal-el

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** migO-dal-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A fortified city of Naphtali (Josh. 19:38).

## Metaphysical

The exaltation of divine strength in consciousness. Naphtali refers to the faculty 
of strength; a tower bespeaks something lofty and exalted, and in the nature of a stronghold or fortress. 
This tower, being of God, denotes divine strength. (See MAGADAN.)
