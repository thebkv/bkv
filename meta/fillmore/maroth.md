# Maroth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-roth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Judah (Mic. 1:12).

## Metaphysical

"The inhabitant of Maroth waiteth anxiously for good, because evil is come down 
from Jehovah unto the gate of Jerusalem." The margin causes this text to read, "The inhabitress of 
Maroth is in travail for good."

Maroth ( bitterness , unhappiness, sorrows, griefs) signifies the soul's working out its salvation 
and, in the midst of hard, trying, painful experiences that seem to reach the very "gate of Jerusalem" (the 
spiritual or innermost heart center), holding steadfastly to the good. (See Rev. 12:2; Gal. 4:19, and Isa. 
53:11, first clause.)
