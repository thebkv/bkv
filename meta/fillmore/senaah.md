# Senaah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** se-na'-ah

## Meanings

- thorny
- bristling
- thorn bush
- thorn hedge
- brambly

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of either a city or a man, whose inhabitants or "children" returned from the 
Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:35).

## Metaphysical

An enemy to error (thorny, brambly, the name of a city or a man of Judah, whose 
inhabitants or "children" returned from the Babylonian captivity). Some authorities connect this name 
with Hassenaah. (See HASSENAAH.)
