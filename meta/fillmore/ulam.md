# Ulam

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** u'-lam

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a An Israelite, of the tribe of Manasseh (I Chron. 7: 16). b Son of Eshek, descended from Saul of 
the tribe of Benjamin (I Chron. 8:39).

## Metaphysical

Pioneer thoughts of understanding (a man of Manasseh) and of faith (a man of 
Benjamin) that go ahead, that enter first into new ideals and states of consciousness (anterior, 
beforehand, first of all, porch, vestibule).
