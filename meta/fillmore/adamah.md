# Adamah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-a-mah

## Meanings

- ground
- red earth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A fortified city of Naphtali (Josh. 19:36; also verses 32 and 35).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts in consciousness, or a thought center, that is quite firmly 
established in a belief in material or physical strength ( ground , red earth; a fortified city of Naphtali, 
Naphtali referring to the strength faculty in man) .
