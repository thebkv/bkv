# Zabud

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** za'-bud

## Meanings

- given
- presented
- bestowed
- endowed
- dowered

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Nathan. He was "chief minister, and the king's friend," in the reign of Solomon (I Kings 
4: 5). Zabad, Zabud, and Zabbud are virtually the same name.
