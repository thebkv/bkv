# Tabrimmon (A. V., Tabrimon)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Tabrimon), tab'-rim-mon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Benhadad, a king of Syria (I Kings 15:18).

## Metaphysical

A ruling thought in the sense intellect of man that believes in the power of and 
works in harmony with that which the Syrian god Rimmon denotes. (See RIMMON.)
