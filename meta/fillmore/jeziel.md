# Jeziel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je'-zi-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Azmaveth, a Benjamite who came to David at Ziklag (I Chron. 12:3).

## Metaphysical

Spiritual unity (whom God assembles, united of God, assembly of God) brought 
about by the activity of faith and love. (Azmaveth, father of Jeziel, a Benjamite who came to David at 
Ziklag, signifies faith's power to bring results; and David stands for love). These two working together 
in the consciousness of the individual accomplish a great unifying and harmonizing of his thoughts.
