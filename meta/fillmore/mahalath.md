# Mahalath

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-ha-lath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Daughter of Ishmael, who became a wife of Esau (Gen. 28:9). b Daughter of Jerimoth, the son 
of David; she became Rehoboam's wife (II Chron. 11:18).

## Metaphysical

A peaceful, harmonious, light, rhythmically active, and tuneful attitude of the soul, 
expressing on the carnal or human plane of the soul consciousness, and also on a higher and more 
enlightened plane.
