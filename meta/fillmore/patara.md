# Patara

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** pat'-a-rCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A seaport city in Asia Minor (Acts 21:1).

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts in the sense consciousness of man that are the result of 
suppression. These thoughts are of a browbeaten, subdued nature, and work toward bondage (trodden 
down, trampled under foot).
