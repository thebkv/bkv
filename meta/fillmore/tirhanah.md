# Tirhanah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tir'-ha-nah

## Meanings

- bending
- inclination
- kindness
- favor
- condescension

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Caleb, by Maacah his concubine (I Chron. 2:48).

## Metaphysical

A humble, kindly thought tendency, not stiff or unbending, in the enthusiastic, 
faith faculty of mind that Caleb signifies (bending, kindness, inclination, favor, condescension; a son of 
Caleb).

Tiria, tir'-i-CE (Heb agitation; trembling; excitement; reverence; worship; piety; awe; fear;

dread.

Son of Jehallelel, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4: 16).

Metaphysical. An undefined fear or dread, an inner agitation, trembling, or excitement, that 
sometimes comes over the personal in us when by means of prayer and praise (see JUDAH and 
JEHALLELEL) we have entered a very strong realization of the presence of God. We have not known 
that we are in truth spiritual; therefore we stand in awe, at times amounting to fear and dread, of God. 
Especially is this so when we come "face to face" with Him at the center of our being.
