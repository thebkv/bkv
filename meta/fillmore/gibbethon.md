# Gibbethon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Klb'-be-thon

## Meanings

- height
- eminence
- a raised place
- high
- a high house

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in Canaan that was allotted to the tribe of Dan and was given over to the Levites (Josh. 
19:44; 21:23).

## Metaphysical

A group of highly illumined thoughts in consciousness, that belong to the 
judgment faculty (Dan).
