# Jubal

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ju'-bal

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Lamech by his wife Adah. Jubal "was the father of all such as handle the harp and pipe"; 
in other words, he represents a principle of harmony, which might find expression in musical 
instruments. Some suppose him to have been the inventor of the harp and the mouth organ (Gen. 4:21).

## Metaphysical

The natural rhythm, harmony, and joy of life (a constant stream, music, source of 
joy) that are experienced when the soul radiates grace, beauty of thought and character, and comeliness 
(Adah, mother of Jubal), and the body is healthy and strong. (This latter is suggested in the meaning of 
Lamech, the name of the father of Jubal.)
