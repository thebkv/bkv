# Elidad

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-h'-dad

## Meanings

- God is love
- God loves
- loved of God
- God's beloved
- God nourishes

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Chislon, a Benjamite. He was the prince chosen from this tribe to help divide the land of 
Canaan among the Israelites (Num. 34:21).

## Metaphysical

A keen discrimination and insight into Truth, and into man's relation to the 
various phases of Truth. This spiritual insight is the product of an active faith (Benjamin) and love ( God 
is love, God loves) working in unison in man's consciousness; it is a great factor in helping to establish 
in their rightful places in the land of Canaan (the body consciousness) the true Israelitish thoughts, 
aspirations, qualities, and powers through which the body is to be redeemed, raised to spiritual 
expression and manifestation.
