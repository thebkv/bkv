# Megiddon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me-gidO-don

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A form of Megiddo. It is mentioned in Zechariah 12: 1 1 as a valley, a place of mourning: "In that 
day shall there be a great mourning in Jerusalem, as the mourning of Hadad-rimmon in the valley of 
Megiddon."

## Metaphysical

See MEGIDDO for the significance. See HADAD-RIMMON also, and you will 
understand why in this particular instance Zechariah refers to the valley of Megiddon as a place of 
mourning. It is a place of mourning only when seeming error has for the time being apparently replaced 
one's higher ideals and aspirations.
