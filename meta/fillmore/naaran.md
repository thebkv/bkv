# Naaran

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** na'-a-ran

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town of Ephraim (I Chron. 7:28). In Joshua 16:7 it is called Naarah.

## Metaphysical

The significance is virtually the same as that of NAARAH and NAARAI, which 
see. The idea of youthfulness in these names is that of a fully developed youth, of marriageable age.
