# Bidkar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** bld'-kar

## Meanings

- thrusting through
- son of stabbing, i. e., a stabber
- in pain

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A captain of Jehu's, who aided Jehu and his men in destroying all of Ahab's house (II Kings

9:25).

## Metaphysical

A piercing, destructive thought in individual consciousness, a strong belief in 
retribution (thrusting through, son of stabbing, a stabber), without the saving power of true justice, 
which includes mercy.
