# Netophah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ne-to'-phah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town in Judah, some of whose inhabitants returned from the Babylonian captivity (Ezra 2:22). 
It is named with Beth-lehem in Nehemiah 7:26.

## Metaphysical

Thoughts of Truth, from the ideal or Truth's primary essence, emanating from the 
inner source of one's being, little by little, as one is able to receive them (spontaneous dropping, 
distillation, speech, discourse, prophecy ). "For it is precept upon precept, precept upon precept; line 
upon line, line upon line; here a little, there a little" (Isa. 28: 10; in the margin "rule" is given in place of 
"line"; see HELDAI, the Netophathite).
