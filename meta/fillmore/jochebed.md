# Jochebed

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** joch'-e-bed

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Daughter of Levi, Jacob's son, wife of Amram, and mother of Moses, Aaron, and Miriam (Exod. 
6:20; Num. 26:59).

## Metaphysical

The religious soul of man exalting, honoring, and glorifying Jehovah, Christ, I 
AM, by expressing the love (Levi, the love faculty) and Truth of Spirit. When man glorifies God and 
exalts Him in his thoughts, man in turn is exalted and enriched (Jah makes great).
