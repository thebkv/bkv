# Dorcas

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** dor'-cas

## Meanings

- gazelle
- doe

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Christian woman at Joppa who was noted for her good works. She is called Tabitha also. She 
died, but was resurrected by the ministrations of Peter (Acts 9: 36-40).
