# Beth - azmaveth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth - az'-ma-veth

## Meanings

- house of Azmaveth
- house of death's strength

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town of Benjamin (Neh. 7:28); it is called Azmaveth in Ezra 2:24 and in Nehemiah 12:29.
