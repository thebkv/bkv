# Abida

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-bl'-da

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Midian (Gen. 25:4).

## Metaphysical

The belief that knowledge comes through the senses (father of knowledge, i. e., 
knowing). The Midianites, descendants of Midian, were enemies of the Israelites. They represent 
contentious thoughts, and judgment or discrimination in sense consciousness. The judgment of the 
senses, based on outer appearances, produces discordant thoughts, jealousies, and so forth.
