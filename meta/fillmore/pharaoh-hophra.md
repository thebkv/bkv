# Pharaoh Hophra

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pha'-raoh hoph'-rCE

## Meanings

- priest of the sun
- priest ofRa

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A king of Egypt, whom Jeremiah prophesied against (Jer. 44:30).

## Metaphysical

A ruling religious thought of the Egyptian phase of consciousness in man. (See 
EGYPT and PHARAOH.)
