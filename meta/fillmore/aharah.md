# Aharah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-har'-ah

## Meanings

- after the brother

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Benjamin (I Chron. 8:1.) He is called Ahiram, Aher, and Ehi in other places in the Bible; 
see Numbers 26:38; I Chronicles 7:12; Genesis 46:21.

## Metaphysical

That in man's spiritually awakening consciousness which follows after lofty, 
kindly, brotherly, constructive ideals ( after the brother; a Benjamite).
