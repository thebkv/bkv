# Maonites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Maon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Mentioned with the Sidonians and the Amalekites as a people that oppressed Israel (Judg.

10:12). They are thought to be the same people as the Meunim of II Chronicles 26:7.

## Metaphysical

Carnal thoughts that believe in the reality and abidingness of error. (See 
MEUNIM, meaning dwellings, homes, habitations.)
