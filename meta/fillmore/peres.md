# peres

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pe'-res

## Meanings

- cloven
- parted
- divided
- hoofed
- horse
- Persia

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A word that appeared in the handwriting on the wall that Daniel interpreted for King Belshazzar 
of Babylon: "PERES; thy kingdom is divided, and given to the Medes and Persians" (Dan. 5:28).

## Metaphysical

Here, PERES refers to the dividing or breaking up of sense confusion (Babylon).
