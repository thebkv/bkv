# sheep

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

Sheep are the most harmless and innocent of animals. They represent the natural 
life that flows into man's consciousness from Spirit. It is pure, innocent, guileless, and when we open 
our

mind to this realization of Spirit life we open the gate by the sheep market (John 5:2; also see this text in 
the A. V.).
