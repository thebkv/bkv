# Zeredah (A. V., Zereda, Zeredathah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Zereda, Zeredathah), zer'-e-dah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in Palestine that was the birthplace of Jeroboam.

## Metaphysical

As a city of Ephraim, Zeredah bespeaks the sharp, cold, piercing, penetrating 
nature of the will as expressing in personal consciousness, apart from the very needful balancing 
faculties of love and wisdom

The Zeredah of II Chronicles 4:17 has virtually the same significance as the Zarethan of 
I Kings 7 :46. (See ZARETHAN.)
