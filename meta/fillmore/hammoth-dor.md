# Hammoth-dor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ham'-moth-d™r

## Meanings

- hot springs of Dor

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Naphtali that was given to the Gershonite Levites (Josh. 21:32). It is supposed to he the 
same place as Hammath of Joshua 19:35 and Hammon of I Chronicles 6:76.

## Metaphysical

See HAMMATH and DOR. Strength must be established in the never failing, 
inexhaustible life and love of God in order to become abiding and enduring. Ian's thought of life must be 
cleansed from mortal beliefs.
