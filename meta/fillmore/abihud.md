# Abihud

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-bl'-hud

## Preserved OCR Source

Abihud, a-bl'-hud (Heb.)— father of majesty; father of vigor, youth; father of praise. Grandson of 
Benjamin (I Chron. 8:3). Metaphysical. The idea that true authority, praise, wholeness, and might have 
their inception in, and come from, God (Spirit).
