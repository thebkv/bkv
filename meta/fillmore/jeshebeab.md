# Jeshebeab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-sheb'-e-ab

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A priest in David's reign, who had charge of the fourteenth course in Temple service (I Chron.

24:13).

## Metaphysical

The fact that man is the dwelling place of God (seat of the father, temple of the 
Father ). The individual awakening to the truth that the heavenly Father's seat, abiding place, or 
established place of rule, is within man.
