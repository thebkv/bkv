# Helon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** he'-lon

## Meanings

- strong
- firm
- stable
- perseverance

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

His son Eliab, of the tribe of Zebulun, was chosen to stand with Moses and Aaron in the 
numbering of the men of the children of Israel (Num. 1: 9).

## Metaphysical

Strength of character, courage, and perseverance established in the consciousness 
through order. (Zebulun represents the faculty of order. Of the disciples of Jesus Christ, James the son of 
Alphus stands for order. Order, we are told, is heaven's first law. There can be no peace, harmony, or 
right relation of ideas without order. When Oall things" are thought and done "decently and in order" in 
the individual, when divine order is established in him, his strength and courage are greatly increased.)
