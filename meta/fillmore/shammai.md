# Shammai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sham'-mai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Onam, and father of Nadab and Abishur, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 2:28). b Two 
other men named among the descendants of Judah (I Chron. 2:44; 4: 17).

## Metaphysical

See SHAMHUTH.

Shammoth, sham'-moth (Heb amazement; awe; laid waste; desolation; destruction; ruin; 
rumor; report; repute; fame; renown.

One of David's mighty men (I Chron. 11:27). In II Samuel 23:25, he is called Shammah. 
Shamhuth of I Chronicles 27:8 is thought to be the same man.

Metaphysical. See SHAMHUTH.
