# Rabbah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** rab'-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A city of Ammon (Deut. 3:11). b A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:60).

## Metaphysical

Groups of thoughts whose central idea is greatness through numbers; the idea that 
anything becomes great by reason of its becoming increased in numbers (numerous, multiplied, 
accumulation, growth, increase, abundance, great, exalted, a great city, a metropolis).
