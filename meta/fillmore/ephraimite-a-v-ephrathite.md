# Ephraimite (A.V., Ephrathite)

- **Corpus:** MBD

## Meanings

- o/ or belonging to Ephraim

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A person who belonged to the Israelitish tribe of Ephraim (I Sam. 1:1).
