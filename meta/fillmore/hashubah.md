# Hashubah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha-shu'-bah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Zerubbabel, of royal lineage (I Chron. 3:20).

## Metaphysical

An inspiration, or enlightenment of thought, brought about by association with 
and a high estimation of true, spiritual ideals.
