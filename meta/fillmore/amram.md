# Amram

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** am'-ram

## Meanings

- kind red of the lofty One, i. e., of God
- exalted people
- high people

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Father of Moses and of Aaron (Exod. 6:20). b A son of Bani (Ezra 10:34).

## Metaphysical

A very high conception of oneness with God and of the true thoughts of man's 
consciousness as being emanations from the ideas of the one Mind (kindred of the lofty One, i. e., of 
God, exalted people ) .
