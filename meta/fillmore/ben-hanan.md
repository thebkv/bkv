# Ben-hanan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ben-ha'-nan

## Meanings

- son of kindness
- very gracious
- of one gracious
- son of grace

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Shimon, a descendant of Judah (I Chron. 4:20).

## Metaphysical

The quality of graciousness and kindliness (son of kindness, very gracious), which 
is greatly strengthened in consciousness and is given freedom of expression through praise (Judah).
