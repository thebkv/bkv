# Chisloth-tabor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** chls'-loth-ta'-b™r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Israel, on the side of Mount Tabor (Josh. 19: 12). It is called Tabor in verse 22, and is 
named as one of the cities of Issachar. It is the same city as Chesulloth.

## Metaphysical

The strength of high, exalted thoughts and ideals (loins of Tabor; loins refer to 
strength). (See TABOR and CHESULLOTH.)
