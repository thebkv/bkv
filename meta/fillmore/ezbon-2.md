# Ezbon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_symbol

## Metaphysical

An influential thought, belonging to the power faculty in man (Gad being the son 
of Jacob, and of the tribe of Israel that symbolizes the faculty of power in the individual), that is of an 
attentive, obedient, hearing attitude of mind (giving ear, hearkening, hearing). When man considers and 
lends his power toward making himself attentive and obedient to the things of Spirit, he will be heard of 
Jehovah (Jah hears) and his consciousness of power will be greatly increased.
