# Beth-lebaoth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-leb'-a-oth

## Meanings

- house of lionesses
- place of lionesses

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the land of Canaan that was allotted to the Israelitish tribe of Simeon (Josh. 19:6). It is 
called Lebaoth in Joshua 15:32.

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of conquering power (house of lionesses), governed by a willing, 
receptive, obedient spirit (Simeon) .
