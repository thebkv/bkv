# Galatia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- Gallic Greece
- land of the Gauls
- white as milk

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A province in Asia Minor where Paul did much teaching (Acts 16:6; 18:23).

## Metaphysical

Pure truths, but the simplest of truths, that belong to babes in Christ. In Acts 16:6 
Galatia signifies a state of thought that is not yet ready for the operation of the word of Truth (Paul).
