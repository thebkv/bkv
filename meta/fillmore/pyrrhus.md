# Pyrrhus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** pyr'-rhus

## Meanings

- fiery red
- firelike
- kindled
- inflamed

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Sopater, the latter a companion of Paul's on one of his missionary journeys (Acts 20:4).

## Metaphysical

A thought of great vitality, of abundant life, but not expressing so harmoniously 
as is needful if one is to keep in poise and health ( fiery red, firelike, kindled, inflamed).
