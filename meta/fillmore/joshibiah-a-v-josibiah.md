# Joshibiah (A. V., Josibiah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Josibiah), josh-i-biO-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Simeonite, father of a prince of Simeon (I Chron. 4:35).

## Metaphysical

Obedience to, and therefore conscious oneness with, Jehovah, I AM, Christ (seat 
of Jehovah, whom Jehovah causes to dwell, Jehovah enthrones). Joshibiah, at this stage of individual 
unfoldment, simply signifies a recognition and inner acknowledgment of the Truth; it has not as yet 
been worked out in actuality.
