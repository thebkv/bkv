# Milcom

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ml'-com

## Meanings

- their king

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A form of Molech, the god of the Ammonites. Solomon worshiped this god, along with gods of 
other heathen nations about Israel; therefore the kingdom was taken away from his descendants (I Kings 
1 1:5). Josiah, a later king of Judah, tore down the high places that Solomon had built for the worship of 
Milcom, Chemosh, and Ashtoreth, the gods of the Ammonites, Moabites, and Sidonians (II Kings 
23:13).
