# Iram

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** i'-ram

## Meanings

- urban
- city-wise
- congregation
- civic body

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A chief of Edom (Gen. 36:43). The Edomites were descended from Esau (see 40th to 43d

verses).

## Metaphysical

A thought of the outer physical or body consciousness (Edom) that pertains to 
cooperation, to wisdom in dwelling beside and harmonizing with other thoughts. While this thought is 
of a ruling and directive character (a chief), it cannot work alone; it belongs to a group of thoughts, a 
consciousness (congregation, civic body).
