# Jediael

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** je-dl'-a-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Benjamin (I Chron. 7:6). b Son of Shimri, one of David's mighty men (I Chron. 1 1:45). 
c A Manassite who came to David at Ziklag (I Chron. 12:20). d A son of Meshelemiah, a doorkeeper in 
the Temple (I Chron. 26:2).

## Metaphysical

The knowledge of God, spiritual knowing, becoming active in the awakening 
consciousness of the individual.
