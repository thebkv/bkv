# Urbanus

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Lat
- **Pronunciation:** r-ba'-nus

## Meanings

- of the city
- urbane
- cultivated
- refined
- polite

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Christian whom Paul saluted in his letter to the Christians at Rome (Rom. 16:9)

## Metaphysical

A very highly refined thought activity that has been awakened to and is actively 
expressing spiritual ideals (of the city, urbane, cultivated, refined, polite, a Christian at Rome who was 
one of Paul's fellow workers in Christ).
