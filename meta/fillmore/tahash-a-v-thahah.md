# Tahash (A. V., Thahah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Thahah), ta'-hash

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Nahor's son by his concubine Reumah (Gen. 22:24). Nahor was Abraham's brother.

## Metaphysical

An increase of life activity in the organism. At this phase of individual unfoldment 
this life activity is more animal than spiritual (commentators agree that the name Tahash refer to the skin 
of some animal, dyed red; red bespeaks the life activity).
