# /Enon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** 2'-non

## Meanings

- springs
- (natural) fountains

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place where John the Baptist was baptizing, "because there was much water there" (John

3:23).

## Metaphysical

John the Baptist represents the intellectual concept of Truth. His baptism 
symbolizes a mental cleansing through the word of denial. Water represents cleansing. A spring of water 
refers to a natural rising, in consciousness, of the cleansing, life-giving power of the thought and word of 
purification and life. /Enon was near Salim (peace), and so represents a purifying phase of thought that, 
while seemingly belonging to the natural, or outer, intellectual man, is closely associated with spiritual 
life and peace.
