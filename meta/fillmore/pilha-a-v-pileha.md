# Pilha (A.V., Pileha)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Pileha), pil-hCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One who joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant, a chief of the people (Neh. 10:24).

## Metaphysical

A breaking out of, away from, error, sense confusion (Babylon), by bringing forth 
a degree (a slice, a fragment) of dominion over one's vital thoughts and forces (a rider). Thus that which 
Pilha signifies becomes a server and worshiper of God, the divine principle of life and good, in the body 
temple (a husbandman, labor, work, sendee, worship; one who joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant 
after the return from Babylon).
