# Morashtite (A.V., Morasthite)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of ox belonging to Moresheth-gath

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An inhabitant of Moresheth-gath; here the name refers to Micah the prophet (Jer. 26: 18; Mic.

1 : 1 ).

See MORESHETH-GATH and MICAH.
