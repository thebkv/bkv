# Asarel (A. V., Asareel)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Asareel), as'-a-rel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jehallelel, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4: 16).

## Metaphysical

A certain restriction of error in consciousness, of the purely selfish and personal; 
this is the result of consecration to God (vow with God, oathbound, fettered by God) .
