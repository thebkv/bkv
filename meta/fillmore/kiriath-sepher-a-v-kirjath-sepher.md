# Kiriath-sepher (A. V., Kirjath-sepher)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Kirjath-sepher), kirO-i-ath-se'-pher

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A former name of the city of Debir (Josh. 15:15). Kiriath-sannah is the same place.

## Metaphysical

Thoughts relating to understanding. The central idea here seems to be that of 
storing up knowledge by the study of books, by memorizing, and so forth. The definitions of Kiriath- 
sepher point more to the outer than the inner. But man must rise to a comprehension of the truth that the 
source of true knowing is the Spirit of truth at the very center of his own being. (See DEBIR for further 
information regarding this secret inner place where man contacts divine wisdom.)
