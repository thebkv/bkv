# Abishua

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ab-;-shu'-a

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Third in descent from Aaron, and high priest of Israel (I Chron. 6:4, 50); grandson of Benjamin 
(I Chron. 8:4).

## Metaphysical

A ruling thought of God as being the source of deliverance and abundance (father 
of deliverance, my father is opulence).
