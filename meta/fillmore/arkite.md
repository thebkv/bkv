# Arkite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ark'-lte

## Meanings

- fugitive
- blind passions
- brutish appetites
- a tusk

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A member of a family or tribe descended from Canaan, son of Ham (Gen. 10:17).

## Metaphysical

A thought pertaining to the carnal consciousness in man.
