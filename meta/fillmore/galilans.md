# Galilans

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** gal-i-li'-ans

## Meanings

- o/'or belonging to Galilee
- rolled
- circle
- circuit

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Natives of Galilee (Acts 2:7. The text here refers especially to the disciples of Jesus who had just 
received the Holy Spirit baptism and were preaching the Gospel in "other tongues, as the Spirit gave 
them utterance."

## Metaphysical

Active life thoughts, illumined thoughts in the activity of life consciousness that 
exalt the Christ, they having received the Truth "at the feast" of spiritual communion and appropriation 
(John 4:45).
