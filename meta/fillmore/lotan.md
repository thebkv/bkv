# Lotan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** lo'-tan

## Meanings

- covered
- wrapped up
- hidden
- secret
- dark

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Seir the Horite (Gen. 36:20). He was a chief of the Horites, and his sister Timna was 
concubine to Eliphaz, Esau's son (see verses 12, 22, and 29 of the 36th chapter of Genesis).

## Metaphysical

A secret, hidden, ignorant ruling thought activity in the realm in man that the 
Horites symbolize. (See HORITES.)
