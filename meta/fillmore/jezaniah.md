# Jezaniah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jez-a-niO-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a "The son of the Maacathite," who came to Gedaliah at Mizpah (Jer. 40:8). He is called 
Jaazaniah in II Kings 25:23. b Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah is the same person as the Azariah of Jeremiah 
43:2. (See Jeremiah 42: 1, both text and margin.)

## Metaphysical

An uplifting thought in consciousness, based on the divine will (Jah determines ) 
and on the assurance that Jehovah hears us, perceives our true desires, and establishes them to us. The 
sharp weapon of Jehovah is the word of God (see Heb. 4:12). (See JAAZANIAH and AZARIAH.)
