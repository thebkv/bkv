# Shishak

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shi-shak

## Meanings

- present of a bag or pot
- like a river

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

King of Egypt, to whom Jeroboam fled from King Solomon of Israel (I Kings 1 1:40). Later, in 
the reign of Rehoboam, Solomon's son, this king came against Jerusalem and "took away the treasures 
of the house of Jehovah" (I Kings 14:25, 26).

## Metaphysical

A ruling thought over the phase of darkened, ignorant, sense consciousness that 
Egypt signifies.
