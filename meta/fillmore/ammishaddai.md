# Ammishaddai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** am-mi-shad'-dai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Ahiezer. Jehovah chose Ahiezer, through Moses, to be leader of the tribe of Dan at the 
time of the numbering of the Israelites in the wilderness (Num. 1: 12) .

## Metaphysical

The efficiency and the power of which man becomes conscious when he 
recognizes his unity with omnipotent God (people of the Almighty, my kinsman is the Almighty); also the 
assurance of divine oversight and supply (people of Providence) .
