# Dodavahu (A.V., Dodavah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Dodavah), dod-av'a-hu

## Meanings

- beloved of Jehovah
- Jehovah is loving

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Jehovah loves him.

A man of Mareshah, whose son Eliezer "prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying. Because thou 
hast joined thyself with Ahaziah, Jehovah hath destroyed thy works" (II Chron. 20:37; margin, "made a 
breach in").

## Metaphysical

The love of God in expression in individual consciousness (Jehovah is loving). 
Those who love God keep His commandments, and they are the beloved of Jehovah. They are blessed 
accordingly. The same may be said of that loving quality in us which Dodavahu signifies.
