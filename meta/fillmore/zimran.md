# Zimran

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zim'-ran

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Eldest son of Abraham by Keturah (Gen. 25:2).

## Metaphysical

A positive expression of joy, harmony, and grace (celebrated in song, sung, 
jubilee, dancer, gazelle ); the first conscious result of a union of the awakening faith in the individual 
(Abraham) with the aspiration of the soul for higher bodily attainment (Keturah).
