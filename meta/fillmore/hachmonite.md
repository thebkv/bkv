# Hachmonite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or pertaining to Hachmoni

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelitish family to whom Jashobeam and Jehiel belonged (I Chron. 11:11; 27:32).

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to the wisdom (Hachmoni) consciousness in man. (See 
HACHMONI.)
