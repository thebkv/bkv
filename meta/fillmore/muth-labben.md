# Muth-labben

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** muth'-lab'-ben

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A word mentioned in the title of the 9th Psalm.

## Metaphysical

The overcoming power of a holy life, or of thoughts untainted by limiting and 
error beliefs and activities (with virgin voice for boys, voice relating to power, and virgins to 
unadulterated Truth both in thought and in living). Even unto the death of the son here refers to the 
passing out from consciousness of thoughts and tendencies that connect one with the human limitations 
of generation. Forever and forever bespeaks the perpetuity of Truth.
