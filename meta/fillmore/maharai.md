# Maharai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma'-ha-rai

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"The Netophathite," one of David's guard (II Sam. 23:28).

## Metaphysical

A quickness of thought and action, based on spiritual intelligence and ability 
(quick, swift, prompt, apt, skilled) but with a very human tendency, as yet, to rush headlong, without due 
deliberation (impetuous, hasty, rash).
