## Metaphysical

The name means virtually the same as Ben-hanan. with perhaps more of a truly 
spiritual application. The expression in consciousness of this kindly, benevolent spirit (Ben-hesed— son 
of loving-kindness, son of mercy), is necessary, that the qualities needed to sustain peace and its 
associate thoughts (Solomon and his household) throughout one's being may be realized.
