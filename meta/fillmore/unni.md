# Unni

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** un'-ni

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Levite, a doorkeeper in the tabernacle, who was appointed one of the musicians to assist in 
bringing the Ark to Jerusalem, in the reign of David (I Chron. 15:18, 20).

## Metaphysical

Unni (labor, affliction, oppression, humbled, impoverished ) refers to the result, in 
man's consciousness and world, of the very human belief in hard work, labor, instead of expression in 
joyous, loving service. In other words it would typify the "bondservant" type of Christian service instead 
of the "son" type (see the 4th chapter of Galatians).
