# Hermons (A.V., Hermonites)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- the peaks of Hermon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The three peaks of Mount Hermon (Psalms 42:6).

## Metaphysical

The exaltation of the triune God, the lifting up, in conscious recognition by man, 
of the three phases, or activities, of the Infinite— Father, Son, and Holy Spirit— mind, idea, expression.
