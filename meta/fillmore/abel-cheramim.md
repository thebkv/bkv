# Abel-cheramim

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-bel-che-ra'-mim

## Meanings

- plain of the vineyards
- meadow of the vineyards

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A small town to the east of the Jordan (Judg. 1 1:33).

## Metaphysical

A fixed state of thought in which the idea of the substance of life predominates. 
Abel, or meadow, stands for substance, while a vineyard (grapes) always suggests life.
