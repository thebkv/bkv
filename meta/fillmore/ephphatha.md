# ephphatha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** eph'-pha-tha

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A word that was used by Jesus when He healed the man who was deaf and who had an 
impediment in his speech (Mark 7:34).

## Metaphysical

An inner freeing, healing thought or word of the Christ that releases all tension in 
consciousness and opens the mind and the body to spiritual receptivity and wholeness.
