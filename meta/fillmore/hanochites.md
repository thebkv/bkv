# Hanochites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Chanok or Hanoch

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The family, or descendants, of Hanoch the son of Reuben (Num. 26:5).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts that spring from and belong to that in consciousness which Hanoch 
symbolizes. (See HANOCH.)
