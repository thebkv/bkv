# Beri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-rl

## Meanings

- well
- fountain
- expound
- declare
- my son
- my productions

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Zophah, of the tribe of Asher (I Chron. 7:36).

## Metaphysical

A conception of the life idea and its constant activity in man (well, fountain). The 
thought of fruitfulness, of ability to perform, to do, to originate and bring forth, is also involved in the 
meaning of Beri (my son, my productions ). Beri suggests too that understanding quality in man which 
interprets the truth about life and substance and makes this truth clear to his consciousness (expound, 
declare ).
