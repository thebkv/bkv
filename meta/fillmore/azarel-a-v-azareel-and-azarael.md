# Azarel (A. V., Azareel and Azarael)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Azareel and Azarael), az'-ar-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The name of several Israelitish men (I Chron. 12:6; 25:18; 27:22; Ezra 10: 41; Neh. 12:36).

## Metaphysical

The attitude of mind in which the would-be overcomer recognizes and 
acknowledges God, Spirit, as his strength and his assistance in every kind of need (whom God succors).
