# Rachel (in A.V., Jeremiah 31:15, Rahel)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Jeremiah 31:15, Rahel), ra'-chel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The younger daughter of Laban, and one of Jacob's two wives (Gen. 29:28). She was the mother 
of Joseph and Benjamin (Gen. 30:24; 35:18).

## Metaphysical

The feminine, receptive, or soul phase of the pure natural life in the organism of 
man (ewe, lamb). This phase of life that Rachel signifies is as yet in a transitory state (migrating). It is 
fruitful only on the higher, spiritual plane of ideas. (Rachel was for a time barren; her two sons, Joseph 
and Benjamin, were the result of prayer, and they represent the faculties of imagination and active faith. 
They, especially Joseph, were accepted above their brothers, the other sons of Jacob who were the 
offspring of Leah— the human soul— and the two handmaids. Jacob loved Joseph and Benjamin more 
dearly than his other sons.)
