# Immanuel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** im-man'-u-el

## Meanings

- God with us
- God is with us

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A prophetic name of Jesus Christ (Isa. 7:14; Matt. 1:23). The Greek form is Emmanuel.

## Metaphysical

The consciousness that God is with us and we are one with Him. The 
understanding of how the "Word became flesh" in Jesus Christ and is now being made flesh in us even 
as it was in Him. "We shall be like him; for we shall see him even as he is" (I John 3:2).
