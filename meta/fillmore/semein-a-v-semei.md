# Semein (A. V., Semei)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** Semei), sem'-e-in

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Mattathias, and son of Josech, in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:26).

## Metaphysical

The understanding that attention and obedience to Spirit increase one's power of 
spiritual perception and discrimination, and truly exalt and ennoble the character (heard distinctly, 
understood, hearkening, obeying, famed).
