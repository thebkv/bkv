# Gomorrah (A.V., Matthew 10:15; Rom. 9:29; II Pet. 2:6; Jude 7, Gomorrha)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Matthew 10:15; Rom. 9:29; II Pet. 2:6; Jude 7, Gomorrha), go-mor'-rah

## Meanings

- -overbearing
- material force
- tyranny
- oppression

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of the plain. With Sodom it was destroyed by fire and brimstone rained upon it by 
Jehovah out of heaven (Gen. 19:24).

## Metaphysical

A state of mind in man that is adverse to the law of Spirit. It is submerged in sense 
and is very tyrannical in its nature. (See SODOM.)
