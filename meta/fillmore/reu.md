# Reu

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** re'-u

## Meanings

- leading to pasture
- feeding a flock
- shepherd
- friend
- companion

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Peleg and father of Serug, descended from Shem (Gen. 11:18).

## Metaphysical

The cooperative feeling, the feeling of fellowship (friend, companion), unfolding 
in individual consciousness to a sense of loving, active responsibility for the welfare of others (leading 
to pasture, feeding a flock, shepherd ).
