# Ira

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** i'-rCE

## Meanings

- wakeful
- watchful
- watcher
- citizen
- archangel

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Jairite who was chief minister to David (II Sam 20:26). b Son of Tkk esh the Tekoite (II Sam. 
23:26). c Ira the Ithrite (II Sam. 23:38).

## Metaphysical

A positive, watchful, prayerful, overcoming attitude of mind. This is a great 
protection to the individual who cultivates it. (See IKKESH.)
