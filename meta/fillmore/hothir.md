# Hothir

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ho'-thir

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Heman. He and his brothers were singers in the Temple worship (I Chron. 25:4).

## Metaphysical

A realization of abundant supply (abundance, fullness to overflowing) from the 
inner Spirit of harmony and wisdom. This fills the whole being and surpasses (excellence) anything that 
can be attained in outer intellectual or material searching. This inner knowledge of wisdom and Truth 
becomes the preserx’er, the source of sustenance, for the whole of man's organism.
