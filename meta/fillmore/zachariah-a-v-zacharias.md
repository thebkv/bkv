# Zachariah (A. V., Zacharias)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Zacharias), zach-ari'-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Barachiah, a righteous man whom Jesus accused the Jewish people of having slain (Matt. 
23 :35). Zechariah is the same name.

## Metaphysical

The significance is the same as that of Zacharias and Zechariah, spiritual 
consciousness (Jehovah has penetrated, Jehovah has remembered, concentrating on Jehovah, memory 
of Jah, Jah is renowned), or the entrance of spiritual thought into man's consciousness and a lifting up of 
spiritual understanding.
