# Omar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** o'-mSr

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Eliphaz, who was one of Esau's sons (Gen. 36:11).

## Metaphysical

The ability of the outer man to receive the higher Truth ideals ( mountaineer ; very 
elevated, summit) and to express them (bringing forth, eloquent, bearing into the light). (See ELIPHAZ 
and ESAU, in regard to Omar as referring to the outer man.)
