# Haahashtari

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha-a-hash'-ta-rl

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of "Ashhur the father of Tekoa," by his wife Naarah (I Chron. 4:6).

## Metaphysical

That in us which bears our high ideals and revelations of Truth quickly to any of 
the various centers or parts of the organism to which the I AM wishes to transmit them— a swift 
messenger of the king.
