# Jamlech

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jam'-lech

## Meanings

- whom He (God) makes king
- Jehovah rules
- he reigns

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A prince of Simeon (I Chron. 4:34).

## Metaphysical

A very prominent thought in the spiritually receptive, attentive, hearing attitude of 
mind in man (a prince of Simeon), which accepts the Christ, Jehovah, spiritual I AM, as the ruling light, 
intelligence, and power in consciousness (Jehovah rules).
