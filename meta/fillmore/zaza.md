# Zaza

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** za'-za

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Jonathan, and brother of Peleth, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 2 :33).

## Metaphysical

The love of God for mankind, as revealed in Christ. This love, when understood 
even in faintest degree by the individual, shines out above and beyond all else in his life and brings forth 
fruit (glittering, sparkling, projecting rays, emitting splendor, flowering, sprouting; a son of Jonathan, of 
the tribe of Judah, Jonathan suggesting the idea of love).
