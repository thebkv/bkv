# Sapphira

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** sap-phi-rCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Wife of Ananias, and his partner in the deception that brought about the death of both of them 
(Acts 5:1-11).

## Metaphysical

See ANANIAS.

A sapphire is a stone of a pure, deep, transparent blue, and blue is the color of Truth. Truth is 
beautiful, but this for which Sapphira stands in the individual must be cleansed from its greed and 
deception before it can really represent Truth in all its purity and can testify (narrator) only to that 
which is wholly true.
