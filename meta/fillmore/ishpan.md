# Ishpan

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ish-pan

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A chief of the tribe of Benjamin (I Chron. 8:22).

## Metaphysical

Faith (a Benjamite chief), a firm, unwavering place in consciousness, but tending 
to a belief in wearing out and dissipation of strength, through activity— the same as ISHPAH, which see.
