# Beth-el

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth'-el

## Meanings

- house of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

This town originally was named Luz. It is situated a few miles north of Jerusalem (Gen. 28: 19; 
35:1-15).

## Metaphysical

Luz means turning away departing. That which seemed separate and apart is 
brought into unity, and the name is then Beth-el. In the individual, Beth-el refers to a certain center near 
the heart, which is called the house of God. It seems material, at first sight. Jacob thought that it was 
material, when he lay down there with a stone for a pillow; but he found there a ladder reaching to 
heaven, and he exclaimed: "Surely Jehovah is in this place; and I knew it not" (Gen. 28: 16-22). So Beth- 
el really symbolizes a consciousness of God, or conscious unity with God.
