# Sorek

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** so'-rek

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A valley where lived Delilah the Philistine woman whom Samson loved and who was the direct 
cause of his downfall (Judg. 16:4).

## Metaphysical

A very material aspect of life (reddish, vine). This life is really of the true choice 
vine, or Christ life; but by the exercise of the phase of the soul that Delilah here signifies (sensuality, 
sense thought, belief, and desire) the life expression takes on a very material and corrupt appearance.
