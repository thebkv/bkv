# Hamulites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Hamul

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The family, or descendants, of Hamul of the tribe of Judah (Num. 26:21).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts belonging to the phase of mind in man that Hamul signifies. (See 
HAMUL.)
