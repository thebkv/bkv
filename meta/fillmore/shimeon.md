# Shimeon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** shimO-e-on

## Meanings

- a hearkening
- a famous one
- obedient

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"Of the sons of Harim," an Israelite. He had married a foreign woman, but gave her up at the 
command of Ezra (Ezra 10:31).

## Metaphysical

A lifting up of the Christ in consciousness (a famous one) by means of an attitude 
that is attentive and obedient to Spirit (a hearkening, obedient).
