# Pamach

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pSr'-nach

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Father of Elizaphan, a prince of the tribe of Zebulun who was chosen to help divide the land of 
Canaan amongst the Israelites (Num. 34:25).

## Metaphysical

A thought belonging to the order faculty in individual consciousness (Zebulun 
symbolizes the faculty of order) that is alert and active (swift, agile, nimble). If the other definitions of 
Parnach are considered, then the reference is to a thought tendency that is very loving and refined but 
needs to become established in strong, positive unselfishness, that the seeming weakness may be 
overcome.
