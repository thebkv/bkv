# Zereth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze'-reth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Ashhur and his wife Helah, of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:7).

## Metaphysical

The bright shining of spiritual understanding into and through the consciousness 
of the spiritually awakening individual (light, oil, splendor, brightness); this is the result of the freeing 
activity of prayer and praise (Judah). This name also bespeaks the manifestation of Truth in the outer life 
and affairs (appearing, coming into manifestation).
