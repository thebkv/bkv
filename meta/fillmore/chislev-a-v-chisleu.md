# Chislev (A.V., Chisleu)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Chisleu), ch;s'-lev

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The third month of the Jewish civil year, and the ninth of their ecclesiastical year (Neh. 1:1;

Zech. 7:1), beginning with the new moon in December. The name came from the Persian names of 
months, which the Jews used after their captivity in Babylon.

## Metaphysical

A hopeful, confident attitude of mind. In individual consciousness divisions of 
time refer to degrees of unfoldment. Things that are spiritual and eternal cannot be measured by days, 
months, and years, but by steps or degrees in unfoldment and attainment. (See TIME.)
