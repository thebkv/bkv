# Parshandatha

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** pSr-shan'-da-thCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of Haman's ten sons. He was put to death with his brothers (Esth. 9:7).

## Metaphysical

Persia means cutting, dividing. One of the results of the rule of the Adversary in 
man (signified by Haman and his father Hammedatha, the Jews' enemy) is a divided mind, a lack of 
peace, poise, and unity. This phase of mind is typified by Parshandatha, Haman's son.
