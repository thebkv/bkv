# Ahishahar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a-hlsh'-a-har

## Meanings

- brother of dawn
- brother of the dawn, i. e., early

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite warrior, son of Bilhan (I Chron. 7:10).

## Metaphysical

The breaking forth of new light into consciousness ( brother of dawn- that which is 
unified with light, understanding) .
