# Mattattah (A.V., Mattathah)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Mattathah), matO-tat-tah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Israelite, son of Hashum. He had taken a foreign wife, but put her away at the injunction of 
Ezra (Ezra 10:33).

## Metaphysical

A thought in the higher consciousness of man (an Israelite) that perceives the 
possibilities that man has through his true, or Christ, self— I AM, Jehovah. This thought is learning the 
necessity of eliminating all error and limitations of the soul in order to make these high attainments (he 
put away his foreign wife at the instigation of Ezra) and gain eternal life, which in its fullness is for the 
whole man— spirit, soul, and body.
