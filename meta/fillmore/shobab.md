# Shobab

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** sho'-bab

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

SHOBAI

a Son of David, born in Jerusalem (II Sam. 5:14). b Son of Caleb, by Azubah (I Chron. 2:18).

## Metaphysical

An apparent falling away from the consciousness of love, divine enthusiasm, and 
faith, that in their highest sense are signified by David and Caleb (turned away, apostate; rebellious, 
hostile, impious, backsliding).
