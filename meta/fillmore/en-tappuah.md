# En-tappuah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** en-tap'-pu-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A fountain, or spring, on the border of Manasseh (Josh. 17:7).

## Metaphysical

Tappuah means apple, or fragrant fruit of some kind; also fragrant breath, 
referring to inspiration and life. En-tappuah (fountain ofTappuah ) refers to the inbreathing life, 
intuition, discrimination, discernment springing into fruitfulness in mind, body, and affairs; in other 
words, bringing forth abundant health, vitality, good judgment, understanding, and substance. (See 
B ETH-T APPUAH and TAPPUAH.)
