# Zethar

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ze'-thar

## Meanings

- splendor
- brilliance- star
- conqueror

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the seven chamberlains, or eunuchs, "that ministered in the presence of Ahasuerus the 
king" (Esth. 1: 10).

## Metaphysical

A purely mental perception and amplification of conquest and dominion 
(splendor, brilliance, star, conqueror) that helps to sustain the puffed-up personal will (a chamberlain 
who ministered to King Ahasuerus of Media and Persia).
