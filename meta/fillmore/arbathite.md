# Arbathite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ar'-bath-lte

## Meanings

- belonging to Arabah
- of the sterile region of the desert

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a An inhabitant of the Arabah or a native of the city of Arab. A designation of Abi-albon, or 
Abiel, one of David's mighty men (II Sam. 23:31; I Chron. 11:32).

## Metaphysical

See ARABAH and ARAB.

When Abiel, or Abi-albon, the Arbathite, one of David's mighty men of valor (a strong, mighty 
thought that has its source in God), arises in consciousness, the restoring of these desert places (Arabah) 
to fruitfulness begins.
