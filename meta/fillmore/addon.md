# Addon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ad'-don

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Evidently a place in Babylonia whence some Jews who could not prove their Israelitish origin 
returned to Jerusalem from the Captivity (Neh. 7:61); called Addan in Ezra 2:59.

## Metaphysical

An aggregation of thoughts of great power and strength in consciousness (strong, 
powerful). These thoughts have their foundation in humility (low, a foundation stone), and they lead to 
freedom from sense confusion (Babylon). However, at a certain stage of his unfoldment the individual 
needs a clearer realization of his spiritual identity: the Jews who returned to Jerusalem from Addon 
could not prove their Israelitish origin.
