# Elul

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e'-lul

## Meanings

- the gleaning month
- vine
- outcry

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Sixth month of the Jewish sacred year, and twelfth month of their civil year (Neh. 6:15). It began 
in our September and lasted until the latter part of October, or into November. Authorities differ as to 
which of our months correspond to the Jewish months mentioned in the Bible.

## Metaphysical

A place of fruition in consciousness (the gleaning month), also the earnest desire 
of the soul for greater fruitfulness (outcry).
