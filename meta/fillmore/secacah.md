# Secacah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** se'-a- cah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Judah, in the wilderness (Josh. 15:61).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of security, of protection, that is built up in the soul by the 
unifying and interweaving action of high spiritual aspiration coupled with abundant praise of God— the 
good, perfect, true, and all-powerful source of all that really is (intem’oven, entwined, protection, 
envelopment, shade, defense, a city of Judah— Judah representing the life faculty in man, which, in its 
highest aspect, expresses as spiritual aspiration, praise).
