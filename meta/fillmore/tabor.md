# Tabor

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ta'-b™r

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A mountain in Palestine, on the border of Issachar (Josh. 19:22). It was from this mountain that 
Barak went to defeat Sisera and the army of Jabin, king of Canaan (Judg. 4:6, 12, 14). b The name of a 
Levitical city of Zebulun (I Chron. 6:77). c "The oak of Tabor" is mentioned in I Samuel 10:3. The 
reference here is believed to be to a grove of oak trees on or near the border of Benjamin.

## Metaphysical

A high place in consciousness, a very exalted group of thoughts, or thought center 
(heaped up, highest part, height, summit, mountain; a mountain in Palestine, a Levitical city, and a 
grove of oak trees, oaks denoting great strength and protection). "Tabor and Hermon rejoice in thy 
name." Mountains always stand for thoughts of a lofty character.
