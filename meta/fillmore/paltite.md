# Paltite

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name

## Meanings

- of or belonging to Palti

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"Helez the Paltite" was one of David's mighty warriors, and a member of his guard (II Sam. 
23:26). In I Chronicles 11:27 he is called "the Pelonite."

## Metaphysical

"The Pelonite" is thought to be the correct rendering. (See PELONITE.)
