# Selah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** se'-lah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A word that occurs many times in the Psalms.

## Metaphysical

The state of mind in prayer in which we relax from our affirmation of Truth and 
thanksgiving for good received and to be received, and "wait" on God in the stillness, that the Holy 
Spirit may reveal more of its inspirations to us and that it may establish our thoughts and our heart more 
firmly in the divine harmony and good.
