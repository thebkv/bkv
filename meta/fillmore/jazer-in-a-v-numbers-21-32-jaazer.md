# Jazer (in A. V., Numbers 21:32, Jaazer)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Numbers 21:32, Jaazer), ja'-zer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A place east of the Jordan that the Israelites took from the Amorites (Num. 21:32). It is 
mentioned with the land of Gilead (Num. 32:1), and in David's reign some of his Hebronite mighty men 
of valor were found at Jazer of Gilead (I Chron. 26:31).

## Metaphysical

Gilead signifies a high place in consciousness where Spirit discerns and witnesses 
to Truth and to all of man's thoughts and acts, that perfect adjustment may be made throughout mind and 
body.

Hebron signifies the front brain, the seat of conscious thought.

Jazer represents the deliverance, strength, and sustenance (help) that come to each individual 
who consciously lays hold of spiritual Truth.
