# Hadadezer

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** had-ad-e'-zer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Rehob and king of Zobah. Hadadezer was a seemingly powerful king defeated by David 
(II Sam. 8:3-12).

## Metaphysical

The ruling thought in the state of consciousness in man that believes that the 
intellect, directed by the senses, is man's greatest possible enlightener and help in all matters.
