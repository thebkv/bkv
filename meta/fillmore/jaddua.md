# Jaddua

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** jad-du'-CE

## Meanings

- skilled
- adept
- very knowing
- wizard

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a One who joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant (Neh. 10:21). b A son of Jonathan of the 
Levites (Neh. 12:11).

## Metaphysical

Thoughts, belonging to our true Israelitish consciousness, established in wisdom, 
understanding, good judgment, and skill (very knowing, skilled). Such a thought, unites with that in us 
which inspires us to higher and better things (Nehemiah), to aid us in keeping the spiritual law and so 
abiding in the blessings of God (one Jaddua joined Nehemiah in sealing the covenant).
