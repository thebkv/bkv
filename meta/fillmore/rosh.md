# Rosh

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** rosh

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Benjamin (Gen. 46:21).

## Metaphysical

Benjamin (son of the right hand, one of Jacob's sons by Rachel) represents an 
active faith. Rosh (principle, head, chief, first, highest, inclination, will) relates to the will. Since Rosh 
was a son of Benjamin the significance is that the will, having been given first place in the 
consciousness of the individual, is acting through faith, or in conjunction with it. (See RHESA, which is 
the Greek form of Rosh.)
