# Romamti - ezer

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ro-mam'-t; - e'-zer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A son of Heman; a musician in the house of Jehovah in David's reign. He was over the 24th 
division of singers (I Chron. 25:4, 31).

## Metaphysical

Praising and exalting God by music and song, especially in His character as helper 
of man. This that Romamti-ezer signifies acknowledges and lauds the power and willingness of God, 
omnipresent Spirit, to heal, to inspire, to uplift, and to restore the individual to spiritual perfection (I 
have exalted his help, I have raised up God's help, highest help, exaltation of help, a musician and 
singer in the house of Jehovah, and the son of a musician and singer).

Romans, ro'-mans (Lat.).

Roman citizens (John 11:48).

Metaphysical. Thoughts and their activities belonging to that in consciousness which Rome 
symbolizes. (See ROME.)
