# Beon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-on

## Meanings

- habitation
- dwelling
- refuge

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The same city as Baal-meon (Num. 32:3). In Hebrew, "b" and "m" have virtually the same sound 
and are often confused. Beon should have been rendered by the early Hebrew copyists as "Meon," a 
contraction of Baal-meon.
