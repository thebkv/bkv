# Hashabiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** hash-a-bli-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

There are several Israelitish men by this name (I Chron. 6:45; 9:14; 25:3, and others).

## Metaphysical

The understanding that Jehovah, the Christ of God, is unified with man, is man's 
intimate friend and associate in working out divine law into expression and true manifestation. The 
Spirit of Christ is active everywhere, in the whole of creation, not alone in man and in every living thing.

All are regarded by Him as worthy of and commanding His love and special, watchful care. God does 
not keep Himself separate from that which He has created, but is present everywhere and at all times, 
actively engaged in bringing about everlasting good.
