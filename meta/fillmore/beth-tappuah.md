# Beth-tappuah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-tap'-pu-ah

## Meanings

- house of apples
- place of fruit trees

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the hill country of Judah (Josh. 15:53).

## Metaphysical

A consciousness of fruitfulness, or abundance-a rich, prosperous consciousness 
(house of apples, place of fruit trees).
