# Hashem

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha'-shem

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Hashem, the Gizonite. His sons were among the warriors who belonged to David's guard (I 
Chron. 11:34). In II Samuel 23:32 he is called Jashen.

## Metaphysical

A thought activity whose central characteristic is that of riches and opulence (fat, 
opulent, rich, wealthy).

## Preserved OCR Source

Hashem, ha'-shem (Heb .)—fat; opulent; rich; wealthy; the named; the upright; the shining. The 
first four definitions of Hashem come from a construction and thought entirely different from the last 
three, and are preferable to the latter.
