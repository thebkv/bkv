# Tola

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** to'-lCE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Issachar and grandson of Jacob (Gen. 46:13). b The son of Puah of the Israelitish tribe 
of Issachar. This Tola was a judge of Israel for twenty-three years (Judg. 10: 1).

## Metaphysical

Life activity (crimson, scarlet, very red) on a seemingly low plane but in process 
of unfoldment to higher and greater expressions (a worm, coccus worm).
