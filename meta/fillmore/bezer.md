# Bezer

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** be'-zer

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A son of Zophah of the Israelitish tribe of Asher (1 Chron. 7: 37) . b A city of refuge in the 
wilderness, for the tribe of Reuben (Deut. 4:43).

## Metaphysical

Divine protection and strength, wherein no enemy of error can enter, but all error 
is restrained, cut off (a fortification, strong city, inaccessible). This consciousness of Truth is a city of 
refuge, a preserving, redeeming quality, in the individual who possesses it.
