# Lael

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** la'-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Lather of Eliasaph, who was prince of the house of the Gershonites (Num. 3:24)

## Metaphysical

A ruling thought belonging to that phase of the love faculty in man for which 
Gershon, son of Levi, stands. (See GERSHON.) This thought is of God, is turned toward God 
(Godward), and is consecrated to God; thus it does its part in elevating the consciousness of the 
individual to a higher and more spiritual standard.
