# Helem

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** he'-lem

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An Asherite (I Chron. 7:35). Zechariah speaks of Helem as one for whom the crowns were to be 
made (Zech. 6: 14); this Helem is evidently the same man as the Heldai of Zechariah 6: 10.

## Metaphysical

The same as the Heldai of Zechariah 6:10, the enduring essence of divine courage, 
strength, and overcoming power of the full-grown and the fully rounded-out man.

In the Helem of Zechariah 6, there is no worldly belief to hinder the expression of the divine 
qualities in true visioning ( dreaming ) and in healing. Therefore a "crown" of life is given to the Helem 
thought, since it has been faithful to the death of all that is of the carnal, worldly belief (see Zechariah 
6:14 and Revelation 2:10, last part).
