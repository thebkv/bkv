# hallelujah (in A.V., Revelation 19:1, Alleluia)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Revelation 19:1, Alleluia), hal-le-lu'-jah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

An expression of praise and joy that was commonly used in Hebrew worship. It stands at the 
beginning of many of the Psalms, in the form, "Praise ye Jehovah," with "hallelujah" given in the 
marginal notes. The Psalms were sung during worship in the Temple (Psalms 150, margin; Rev. 19:1).

## Metaphysical

The whole of man's religious and spiritual nature lifted up in thanksgiving and 
praise to Jehovah, the Christ, the Father within him.
