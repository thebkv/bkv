# Ezri

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ez'-rl

## Meanings

- my help
- help o/'Jchovah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

"And over them that did the work of the field for tillage of the ground was Ezri the son of 
Chelub" (I Chron. 27:26). This was during David's reign over Israel.

## Metaphysical

By denials and affirmations we cultivate the soil of our mind and of our body 
consciousness (ground); thus we make both ready for the bringing forth of abundant realization of 
substance and Truth. These denials of error and affirmations of Truth are, or they reveal, the help of 
Jehovah to us. They assist in making ready the way of the Lord throughout our entire consciousness, 
that better conditions in every way may be established.
