# Mahazioth

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ma-ha'-zi-oth

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Heman and a musician in the Temple, in David's reign (I Chron. 25:4, 30)

## Metaphysical

Clear seeing, keenness of perception, of comprehension; spiritual inspiration, 
expressing as harmony, order, rhythm, music, in individual consciousness.
