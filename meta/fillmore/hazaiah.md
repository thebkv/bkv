# Hazaiah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ha-za'-iah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

The ancestor of a Judahite who dwelt in Jerusalem after the return from the Babylonian captivity 
(Neh. 11:5).

## Metaphysical

The all-seeing, all-knowing Christ Spirit or Spirit of truth in man. This Spirit 
knows and understands (beholds) God, is known of God, and makes God known to man. "But when the 
Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which 
proceedeth from the Father, he shall bear witness of me" (John 15: 26).
