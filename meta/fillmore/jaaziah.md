# Jaaziah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ja-a-ziO-ah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Merari, of the tribe of Levi (I Chron. 24:26, 27).

## Metaphysical

The inner forces of Spirit strengthening, freeing, and lifting the consciousness out 
of the bitter, unhappy state of thought that Merari, father of Jaaziah, represents. This work of the inner 
forces of Spirit is to bring about in the individual a recognition and realization of the truth of his being, 
of his divine sonship. (This latter is suggested by Beno, son of Jaaziah, Beno meaning his son.)
