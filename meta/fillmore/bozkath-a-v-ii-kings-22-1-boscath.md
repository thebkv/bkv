# Bozkath (A. V., II Kings 22:1, Boscath)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** II Kings 22:1, Boscath), boz'-kath

## Meanings

- stony
- height
- craggy

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city in the lowlands of Judah (Josh. 15:39)

## Metaphysical

A group of thoughts which, though humble and lowly, is of an exalted nature 
(height), and firm and strong, though somewhat rugged in character (stony, craggy). Jedidah, mother of 
Josiah— Josiah was a good king of Judah -and Adaiah, her father, were of Bozkath; they signify 
thoughts that belong to the Bozkath phase of consciousness. (See ADAIAH and JEDIDAH.)
