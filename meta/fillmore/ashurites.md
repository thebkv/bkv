# Ashurites

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ash'-ur-ites

## Meanings

- belonging to Ashhur or Ashur

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A people who occupied the district of the plain of Esdraelon. They are named among those over 
whom Abner set Ishboseth, son of Saul, as king (II Sam. 2: 9); they were descendants of Ashur, no 
doubt.

## Metaphysical

Thoughts brought forth by the state of mind that Ashhur represents. (See 
ASHHUR.)

Ashvath, ash'-vath ( Heb.)- -forged; labored; smoothed; wrought; bright; polished; shining; 
thinking; purposing.

Son of Japhlet and descendant of Asher, Jacob's son (I Chron. 7:33).

Metaphysical. Ashvath suggests the process by which man brings the wholeness and perfection 
of Spirit into expression in his mind, body, and affairs. Realization and manifestation of completeness 
are not accomplished in an instant, nor by one great faith step. Moment by moment, day after day, and 
year after year one must work patiently, faithfully, and understandingly toward the goal of 
Christlikeness. This perfecting work is emphasized in the meaning of Ashvath (forged, labored, 
wrought, smoothed; polished, bright, shining, thinking, purposing), also in many Bible texts, such as 
Malachi 3:1-3; Philippians 3:12-14;

Corinthians 9:24, 25, and Isaiah 28: 10. "But the path of the righteous is as the dawning light.

That shineth more and more unto the perfect day."

(Prov. 4:18.)
