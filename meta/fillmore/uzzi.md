# Uzzi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** uz'-zi

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Son of Bukki, and descended from Aaron, of the Kohathite Levites (I Chron. 6:5). b Son of 
Tola and grandson of Issachar (I Chron. 7:2). c Son of Bela and grandson of Benjamin (I Chron. 7:7). d 
There were also others of the same name. The name is a contraction of UZZIAH, which see.

## Metaphysical

A realization of strength and power as coming from or through Jehovah, Christ, I 
AM (Jehovah is m J strength, Jah is strong, mighty is Jehovah, power of J ah).
