# Bene-berak

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** ben'-e-be'-rclk

## Meanings

- sons ofBerak
- sons of lightning

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A city of Canaan that was allotted to Dan (Josh. 19:45).

## Metaphysical

A quick, fiery, active state of consciousness in executing judgment (sons of 
lightning, a city of Dan, Dan meaning a judge). Unless these thoughts are guided by reason and are 
balanced by some of the other faculties of the mind— love, order, and the like— they al e likely to bring 
about disastrous results instead of the good ones desired. (See BARAK.)
