# Melchi

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** mel'-chi

## Meanings

- my king
- my ruler
- my counsel
- my judge
- of ox belonging to the king

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Two men named in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Luke 3:24, 28).

## Metaphysical

A phase of the will ruling in man (my king, my ruler); there is also a suggestion of 
understanding and good judgment, as well as dominion, in the meaning of this name (my counsel, my 
judge).
