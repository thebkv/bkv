# Methushael (A.V., Methusael)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Methusael), me-thuO-shCE-el

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A descendant of Cain's (Gen. 4:18).

## Metaphysical

The idea that man is a spiritual and perfect being is of God, and is inherent even in 
the body consciousness of man (Cain and his descendants refer to the outer or physical consciousness). 
Methushael refers also to the error idea of death, to disintegration of the outer organism, that 
unenlightened man thinks is desirable, inevitable, and of God. Thus unenlightened man is divided 
against himself, holds to both good and evil; as long as he does this he must reap accordingly.
