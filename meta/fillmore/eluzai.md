# Eluzai

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** e-lu'-za-i

## Meanings

- God is my strength
- God is my refuge
- God is my place of safety

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A Benjamite, a mighty man, who came to David at Ziklag when David was hiding there from 
Saul (I Chron. 12:5).

## Metaphysical

Faith in God as one's strength and as one's protection and vindication. This faith 
thought (Eluzai was a Benjamite) acts jointly with love (David) in consciousness, while the personal will 
(Saul) is still being dominated by the selfishness of sense and would kill out the spiritual quality of love 
if it could.
