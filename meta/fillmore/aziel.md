# Aziel

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** a'-z;-el

## Meanings

- strength of God
- might of God
- power of God

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A musician in David's reign (I Chron. 15:20); he is called Jaaziel in verse 18.

## Metaphysical

A thought of spiritual strength and power. (See JAAZIEL.)
