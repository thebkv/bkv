# Melech

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** me'-lech

## Meanings

- king
- ruler
- counselor
- judge

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Micah, a Benjamite, descended from Saul through Jonathan (I Chron. 8:35)

## Metaphysical

thoughts of the phase of consciousness to which it belongs. (King pertains to the will, or to a ruling
