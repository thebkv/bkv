# Hassenuah (in A.V. Hasenuah in I Chronicles 9:7; Senuah in Nehemiah 11: 9)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** has-se-nu'-ah 


## Meanings

- the thorny
- the bristling
- the pointed
- the violating
- the hateful

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a A Benjamite (I Chron. 9:7). b A man by this name is mentioned in Nehemiah 11:9. His son, 
Judah, was second over the city.

## Metaphysical

An active faith thought (Benjamite) in consciousness, which works direct to the 
point (the pointed ) in seeking to establish the individual in Truth. But there must be a lack of love, the 
harmonizing element, in the activity of this thought; it is thorny and bristling in its manner, and is 
looked upon as hateful and harsh (the violating) by the thoughts, tendencies, and habits that are not in 
line with Truth.
