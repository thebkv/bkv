# Jesher

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

Son of Caleb by Azubah his wife (I Chron. 2:18).

## Metaphysical

a life experiences and radiates light, peace, joy, and harmony.
