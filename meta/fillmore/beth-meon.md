# Beth-meon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** beth-me'-on

## Meanings

- Beth-baal-meon
- house of habitation
- place of dwelling

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A town of Reuben. It seems to have been in the hands of Moab at the time mentioned in 
Jeremiah 48:23; it is the same place as Baal-meon.
