# Lycia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** li-ci-CE

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A district in Asia Minor, part of it projecting into the Mediterranean Sea. Paul, while being taken 
to Rome as a prisoner, came to Myra, a city of Lycia (Acts 27:5).

## Metaphysical

A state of consciousness that is very intense, emotional, wild, and devouring in its 
nature (wolfish, rapacious, inflaming, destroying).
