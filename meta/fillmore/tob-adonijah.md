# Tob-adonijah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** tob-CEd-o-m'-jah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

One of the Levites whom Jehoshaphat sent throughout Judah to teach the book of the law to the 
people (II Chron. 17:8).

## Metaphysical

An acknowledgment of God as good. Especially does this refer to God in His 
character as I AM and as divine law— the working out of the divine idea of man into manifestation (my 
Lord Jehovah is good, goodness of the Lord Jah; "Lord" refers to God as law, while "Jehovah" is the 
Christ, or perfect-man idea in Divine Mind, and exists as the true self, the spiritual I AM, of every 
individual).
