# Naarah (in A.V., Joshua 16:7, Naarath)

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** Joshua 16:7, Naarath), na'-a-rah

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

a Wife of "Ashhur the father of Tekoa," of the tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4:5). b A town on the 
border of Ephraim (Josh. 16:7).

## Metaphysical

Ashhur represents the establishing of CE more firm and abiding idea regarding the 
body of man. (See ASH-HUR.) Naarah, wife of Ashhur (meaning girl, young virgin, posterity, youth, 
NABAL

poured out), refers to the soul's expressing the youthful, renewing thought that, unified with that which 
Ashhur signifies, aids in establishing this truth in the outer man.

The Naarah that was a border city of Ephraim signifies the will's (Ephraim) laying hold of and 
radiating the youth idea.
