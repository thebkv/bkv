# Hermon

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** her-mon

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A mountain that marked the boundary of the land beyond the Jordan that the Israelites took from 
the Amorites (Deut. 3:8; Josh. 12:1). Hermon is also mentioned in Psalms 89:12; 133:3, and Song of 
Solomon 4:8.

## Metaphysical

A high, sublime state of mind (lofty, prominent, sacred mountain).
