# Pisidia

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Gk
- **Pronunciation:** pi-sid'-i-CE

## Meanings

- pitchy
- tenacious
- clinging
- a channel of water

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A province in Asia Minor. One of the two Antiochs of the New Testament was a city of Pisidia 
(Acts 13:14).

## Metaphysical

One significance of Asia is that it refers to a state of consciousness impregnated 
by old, decayed, worn out, material ideas that should be left behind by the individual who would 
progress spiritually. Pisidia, a province in Asia Minor (pitchy, tenacious, clinging), signifies the 
apparent tenacity with which the old ideas represented by Asia stick to one. However, there is cleansing 
at hand (a channel of water; Paul taught the Truth in Pisidia).
