# Ziphah

- **Corpus:** MBD
- **Type:** mbd_name
- **Language:** Heb
- **Pronunciation:** zi'-phah

## Meanings

- flux
- fluidity
- liquid
- flowing
- pure
- refined
- clean

## Biblical / Dictionary Context

A brother of Ziph, and son of Jehallelel of the Israelitish tribe of Judah (I Chron. 4 :16).

## Metaphysical

The significance of Ziphah is the same as that of Ziph, a cleansing, purifying, 
refining thought in action in the consciousness. In Ziphah we also see established the effect of this 
thought in cleanliness and purity of thought (flux, flowing, clean, pure, refined).
