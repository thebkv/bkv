# A title of great honor applied by the Hebrews to their doctors and lawyers

- **Type:** general_term
- **Scan page:** 782

## Metaphysical

See RABBI.

In the foregoing text Rabboni refers to the risen Jesus, spiritual I AM in consciousness, as the 
great demonstrating teacher and ruling power. Teaching of Truth is done most effectively by living 
Truth and by demonstrating it in one's life; one must realize one's inherent I AM dominion in order to 
demonstrate fully the good that one is idealizing in mind. Mary used the word Rabboni as a term of 
endearment, in addition to the respect and honor that were due to Jesus as a great teacher and 
demonstrator.

## Scripture References

- John 20:16

## Source Text

A title of great honor applied by the Hebrews to their doctors and lawyers— teachers. It was 
applied by Mary Magdalene to the risen Jesus (John 20:16).
