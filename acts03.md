## 03 — RISE UP AND WALK

You may have lived with something for so long that you no longer expect it to change. The lame man has spent his life being carried to the temple gate, asking others for enough money to get through another day. But Peter gives him something greater than money. In the name of Jesus Christ, he commands him to rise and walk, takes him by the hand, and lifts him up. The man enters the temple on his own feet, walking, leaping, and praising God. **Jesus can raise you out of a condition you have never been able to overcome, and give you a new way to live.** Peter then tells the crowd to repent and turn to God, so that their sins may be blotted out and times of refreshing may come from the Lord.

## The Savior Test

> **“Rise, take up thy bed, and walk.” — John 5:8**

Jesus had already shown this same power when He healed the man at Bethesda. Now Peter acts in His name, and another man rises and walks. The risen Jesus continues His work through those who believe in Him.
